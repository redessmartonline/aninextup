# AniNextUp — image permissions review (2026-10-16)

## Evidence examined
The uploaded GitHub Actions artifact `adsense-editorial-evidence.zip` contains `adsense-image-rights-evidence.csv` with **234 occurrences of 141 distinct image URLs** across the 88-article inventory. These URLs were grouped by distinct asset and source type; they were **not individually licensed by their hosts**.

| Category | Distinct URLs | Review decision |
|---|---:|---|
| YouTube video thumbnails (`i.ytimg.com` / `img.youtube.com`) | 65 | NOT CLEARED for standalone republication |
| Third-party promotional image hosts | 47 | NOT CLEARED; rights-holder press terms or authorization required |
| Site-hosted or relative-path assets | 29 | PROVENANCE UNKNOWN; local hosting does not prove authorship |
| **Total** | **141** | **0 documented licenses in the supplied CSV** |

The source CSV marks all 234 occurrences `UNVERIFIED`, with blank license evidence. These statuses indicate **missing evidence**, not proof of infringement.

## Verified general platform and AdSense terms
- Google Publisher Policies prohibit copyright-infringing material: https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/10502938
- YouTube's terms explicitly allow showing videos through its embeddable player but do not, by that statement, grant general independent republication rights to video thumbnails: https://www.youtube.com/t/terms

## Remediation priority
1. **47 third-party promotional URLs:** check the copyright holder's press/media kit and explicit reuse terms (including commercial, territory, attribution and modification restrictions); request permission or substitute an authorized image if not supported.
2. **29 site-hosted URLs:** trace original author/source and retained license, including any downloaded anime promotional covers; classify genuinely original designs separately from compositions containing third-party artwork.
3. **65 YouTube thumbnails:** verify that each is displayed as part of a supported player/thumbnail integration, not treated as a freely licensed image; preserve official trailer playback while correcting standalone reuse when required.
4. Record the exact rights-holder terms URL or written grant and date **per distinct asset** before changing its status to CLEARED. If no evidence can be obtained, document a rights-based legal exception after competent review or replace the image with a genuinely authorized alternative.

**No copyright clearance, permission requests, image replacements or publication changes are claimed here.** The article inventory does not include copyright ownership documents. Do not delete covers en masse or disable trailers based on a missing-evidence flag alone.
