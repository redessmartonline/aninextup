Privacy Policy

This policy explains how information may be processed when you visit AniNextUp, including cookies, advertising services and embedded media.

Last updated: October 17, 2026.

AniNextUp is an informational website about anime releases and streaming availability. This policy explains the types of information that may be processed when you visit the site.

Server and technical data

Our hosting and infrastructure providers may process standard technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, requested pages and timestamps for security, reliability and traffic measurement.

Analytics

AniNextUp uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4), a service provided by Google, to understand how visitors use the site. GA4 may process information such as pages viewed, browser and device details, referral source and approximate location. We use these statistics to measure traffic and improve the site. For more information, read Google’s Privacy Policy.

Embedded media

Some articles embed official videos using YouTube's privacy-enhanced domain. When you interact with embedded media, the video provider may process information according to its own privacy policies.

Cookies and advertising

Use “Update cookies preferences” in the footer to change your choice. Google Analytics loads only when tracking is allowed, and the Google advertising script loads only when targeting is allowed. Changing your choice reloads the page.

AniNextUp may use Google AdSense and other advertising services to support the site. Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to AniNextUp or other websites.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables Google and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. Users can manage or opt out of personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings.

If other third-party advertising vendors or networks are used, they may also use cookies or similar technologies subject to their own privacy policies and opt-out mechanisms. Where required, AniNextUp will provide or use appropriate consent controls for advertising and related technologies.

External links

Our guides may link to streaming services, studios and other official sources. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

Policy updates

We may update this policy when site features or service providers change. The revision date above identifies the latest version.
