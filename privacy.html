Privacy Policy

Understand how AniNextUp handles technical data, contact messages, cookies, analytics, advertising and embedded videos, and how to manage your choices.

Email newsletter subscriptions

When you enter your email and submit the subscription form, your newsletter subscription becomes active immediately. No email confirmation step is required. We use your address to send a daily digest when new AniNextUp articles or updates are available. We store subscription status, signup dates and delivery progress in Cloudflare D1, and use Resend to deliver email. We do not publish your email address or add contact-form users to the newsletter.

Every digest includes an unsubscribe link. Unsubscribing stops future digests; a limited suppression record remains to prevent unwanted delivery. You can request deletion through our contact page. Temporary hashed request identifiers help limit abuse and expire after 48 hours. Any unconfirmed records left from the previous confirmation-based form are removed after seven days beyond their expiry. We do not sell newsletter addresses.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you visit AniNextUp, read an article, play an embedded trailer or contact us. AniNextUp is an independent website covering anime releases and streaming availability. This policy covers our website; it does not replace the policies of streaming platforms, video providers or other websites you choose to visit.

Who to contact about privacy

You can send a privacy question through our Contact page or email redessmartonline@gmail.com. Identify your request as a privacy matter and explain which interaction concerns you. Please avoid including sensitive documents, account passwords, payment information or private information about someone else.

Reading our articles does not require an AniNextUp account. Contacting us is optional. If you send a message, we need enough information to understand the request and, when appropriate, reply. Our About page explains the purpose of the site and our editorial identity.

Technical information when you visit

Delivering a webpage involves processing technical information. Our hosting and infrastructure services, including Cloudflare, may process your IP address, requested address, request time, browser details, device information and security signals. This supports page delivery, troubleshooting, reliability and protection against abusive requests.

These technical operations are separate from optional audience analytics. Rejecting analytics cookies does not prevent the network requests needed to load an article or deliver a message. An IP address can provide an approximate geographical indication; it should not be confused with permission to access a device's precise GPS location.

Information you provide through contact

Our contact form asks for your name, email address, selected subject and message. We use this information to review inquiries, investigate corrections or rights concerns, respond where appropriate and maintain relevant correspondence. Information you include in the message becomes part of that correspondence, so share only what is necessary.

The form is handled through our website infrastructure and is configured to use Resend for email delivery to our contact mailbox. Successful delivery depends on the service being available and correctly configured. If the form reports a delivery error, use the email address above. Your email provider and ours also process the message under their respective services.

The form includes an extra field intended to identify automated spam and validates required fields and message length. We do not need identity documents for an ordinary editorial correction. If you are making a privacy request, any verification should be proportionate to the information involved.

Google Analytics and audience measurement

AniNextUp uses Google Analytics 4 to understand readership and improve the site. When enabled, analytics can process information about viewed pages, interactions, referral sources, browser and device characteristics and approximate location. These statistics help us understand which guides readers use and where navigation or content needs improvement.

Our analytics initialization is assigned to the tracking category in the cookie consent system. It is configured to run when that category is allowed. We do not need your name or contact message to measure article traffic, and you should not put personal information into site search fields or page addresses.

Google's handling of information is described in Google's Privacy Policy. Information associated with your Google services and account settings is controlled through Google, rather than through an AniNextUp account. Our cookie choices concern this website and do not change settings across your other Google services.

Cookies and similar browser storage

Cookies are small pieces of information stored by a browser. Websites can also use other browser storage mechanisms. These technologies can remember choices, support functionality or help measure visits. Their purpose matters: remembering a privacy preference is different from identifying repeat visits for analytics or personalizing advertising.

Our cookie interface is supplied by TermsFeed. Loading that externally hosted component requires a connection to its provider. The consent configuration starts with the strictly necessary category and offers acceptance and rejection controls. A stored preference helps the interface remember your choice on later visits in the same browser.

Different browsers, private browsing sessions and cleared storage may have different choices. A selection on your phone does not automatically apply to your computer. If you clear cookies, you may be asked again. Browser restrictions or extensions may also affect whether third-party components load.

How to change your cookie choices

Select Update cookies preferences in the site footer to reopen the preferences center. Review the available categories and save your choice. The consent system is configured to refresh the page when a preference changes so that the selected settings can be applied.

You can also manage cookies through your browser's settings, including deleting stored cookies or restricting third-party storage. Blocking every cookie may affect remembered preferences. Withdrawing optional consent concerns subsequent processing; it does not automatically remove information already sent to a provider or erase existing cookies from every device.

If the preferences interface fails to open, you can use browser controls and contact us with the affected page and browser details. Do not send a complete browsing history or an unedited diagnostic file containing private information just to report a cookie issue.

Advertising and personalization

AniNextUp may use Google advertising services to support publication. Advertising technologies can process device identifiers, browsing interactions and information used to deliver or measure ads. Whether an advertisement appears can depend on the page, service availability, browser settings and applicable consent choices.

Our consent approach separates targeting from tracking. Where a Google advertising script is configured as targeting, that category governs its activation. Accepting analytics should not be understood as a request for personalized advertising. This policy does not mean that every article displays an ad or that any particular advertiser is endorsed by AniNextUp.

Google provides additional advertising controls through Google Ads Settings. Those controls supplement our website preferences. Provider settings, browser settings and our consent interface have different scopes; changing one does not necessarily change all the others.

YouTube trailers and external resources

Articles may include official trailers using YouTube's privacy-enhanced embed domain. Some players are created after you press a play button. A preview image, an existing embedded player or another externally hosted resource can still involve a request to a third party. The exact requests depend on the page and your interaction.

Playing an embedded video connects you with YouTube's service. The provider may process technical and interaction information under its own policies. The privacy-enhanced domain should not be interpreted as a promise that no information is transferred. You can read an article without choosing to play its trailer.

Links to studios, streaming platforms and sources lead to independently operated websites. Their account systems, payments, cookies and privacy choices are outside our control. A subscription or privacy request involving a streaming service should be directed to that service.

Service providers, disclosure and international processing

Information may be processed by providers involved in hosting, security, consent management, analytics, advertising and email delivery as described above. These services perform different functions and do not all receive the same information. An email delivery provider, for example, processes a submitted message; that is distinct from a video provider receiving a playback request.

Providers may operate infrastructure in multiple countries, so processing is not necessarily confined to your country. Their policies explain their own international processing arrangements. We may also need to disclose relevant information when required by applicable law or to address security, abuse or rights concerns.

Retention and security

Retention depends on the information and its purpose. Contact correspondence, infrastructure logs, consent records and analytics data can have different retention settings. We do not state a single deletion deadline for all of these systems. Provider retention and backup practices may also affect when information is removed.

We use the site's hosting and delivery protections, but no website or email system can promise absolute security. Limit the personal information you submit. If a message contains information you want removed, identify the correspondence when contacting us so that the request can be assessed.

Your requests and policy changes

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights concerning access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability or withdrawal of consent. Contact us to explain your request. These rights can be subject to conditions, and we may need to establish that a request concerns your information before disclosing it.

Our anime coverage is intended for a general readership. Children should not submit personal information without appropriate parental involvement. If you believe a child has sent personal information that requires attention, contact us and identify the relevant submission.

We update this policy when relevant practices or services change. The revision date above identifies this version. For questions about this policy or the handling of your information, use our Contact page or the email listed above.

Article comments

If you submit a comment, we store the article, your chosen display name, comment text, submission date, optional reply reference and moderation status in Cloudflare D1. We do not request an email address for comments. Approved names and comments are public; pending and rejected comments are not included in the public comment feed. Please avoid posting personal information. Display names are not verified identities. We process a short-lived hash derived from your IP address to limit spam; the raw IP is not saved in the comments database, and these rate-limit records are removed on subsequent submissions after 24 hours. Use our contact page to request removal or report a comment, including the article URL and comment identifier. Sharing buttons open the service you choose; copying a link does not send it to a social network.
