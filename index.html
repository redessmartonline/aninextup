HOW TO USE THIS GUIDE

Read anime release dates with the right context

A date can describe a Japanese broadcast, a theatrical premiere or a streaming launch. AniNextUp separates those events so a local premiere is not mistaken for worldwide availability.

HOW WE VERIFY →
01 · SCHEDULES

First premieres and weekly episodes

A first premiere is the initial release of a series, season, special or film. A weekly episode belongs to a series already airing. The Today and This Week pages keep these schedules separate.

02 · TERRITORIES

A release date has a market

A Japanese cinema or broadcast date does not automatically confirm an international streaming date. Platform and territory details are shown only where the available announcement supports them; otherwise the guide marks them as unconfirmed.

03 · SOURCES

Confirmed information stays distinct

We prioritize announcements from official sites, studios, distributors and streaming services. Rumors and missing details are not presented as confirmed facts. Open a guide’s source links or read our editorial policy.
