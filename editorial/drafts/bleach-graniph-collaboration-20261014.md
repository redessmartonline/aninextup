## Article draft (English)

Graniph has unveiled a new collection inspired by **BLEACH: Thousand-Year Blood War**, bringing designs based on the anime's Soul Reapers and their Zanpakutō to clothing and accessories. The collaboration was announced on **October 14, 2026**, with **17 items** in the lineup, according to Aniplex's official news listing.

The collection's **in-store release date in Japan is October 20, 2026**. Graniph is accepting advance online reservations, with its promotional double-points period listed through October 19. Availability, pricing and shipping options outside Japan should be checked directly on the retailer's official product pages.

### What is included in the BLEACH x Graniph collection?

The official catalog includes T-shirts, sweatshirts, hoodies, shirts, knitwear, trousers, outerwear, headwear, bags, a wallet and other small accessories. Several designs reference Zanpakutō release commands and characters from the series. A special collaboration gallery shows the designs in more detail.

### When does BLEACH x Graniph release?

Graniph lists **Tuesday, October 20, 2026**, as the in-store launch date. Its site also lists online advance reservations. Some accessories have purchase limits during October 14–20, so shoppers should consult the official product pages for the latest restrictions.

### Is this a new BLEACH anime announcement?

No. This announcement concerns licensed merchandise, **not** a new season, episode or streaming release. It should be categorized as a franchise collaboration rather than a broadcast update.
