## October 15: DayRe: reveals the official ending-theme music video

Sony Music announced the official music video for “Mikansei Ordinary” (ミカンセイオーディナリー) on October 15, 2026. Performed by DayRe:, the song serves as the ending theme for the mecha anime *Dandivine*. The label's announcement also introduced new single artwork and the complete track listing.

The artist music video should not be confused with the anime's creditless ending animation. They are distinct releases, even though both feature the same theme song.

### Watch the official “Mikansei Ordinary” music video

Sony Music links the video directly from its announcement. Use the existing AniNextUp lazy YouTube player component with ID `wNax51a6bAw`, linking to the official upload at https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wNax51a6bAw. Preserve the anime's existing main PV and fallback link.

### November 25 CD release and limited-edition artwork

The label currently lists **November 25, 2026** for the single's physical release. The regular CD is listed at ¥1,900 including tax, and the limited CD-plus-Blu-ray edition at ¥2,900. The limited edition jacket shows Dandivine characters Haru, Natsu and Aki in school uniforms.

These dates and prices refer to the Japanese music release, **not** to a new anime premiere or streaming date. Earlier reporting used a different CD date; the current label listing is the preferred source.

### Track credits and Blu-ray extras

Sony Music credits the title song's lyrics to 福森優光 (sixth floor) and composition and arrangement to 鶴﨑輝一. The other CD tracks are “truly moonly” and “標の花.” The limited-edition Blu-ray contains the artist music video and the anime's creditless ending, listed separately.

## What remains unchanged for viewers

*Dandivine* began airing in Japan on October 7, 2026, with Crunchyroll carrying the show in supported regions. The October 15 music announcement does not establish a revised broadcast schedule, new episode count, or additional international availability.
