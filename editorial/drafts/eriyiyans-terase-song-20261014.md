## Eriyiyans Releases “TERASE” Song and Collaboration Video Ahead of GROTESQQQUE

Aniplex announced the digital release of “TERASE,” an insert song featured in the Eriyiyans segment of the anime film GROTESQQQUE, on October 14, 2026. The official announcement also scheduled a collaboration video for 5 p.m. Japan time on the same day.

The track is performed by Mika Ichinose. Ken Kamikita wrote the lyrics, composed the music and arranged the song. Both artists contributed comments to the official announcement.

### When did TERASE come out?
The digital single launched on October 14, 2026. Fans can find supported listening services through the official music link: https://kmu.lnk.to/TERASE.

### What is the collaboration video?
Aniplex announced a video connecting the song with Eriyiyans and scheduled its premiere for October 14 at 17:00 Japan time. This is a music release and promotional video, not a new television-season announcement.

### When does GROTESQQQUE release?
Aniplex lists November 6, 2026 as the Japanese theatrical opening for GROTESQQQUE. No international release date is established by this music announcement.

### Who performs TERASE?
Mika Ichinose is the vocalist. Ken Kamikita is credited with lyrics, composition and arrangement. The official announcement includes remarks from both musicians.
