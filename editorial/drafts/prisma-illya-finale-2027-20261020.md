## Article text

Fate/kaleid liner Prisma Illya FINALE will begin Japanese television broadcasts in 2027 on TOKYO MX, ABC TV and AT-X. The franchise confirmed the broadcasters at its October 18 Machi Asobi stage event. This is a year-level announcement: the update does not supply a premiere month or first-episode time.

### Release at a glance

- Format: television anime continuing the Prisma Illya series.
- Confirmed window: 2027.
- Confirmed Japanese broadcasters: TOKYO MX, ABC TV and AT-X.
- Exact date, episode count and international streaming: not established by the cited announcements.

### What changed in the October announcement?

The 2027 television return had already been announced in July. The October update adds named Japanese broadcasters. Readers following the continuation should therefore treat this as a broadcast update, rather than a newly revealed release day.

The distinction also matters for overseas viewers. A Japanese channel announcement does not identify a streaming service for other countries, a simulcast hour or an English dub schedule.

### Where does FINALE fit?

KADOKAWA describes FINALE as the television conclusion of the Prisma Illya series, the Fate/stay night spinoff centered on Illya. The project continues an existing screen storyline; newcomers should not assume it is a standalone reboot.

The July announcement also revealed returning staff and cast alongside a second teaser visual. Those materials provide context for the continuation, while the later broadcaster notice is the source for the October news.

### Where can viewers watch it?

The confirmed options currently named in this update are Japanese television channels. An international platform should be added only when a distributor announces the license and its territories.

For the precise premiere schedule, follow the franchise's official notices. Neither a January date nor worldwide streaming availability should be inferred from the confirmed year.
