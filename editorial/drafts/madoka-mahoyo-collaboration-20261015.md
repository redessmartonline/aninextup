# Madoka Magica and Witch on the Holy Night Reveal Joint Anime Film Visual and Ticket Campaign

Two Aniplex-backed anime films have crossed paths in a newly announced promotional campaign in Japan. **Puella Magi Madoka Magica the Movie: Walpurgisnacht Rising** and **Witch on the Holy Night** now share an official collaboration visual and a ticket-linked poster giveaway, according to a dedicated Aniplex campaign page.

The collaboration connects a film already in Japanese theaters with another scheduled to open on **November 20, 2026**. It is a cross-promotion between separate productions, not an announcement that their characters will appear together in a new movie or television series.

## What was announced?

Aniplex has launched a campaign page presenting both films together, with promotional artwork and information about a two-film viewing promotion. The page also describes a theater banner and special flyers featuring the collaboration design.

The official campaign page identifies **Walpurgisnacht Rising** as currently screening and lists **Witch on the Holy Night** for November 20. This is promotional artwork, not a new standalone anime release.

## How does the two-film ticket campaign work?

### Eligibility and entry dates

The Japanese promotion asks applicants to combine evidence of watching **Walpurgisnacht Rising**—a ticket stub photo or admission QR-code screenshot—with a **Witch on the Holy Night** advance ticket. The advertised prize is a collaboration poster.

The qualifying viewing window runs **October 14 through November 6, 2026, at 11:59 p.m. Japan time**. Entries are accepted **October 14 through November 9, 2026, at 11:59 p.m. Japan time**. Readers should check the official campaign's detailed rules for restrictions and eligibility before applying. No international giveaway is confirmed.

### Where can fans see the artwork in person?

Aniplex says a large banner featuring the visual will be displayed at **Shinjuku Wald 9** from **October 17–29, 2026**. Promotional B5 flyers are scheduled for Shinjuku Wald 9 from **October 17 at 10 a.m.**, and at Machi Asobi in Tokushima on **October 17 and 18 at 10 a.m.** while supplies last.

## When does Witch on the Holy Night open?

The Japanese theatrical release is scheduled for **November 20, 2026**. Aniplex previously confirmed the date in its official film news. The collaboration does not establish an international theatrical or streaming date.
