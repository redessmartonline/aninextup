## Proposed article

The anime adaptation of Kyoko Hikawa's *From Far Away* (*Kanata kara*) has shared a creditless opening video as its October 2026 television run gets underway. The opening uses the song "Touhikou" by Genin wa Jibun ni Aru., according to reporting on the October 13 announcement.

The fantasy story follows Noriko, who finds herself in an unfamiliar world and encounters the mysterious warrior Izark. The adaptation is produced by Studio DEEN and directed by Noriyuki Abe.

### What is a creditless opening?

A creditless sequence presents the opening animation without overlaid staff and cast credits, making it easier to view the artwork and choreography. This is a promotional video, not a new episode or a new-season announcement.
