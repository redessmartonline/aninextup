The fantasy anime *I Lost My Adventurer's License, but It's Fine Because I Have an Adorable Daughter Now* will air in Japan in January 2027. The October 15 production update also unveiled a new teaser promotional video and visual, plus the two lead actors. The exact broadcast day and international streaming service have not been confirmed.

## When is the anime coming out?

The official website confirms **January 2027** as the Japanese broadcast window. No exact date, time or episode count has been given. Fans should not confuse the confirmed month with a specific premiere day.

## Who voices Douglas and Rabi?

### Hiroki Yasumoto as Douglas

Douglas is a former powerful enhancement mage who loses his adventurer's license and the position that defined his life.

### Yuka Iguchi as Rabi

Rabi is a girl affected by a curse. Her meeting with Douglas leads to a new journey centered on the bond they gradually build.

## What is the anime about?

Adapted from Manimani Ononata's fantasy light novels, the story begins with Douglas facing an uncertain future after leaving his former hero party. Instead of chasing the rank he lost, he travels with Rabi, helping others as their relationship develops into a family-like connection. The teaser offers an early look at this gentler side of the fantasy-adventure genre.

## Production staff

The official staff page names 稲葉友紀 as director, series composer and screenwriter, 吉川佳織 as character designer and chief animation director, and studioぱれっと as the animation studio. Music credits go to 王舟 and 奥野大樹, with Space Shower Music overseeing music production. The original character designs are by Fuzichoco.
