## From Far Away Anime Releases Creditless Opening Video

The 2026 TV anime adaptation of Kyoko Hikawa's *From Far Away* (*Kanata Kara*) has shared its opening animation without staff credits. The video accompanies “Touhikou” (逃光行), performed by Genin wa Jibun ni Aru. The release offers a clearer look at the fantasy world and characters already introduced in the television series. It does **not** announce a new season.

## Opening and ending music

The production's official music page names Genin wa Jibun ni Aru for “Touhikou” (逃光行). The ending, “Hikari no Soba de” (光の側で), is performed by Nanashi no Taro. These are distinct releases.

## Premiere, staff and story

The anime premiered in Japan on October 4, 2026 and is available through Crunchyroll in supported territories. Noriyuki Abe directs at Studio DEEN, with Rika Takasugi handling series composition, Eiji Abiko designing characters and Takeshi Hama composing music. The story follows Noriko, a schoolgirl transported to a world whose language she does not understand, and Izark, a swordsman involved in a dangerous prophecy.
