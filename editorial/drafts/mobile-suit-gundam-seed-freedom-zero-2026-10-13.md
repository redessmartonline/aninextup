## When is Gundam SEED FREEDOM ZERO coming out?

The official Japanese Gundam site confirmed **Friday, January 15, 2027** as the domestic theatrical opening date in its October 9, 2026 announcement. The North American official site separately announced a **February 2027** cinema window, without a specific date or list of participating theaters. These are two different release windows; the Japanese date should not be presented as the North American date.

The announcement also unveiled a teaser visual featuring Shinn Asuka, with Kira Yamato and Athrun Zala behind him, and a new trailer.

## What is the story about?

The film takes place in **Cosmic Era 74** following the collapse of Gilbert Durandal's Destiny Plan. Cagalli Yula Athha and others are working toward a new international peacekeeping organization, COMPASS, as Shinn struggles to find his place after the previous conflict.

When mobile suits are moved to a provisional COMPASS base on Onogoro Island, the **Strike Freedom Gundam is stolen**. Shinn and his allies are drawn into another conflict as they attempt to recover the mobile suit and prevent the incident from escalating.

The story was developed from material written during the planning period of *SEED FREEDOM*, with screenplay credits including Chiaki Morosawa, Shigeru Morita and Mitsuo Fukuda. It is a **prequel to SEED FREEDOM**, not a sequel set after the 2024 movie.

## Confirmed Japanese cast

The production's official staff and cast page lists:

- **Kenichi Suzumura** — Shinn Asuka
- **Maaya Sakamoto** — Lunamaria Hawke
- **Houko Kuwashima** — Agnes Giebenrath
- **Daisuke Ono** — Leonard Valway
- **Soichiro Hoshi** — Kira Yamato
- **Akira Ishida** — Athrun Zala
- **Tomokazu Seki** — Yzak Jule

For the English-language version, the North American announcement confirms **Matthew Erickson** as Shinn, **Matt Hill** as Kira and **Sam Vincent** as Athrun.

## Who is making the film?

**Mitsuo Fukuda** directs the movie, with **Hisashi Hirai** credited for character design and animation direction. **Toshihiko Sahashi** provides the music. The official staff listing credits SUNRISE with planning and production and Bandai Namco Filmworks with production.
