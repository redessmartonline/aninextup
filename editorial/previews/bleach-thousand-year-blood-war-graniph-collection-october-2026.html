BLEACH x Graniph Collection: 17 Items Launch October 20

Graniph has announced 17 BLEACH Thousand-Year Blood War collaboration items, with an October 20, 2026 Japanese in-store launch.

By AniNextUp Editorial · October 14, 2026

Release at a glance

Subject: BLEACH: Thousand-Year Blood War · Confirmed date: October 20, 2026 (Japan stores)

What was announced?

Graniph has revealed a collection of 17 BLEACH: Thousand-Year Blood War items inspired by Zanpakuto releases. The range includes apparel, bags and accessories. Online preorders are offered, while Japanese stores list an October 20, 2026 launch. This is a merchandise collaboration, not a new anime release.
