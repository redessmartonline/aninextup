Eriyiyans TERASE Song Released Ahead of GROTESQQQUE Film

Aniplex announced the October 14, 2026 TERASE song release and collaboration video for Eriyiyans, ahead of the GROTESQQQUE film.

By AniNextUp Editorial · October 14, 2026

Release at a glance

Subject: GROTESQQQUE — Eriyiyans · Confirmed date: October 14, 2026 (song); November 6, 2026 (film, Japan)

What was announced?

The song TERASE is performed by Mika Ichinose, with lyrics, composition and arrangement by Ken Kamikita. Aniplex announced the digital single on October 14, 2026 and scheduled a collaboration video for 5 p.m. Japan time that day. GROTESQQQUE is scheduled to open in Japanese theaters on November 6, 2026. The video premiere time is not the original article publication time.

Read the Aniplex official TERASE announcement and additional official information. Dates outside Japan and any unannounced streaming availability should not be assumed.
