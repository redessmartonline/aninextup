ABOUT THIS SCHEDULE

How to read today’s anime schedule

Today combines two different kinds of releases. The sections above label them separately so viewers can tell a new premiere from another episode in an ongoing season.

01 · WEEKLY EPISODES

Episode dates and local times

The current weekly selection follows Crunchyroll’s Brazil calendar, with Japanese audio and Portuguese subtitles. Times use Brasília time (UTC−3). They do not establish availability or release hours in other countries.

02 · FIRST PREMIERES

New series, seasons and films

This section is for a title’s first scheduled release, including a new series, season, special or movie. It does not count each weekly episode as another premiere. Dates may refer to Japanese theaters or broadcasts when no international launch has been announced.

03 · CHANGES

Availability can vary

Schedules can change, and a platform announcement may apply only to certain territories. We mark information that has not been confirmed and link to the editorial policy and contact page for sourcing and corrections.

For the latest weekly lineup, check Crunchyroll’s official schedule. For release dates across months, use the anime release calendar.

VIEWING QUESTIONS

What anime is releasing today?

Are these new anime series or new episodes?

The weekly episode section covers installments of ongoing series in the stated regional schedule. First premieres are listed separately for new series, seasons, specials and films. A day without a new premiere can still have weekly episodes.

Can I use these times for the US or UK?

The current episode selection covers Brazil in Brasília time, with Japanese audio and Portuguese subtitles. It does not verify US or UK release times, English subtitles or English dubs. Open the individual streaming guide to check what is known for the title and region.

Why can a weekly schedule be unverified?

A previous week’s episode numbers and times do not establish today’s lineup. When the verified date range expires, we display a coverage notice until an official schedule can be checked. An empty list is not proof that nothing is airing.

Where can I find anime coming out next month?

Use our November 2026 anime release dates and streaming guide for dated premieres. Use anime releasing this week to plan the current week and the anime release calendar for later dates.

Does a Japan release date apply in my country?

A Japanese broadcast or cinema opening does not automatically confirm a local streaming release. Open the individual viewing guide for the announced platform, territory and remaining unknowns.
