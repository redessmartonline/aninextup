ABOUT THIS SCHEDULE

How to read today’s anime schedule

Today combines two different kinds of releases. The sections above label them separately so viewers can tell a new premiere from another episode in an ongoing season.

01 · WEEKLY EPISODES

Episode dates and local times

The weekly list follows the published Crunchyroll Fall 2026 lineup. Choose one of the listed region groups to see its displayed time. A date can shift to the next calendar day in some regions; check the label shown beside the time.

02 · FIRST PREMIERES

New series, seasons and films

This section is for a title’s first scheduled release, including a new series, season, special or movie. It does not count each weekly episode as another premiere. Dates may refer to Japanese theaters or broadcasts when no international launch has been announced.

03 · CHANGES

Availability can vary

Schedules can change, and a platform announcement may apply only to certain territories. We mark information that has not been confirmed and link to the editorial policy and contact page for sourcing and corrections.

For the latest weekly lineup, check Crunchyroll’s official schedule. For release dates across months, use the anime release calendar.
