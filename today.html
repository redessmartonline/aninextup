How to read today’s anime schedule

Today combines two different kinds of releases. The sections above label them separately so viewers can tell a new premiere from another episode in an ongoing season.

01 · WEEKLY EPISODES

Episode dates and local times

The verified selection follows Japanese broadcaster notices. Choose UTC, US Eastern, US Pacific, UK, Japan or Brasília time to compare the same scheduled airing. A converted clock time does not establish viewing rights in that country.

02 · FIRST PREMIERES

New series, seasons and films

This section is for a title’s first scheduled release, including a new series, season, special or movie. It does not count each weekly episode as another premiere. Dates may refer to Japanese theaters or broadcasts when no international launch has been announced.

03 · CHANGES

Availability can vary

Schedules can change, and a platform announcement may apply only to certain territories. We mark information that has not been confirmed and link to the editorial policy and contact page for sourcing and corrections.

VIEWING QUESTIONS

What anime is releasing today?

Are these new anime series or new episodes?

The episode section covers selected ongoing-series broadcasts on Japanese television. First premieres are listed separately for new series, seasons, specials and films. A day without a new premiere can still have weekly episodes.

What time do anime episodes release in the US or UK?

Select US Eastern, US Pacific or United Kingdom above to convert the Japanese TV airing into that time zone. These conversions do not verify US or UK streaming hours, English subtitles or English dubs. Open the individual streaming guide to check what is known for the title and region.

Why can a date have no verified selection?

These entries apply to specific broadcaster dates. They are not automatically repeated into the next week. Once the checked dates have passed, a coverage notice replaces the episode cards until new listings are verified. An empty list is not proof that nothing is airing.

How do I check today’s anime episodes on Crunchyroll?

Crunchyroll’s calendar help page directs viewers to sign in on its website, open Categories and select the release calendar. Browse the current day or week, then open a listed episode for details. The calendar is a website feature; the help page says it is not available in the mobile app.

Does “anime releasing today” include English dubs?

A subtitle release and a dubbed release can have different schedules. This page’s verified broadcast selection does not confirm an English dub schedule. If you want an English dub, check the language label on the service’s episode listing rather than assuming the first release contains every audio version.

Where can I find anime coming out next month?

For next month, use our December 2026 anime release guide. For the current month, see November 2026 release dates. Use anime releasing this week to plan the current week and the anime release calendar for later dates.

Does a Japan release date apply in my country?

A Japanese broadcast or cinema opening does not automatically confirm a local streaming release. Open the individual viewing guide for the announced platform, territory and remaining unknowns.
