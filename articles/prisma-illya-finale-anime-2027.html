Prisma Illya FINALE: 2027 Release, Cast & Broadcast Update

Fate/kaleid liner Prisma Illya FINALE will begin Japanese television broadcasts in 2027 on TOKYO MX, ABC TV and AT-X. The October 18 announcement confirms the broadcasters, while an exact premiere month, episode count and international streaming service remain unannounced in the cited materials.

What changed in the October 18 announcement?

The franchise revealed the named Japanese broadcasters during its Machi Asobi stage event on October 18. The official broadcaster notice confirms a 2027 television start on TOKYO MX, ABC TV and AT-X. It does not give an opening day or weekly airtime.

The 2027 television project was already announced in July. The October update therefore adds broadcast destinations rather than a new premiere date. Readers should keep that distinction when comparing older announcement stories with the latest schedule information.

Is FINALE a television series or another movie?

FINALE is a television production. The July announcement from KADOKAWA presents it as the anime series' conclusion. The official site connects the project to the previously announced continuation of Prisma Illya Licht: The Nameless Girl.

This is a continuation of the Prisma Illya story, rather than an announced standalone reboot. Viewers looking for a first entry should distinguish the earlier television installments and films from the forthcoming FINALE release.

Returning studio, staff and Japanese cast

The official FINALE website lists SILVER LINK. as the animation studio. Shin Oonuma returns as director, with Ken Takahashi as series director, Kenji Inoue and Hazuki Minase on scripts, and Kazuya Hirata on character design.

  • Mai Kadowaki as Illyasviel von Einzbern
  • Kaori Nazuka as Miyu Edelfelt
  • Chiwa Saito as Chloe von Einzbern
  • Sumire Morohoshi as Pandora
  • Natsuki Hanae as Julian Ainsworth
  • Katsuyuki Konishi as Darius Ainsworth

These are Japanese voice credits. They do not establish an English dub cast or a dubbed release schedule.

What is the final story about?

The official introduction places Illya, Miyu and Chloe against Darius as the consequences of opening Pandora's box threaten their world. The continuation builds on the Ainsworth storyline and the mystery surrounding Pandora. That framing is also why FINALE should not be presented as an unrelated new Fate adaptation.

Should you watch earlier entries before FINALE?

Yes, especially if Prisma Illya is new to you. The official announcement calls FINALE the end of the anime series, and KADOKAWA’s July update describes it as a continuation tied to the film Licht: The Nameless Girl. The current synopsis begins after Illya’s battle with the Ainsworth family and centers on Darius, Pandora, Illya, Miyu and Chloe, so the returning conflict matters to the setup. A practical catch-up route is to watch the TV entries in release order—Prisma Illya, Zwei!, Zwei Herz! and 3rei!!—then see Licht before FINALE. The franchise also has other films and specials; the new announcement does not say that every side story is required. Use the official series portal and the official Licht film site to identify those earlier entries. This is a practical viewing route, not a checklist issued by the production committee.

How should readers compare the July and October updates?

The two announcements answer different questions. KADOKAWA introduced FINALE as a 2027 television project in July; the October 18 notice later named TOKYO MX, ABC TV and AT-X. The newer broadcaster list does not narrow the year to a particular month or day. If another article gives a specific premiere date without a separate production or station notice, that date should be treated as unconfirmed. The same distinction applies to airtime: a confirmed channel list is not yet a weekly schedule. Checking the official FINALE site and the listed Japanese stations is the clearest way to follow a later timetable without confusing a new detail with the earlier announcement.

What does the returning cast confirm?

The six published Japanese cast credits cover Illya, Miyu, Chloe and Pandora, along with Julian and Darius from the Ainsworth side of the conflict. They confirm returning roles at the center of the announced story, but the production has not described this as a complete cast list. These names also do not confirm an English dub, dubbing studio or overseas schedule. For someone deciding whether FINALE stands alone, the returning characters and the official synopsis point to a direct continuation of the Ainsworth storyline rather than a separate entry. Viewers can treat the July cast notice as a first group of credits and wait for later announcements for additional roles.
