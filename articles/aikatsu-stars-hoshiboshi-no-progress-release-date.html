Aikatsu Stars! Movie: January 22, 2027 Release Date & Trailer

When does the new Aikatsu Stars! movie release?

Aikatsu Stars! ~Hoshiboshi no Progress~ opens in Japanese cinemas on January 22, 2027. BN Pictures announced the date at its October 17 fan meeting in Ikebukuro, alongside the main trailer and key visual. The Japanese title is アイカツスターズ！ ～星々のProgress～; “Progress of the Stars” is an English rendering of the subtitle.

The full promotional video is linked by both the film’s website and BN Pictures’ press release. It previews the main theme, “Five stars,” performed by Sena, Rie, Miki, Nanase and Kana.

What is Hoshiboshi no Progress about?

With graduation approaching, Yume and her friends receive an invitation from Elza of Neo Venus Ark to perform at Venus Jamboree, an event gathering leading idols from around the world. A mysterious passenger ship, Petit Venus Ark, arrives as the girls face decisions about their dreams and futures.

Returning cast and production staff

The official film website lists:

  • Miyu Tomita as Yume Nijino
  • Ayaka Asai as Laura Sakuraba
  • Megumi Yamaguchi as Koharu Nanakura
  • Natsumi Murakami as Ako Saotome
  • Yume Miyamoto as Mahiru Kasumi
  • Yoko Hikasa as Elza Forte

BN Pictures handles production, with Teruo Sato directing, Yuko Kakihara writing the screenplay and Yukiko Aikei designing the characters.

Is it a new film or an anniversary rerelease?

This is the new film announced as part of the franchise’s tenth-anniversary project. BN Pictures first revealed it in July with an early-2027 window; the October announcement supplies the exact opening date. It should be distinguished from the separate anniversary screening of the original movie.

International release, streaming and tickets

The sources checked for this guide announce a Japanese theatrical opening. They do not name an international release date or streaming service. For Japanese screenings, use the official theater list; the newly announced second round of advance ticket cards goes on sale October 23.

What the film’s story adds to Aikatsu Stars!

The official synopsis places Yume and her friends near graduation, when Elza of Neo Venus Ark invites them to perform at the Venus Jamboree. The event brings leading idols together, while the arrival of the passenger ship Petit Venus Ark adds a new setting to the story. The announcement frames the film around choices about dreams and the future; it does not say that the characters leave the franchise’s idol-performance focus behind.

The main theme, “Five stars,” is performed by Sena, Rie, Miki, Nanase and Kana, the voices associated with the Aikatsu Stars! cast, according to the official film announcement. The trailer and official materials are the best way to identify what is newly introduced, while the synopsis provides the confirmed premise. The title’s subtitle, Hoshiboshi no Progress, is presented in Japanese as 星々のProgress; “Progress of the Stars” is an explanatory English rendering, not an announced official international title.

Japan theatrical release and overseas availability

The Japanese theatrical opening is January 22, 2027. The official theater page is the place to check participating cinemas and ticket details in Japan. A Japanese cinema listing does not establish a North American or Latin American theatrical date, a digital release, or a streaming platform. None of those international arrangements is confirmed in the official material reviewed for this guide.

The film was announced as a new movie, not as a simple rerelease of the 2016 television series. Viewers familiar with Aikatsu Stars! can follow the returning characters and cast, while newcomers may want to use the official synopsis and trailer to understand the graduation and performance context. AniNextUp will update this page if BN Pictures publishes overseas distribution or streaming information; until then, the Japanese date is the only confirmed release to list.
