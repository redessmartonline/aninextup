Aikatsu Stars! Movie: January 22, 2027 Release Date & Trailer

When does Aikatsu Stars! ~Hoshiboshi no Progress~ release?

Aikatsu Stars! ~Hoshiboshi no Progress~ opens in Japanese theaters on January 22, 2027. BN Pictures announced the date on October 17, 2026, alongside the main trailer and key visual. The official movie site lists a nationwide Japanese theatrical opening. That is a cinema date for Japan; it is not a confirmed global release or a streaming date.

What is the new Aikatsu Stars! movie about?

The story takes place shortly before graduation. Yume Nijino and her friends receive an invitation from Elza Forte of Neo Venus Ark to perform at Venus Jamboree, a live event bringing together top idols from around the world. The official synopsis also introduces the mysterious passenger ship Petit Venus Ark. As the performance approaches, the characters balance their work as idols with decisions about their dreams, plans and future paths.

This premise keeps the series’ familiar focus on performances and friendship while placing the characters at a transition point. The announcement does not reveal the show’s final stage, the full set list or how each character’s future decision turns out. Those details remain story reveals rather than facts that can be confirmed from the synopsis. Related reading: The Apothecary Diaries Movie: Luoqing and Yinshan Character PV.

Is this a new movie or the 10th-anniversary rerelease?

Hoshiboshi no Progress is a newly produced film for the Aikatsu Stars! 10th-anniversary project. It is separate from the summer 2026 revival screening of the earlier Aikatsu Stars! The Movie, which played for a limited three-week run under the title Anniversary Stars. The two projects mark the same milestone but have different stories and release dates: the revival was a return to cinemas for the earlier film, while the January 2027 title is the new Yume-and-friends story.

The Japanese subtitle is 星々のProgress. “Progress of the Stars” is a helpful English rendering, but the official site has not announced a separate international English title. Search results may use translated wording; the Japanese title and January 22 date are the firm identifiers. Related reading: Maebashi Witches Movie: October 23 Release, New Scenes & Trailer.

Do you need to watch the television series first?

The official announcement does not publish a required viewing order. Its synopsis introduces the invitation and event, while the cast list returns to Yume, Laura, Koharu, Ako, Mahiru and their fellow idols. Viewers familiar with the television series will recognize the characters and their relationships, especially as graduation approaches. Newcomers can follow the announced premise, but the producers have not described the film as a standalone entry or provided a catch-up guide. Watching the earlier series is a reasonable way to understand the group’s history; treat that as viewing advice, not an official prerequisite.

Who is in the cast and production staff?

The official film site lists Miyu Tomita as Yume Nijino, Ayaka Asai as Laura Sakuraba, Megumi Yamaguchi as Koharu Nanakura, Natsumi Murakami as Ako Saotome, Yume Miyamoto as Mahiru Kasumi, Minami Tsuda as Hime Shiratori, Sumire Morohoshi as Tsubasa Kisaragi, Azusa Tadokoro as Yuzu Nikaido, Ayaka Ohashi as Yozora Kasumi, Reina Ueda as Lily Shirogane, Yoko Hikasa as Elza Forte, Natsumi Fujiwara as Rei Kizaki, Nako Eguchi as Kirara Hanazono and Kaori Maeda as Aria Futaba. Related reading: Witch on the Holy Night Movie: Release Date and Theaters.

BN Pictures is responsible for planning, original work and production, with Bandai credited for the original concept. Teruo Sato directs, Yuko Kakihara writes the screenplay and Yukiko Aikei designs the characters. BN Pictures also handles distribution in Japan. These are the currently credited principal roles; the official staff page is the place to check if more production credits are added.

What songs are in the trailer?

The main theme is “Five stars,” performed by Sena, Rie, Miki, Nanase and Kana, with lyrics by Miho Karasawa and composition and arrangement by Kenichi Nagahashi of agehasprings Party. The official site also lists the ending theme “Anata e,” performed by Sena, Rie, Miki, Miho, Nanase, Kana, Luka and Risa, with lyrics by Karasawa and composition and arrangement by Tomohiro Akiura.

How can you get tickets, and where will the movie screen?

The official theater list identifies Japanese cinemas showing the film and is the best place to check participating locations as the opening approaches. The second MoviTicket card went on sale October 23, 2026, for ¥1,600. Its purchase bonus is a five-postcard set, randomly selected from three designs; the bonus is limited and some theaters may not sell it. Check the official ticket page or a listed theater for current stock and purchase conditions before traveling.

Will Aikatsu Stars! be available outside Japan or on streaming?

The official release information reviewed for this update confirms the January 22 Japanese cinema opening but does not announce an overseas theatrical date, international distributor or streaming service. A Japanese theater listing should not be read as a worldwide rollout. If you are outside Japan, the practical answer is that a legal local viewing option has not been confirmed in these sources. We will update this guide if BN Pictures or the official franchise portal names a distributor or platform.
