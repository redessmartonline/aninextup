When is the new Otherworldly Munchkin release date?

Otherworldly Munchkin: Let’s Speedrun the Dungeon with Only 1 HP! begins its Japanese television run in the January 7, 2027 late-night slot. The November 18 announcement lists TOKYO MX and BS11 at 24:30 JST. On a conventional calendar, that is Friday, January 8 at 12:30 a.m. in Japan. This replaces the original October 2025 launch plan.

The announcement comes from the anime’s official broadcast notice. If you saved an old release calendar, change its date. The relevant distinction is between a Japanese broadcaster’s programming day and the calendar day on which the episode actually begins. Both January 7 and January 8 can describe this premiere, provided the time is stated alongside the date.

Is the premiere January 7 or January 8?

Japanese late-night schedules often continue counting beyond 24:00. Here, Thursday 24:30 means half an hour after Thursday ends: Friday 00:30. To set a normal calendar reminder in Japan, use January 8 at 00:30. To find the entry in the broadcaster’s Thursday schedule, look under January 7. Entering January 7 at 00:30 in an ordinary calendar would put your reminder a full day early.

The same issue affects the later stations. A 25:00 listing becomes 01:00 the following morning; 26:30 becomes 02:30. We have normalized each slot below so you can compare the channels without doing the midnight calculation yourself. These are the station starts recorded on the official On Air page, rather than dates inferred from a weekly calendar.

Why was Otherworldly Munchkin delayed? Was it canceled?

The production committee postponed the October 2025 broadcast to improve the anime’s quality. Its September 16, 2025 notice did not give a replacement schedule. The November 18, 2026 announcement now supplies one, so the project has a scheduled premiere rather than an indefinite delay.

The notice did not identify a specific production incident or give a detailed account of the work required. We therefore do not attribute the delay to a particular employee, studio dispute or financial problem. Older pages that say the new date is undecided describe the situation before this update. Their original October date should not be treated as a second, competing release plan.

Where can you watch Otherworldly Munchkin?

Crunchyroll previously announced that it plans to stream the anime. Its official postponement report names North America, Central America, South America, Europe, Oceania, the Middle East, North Africa and India. That platform announcement predates the new television schedule.

As of our November 18 source check, the materials reviewed do not establish a revised international episode-one upload time or an English-dub release date. The Japanese station schedule cannot fill those gaps. For viewers outside Japan, the practical answer is to look for the series on Crunchyroll in the announced territories, then check its local listing when the winter schedule is published. We have not verified a Netflix release.

What is the anime about, and who is making it?

Yukito Kirihara is reincarnated into another world after trying to save his sister Sana. He discovers that she is connected to that world too and sets out to find her, despite having only one hit point. The premise puts survival and resourcefulness at the center of the adventure. You do not need to have read the manga to start with the television premiere.

The current official staff and cast page credits Naoya Fukushi as director, Hiroshi Hosokawa for series composition, and Durandal for animation. Kazuki Ura voices Yukito, Kana Yuki voices Charlotte, Yui Ishikawa voices Lumilia, and Kaori Maeda voices Liverna. Yoko Hikasa plays Nephilia Curse, while Yuichiro Umehara is listed as Jaraza. Use these current credits when comparing the announcement with older production reports.

The new music announcement names LIL LEAGUE’s “Sai” as the opening and ZAQ’s “Zanka” as the ending. ZAQ’s track is scheduled for digital release on January 8. A song’s digital release time is separate from both the television premiere and any streaming episode release.

Where can you read the manga in English?

Kodansha publishes the English digital manga. Start with Volume 1 if you want the setup before the anime. Its series page links to the available volumes and a chapter preview; retailer availability depends on your location. The manga is credited to Yuu Shimizu and Makoto Aogiri.

If you prefer to watch without spoilers, the opening premise is enough preparation. Later volume descriptions can reveal developments beyond the first episode, so use the first-volume listing instead of reading every synopsis. The November broadcast announcement does not specify a final episode count or how many manga volumes the anime will adapt.
