When does Bass X Machina release?

Bass X Machina premieres on Netflix on November 3, 2026. Netflix's official media center lists that date for the United States, while its broader animation announcement presents the series as part of the platform's global animated slate.

Where to watch

Bass X Machina is a Netflix series. Availability is tied to Netflix's regional catalogs, so AniNextUp will keep the release guide updated if the service publishes territory-specific differences.

What is Bass X Machina about?

The story takes place in a lawless steampunk West populated by violent outlaws, machines and supernatural threats. A father is forced into the roles of judge, jury and executioner while trying to protect his family, even as every violent decision risks pushing those same people away.

Who is making the series?

LeSean Thomas created the series and Studio Mir handles animation. Brian Tyree Henry stars and is also an executive producer alongside Thomas, Jennifer Wiley-Moxley and Chad Handley. The cast also includes Tati Gabrielle and Cree Summer.
