When did Sasaki and Peeps Season 2 premiere?

Sasaki and Peeps Season 2 began on October 7, 2026. As of October 25, the sequel is airing in Japan. The main practical detail is that there is no single schedule for every viewer: Japanese television, Japanese streaming services and Crunchyroll publish separate listings, and their clocks should not be mixed together.

The official anime schedule lists AT-X on Wednesdays at 10:00 p.m. Japan Standard Time (JST), starting October 7 with a one-hour special. Tokyo MX also began that night at 11:00 p.m. JST, with a one-hour premiere slot. BS NTV's first broadcast was Thursday, October 8 at 11:00 p.m. JST. The difference is the channel timetable, not a second premiere for another season. For each station, the official schedule lists later installments in regular 30-minute weekly slots.

What is Season 2 about?

The official introduction starts with Sasaki, a tired office worker who buys a Java sparrow at a pet shop. The bird, Peeps, is a renowned sage reborn from another world. Peeps gives Sasaki magic and a way to cross between modern Japan and a fantasy setting. Sasaki uses that access to trade goods, run a restaurant and train in magic, hoping the other world can offer relief from corporate life. The story then complicates that escape: on his way home from work, Sasaki encounters a person with supernatural abilities. He uses otherworldly magic to get through the encounter, is mistaken for an ability user himself, and is recruited by a Japanese government office dealing with paranormal phenomena. That premise explains the series' unusual mix of fantasy commerce, workplace comedy and modern supernatural conflict. Season 2 continues those threads with Peeps and the returning cast; the official synopsis, rather than fan speculation, is the safest guide to what the adaptation has confirmed.
