Where to watch Episode 1

Open Episode 1 on Krosmoz and check that the title says “Mushroom Pie.” The upload is about 22 minutes long. Krosmoz also puts the trailer and opening sequence in its official Welsh & Shedar playlist, so starting at the top of the playlist can land you on a preview. If you are sending the first episode to someone, use the episode link. If you plan to follow the series each week, save the playlist as well.

Ankama announced free YouTube access worldwide outside Japan. That covers the United States, Canada and the United Kingdom under the announced release scope. You can start with the public Krosmoz upload instead of looking through a subscription anime catalog. If YouTube reports a location restriction, read the message on the video page. Switching the audio language changes what you hear, while the publisher’s territory settings govern where the video is available.

You may also see the title written as Aoki Denshō: Welsh & Shedar or Aoki Denshou Welsh and Shedar. The Japanese title is useful for finding the production’s broadcast page. For the free international upload, the shortest useful search is Welsh & Shedar Mushroom Pie Krosmoz. The channel name and episode subtitle do more to identify the right video than adding a country or the word “dub” to your search.

How to turn on the English dub

Ankama lists English, French and Japanese audio for the international release. On the episode’s YouTube watch page, open Settings → Audio track → English (US) if the player starts in another language. Tap the picture first if the controls are hidden on your phone. English dialogue can be selected from the audio menu on the same video, even when its title gives no indication of which track the player will start with.

Check captions separately. If you prefer the Japanese performances with English subtitles, choose Japanese under Audio track, then choose English under Subtitles/CC. If you want spoken English with captions off, choose English under Audio track and turn CC off. The caption setting changes the text on the screen. The audio setting changes the voices. That distinction matters if you opened the right episode and can already read English but still hear French or Japanese.

If the audio menu is missing from the player you opened, try the direct watch page in the YouTube app or a desktop browser. Check the title before troubleshooting: a trailer, an opening clip and the first full episode can all have different player options. For a shared screen or TV, check the audio again after opening the video there. YouTube can start videos using your language preferences, which can differ from the language you last chose on another device. YouTube’s audio-language help explains where to find the controls.

Those conversions describe the first YouTube premiere. For later episodes, use the dated schedule below and check the individual video page for its local premiere time. Why some episode calendars show October

The Japanese television run started October 2 on Tokyo MX. The Japanese production site lists its domestic schedule under the Japanese title. The November 5 launch belongs to Ankama’s separate international YouTube rollout. For example, a listing that places Episode 1 on October 2 can be correct for Japan while the Krosmoz upload of that episode still has a November date. Check the listed broadcaster or channel before deciding whether a date applies to you.

Episode numbers can also make the two runs look inconsistent. A Japanese schedule may already show an installment beyond the first pair while the international playlist is still catching up. If your goal is to follow the English dub, use the numbered Krosmoz uploads and the international schedule together. Search by title and episode number when opening discussions, too. A post labeled “this week’s episode” could refer to a later installment and give away a development you have yet to see.

Do you need to watch DOFUS or Wakfu first?

Start with “Mushroom Pie” if you are new to the Krosmoz. Welsh is a cook from Brassica whose everyday life is interrupted by Asalar, a dragon. The story gives him a direct reason to leave home and introduces a separate conflict around Queen Zabelle and her son Shedar. Joris connects the new cast to the wider world, but following Welsh’s first decisions gives newcomers a clear starting point even if they do not recognize Joris yet.

For a franchise-first viewing order, put DOFUS – Book I: Julith before Welsh & Shedar. Ankama places the series about 30 years after Joris’s adventure in the film. The film is therefore useful if you specifically want more background on him. If your interest is in Welsh and Shedar, watch the first episode now and save the film for later. There is no need to catch up on multiple seasons of Wakfu or play a game before you press play.

The franchise name can be confusing if you arrive from a game guide. DOFUS is the name of the game and appears in the film title. Within the setting, Dofus are powerful eggs. The Ivory Dofus is an object Welsh seeks in the series. Keeping those uses separate makes the basic setup easier to follow without a lore recap. For production context, the anime pairs creator Anthony Roux’s Krosmoz setting with animation by Japan’s Studio Massket.

Where to look for the DOFUS rewards

If you play DOFUS, DOFUS Retro or DOFUS Touch, open the description under the official episode before leaving YouTube. Ankama’s launch announcement describes three activities. The simplest uses a code provided in the description. The other two involve clues inside the episode: letters that contribute to Shedar’s riddle and a separate hidden code that advances a series of in-game quests.

The first episode’s advertised gifts include Minikitt, based on Welsh’s cat, and a Mushroom Pie consumable. For a quick reward check, start with the description and follow the publisher’s directions for your game. For the riddle, keep a note of which episode each pair of letters comes from. For the quest, distinguish the hidden code from the public gift code, since they serve different purposes. Viewers interested only in the story can ignore all three activities and continue straight to the next episode.
