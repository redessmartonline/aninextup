How to use this seasonal calendar

Dates in the table are the announced premieres for the relevant title, while the streaming column summarizes known services and may not describe every country. A Japanese television debut does not necessarily occur at the same local time as an international simulcast. For exact episode times and exceptions, follow each linked series guide and its cited production or distributor schedule. Related reading: Anime Releasing in December 2026: Confirmed Dates.

October: finding the right show and platform

The October table is a starting point for shows whose season or release began during the month. For example, The Apothecary Diaries Season 3 began on October 2 and Cyberpunk: Edgerunners 2 arrived on Netflix on October 20. They use different distribution models: one is a television season, while the other is a standalone Netflix story. The individual guides explain the available viewing information for each.

The Crunchyroll fall lineup lists territories separately for its shows. Check the local catalog for available episodes and languages. A subtitled simulcast does not establish a dub release date.

November: streaming premieres and Japanese cinemas

November combines new streaming titles with films scheduled for theaters in Japan. Those formats answer different questions: a streaming premiere tells viewers when a service plans to add a title, whereas a Japanese cinema opening identifies a theatrical market. International theaters and home-streaming dates can be announced later and may not match the Japanese date.

Use the November release guide to compare the individual dates, then follow each title's source or local listing before making plans. When a film has a Japanese opening but no announced date for your country, keep that later date as unconfirmed rather than assuming simultaneous availability.

December: exact dates and month-only windows

The Apothecary Diaries: The Late Lady’s Treasure is scheduled to open in Japanese theaters on December 11. Crunchyroll and Sony Pictures Entertainment have announced international theatrical distribution, with release dates and ticket details to follow; the distributor's announcement distinguishes that plan from the Japanese opening.

There is also a December window without an exact day in the sources checked: Disney Japan has announced Twisted-Wonderland The Animation: Episode of Savanaclaw for Disney+ in December. The December guide presents that month-only announcement separately from the film's dated opening. Check your regional Disney+ listing for local availability and any later date.

How to read the dates and keep up

A season premiere date is not a calendar of every later episode. Weekly releases can cross into November or December even when the series began in October, and a platform may display a different local day from a late-night Japanese broadcast. For ongoing shows, consult the current episode listing instead of adding a new season premiere to the table each week.

This hub covers the whole October–December season; the linked monthly guides provide a closer look at each month. As of October 26, some October premieres have already passed while later releases remain upcoming. Dates and rights can change, so the production, broadcaster or streaming service is the best place to verify the latest details before watching or booking a ticket.
