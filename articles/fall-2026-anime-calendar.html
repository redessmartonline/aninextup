Fall 2026 begins with a dense October TV premiere schedule and continues with streaming originals and theatrical projects in November and December. This seasonal hub connects AniNextUp’s confirmed release guides without treating unannounced dates as placeholders.

How to use this seasonal calendar

Dates in the table are the announced premieres for the relevant title, while the streaming column summarizes known services and may not describe every country. A Japanese television debut does not necessarily occur at the same local time as an international simulcast. For exact episode times and exceptions, follow each linked series guide and its cited production or distributor schedule.

November 2026 anime releases

November includes Netflix’s Bass X Machina and Fool Night, Prime Video’s worldwide release of Demon’s Crest, and Japanese theatrical projects including Rakuen Tsuihou: Kokoro no Resonance, Witch on the Holy Night, VOTOMS: The Gray Witch Part 1 and Takopi’s Original Sin Movie. See the complete November 2026 guide.

December 2026 anime releases

December currently has fewer officially dated releases. The Apothecary Diaries: The Late Lady’s Treasure is confirmed for Japanese theaters on December 11. We intentionally keep undated titles out of the calendar until an official day is announced. See the December 2026 guide.
