Why Ash Vamps is at the center of the mystery

Set near the end of the Hundred Year War, the film's investigation begins with an unusual pattern: Ash Vamps repeatedly survives missions that kill her comrades. That makes the question of her survival a narrative hook within the franchise's military setting. The announced synopsis does not explain the cause, so any answer would be speculation rather than a verified plot detail.

What the Part 1 announcement actually confirms

November 20 is the Japanese theatrical opening for the first film in a planned two-part project. It does not establish when Part 2 will appear, nor does it identify an international streaming service. Readers following the complete story should wait for separate official announcements on those points.

When does The Gray Witch Part 1 release?

Armored Trooper VOTOMS: The Gray Witch Part 1 opens in Japanese theaters on November 20, 2026. It is the first half of a new two-part theatrical project; a release date for Part 2 has not yet been announced.

What is The Gray Witch about?

The story is set near the end of the Hundred Year War and follows an investigation into Ash Vamps, a soldier nicknamed the Witch because she repeatedly returns alive from missions that kill the rest of her unit. The premise returns VOTOMS to its military science-fiction roots while introducing a new central mystery.

Who is making the movie?

Mamoru Oshii directs the project. Sunrise produces the animation with production cooperation from Production I.G, while Bandai Namco Filmworks handles distribution.

Is November 20 the release date for both films?

No. The announced November 20, 2026 Japanese theatrical opening applies only to The Gray Witch Part 1. The project is planned in two parts, but the cited production details do not give Part 2 a date. A planned two-part format also does not establish a streaming release for either film.

Do you need to wait for Part 2?

Part 1 is the first installment of a planned two-part theatrical story. The November 20 date applies to Part 1 only; the production has not announced a date for Part 2 in the cited release information. Readers planning to watch both installments should not assume a second film will open the following week or month.

Where to watch

The November 20 date is for the Japanese theatrical release. No international streaming platform has been confirmed, so AniNextUp will not list one until an official announcement is made.
