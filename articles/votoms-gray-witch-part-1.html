When does VOTOMS: The Gray Witch Part 1 release?

Armored Trooper VOTOMS: The Gray Witch Part 1 opens in Japanese theaters on November 20, 2026. The date applies to the first film in a two-part theatrical project. The official announcement does not give a date for Part 2, so the November opening should not be read as the release date for the complete story.

What is the story about?

The official synopsis places the action near the end of the Hundred Year War and identifies Gilgamesh and Balarant as the opposing powers. The investigation centers on Ash Vamps, an Armored Trooper pilot whose repeated survival of missions that kill her unit has earned her the name “the Witch.” Maki Stauffenberg is assigned to investigate Ash and becomes involved in a dangerous operation involving an AT drop. That setup gives readers a clear question to follow: what happened on Ash’s missions, and what will Maki discover?

The announcement presents the Witch name as a reputation attached to Ash by other soldiers. It does not confirm magic or explain how she survives. The cause remains a plot mystery, so claims that reveal it before the film opens would go beyond the published synopsis. Related reading: Ascendance of a Bookworm Part 4 Anime Officially Announced.

Can newcomers watch Part 1 without seeing the older series?

The film announcement gives a setting, central mystery and two principal characters, which is enough to understand the basic premise. However, the official material reviewed here does not label the movie a standalone entry, recap, reboot or direct sequel, and it does not publish a recommended viewing order. Viewers unfamiliar with VOTOMS should therefore treat the war and character details above as confirmed orientation, not as a promise that no prior knowledge matters. Check the official project page for any viewing guidance added before release.

Who are Ash and Maki, and who voices them?

Ash Vamps is the pilot under investigation; Maki Stauffenberg is the officer assigned to examine her case. Miyuki Sawashiro voices Ash, while Mabuki Ando voices Maki. The announced supporting cast includes Yūichi Nakamura, Satoshi Tsuruoka, Tomokazu Sugita, Kenta Miyake, Chafūrin, Hōchū Ōtsuka and Daisuke Ono. The official cast notice identifies these performers, but the available announcement does not describe every character’s relationship to the two leads.

Who is making the new VOTOMS film?

Mamoru Oshii directs. The project’s official credits list original creator and general supervisor Ryosuke Takahashi, original mechanical designer Kunio Okawara, mechanical designers Shinobu Tsuneki and Yoshihiro Sono, and composer Kenji Kawai. Sunrise is producing the animation, Production I.G is providing production cooperation, and Bandai Namco Filmworks is distributing the film. Those credits help distinguish the confirmed creative team from assumptions based only on the VOTOMS name. Is November 20 the release date for both parts?

No. November 20, 2026 is the confirmed Japanese theatrical opening for Part 1. The project is announced as two theatrical parts, but no Part 2 date appears in the official material checked for this update. The announcements also do not state an episode count or a home-video schedule. Until the distributor posts those details, viewers should treat them as unannounced rather than infer a gap between the films.
