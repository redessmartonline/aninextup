Why Ash Vamps is at the center of the mystery

Set near the end of the Hundred Year War, the film's investigation begins with an unusual pattern: Ash Vamps repeatedly survives missions that kill her comrades. That makes the question of her survival a narrative hook within the franchise's military setting. The announced synopsis does not explain the cause, so any answer would be speculation rather than a verified plot detail.

When does The Gray Witch Part 1 release?

Armored Trooper VOTOMS: The Gray Witch Part 1 opens in Japanese theaters on November 20, 2026. It is the first half of a new two-part theatrical project; a release date for Part 2 has not yet been announced.

What is The Gray Witch about?

The story is set near the end of the Hundred Year War and follows an investigation into Ash Vamps, a soldier nicknamed the Witch because she repeatedly returns alive from missions that kill the rest of her unit. The premise returns VOTOMS to its military science-fiction roots while introducing a new central mystery.

Who is making the movie?

Mamoru Oshii directs the project. Sunrise produces the animation with production cooperation from Production I.G, while Bandai Namco Filmworks handles distribution.

Is November 20 the release date for both films?

No. The announced November 20, 2026 Japanese theatrical opening applies only to The Gray Witch Part 1. The project is planned in two parts, but the cited production details do not give Part 2 a date. A planned two-part format also does not establish a streaming release for either film.

Where to watch

The November 20 date is for the Japanese theatrical release. No international streaming platform has been confirmed, so AniNextUp will not list one until an official announcement is made.

New details announced before the premiere

The official VOTOMS site has continued to add information since the first May announcement. Its October 23 update names “名も無き詩人のように” by Mika Nakashima as the theme song, with music provided by Toshihiko Takamizawa of THE ALFEE. The same update released a main visual and a final trailer. These are concrete additions to the project’s presentation; they do not change the confirmed November 20 Japanese theatrical opening for Part 1.

The official cast announcement names the two central roles: Miyuki Sawashiro voices Ash Vamps, the AT pilot feared as the Witch, and Maki Stauffenberg, the officer assigned to investigate Ash, is voiced by Mabuki Ando. Other announced cast members include Yūichi Nakamura, Satoshi Tsuruoka, Tomokazu Sugita, Kenta Miyake, Chafūrin, Hōchū Ōtsuka and Daisuke Ono. The official site continues to publish character and mechanical details, so this page should be checked against that source as the release approaches.

How to read the two-part announcement

“Part 1” is important in the title: the production describes this as a two-part theatrical project, but the November date applies to the first film only. No date for the second part appears in the official announcement reviewed here. A ticket for the first film or a trailer for it cannot be used to infer when the follow-up will open.

The setting near the end of the Hundred Year War and Ash’s repeated survival give the film a military mystery to follow. The official synopsis leaves the cause of that survival unexplained. Calling her a witch is how other soldiers view her; it is not confirmation of a supernatural explanation. For international viewers, the Japanese theatrical opening is confirmed, while overseas cinema bookings and streaming arrangements remain unannounced.
