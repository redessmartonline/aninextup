Armored Trooper VOTOMS: The Gray Witch Part 1

Armored Trooper VOTOMS: The Gray Witch Part 1 opens November 20, 2026 in Japan. Mamoru Oshii directs the new Sunrise theatrical anime.

Release date and availability

Part 1 of the new two-part VOTOMS theatrical project opens in Japan on November 20, 2026. The release date for Part 2 has not yet been announced.

What is it about?

Set near the end of the Hundred Year War, the story follows an investigation into Ash Vamps, a soldier nicknamed the Witch because she repeatedly survives missions that kill the rest of her unit.

The November 20 date refers to the Japanese theatrical release. AniNextUp will update this guide when additional international or streaming availability is officially confirmed.
