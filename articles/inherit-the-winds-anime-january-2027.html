Inherit the Winds Anime: January 2027 Premiere, November Theater Preview and New Visual

The original Shinsengumi anime Inherit the Winds is scheduled for Japanese television in January 2027. Its production has also revealed a new illustration for the limited theatrical preview beginning November 13, 2026.

When does Inherit the Winds premiere?

Inherit the Winds (Kaze wo Tsugu Mono) is set to begin broadcasting in Japan in January 2027 on the CBC/TBS network's Agaru Anime block, which airs on Sundays at 11:30 p.m. Japan Standard Time. The production has not yet specified the exact first-episode date. No international streaming platform or regional availability has been officially confirmed.

October 13: new theatrical preview visual

On October 13, the official website unveiled a new illustration to celebrate the upcoming theatrical preview, Inherit the Winds: Hajimari. The image shows Jinsuke Tachikawa, Soji Okita and Hyogo Koganei enjoying a gentle breeze beneath a blue sky. Merchandise featuring the artwork was announced alongside the reveal. The theatrical preview begins November 13, 2026, for a limited two-week engagement in Japan; it is separate from the January 2027 TV broadcast.

Is the November 13 theatrical preview the television premiere?

No. Inherit the Winds: Hajimari is a limited Japanese theatrical preview beginning November 13, 2026. The regular television anime is separately scheduled for January 2027 on CBC/TBS's Agaru Anime block, with no exact first-episode day announced in the cited production update. The theatrical event and TV broadcast should not be treated as the same release.

What is Inherit the Winds about?

The original historical drama is set in Kyoto in June 1863. An unidentified, memoryless young man is brought to the Mibu Roshigumi quarters. Soji Okita calls him Jinsuke Tachikawa, setting him on a path alongside the swordsmen who will become the Shinsengumi. As political turmoil intensifies, Jinsuke's lost memories become central to the story.

Production staff

Tomohiro Kawamura directs the series, with scriptwriting by Yutaka Narui, character designs by Fumi Ebina and music by Shigeru Kishida. Live2D Creative Studio and Drive handle animation production, combining Live2D techniques with traditional cel-style animation.
