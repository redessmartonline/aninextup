Inherit the Winds: January 2027 Anime, Cast & Theater Preview

The original Shinsengumi anime Inherit the Winds is scheduled for Japanese television in January 2027. Its production has also revealed a new illustration for the limited theatrical preview beginning November 13, 2026.

When does Inherit the Winds premiere?

Inherit the Winds (Japanese title Kaze wo Tsugu Mono, 風を継ぐもの) is scheduled to begin Japanese television broadcast in January 2027. The official announcement places it in CBC and TBS's Sunday “Agaru Anime” block at 11:30 p.m. Japan Standard Time (JST, UTC+9), carried on 28 network stations. The first episode's exact calendar date has not been announced. A limited theater edition opens first, but it is separate from the regular TV premiere.

November 13 theater preview: what audiences need to know

The theatrical edition, titled Inherit the Winds: Hajimari, opens in Japan on November 13, 2026 for a limited two-week engagement. The official site describes it as a theatrical advance screening before TV. The cited notice does not state a runtime, total episode count or international cinema rollout. A November Japanese screening date does not establish a release elsewhere.

Aniplex has announced cast appearances at selected screenings on November 13 and 14. On opening day, the schedule lists Shinjuku Wald 9 after the 10:00 screening and before the 12:50 screening; TOHO Cinemas Hibiya after the 13:40 screening and before the 16:45 screening; and United Cinemas Toyosu after the 17:10 screening and before the 19:55 screening. On November 14, greetings are scheduled at United Cinemas Aqua City Odaiba after the 11:55 screening and before the 15:00 screening, and at TOHO Cinemas Shinjuku after the 15:25 screening and before the 18:30 screening. All times are Japan local time. Standard reserved tickets cost ¥3,000; Hibiya premium seats cost ¥4,100 or ¥6,200, and Toyosu paired seats cost ¥7,700. Guests may change, so check Aniplex's notice before traveling.

The first-week gift is a B6 illustration card drawn by character designer Fumi Ebina, offered November 13–19 while supplies last. A second card begins November 20 and is designed to connect with the first, but its details were still pending in Aniplex's October 19 notice. These are cinema attendance bonuses, not a streaming extra or a guarantee that every theater will have stock.

What is the story about?

The original historical drama begins in Kyoto in June 1863, during the Bakumatsu period. A young man with no memory is brought to the Mibu Roshigumi quarters. Soji Okita gives him the name Jinsuke Tachikawa, and the story follows his life among the group as it grows into the Shinsengumi. The official synopsis makes Jinsuke's lost memories central: recovering them is expected to change the world around him. The setting draws on a real historical organization, but Jinsuke and his personal mystery belong to the anime's fictional narrative, not a documentary account.

The October 13 theatrical visual shows Jinsuke, Okita and Hyogo Koganei beneath a blue sky, while the article's hero is an earlier official teaser and is captioned as such. The production describes the series as a story about young people caught in a turbulent era.

Confirmed cast and production

Daisuke Sakuma voices Jinsuke Tachikawa, Yuki Kaji is Soji Okita, Aoi Yuki is Hyogo Koganei, Koki Uchiyama is Toshizo Hijikata and Yuichi Nakamura is Isami Kondo. The announced supporting cast includes Nichika Omori as Kurosuke, Takeo Otsuka as Keisuke Yamanami, Shimba Tsuchiya as Sanosuke Harada, Ryohei Kimura as Shinpachi Nagakura, Ayumu Murase as Heisuke Todo and Yasuyuki Kase as Kamo Serizawa. Takaya Kamikawa has a special appearance as Shinbei Umeda. This confirms the named ensemble, not which characters appear in every episode.

Tomohiro Kawamura directs, Yutaka Narui writes the series, Fumi Ebina designs the characters and Shigeru Kishida composes the score. Live2D Creative Studio and Drive are credited with animation production. The official site describes a blend of Live2D techniques and cel animation. Frequently asked questions

Is there an exact January 2027 release date?

No. January 2027 and the Sunday 11:30 p.m. JST slot are confirmed; the first-episode date has not been announced.

Can I stream the November 13 version?

No online release is confirmed in the cited sources. November 13 is the start of a limited Japanese theatrical engagement for Hajimari.

Is the anime based on a manga?

The official site calls this an original TV anime. It uses the Mibu Roshigumi and Shinsengumi as its historical setting, with an original amnesia mystery at its center; the cited announcements do not describe it as a manga adaptation.
