When does the Sekiro anime come out?

Sekiro: No Defeat is scheduled for January 2027. The production's September 25 announcement confirms an eight-episode television series. The current official TV website repeats that window; the cited announcements do not provide a specific first-episode date or release hour.

Where will Sekiro: No Defeat stream?

Crunchyroll's announced release is January 2027, with weekly episodes. The announced territories cover the world excluding Japan, China, Korea, Russia and Belarus. Those exclusions matter when checking a local catalog.

See Crunchyroll's series announcement and UnGeek's September 28 coverage of the streaming announcement. A detailed international timetable, dub schedule and language lineup are not established by the sources cited here.

How is the series different from the movie?

The production explains that the project began as an eight-part series and was first presented as an edited theatrical version. The television version retains the film's story while adding new episodes, scenes and cuts.

The Japanese film opened on September 4, 2026. That earlier cinema date refers to a different release format, while January 2027 is the window to follow for the series.

What is the story about?

Based on FromSoftware's Sekiro: Shadows Die Twice, the anime follows the shinobi Wolf and his young lord, Kuro, in the land of Ashina. Kuro's Dragon's Heritage can grant immortality, drawing the attention of people seeking that power. Their struggle centers on loyalty, survival and the consequences of that bloodline.

The official synopsis introduces the conflict; this guide avoids assuming which of the game's possible endings the adaptation will use.

Cast, studio and production staff

The TV anime's cast and staff page credits:

  • Daisuke Namikawa as Wolf
  • Miyuki Satou as Kuro
  • Kenjiro Tsuda as Genichiro Ashina
  • Shizuka Ito as Emma
  • Takaya Hashi as Owl

Kenichi Kutsuna directs, Takuya Sato handles series composition and Takahiro Kishida designs the characters. Animation is by Qzil.la, with production by ARCH. The music credits include Shuta Hasunuma and Ryuichi Sakamoto's “Blu” as the theme.
