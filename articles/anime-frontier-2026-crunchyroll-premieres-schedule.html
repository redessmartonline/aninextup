Anime Frontier 2026 Crunchyroll Premieres: Schedule, Rooms & What to Watch

Crunchyroll will hold four screening blocks at Anime Frontier 2026, on Friday, December 4 and Sunday, December 6. Find the times and rooms for Magic Knight Rayearth, Shangri-La Frontier Season 3, the Aniplex premieres and more.

Crunchyroll’s Anime Frontier 2026 schedule

Crunchyroll has announced four screening blocks for Anime Frontier, two on Friday, December 4, and two on Sunday, December 6. The convention runs December 4–6 at the Fort Worth Convention Center, 1201 Houston Street, Fort Worth, Texas. The schedule below uses local Central Standard Time. Rooms and program details can change, so check the convention’s latest listing before heading to a screening.

Announced Crunchyroll screenings at Anime Frontier 2026

These are session windows. Crunchyroll’s announcement gives a start and end time for each block, with no title-by-title schedule within the Sunday sessions. If you are coming for a specific episode, plan around the block’s start time. In particular, the room for the Sunday morning premieres is 203ABC. The afternoon premieres are back on the Mainstage, which the announcement identifies as Ballroom AB.

Friday, December 4: premieres and Magic Knight Rayearth

The first Crunchyroll premiere session runs from 3:30 to 5:00 p.m. in Ballroom AB. Its individual titles are still to be announced. If you are buying a badge for a particular series, wait for that series to appear in the official program. The fact that a title has a Crunchyroll release or another screening at the convention gives you no way to place it in the unannounced Friday lineup.

The evening session starts at 5:45 p.m. in Room 202A and runs until 7:05 p.m. It shows the first three episodes of the 2026 version of Magic Knight Rayearth. That detail matters if you are looking up the anime beforehand, since searches can also turn up the earlier adaptation. The schedule gives attendees a chance to watch the opening episodes together on a big screen. The 45 minutes between Friday’s announced sessions allow time to change rooms, subject to entry lines and schedule updates.

Saturday, December 5: the Made in Abyss screening

The announced Crunchyroll lineup lists its screenings on Friday and Sunday. If you are attending Saturday for anime premieres, the separate event to look up is HIDIVE’s North American premiere of Made in Abyss: Awakening Mystery. HIDIVE announced December 5 for the film at Anime Frontier. The convention program is the place to confirm any session details, and our Made in Abyss screening and release guide explains where the film fits in the series and what HIDIVE has said about 2027 distribution.

Sunday, December 6: two blocks of early screenings

Start in Room 203ABC at 11:00 a.m. for Fate Rewinder, the first episode of Medaka Kuroiwa Is Impervious to My Charms Season 2 and the first episode of Shangri-La Frontier Season 3. The last two continue existing TV series. If you are choosing what to catch up on before the convention, the season numbers tell you which earlier installments to look for. See our Medaka Kuroiwa Season 2 guide and Shangri-La Frontier Season 3 guide for their wider release information.

The Aniplex block runs from 1:45 to 3:45 p.m. in Ballroom AB. The four announced first episodes are Here U Are, BLESS, Dengeki Daisy and The Guy She Was Interested in Wasn’t a Guy at All. The block is a convenient choice if you want to sample several new adaptations at the same event. Use the official session start time even if you are interested in only one title. The announcement gives no individual slot within the block.

There is a gap of one hour and 45 minutes between the scheduled end of the morning session and the start of the afternoon session. That is time for lunch or other programming, with a room change to account for. If the Aniplex block is your priority, check where the Mainstage line forms before leaving for another part of the convention. A session’s published time tells you when the programming is scheduled to begin, while the organizer’s instructions govern entry.

Badges, room entry and local times

Use the Anime Frontier website to check the badge for the day you plan to attend and the convention FAQ for registration and show hours. Registration, exhibit hall access and screenings have different hours. The FAQ lists Sunday registration from 8:00 a.m. and Sunday show hours from 10:00 a.m. If you still need to collect a badge, build that stop into your plan before the 11:00 a.m. premiere block.

For the four blocks in this guide, use the room names exactly as they appear in the schedule: Ballroom AB, 202A and 203ABC. On the day, check the convention’s program for changes to those rooms and for any queue, capacity or entry instructions attached to the individual session. If you are coordinating with friends from another time zone, send the local time together with the room. On those December dates, 11:00 a.m. in Fort Worth is noon Eastern and 1:00 p.m. in Santo Domingo.

Will these episodes stream on the same dates?

The December 4 and 6 times are for in-person screenings in Fort Worth. The episode listings on streaming services follow their own dates and territory rules. For example, Shangri-La Frontier Season 3 has a January 10, 2027 premiere date, while its first episode is in the December 6 convention program. The Aniplex block likewise gives attendees a preview of each featured show. Check the title’s own release listing when planning to watch from home.

If you want an early look at a returning series, Sunday morning has two season openers in the same room. If you prefer to try new shows from the first episode, the afternoon Aniplex session fits that plan. Friday’s Magic Knight Rayearth session is the choice with three consecutive episodes of one anime. For other dates and platforms, see the AniNextUp release calendar.

What should you watch before the convention?

For Sunday morning, finish Season 2 of Shangri-La Frontier if you want to go into the third-season opener with the returning characters and ongoing story fresh in mind. For Medaka Kuroiwa, Season 1 is the place to get acquainted with Mona and Medaka before their second-season premiere. If you only have time to catch up on one series, choose the one you most want to follow after the convention. The two episodes share a session, so you can see the other opener without planning a separate trip across the venue.

If you are choosing a day for a group with different tastes, Sunday offers both returning seasons and four first episodes of new shows. Friday offers a longer introduction to one story through three Magic Knight Rayearth episodes. For the afternoon Aniplex block, you can read the BLESS release guide before the event, then use the convention program for room and entry updates. Save the room alongside the start time when you share plans with someone who will meet you inside.
