The three new special episodes arrive on October 25, 2026. Netflix’s romance-anime announcement confirms the date for its release. The production separately publishes Japan’s television and streaming schedule.

Later Japanese television broadcasts

BS11 lists the specials for October 26 at 25:00–26:30 in broadcast notation, which is October 27 at 01:00–02:30 JST on a standard calendar. AT-X lists October 31 at 19:30–21:00 JST. These later television showings are separate from the October 25 first broadcast and streaming start.

Where can I watch outside Japan?

Netflix is the international service confirmed by its own announcement. Japan’s longer platform list also includes Prime Video, Disney+, Hulu, U-NEXT and others, but a Japanese listing is not evidence that each service has the specials in every country. Are these Season 3 or three special episodes?

Netflix labels the release “Season 2 — Special Episodes,” and the official Japanese announcement confirms three episodes. The 90-minute television slot groups them into one broadcast event; it does not turn them into a newly announced full season or establish how every streaming service will package them.

Story and cast updates

The specials continue Miyo and Kiyoka’s relationship as their wedding approaches. The production introduces Kimio Nagaba, Miyo’s former school classmate, voiced by M・A・O. Reina Ueda and Kaito Ishikawa voice Miyo Saimori and Kiyoka Kudo.

What should I watch before the specials?

Watch the existing anime seasons before starting this continuation. The specials return to Miyo and Kiyoka after the relationship and supernatural conflicts established in the television series; they are not a fresh adaptation that introduces the couple from the beginning. Netflix groups the new release with Season 2, which helps distinguish it from the first season’s earlier bonus episode. If your catalog displays several entries or season selectors, check the episode descriptions rather than choosing by the word “special” alone.

How do these differ from the earlier OVA?

The official on-air page separately lists an OVA identified as Episode 13. That older episode and the three new October 2026 specials are different releases. This distinction matters when searching for “My Happy Marriage special episode”: an older streaming listing or review may describe Episode 13 rather than this new three-part event. The October 25 announcement is the relevant notice for the new continuation. It confirms three episodes, while leaving international catalog presentation to each streaming service.

What does the story focus on?

The romance begins with an arranged engagement between Miyo, a young woman mistreated by her family, and Kiyoka, a military officer with a forbidding reputation. Their growing trust sits alongside inherited supernatural abilities and family expectations. For returning viewers, the specials bring the wedding and the couple’s future into focus. Kimio’s arrival adds a connection to Miyo’s school years: the official character description says she welcomes their reunion but carries a troubling shadow beneath that reaction. The announcement does not reveal how her involvement resolves.

Returning production team and music

Netflix names Takehiro Kubota as director and Kinema Citrus as the animation studio. Reina Ueda returns as Miyo, opposite Kaito Ishikawa as Kiyoka, with M・A・O joining as Kimio Nagaba. The production also announced Kashitaro Ito’s ending theme, “Prelude no Temae ni ~for me&you~.” Its music-service rollout begins October 26 at midnight in Japan. That is the song’s release schedule, not an alternative premiere time for the episodes.

How to read the time-zone information

Japan uses JST, UTC+9. The October 25 television start at 19:00 JST corresponds to 10:00 UTC; the domestic streaming start at 20:30 JST corresponds to 11:30 UTC. Those conversions describe the Japanese schedule only. They do not promise that your Netflix catalog will update at either converted time. The official domestic notice says distribution begins progressively and cautions that schedules can change. For international viewing, the confirmed Netflix date and your local title page remain the useful references.

What is still unconfirmed for international viewers?

The cited Netflix announcement confirms a date, but does not list a worldwide launch hour, the language options for every territory, or whether all local catalogs will display the specials identically. The Japanese TV block lasts 90 minutes, including the broadcaster’s scheduling allowance; it should not be reported as a verified 90 minutes of uninterrupted animation. Until episode listings appear, avoid treating that slot as the combined exact runtime. The practical check on release day is the new episode list under the local series page, together with its available subtitle and audio menus.

The cited announcements also do not specify a separate English-dub timetable. Naming the Japanese cast confirms those performances, but does not establish which dubbed tracks will be available on October 25 in your country. Check the local audio menu when the episodes appear.
