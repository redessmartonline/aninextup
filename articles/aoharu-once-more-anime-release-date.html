Aoharu Once More Anime Release Date, Teaser & Where to Watch

When does Aoharu Once More come out?

Aoharu Once More will begin airing on TV Tokyo in Japan in January 2027. Animation ID and Imagica Infos announced the broadcast window on November 11, 2026, along with the two lead voices and the first teaser featuring them. As of that announcement, the broadcaster has not specified the first episode’s day or time. January is the confirmed window to put on your watchlist.

The Japanese title is アオハル・ワンスモア. Use it if you want to search TV Tokyo’s listings or follow the production’s Japanese updates. The English title, Aoharu Once More, is also the title TOKYOPOP uses for the original manga. When you search in English, add “anime” or “TV Tokyo” for the series and “Toworu Miyata” or “TOKYOPOP” for the book. Otherwise, an ebook release date can easily look like an anime listing.

Is there a screening before the TV premiere?

Animation ID announced an advance screening and cast talk at Animate Theater in Higashi-Ikebukuro, Tokyo. The organizer lists December 22, with doors planned for 6 p.m. and the event for 7 p.m. Japan time. The program includes a preview of the anime and a conversation with Tasuku Kaito and Kazuki Ura. Tickets were announced through a LivePocket lottery.

The announcement pairs December 22 with a weekday that differs from the 2026 calendar. Check the official ticket listing for the current date and time before making travel plans. If you are looking for an episode to watch from home, follow the January TV announcement and subsequent platform news. The advance event is a venue screening with its own ticketing process.

What is Aoharu Once More about?

Hayato Kobayashi and Ritsu Onishi were close in high school. They spent time together around the school library, but neither put a name to the relationship before graduation. They went their separate ways, and it took time for Hayato to understand what he had felt. Years later, the two run into each other at work. Hayato sees a chance to reopen a conversation they left unfinished as teenagers.

The premise gives the romance two points of view in time. The school scenes show the closeness Hayato remembers, while the reunion gives him a chance to ask what that closeness meant to Ritsu. Having history together gives Hayato a reason to try again, and it also makes it easy for him to assume he knows how Ritsu feels. The tension comes from what they can finally say as adults and what still takes courage to ask.

For readers deciding whether to start with the manga, this is a boys’ love reunion story with both school memories and a present-day workplace setting. Knowing how the two first met helps explain why the workplace reunion has weight. The official teaser is the better starting point if you want to hear the performances before reading beyond the setup.

Who voices Hayato and Ritsu?

Tasuku Kaito voices Hayato Kobayashi and Kazuki Ura voices Ritsu Onishi. The production’s announcement points to the first teaser as a chance to hear both leads. The two actors also played the characters in the drama CD, according to the cast comments in Imagica Infos’ press release. The CD and the TV adaptation are separate releases.

Rikako Machida directs the adaptation. Imagica Infos and Imageworks Studio are credited for animation production, with Morrigan (WAVE) composing the music. BALLOON presents the series as a light anime. That describes the production approach. The official listings establish its TV broadcast, while a complete episode count and running time have yet to be announced. The teaser gives an early look at the visual presentation and how the two performances sound together.

Where can you read the Aoharu Once More manga in English?

TOKYOPOP publishes Toworu Miyata’s manga in English under its LoveLove imprint. The paperback’s ISBN is 9781427888174. Penguin Random House lists the paperback at 200 pages with a September 22, 2026 release date, and its ebook listing gives September 15, 2026. Renta also lists the licensed English digital volume. TOKYOPOP labels the manga for readers aged 18 and up.

Look for the book under Miyata’s name, then choose the edition that matches how you read. The ISBN above identifies the paperback if you are ordering through a bookstore or requesting it from a library. For a digital copy, check the publisher and language on the store page, because searching by the Japanese title can surface the original ShuCream edition. The official anime page credits Miyata’s manga as the source and identifies from RED as the publication in which it ran.

If you want to read before the broadcast, the English volume is a direct way into Hayato and Ritsu’s story. If you prefer to see the reunion unfold first on television, the early teaser gives you enough context to identify both leads without reading the manga’s later developments. The production has yet to specify exactly how much of the book the TV adaptation will cover.
