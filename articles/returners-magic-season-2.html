Why the date can look different on TV guides

Fuji TV's 24:45 slot belongs to the October 7 late-night broadcast schedule, but the clock reads 12:45 a.m. on October 8. A calendar app using ordinary 24-hour notation may therefore display a different date without contradicting the broadcaster. Crunchyroll's international release should be checked separately because a Japanese TV slot is not automatically a global simulcast time.

When does A Returner's Magic Should Be Special Season 2 premiere?

Season 2 premieres October 7, 2026. Fuji TV schedules the first episode in its +Ultra block at 24:45 JST, equivalent to 12:45 a.m. on the following calendar day in standard time notation.

Where to watch

Crunchyroll streams Season 2 as part of its Fall 2026 simulcast lineup, with announced availability worldwide except Japan, China, Korea and Mongolia. Crunchyroll's updated Fall 2026 schedule lists subtitled episodes on Wednesdays at 10:15 a.m. Pacific Time; this is separate from the Japanese television slot.

What happens in Season 2?

Desir continues trying to prevent the catastrophic future he already experienced. After building a party with Romantica, Pram and Azest at Hebrion Academy, he faces a new Shadow World crisis while a nightmare involving Romantica and the destructive demon Dadaneuph points toward a larger threat.

Who is making Season 2?

Taishi Kawaguchi directs at Arvo Animation, with Takamitsu Kono handling series composition and scripts, Hiromi Kato on character design and Kenta Higashiohji composing the music.

What to remember from Desir's first attempt

Desir's defining advantage is knowledge of a disastrous future, but that knowledge also gives him a burden his academy companions do not share. His party with Romantica, Pram and Azest is central to the sequel's announced Shadow World crisis. Returning viewers can use those relationships as the bridge between seasons; the synopsis does not confirm that Desir's interventions will avert the new threat. This is a viewing guide based on the published setup, not a forecast of the season's ending.

Opening theme

FLOW performs the Season 2 opening song “Sorrow.” The band also performed the first season's opening, giving the continuation a musical connection to the original run.

What makes Desir's second chance urgent

Desir remembers a disastrous future that his companions have not lived through. That difference in knowledge makes his attempts to protect Romantica, Pram and Azest more than ordinary school training. The new Shadow World crisis adds pressure to act, although the announced synopsis does not reveal whether he can prevent the outcome he fears.
