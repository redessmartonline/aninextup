When did Season 2 premiere in Japan?

When did Season 2 premiere in Japan? Aniplex's official broadcast and streaming notice lists Fuji TV's +Ultra block from Wednesday, October 7, 2026 at 24:45 JST. In ordinary clock notation, 24:45 on Wednesday is 12:45 a.m. on Thursday, October 8. Japanese television schedules commonly keep a late-night program attached to the preceding evening's lineup; calendar apps that do not use hours beyond 23:59 may therefore display October 8. The official listing also gives Kansai TV's Thursday 26:15 slot, with the first episode at 26:25, Tokai TV's Saturday 26:00 slot, and AT-X's Saturday 9:30 p.m. slot. These are separate Japanese broadcasts, not separate premiere dates.

Where can you stream the new episodes? Crunchyroll carries Season 2 as part of its Fall 2026 simulcast lineup. Its schedule lists the subtitled release on Wednesdays at 10:15 a.m. Pacific Time. That is a platform release time for the listed territories, not the Fuji TV broadcast time; the two schedules use different time zones and distribution windows. Crunchyroll describes its availability as worldwide except Japan, China, Korea and Mongolia. Catalog rights can vary, so viewers should confirm the show page in their own country before subscribing or planning a watch party.

Where can you stream the new episodes?

Japan also has a separate set of streaming options. Aniplex lists d Anime Store, d Anime Store Nico Nico Branch, d Anime Store for Prime Video, d Anime Store for LINE Manga, U-NEXT and Anime Hodai from October 7 at 25:15 JST. That converts to 1:15 a.m. on Thursday, October 8. A broader group of Japanese services, including FOD, ABEMA, Hulu, Lemino and Prime Video, is listed from October 11 at noon JST. Does the English dub release at the same time? Crunchyroll announced the English dub for the same day as the Japanese-language premiere: Wednesday, October 7. Its Fall lineup lists the dub airtime alongside the subtitled Wednesday 10:15 a.m. PT slot. The same-day announcement means viewers did not have to wait for a later dub launch, though an episode may appear at a different local clock time after conversion. Crunchyroll announced Austin Tindle as Desir, Sarah Roach as Romantica, Macy Anne Johnson as Pram, and Anastasia Muñoz as Azest in its cast-and-crew notice. Check the platform listing for the audio options available in your region.

What is the Season 2 story about? The official synopsis continues Desir Herrman's second chance after humanity's defeat by the Shadow Worlds. Sent thirteen years into the past, he is reliving academy life and gathering three allies: wind mage Romantica Eru, swordsman Pram Schnaizer and ice magic knight Azest Kingscrown. Their task is to confront a Shadow World that has appeared again and correct the history behind it. The season's new setup adds Desir's nightmare of Romantica's death and identifies the Devil of Destruction as the figure connected to that fear. This gives returning viewers a clear new conflict without revealing how the group resolves it.

Who is making the second season? Aniplex lists Taishi Kawaguchi as director, Takamitsu Kono for series composition and scripts, Hiromi Kato for character design, and Kenta Higashiohji for music. Arvo Animation is the animation studio, produced by HAYATE. The principal Japanese cast returns: Takuma Terashima voices Desir, Sayumi Suzushiro voices Romantica, Natsumi Fujiwara voices Pram, and Asami Seto voices Azest.
