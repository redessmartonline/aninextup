A Returner's Magic Should Be Special Season 2

A Returner's Magic Should Be Special Season 2 premieres October 7, 2026 on Crunchyroll.

RELEASE DATEOctober 7, 2026
STREAMINGCrunchyroll

When does A Returner's Magic Should Be Special Season 2 premiere?

Season 2 premieres October 7, 2026. Fuji TV schedules the first episode in its +Ultra block at 24:45 JST, equivalent to 12:45 a.m. on the following calendar day in standard time notation.

Where to watch

Crunchyroll streams Season 2 as part of its Fall 2026 simulcast lineup, with announced availability worldwide except Japan, China, Korea and Mongolia. Exact episode timing on the service can differ from the Japanese television slot.

What happens in Season 2?

Desir continues trying to prevent the catastrophic future he already experienced. After building a party with Romantica, Pram and Azest at Hebrion Academy, he faces a new Shadow World crisis while a nightmare involving Romantica and the destructive demon Dadaneuph points toward a larger threat.

Who is making Season 2?

Taishi Kawaguchi directs at Arvo Animation, with Takamitsu Kono handling series composition and scripts, Hiromi Kato on character design and Kenta Higashiohji composing the music.

Opening theme

FLOW performs the Season 2 opening song “Sorrow.” The band also performed the first season's opening, giving the continuation a musical connection to the original run.
