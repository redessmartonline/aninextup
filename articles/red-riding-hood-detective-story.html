Red Riding Hood: A Detective Story Premieres January 2027 on Crunchyroll

What the January window establishes

January 2027 is a confirmed broadcast month, not an episode-by-episode timetable. Crunchyroll's announced rights outside Asia also leave Asian distribution as a separate question. Viewers should therefore distinguish the Japanese broadcaster list from the international streaming territories instead of assuming that one announcement covers every market.

When does Red Riding Hood: A Detective Story release?

The TV anime will begin broadcasting in January 2027 on TOKYO MX, BS NTV and Yomiuri TV in Japan. An exact premiere day has not been announced yet.

Where can you watch it?

Crunchyroll has confirmed it will stream the anime worldwide excluding Asia when the series premieres in January 2027. Related reading: The Detective Is Already Dead Season 2: Release Date, Episodes & Where to Watch.

New cast announced

The newly announced cast includes Takuya Sato as Jill, Atsushi Tamaru as Antonio, Motoko Kumai as Rodrigo, Kotaro Nishiyama as Paolo, Sakiko Uran as Thumbelina and Ryoko Shiraishi as narrator. They join Yume Miyamoto as Red Riding Hood and Akira Sekine as Pinocchio.

Studio and staff

Itsuro Kawasaki directs and writes the anime at BENTEN Film. Manami Minakata and Yuko Yahiro handle character designs, while Jun Ichikawa composes the music.

What is the anime about?

The series turns familiar fairy-tale settings into mystery cases. Red Riding Hood travels through the storybook world and repeatedly finds herself caught up in incidents, using her curiosity and sharp reasoning to solve each mystery. What the October announcement means for viewers

The production now has a January 2027 Japanese broadcast window, a fuller cast and confirmed Crunchyroll distribution outside Asia. The exact first-airing day and streaming arrangements within Asia remain separate unanswered questions; the new promotional video does not establish either.

Why the fairy-tale detective premise stands out

Red Riding Hood's role is different from that of a conventional fairy-tale protagonist: the announced premise puts her in the position of investigating incidents rather than simply escaping a familiar danger. For viewers, that changes the central question from “what happens in the original tale?” to “how will this mystery be solved?” This is AniNextUp's interpretation of the synopsis, not a claim about the structure of any unshown episode.

How to read the streaming announcement by region

The January 2027 broadcast window and Crunchyroll's announced distribution rights answer two separate questions. Japanese broadcaster names identify domestic television outlets; “worldwide excluding Asia” describes Crunchyroll's stated streaming territory. Neither statement establishes which platform will carry the show within Asia. If you live in an excluded territory, wait for a separate local distributor announcement rather than assuming that the Japanese broadcast or Crunchyroll listing applies to you. What to check when the first episode gets a date

A specific premiere day, a local streaming time and available subtitle or dub languages are distinct details. Once a dated schedule appears, readers should check the official anime site for Japanese broadcast information and their regional platform listing for actual access. The confirmed January window alone cannot be used to calculate a reliable episode countdown.
