Red Riding Hood: A Detective Story Premieres January 2027 on Crunchyroll

What the January window establishes

January 2027 is a confirmed broadcast month, not an episode-by-episode timetable. Crunchyroll's announced rights outside Asia also leave Asian distribution as a separate question. Viewers should therefore distinguish the Japanese broadcaster list from the international streaming territories instead of assuming that one announcement covers every market.

When does Red Riding Hood: A Detective Story release?

The TV anime will begin broadcasting in January 2027 on TOKYO MX, BS NTV and Yomiuri TV in Japan. An exact premiere day has not been announced yet.

Where can you watch it?

Crunchyroll has confirmed it will stream the anime worldwide excluding Asia when the series premieres in January 2027.

New cast announced

The newly announced cast includes Takuya Sato as Jill, Atsushi Tamaru as Antonio, Motoko Kumai as Rodrigo, Kotaro Nishiyama as Paolo, Sakiko Uran as Thumbelina and Ryoko Shiraishi as narrator. They join Yume Miyamoto as Red Riding Hood and Akira Sekine as Pinocchio. Related reading: The Detective Is Already Dead Season 2: Release Date, Episodes & Where to Watch.

Studio and staff

Itsuro Kawasaki directs and writes the anime at BENTEN Film. Manami Minakata and Yuko Yahiro handle character designs, while Jun Ichikawa composes the music.

What is the anime about?

The series turns familiar fairy-tale settings into mystery cases. Red Riding Hood travels through the storybook world and repeatedly finds herself caught up in incidents, using her curiosity and sharp reasoning to solve each mystery.

Why the fairy-tale detective premise stands out

Red Riding Hood's role is different from that of a conventional fairy-tale protagonist: the announced premise puts her in the position of investigating incidents rather than simply escaping a familiar danger. For viewers, that changes the central question from “what happens in the original tale?” to “how will this mystery be solved?” This is AniNextUp's interpretation of the synopsis, not a claim about the structure of any unshown episode.

How to read the streaming announcement by region

The January 2027 broadcast window and Crunchyroll's announced distribution rights answer two separate questions. Japanese broadcaster names identify domestic television outlets; “worldwide excluding Asia” describes Crunchyroll's stated streaming territory. Neither statement establishes which platform will carry the show within Asia. If you live in an excluded territory, wait for a separate local distributor announcement rather than assuming that the Japanese broadcast or Crunchyroll listing applies to you.

What is the anime based on?

The television series adapts Aito Aoyagi's Japanese mystery novels, published by Futabasha. Crunchyroll reports that the original series began publication in August 2020 and had four volumes in Japan when the October anime announcement appeared. The Japanese title is Akazukin, Tabi no Tochuu de Shitai to Deau; the announced English title is Red Riding Hood: A Detective Story.

The source combines recognizable fairy-tale settings with detective fiction. According to the anime's official introduction, Red Riding Hood encounters cases during her journey and uses her curiosity and intelligence to solve them. Familiar names offer a starting point, but the anime's own version of each encounter should be judged as its episodes are released. What did the main trailer announcement include?

The October update paired a main promotional video and key visual with the January broadcast window and six additional voice credits. The official video is available below. It introduces the adaptation ahead of broadcast, while the cast list provides the Japanese performers associated with the characters announced so far.

Yume Miyamoto voices the traveling Red Riding Hood and Akira Sekine voices Pinocchio. The newly named roles expand that lineup to Jill, Antonio, Rodrigo, Paolo and Thumbelina, as well as the narrator. These are Japanese voice credits; the announcement does not establish an English-dub cast or a dub release date.

Will every episode arrive on the same day?

The announcement identifies the month and broadcasters, but does not give a complete episode count or release timetable. A January start alone cannot confirm a weekly cadence or a full-season drop.
