When does the ELECEED anime come out?

ELECEED is scheduled for April 2027. The anime's official website confirmed the month on October 21, 2026. Its accompanying English-language announcement also describes an April worldwide premiere. An exact first-episode date has not been announced.

This narrows the earlier early-2027 window. The update gives the adaptation a spring release window; broadcaster and streaming schedules are still to follow.

Where will ELECEED stream?

Crunchyroll has announced that it will stream the series. That identifies a distribution partner, but the announcement should not be read as a guarantee that every territory will have identical availability, subtitles or launch times. Check the service's local listing when the premiere approaches.

The April announcement does not confirm an English dub schedule or a simultaneous dubbed launch. Viewers choosing between subtitled and dubbed episodes should wait for a specific language announcement rather than infer one from the new trailer.

What is ELECEED about?

Jiwoo has extraordinary reflexes and a habit of helping others. His life changes after he takes in a wounded cat: the animal is connected to Kayden, a powerful figure with secrets of his own. Their partnership brings a superhero-style ability story together with the awkward practicalities of living with a cat.

The source comic is available in English on WEBTOON, credited to writer Jeho Son and artist ZHENA. It offers a starting point for readers who want to meet the central pair before the adaptation, without waiting for the television premiere.

Which studio is making the anime?

DandeLion Animation Studio is producing the adaptation. The announced core staff includes director Hiroshi Nishikiori, series composition and script writer Yousuke Kuroda, and character designer Minami Sakura. The number of episodes and the portion of the comic covered by the adaptation have not been announced.

Watch the official English-version trailer

The production released an English version of its promotional trailer alongside the April announcement. An English-language trailer does not, by itself, establish an English dub cast or a dubbed episode release date.

Watch on YouTube if the player is unavailable.

What remains unannounced?

The exact premiere day, episode count, weekly streaming time and English dub release date are not established by the October 21 notices. A month-only announcement is useful for planning, but it is not a complete broadcast schedule.
