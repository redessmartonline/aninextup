When does the Takopi movie release?

November 27, 2026 in Japanese theaters, including Shinjuku Wald 9.

Official video

Is it a new story?

The movie is a theatrical re-edit of all six episodes of the Takopi's Original Sin anime, but it will also include newly added scenes.

What is Takopi's Original Sin about?

Takopi, an alien who came to Earth to spread happiness, meets Shizuka and tries to restore her smile with mysterious Happy Gadgets. His attempts collide with the painful realities surrounding her home and school life.

The November 27 release is currently confirmed for Japanese cinemas. AniNextUp will update this page when an international theatrical or streaming release is officially announced.
