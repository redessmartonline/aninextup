When does the Takopi movie release?

Takopi's Original Sin: Thank You, See You Tomorrow opens in Japanese theaters on November 27, 2026. The theatrical version revisits the complete television anime while adding material created specifically for the movie presentation.

Is it a new story?

The film is a theatrical re-edit of all six episodes of the Takopi's Original Sin anime and includes newly added scenes. It therefore is not a separate sequel, but it offers additional material beyond simply screening the original episodes unchanged.

What is Takopi's Original Sin about?

Takopi is an alien who arrives on Earth hoping to spread happiness. After meeting Shizuka, he uses mysterious Happy Gadgets in an attempt to restore her smile, only to discover that the problems surrounding her family and school life cannot be solved with simple magical tools.

Where to watch

The November 27 release is currently confirmed for Japanese cinemas. An international theatrical or streaming release has not been announced.
