How the movie differs from the television anime

The theatrical version brings the six-episode story together in one cinema presentation and adds newly created scenes. That makes it a different viewing format, not a continuation set after the television ending. Readers who have already watched the series can decide whether the new material and theatrical presentation justify revisiting a story they know; the Japanese cinema date alone does not establish international availability.

When does the Takopi movie release?

Takopi's Original Sin: Thank You, See You Tomorrow opens in Japanese theaters on November 27, 2026. The theatrical version revisits the complete television anime while adding material created specifically for the movie presentation.

Should existing viewers expect a completely new sequel?

No. TBS describes the film as a theatrical re-edit of the six-episode anime with additional newly produced scenes. That makes the November 27 Japanese cinema release a different presentation of the existing story rather than a confirmed sequel set after its ending. The announcement does not detail how much new animation appears or which moments it covers. Read the official TBS film announcement. Related reading: Are You a Landmine, Chihara-san? What does the theatrical edit include?

The film combines material from all six television episodes with newly created scenes. The announcement does not specify the length of that added footage, so it would be premature to describe the film as an entirely new story or to promise particular scenes.

What is Takopi's Original Sin about?

Takopi is an alien who arrives on Earth hoping to spread happiness. After meeting Shizuka, he uses mysterious Happy Gadgets in an attempt to restore her smile, only to discover that the problems surrounding her family and school life cannot be solved with simple magical tools. What the October 23 trailer and song announcement add

TBS released the movie’s full trailer and a newly drawn visual on October 23, then confirmed “らびゅー” by Tele as the theme song. The update gives viewers two concrete pieces of the film’s identity beyond its November 27 Japanese theatrical date: a campaign song and a new visual illustrated by character designer Keita Nagahara. It also confirms that the project’s publicity is now moving from the initial date announcement into the final pre-release period.

The trailer is useful for seeing how the six-episode television story is being presented for theaters, but it does not establish how many minutes of new animation the edit contains. TBS has described the film as a theatrical compilation with newly produced scenes; the amount and placement of that material remain unspecified. The trailer and theme song also do not confirm an international cinema or streaming plan. Readers outside Japan should treat November 27 as a Japan-only date unless a distributor announces additional territories.

When do Japanese advance tickets go on sale?

TBS's official ticket page lists advance movie-ticket card sales from October 23, 2026 at 10:00 a.m. Japan time through the day before the November 27 opening. The listed general price is ¥1,600 including tax. Sales at theater counters vary by cinema, and the page notes that advance sales can end without notice.

These are Japanese ticket details, not confirmation of overseas screenings. Check the official theater and ticket listings for availability before making plans. The ticket page also warns of sensitive material and says viewers under 12 need parental guidance.

What should viewers check before buying a ticket?

The official announcement confirms the Japanese cinema date and describes the film as a re-edit of all six television episodes with newly created scenes. It does not specify the movie’s runtime, the amount of new footage or screenings outside Japan. Those details should remain open until TBS or a distributor publishes them. A trailer can show scenes selected for promotion, but it does not establish how much of the finished theatrical cut is new. For readers comparing updates, the safest distinction is between the confirmed November 27 Japan release and the still-unannounced international plan. The official film site is the best place to check for later theater, ticket and distribution notices.
