When does the Takopi movie release?

Takopi's Original Sin: Thank You, See You Tomorrow opens in Japanese theaters on November 27, 2026. The theatrical version revisits the complete television anime while adding material created specifically for the movie presentation.

Should existing viewers expect a completely new sequel?

No. TBS describes the film as a theatrical re-edit of the six-episode anime with additional newly produced scenes. That makes the November 27 Japanese cinema release a different presentation of the existing story rather than a confirmed sequel set after its ending. The announcement does not detail how much new animation appears or which moments it covers. Read the official TBS film announcement.

What does the theatrical edit include?

The film combines material from all six television episodes with newly created scenes. The announcement does not specify the length of that added footage, so it would be premature to describe the film as an entirely new story or to promise particular scenes.

What is Takopi's Original Sin about?

Takopi is an alien who arrives on Earth hoping to spread happiness. After meeting Shizuka, he uses mysterious Happy Gadgets in an attempt to restore her smile, only to discover that the problems surrounding her family and school life cannot be solved with simple magical tools.

Where to watch

The November 27 release is currently confirmed for Japanese cinemas. An international theatrical or streaming release has not been announced.
