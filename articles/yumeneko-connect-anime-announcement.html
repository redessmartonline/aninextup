Yumeneko Connect Anime Announced: Cast, Studio and Teaser

What was officially announced?

The official Shochiku anime site confirms that Yumeneko Connect is in production as a television anime. The first announcement introduces the premise, lead cast, main staff, animation studio and teaser video. It does not announce a premiere window, broadcaster or streaming service, so those details remain TBA.

Does the announcement trailer confirm a 2027 release?

No. Shochiku's first television anime announcement confirms the adaptation, lead cast, staff and teaser, but does not provide a broadcast year or season. A teaser's publication date is not the anime's premiere date. Until the official production lists a window, the release date and international streaming arrangements should remain to be announced.

Who is in the cast?

Yusuke Kobayashi voices lonely high-school student Yuhi Takamiya. Yu Shinohara (篠原侑) voices Nano, an alien who arrives after hearing Yuhi's wish to make friends and offers him unusual products that send his school life into chaos.

Staff and animation studio

Sota Yokote (横手颯太) directs the adaptation at STUDIO MASSKET. Yoshihiro Watanabe (渡邊義弘) handles character design, Juokuemon Ichikawa (市川十億衛門) oversees series composition and scripts, and Soshi Matsumura (松村颯士) composes the music. Lantis handles music production. Shochiku lists SHOCHIKU ANIME as the production organizer.

What is Yumeneko Connect about?

The story follows Yuhi, who misses his first week of high school and struggles to make friends. His wish for companionship attracts Nano, an alien salesgirl whose bizarre products create a fast-moving series of science-fiction comedy mishaps. The manga by Ko Sawada runs in Kodansha's Weekly Shonen Magazine.

When will Yumeneko Connect premiere?

No release date or broadcast season has been announced. Any specific year, season or streaming destination would be speculation until the production committee publishes a follow-up notice.

What the teaser shows, and what it leaves open

Shochiku’s October 14 announcement presents Yumeneko Connect as a television anime and opens the official website. The teaser introduces Yuhi and Nano and gives a first look at the series’ bright, exaggerated science-fiction comedy. A separate official note features the manga author’s commemorative illustration and comments from cast and staff. These materials confirm that the adaptation is moving into production promotion; they do not confirm a broadcast season, network or streaming service.

The original manga by Ko Sawada runs in Kodansha’s Weekly Shonen Magazine. Its setup follows Yuhi Takamiya, a high-school student who wants friends, and Nano, an alien who arrives after hearing his wish. Her unusual products disrupt his school life. The anime’s official description emphasizes a lively, sometimes provocative comedy built around those products and the heroines. That is a clearer guide to the source’s tone than treating the title as a conventional alien-invasion story.

Why no premiere date is listed yet

An announcement of a television adaptation is a real production milestone, but it is not a schedule. The October 14 material names the lead cast and staff and releases a teaser, yet contains no day, month, broadcaster or platform for the series. The teaser’s upload date is also not an air date. AniNextUp will keep the date marked “not announced” until Shochiku or the production committee publishes a release window.

For now, readers can use the official manga page to explore the source and the anime page for cast, staff and promotional updates. International availability is a separate rights question and cannot be derived from the Japanese production announcement. We will add territory-specific information only when a distributor names a service and the countries it covers.
