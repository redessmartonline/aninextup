When does I Lost My Adventurer's License premiere?

I Lost My Adventurer's License, but It's Fine Because I Have an Adorable Daughter Now is scheduled to premiere on Japanese television in January 2027. The production confirmed the month in its October 15, 2026 update, alongside a teaser visual and promotional video. An exact broadcast day and time have not yet been disclosed.

The January announcement gives fans a confirmed seasonal window, but it should not be confused with a specific worldwide streaming launch. The official production has not confirmed an international platform or regional release schedule.

Does the January 2027 announcement confirm a worldwide release?

No. The production's October 15 teaser confirms a Japanese television premiere window in January 2027, not a global simulcast schedule. A teaser PV and promotional visual show that the adaptation is being promoted, but neither identifies an international streaming service or an exact episode date. Viewers outside Japan should check a future licensed distributor announcement rather than assume the Japanese broadcast date applies to their region.

Who voices Douglas and Rabi?

Hiroki Yasumoto voices Douglas, a former legendary magician whose declining strength leaves him without his adventurer's license. Yuka Iguchi voices Rabi, a young girl Douglas encounters while wandering. Their developing father-daughter bond is central to the adaptation.

Both performers were announced with comments about their characters and the story on October 15. Additional voice cast members have not yet been detailed in the same announcement.

Which studio is animating the series?

studio Palette is producing the animation. Yuki Inaba directs and also oversees series composition and scripts. Kaori Yoshikawa handles character design and serves as chief animation director. Ohshu and Daiki Okuno compose the music, with SPACE SHOWER MUSIC responsible for music production.

What is the anime about?

Douglas was once a powerful enhancement magician, but age and changing circumstances cost him his position in the hero's party and ultimately his adventurer's license. After meeting Rabi, a cursed girl with no family to turn to, he begins a new journey with her.

The story mixes fantasy quests and travel with everyday moments as Douglas and Rabi gradually grow closer. It adapts the light novel written by Manimani Ononata and illustrated by Fuzichoco, published under SB Creative's GA Novel imprint. A manga adaptation is also available through Square Enix's Manga UP! service.
