When does the IjiHaji anime come out?

IjiHaji is confirmed as a television anime for 2027, but an exact premiere date and season have not been announced in the official pages checked on November 21, 2026. The adaptation was revealed on November 20 with a teaser visual. The year is the confirmed release window; it should not be expanded into January, spring or another season without a further announcement.

For your watchlist, save the series under 2027 and leave the day blank. The initial announcement date belongs to the news, not to episode one. Likewise, a date attached to a cast reveal or promotional video describes publicity for the adaptation. It does not mean that the first episode will be released on that day.

What does IjiHaji mean, and what is the full title?

IjiHaji is the shortened name used by the official production for Jimoto no Ijimekko-tachi ni Shikaeshi Shiyou to Shitara, Betsu no Tatakai ga Hajimatta. The Japanese title is 地元のいじめっ子達に仕返ししようとしたら、別の戦いが始まった。. Its wording describes an attempted retaliation against hometown bullies that turns into a different battle.

The short title is easier to remember, while the full Japanese name is useful for finding the original manga and production announcements. Romanized spellings can vary between listings, particularly the long vowels in shiyou. Match the author, characters and Japanese title when checking whether two results refer to the same work. This guide uses IjiHaji as the convenient short name, rather than presenting a literal translation as a newly announced official English title.

What is IjiHaji about? Is it a revenge drama?

Kodansha’s first-volume description introduces Kotaro, who spent his childhood losing challenges against three exceptionally talented local rivals. Akira excelled at wrestling, Kanae at basketball and Shigure at games. As a high-school student, he returns intending to settle those old scores, only for the reunion to turn the premise toward romantic comedy.

The adaptation announcement also identifies the work as a rom-com. That is useful context for a title whose reference to bullying and retaliation might otherwise suggest a straightforward revenge thriller. The appeal described by the promotion lies in a reunion, competitive relationships and romantic misunderstandings, rather than a promise of a grim punishment story.

That is a premise summary, not an evaluation of the completed anime. The initial visual cannot establish how the show will balance every joke with the characters’ earlier experiences. If the competitive setup interests you, the first manga volume offers a more substantial introduction than assuming the tone from an isolated title translation.

Who is in the IjiHaji cast?

Kaori Maeda voices Akira Mikazuki. The official production account announced her casting on November 21, marking the first main-cast reveal. The role is written 三日月アキラ in the announcement. The production website still displays question marks for the principal voice roles in the version checked, so the dated social announcement provides the more recent information.

Is there an IjiHaji trailer yet?

The launch materials checked for this article include a teaser visual; an announcement PV is scheduled for November 24, 2026 at 12:00 JST. The release schedule reported with the announcement calls for four main-cast reveals, one per day at noon in Japan from November 21. Animate Times’ November 20 announcement coverage records that schedule.

November 24 at noon in Japan corresponds to November 24 at 03:00 UTC. That conversion concerns the planned promotional-video release, not the anime premiere. This article does not embed a fan-made substitute or describe scenes from a PV that has not yet been published. Follow the official IjiHaji account and production news page for the actual upload and any schedule change.

Where will IjiHaji stream? Is Netflix or Crunchyroll confirmed?

No streaming platform is identified in the official material reviewed on November 21. A Japanese television adaptation and its release year are confirmed; an international distributor is a separate announcement. There is no verified Netflix or Crunchyroll release, English-dub date or territory list to attach to this guide yet.

Before subscribing for the show, wait for a service to name the title and your country. Check whether its announcement concerns subtitles, a dub or both, and whether it supplies an actual premiere time. A platform’s other romantic comedies do not determine its rights to IjiHaji. Until those details arrive, a watchlist entry is more useful than an invented streaming countdown.

Which studio and staff are making the anime?

The official staff listing names Gekkou as the animation studio, Chihiro Kumano as director, Ippo Hyodo for series composition and Yusaku Nakamura for character design. Kengo Matsumoto is credited as the original manga creator.

These credits identify the announced adaptation team. They do not establish the length of the season, the final manga chapter it will cover or whether later seasons have been commissioned. The useful production details still to watch for include a broadcast schedule and episode information; those should come from a new notice rather than an assumption about how other manga adaptations are packaged.

Where can you read the original manga?

The source is Kengo Matsumoto’s manga, published by Kodansha. The publisher’s series catalog provides Japanese book listings and a first-episode preview link. Its Volume 1 record gives an August 8, 2023 publication date and ISBN 9784065325230. These are details of the original Japanese book, not an English edition or the television premiere.

Start at the beginning to see how the reunion is introduced. Choosing the latest volume simply because it appears first in a shop’s results can skip the premise and reveal later relationships. The anime’s official books section currently displays Volumes 1–9; that catalog does not tell viewers how many volumes the season will animate.

No authorized English manga edition was established from the publisher pages reviewed for this article. If you prefer to read in English, look for a separate licensing announcement that names the publisher and format. A translated title on a news site is not evidence that a translated book has been released.
