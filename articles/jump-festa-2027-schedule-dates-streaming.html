Jump Festa 2027 Schedule: Dates, Stages & Streaming

Jump Festa 2027 takes place on December 19–20, 2026, at Makuhari Messe in Chiba, Japan. The official Super Stage timetable is available, with Kagurabachi opening Saturday’s program and One Piece closing Sunday’s. Despite the name, this edition happens in December 2026, so searches and calendar reminders should use those dates.

This guide brings the main-stage times together in English and explains the difference between a confirmed panel, a confirmed online broadcast and an anime announcement. A franchise appearing on the program does not, by itself, confirm a new season, movie or release date.

When is Jump Festa 2027?

The event runs on Saturday, December 19, and Sunday, December 20. The organizer lists general opening hours of 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Japan Standard Time, with final admission at 4:30 p.m. Some stage programs continue after the general closing time: Naruto finishes at 5:25 p.m. on Saturday, while One Piece finishes at 5:35 p.m. on Sunday. Related reading: Anime Releasing in December 2026: Confirmed Dates.

The venue is Makuhari Messe’s International Exhibition Halls 1–9 and 11. Admission is free but requires advance application and invitation. Free admission should therefore not be read as unrestricted walk-in entry. The official rules limit attendance, including companions, to people with an address in Japan; check eligibility before arranging travel.

How to convert the stage times

Japan does not change its clocks for daylight saving time. In December, subtract nine hours from JST for UTC, fourteen hours for U.S. Eastern Standard Time, and seventeen hours for U.S. Pacific Standard Time. The result can fall on the previous calendar date, particularly for audiences in North America.

For example, Saturday’s 11:40 a.m. Kagurabachi session starts at 2:40 a.m. UTC on December 19. That is 9:40 p.m. Eastern and 6:40 p.m. Pacific on Friday, December 18. Sunday’s 4:45 p.m. One Piece session starts at 7:45 a.m. UTC, 2:45 a.m. Eastern on Sunday, December 20, and 11:45 p.m. Pacific on Saturday, December 19.

Use the Japanese event date together with the timezone when setting reminders. Copying only the clock time into a local calendar can move a session by many hours. The tables preserve the organizer’s timezone so that later schedule changes can be compared directly.

Where can you watch Jump Festa 2027 online?

The official precautions page states that all Super Stage programs will also be distributed online. It says the same for Jump Studio. That confirms online coverage, but it does not establish an individual playable stream address for each panel, an English interpretation track or unrestricted availability in every territory.

Jump Station Online is a separate online-only stage, with distribution planned on the official Jump Channel on YouTube. Do not assume that its channel information automatically describes every Super Stage broadcast. Once individual broadcast links are published, check the stage name and session time against the timetable before joining.

Archive distribution remains undecided in the organizer’s notice. Viewers who cannot watch live should therefore avoid relying on a guaranteed replay. A later official trailer upload is also different from an archived panel: it may contain the promotional footage without the surrounding discussion or guest appearances.

Which anime announcements are confirmed?

The schedule confirms franchise sessions, not the content of unrevealed announcements. Jujutsu Kaisen, My Hero Academia, Chainsaw Man, Dandadan, Spy × Family and Demon Slayer all have Super Stage slots, but those listings alone do not supply a new anime premiere date.

The useful approach is to follow the session for a title you already care about, then check any announcement against its production website or official video channel. A panel can cover manga, merchandise, games or an existing adaptation. Treating every appearance as a promised sequel would turn a schedule into a list of unsupported predictions.

Is there an official trailer to watch?

This is an event schedule, not an anime release announcement. No event-specific promotional video was verified for this guide during the October 28 check. An unrelated franchise trailer would not help establish the panel schedule, so this page does not present one as a Jump Festa 2027 trailer.

When an official event video or relevant announcement trailer becomes available and can be verified, it can be embedded here with its title and source identified. Until then, the confirmed viewing information is the organizer’s online-distribution notice.

What should visitors check next?

Recheck entry applications, stage-viewing requirements and the final timetable before making travel plans. Event entry and permission to watch a particular stage are separate details to confirm in the organizer’s guide. The event also warns that programs may change or be canceled.
