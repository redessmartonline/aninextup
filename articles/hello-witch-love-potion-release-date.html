Hello, I am a Witch and my Crush Wants me to Make a Love Potion! Release Date & Where to Watch

The fantasy romance began its October 2026 run on Crunchyroll, following Rose, a solitary witch whose longtime crush asks her to brew a love potion for someone else.

Why the potion request drives the romance

Rose's task is emotionally complicated because the person requesting the potion is the same knight she has admired for years. Delaying the ingredients becomes a way to prolong their meetings, creating tension between what she has been asked to do and what she actually wants. That makes the premise a character-driven romantic dilemma rather than simply a story about magical recipes.

Why the official story calls this a romance beginning with heartbreak

The official Japanese introduction explains that Rose believes Harij wants the love potion for someone else. She cannot bring herself to refuse his request, so the time needed to gather ingredients also becomes time she can spend with him. This is the central tension of the announced premise: making the potion would fulfill her professional task while seemingly bringing her own hopes to an end. What is it about?

Rose lives quietly as the Good Witch of the Lake. Her four-year crush on Royal Knight Harij seems doomed when he asks her to make a love potion. Rose agrees, stretching out the ingredient-gathering process so she can spend more time with him, only for their relationship to begin changing in unexpected ways.

Why newcomers can start with Episode 1

The series adapts Eiko Mutsuhana's light novels as a new television anime, not a sequel to an earlier animated season. Its central relationship is introduced through Harij's request for a love potion, so viewers do not need previous anime episodes to understand the premise.

Cast and staff

Mikako Komatsu voices Rose and Yohei Azakami voices Harij. Keizo Kusakawa directs at Diomedéa, Wataru Watari handles series composition, Masakazu Ishikawa handles character design, and Satoshi Matsumura composes the music.

What makes Rose and Harij's story different?

The central conflict is not whether Rose knows how to make a potion; it is whether she can be honest about her feelings while helping the person she loves pursue someone else. Harij's visits give the two characters opportunities to learn about each other, turning a seemingly straightforward fantasy request into an awkward, slow-building romance. The anime's appeal rests on their conversations and changing relationship rather than a large-scale battle or quest.
