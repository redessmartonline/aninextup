When did Hello, I am a Witch premiere?

Hello, I am a Witch and my Crush Wants me to Make a Love Potion! began airing in Japan on October 5, 2026. It is a new television adaptation, so newcomers can start with Episode 1. The premiere date marks the beginning of the weekly run; it is not a promise that the whole season became available together.

The official Japanese schedule gives different slots for individual channels. AT-X starts Mondays at 21:30, TOKYO MX at 22:30 and BS Nippon TV at 23:00. These are Japan Standard Time, JST (UTC+09:00). The same programme can therefore reach different viewers at different times without the listings contradicting one another.

Two late-night listings need particular care. RKB lists Monday at 27:00, meaning Tuesday at 03:00 on an ordinary calendar. MBS lists Tuesday at 26:30, meaning Wednesday at 02:30. Anyone converting these slots should move to the following calendar day before converting from JST. Broadcasters warn that their schedules may change. Related reading: Love Live! For Japan, the official site lists d Anime Store, U-NEXT and Anime Hodai from October 5, with new episodes scheduled on Mondays at 22:00 JST. Other Japanese services begin rolling out the series from October 10 at 22:00 JST. For a time-zone-neutral reference, 22:00 JST equals 13:00 UTC. Japan does not observe daylight saving time, while other countries may change clocks during the season. This conversion describes the Japanese streaming slot; it does not confirm that an overseas service releases its episode at the same moment. What is the story about?

Rose lives by a lake and has quietly loved Royal Knight Harij for four years. When he asks her to make a love potion, she assumes he wants it for someone else. She accepts the commission despite her heartbreak, then prolongs the search for ingredients because that gives her more time with him. The official introduction presents their relationship as an awkward romance beginning with apparent rejection.

The useful distinction is between Rose's belief and an established romantic outcome. The premise tells us how she understands the request; it does not justify revealing a final couple resolution or inventing Harij's hidden intentions. His visits and her reluctance create the central tension. Viewers looking for a quieter fantasy romance can approach the show through those conversations rather than expecting the potion to function as a battle objective or a conventional adventure quest. Who voices Rose and Harij?

Mikako Komatsu voices Rose, and Yohei Azakami voices Harij. The official credits name Keizo Kusakawa as director, Wataru Watari for series composition and Masakazu Ishikawa for character design. Diomedéa produces the animation, with music by Hayato Matsumura. The source credits separately identify Eiko Mutsuhana's original work, vient's original character designs and Kamada's manga adaptation, keeping those creative roles distinct.

Can I read the original novel in English?

Yes. Cross Infinite World publishes the English novel and labels the series completed. Its listing credits author Eiko Mutsuhana, illustrator vient and translator Charis Messier. It offers the first ebook and links to Volume 2 through several digital retailers. That is a direct reading route for viewers who want the source story in English. The novel, manga adaptation and television anime are separate editions of the same property. Reading the novel is optional preparation, not required viewing. The publisher's completed-series label concerns the books: it does not establish that the anime will adapt the entire story or confirm a second anime season. No official episode-to-chapter or episode-to-volume equivalence is supplied here.

A song's digital or CD release is a separate event from an episode premiere.
