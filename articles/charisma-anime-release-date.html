Charisma Anime: Short Episodes Begin Ahead of January 2027 Premiere

Charisma shorts begin November 4 on TBS before the January 7, 2027 TV premiere. See the official teaser, Japanese schedule, cast and streaming status.

By AniNextUp Editorial Team · Published: November 4, 2026 · Editorial policy

Charisma begins its short-anime run in Japan on November 4, ahead of the main television series premiering January 7, 2027. The shorts air inside TBS’s Yoru no Brunch, a Wednesday program beginning at 11:56 p.m. Japan Standard Time. That is the program’s start time; the announcement does not give an exact minute for the Charisma segment.

The two dates describe different releases. November marks the start of the short-anime broadcast within another program. January is the scheduled debut of the standalone TV anime on 28 TBS-affiliated stations.

What starts on November 4?

The official November 1 announcement confirms a short Charisma anime inside Yoru no Brunch every Wednesday. It introduces an earlier opportunity to see the franchise in animated form before the January television run.

For viewers building a watchlist, the useful distinction is format. A short segment inside a variety program is not the same release as a full TV-anime episode. The announcement does not identify the shorts as Episode 1 of the January series, give their individual running times, or publish a complete short-episode count.

Treat the November and January broadcasts as separate entries when tracking the schedule. Watching a November short should not lead viewers to assume that the January series has already started.

When does the TV anime premiere?

The TV anime is scheduled for Thursday, January 7, 2027, at 11:56 p.m. JST. Its official site lists a simultaneous nationwide Japanese broadcast across 28 TBS-network stations.

Japan’s broadcast announcement establishes the domestic television schedule. It does not establish an international streaming release at the same moment. A subscription-service listing, regional release time, or English-dub plan would need its own confirmation.

Where can international viewers watch?

The official announcements reviewed for this article identify TBS for the shorts and the TBS network for the TV series. They do not name an international streaming platform.

Viewers outside Japan should therefore check future distribution announcements before assuming Charisma will appear on their usual anime service. The official teaser is publicly linked from the production announcement, but promotional-video access does not mean access to the full series.

What is Charisma about?

The franchise centers on seven men living together in Charisma House. Each has a distinct idea of charisma, while their shared daily life produces the project’s eccentric comedy. The official introduction presents their efforts to build their charisma as an ongoing, often chaotic process.

The premise makes the shared house the organizing point of the story. For someone trying the franchise for the first time, the teaser offers a more direct introduction to the ensemble’s interactions than a schedule announcement alone.

Official teaser and production team

The November 1 production update unveiled a teaser containing footage from the anime. It also confirmed additional cast members Shinnosuke Tachibana and Tomohiro Ono.

Joji Furuta directs the anime, Shu Matsubara handles series composition and scripts, and PIERROT FILMS produces the animation. Dazed Co., Ltd. and Matsubara are credited with the original work, with original character concepts by Ebimo.

Watch the official teaser on YouTube.

What remains unconfirmed?

The reviewed notices do not provide the short segments’ exact running time or total count. They also do not confirm an international streaming service, an English dub, or a full episode count for the January TV anime.

The practical takeaway is to follow two confirmed schedules: the short-anime segments beginning November 4 within Yoru no Brunch, and the TV anime beginning January 7. Further viewing details should be added when the production or an authorized distributor announces them.
