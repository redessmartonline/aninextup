MURCIÉLAGO Anime: January 2027, Cast & Where to Watch

When does the MURCIÉLAGO anime premiere?

The MURCIÉLAGO TV anime is scheduled to begin broadcasting and streaming in Japan in January 2027. The adaptation was announced in July 2026, then narrowed to January during the Anime NYC announcement in August. The official site still gives a month, not a day, station time or episode count. “January 2027” is the confirmed window, not an exact premiere date.

That distinction matters on seasonal calendars: January places the series in winter 2027 but does not say whether episode one arrives in the first week or later. The production has not published a weekly Japanese schedule, so exact station times remain unknown.

Where can you watch MURCIÉLAGO?

HIDIVE announced an exclusive simulcast in the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, Ireland, Australia and New Zealand as part of its 2027 line-up. That territory list comes from AMC Global Media’s July 6 announcement; it is not a worldwide availability promise. Viewers elsewhere should check local catalogs closer to launch.

Japan is separate: the official anime site confirms January broadcast and streaming, but has not named a Japanese platform or network. For people asking about Crunchyroll, the official international announcement names HIDIVE in those six territories and does not announce Crunchyroll. No English dub date or subtitle language list has been published.

What is the story about?

The official introduction describes a “fight evil with evil” premise. Kuroko Koumori, a former death-row inmate, is selected under extraordinary legal measures to work as a state-sanctioned executioner. Her partner and watchdog, Hinako Tozakura, has exceptional driving skills and helps pursue violent offenders. Kuroko’s character profile says she was responsible for 715 deaths before her appointment, underscoring how deliberately extreme the story’s justice system is.

The teaser announcement says the pair receives repeated requests to eliminate criminals and races through the fictional town of Rururuke. The production has not said which manga cases the first season adapts, named a season-long antagonist or published an episode synopsis. Viewers should not assume a particular arc or volume will be covered.

Who is in the MURCIÉLAGO cast?

The official site lists three principal characters and Japanese voice actors. Hitomi Ueda plays Kuroko, the dangerous state-appointed executioner. Minori Suzuki voices Hinako, Kuroko’s monitor and partner, whose “brain navigation” ability and driving make her an active part of the cases. Sumire Uesaka plays Chiyo Yanaoka, Kuroko’s girlfriend and the daughter of the Yanaoka crime-family leader. Chiyo’s relationship with Kuroko adds personal friction alongside the investigations.

The October 21 official cast announcement released character designs and comments from the three actors. Its teaser PV is the first video to introduce their voices together. This is the announced main cast, not a complete cast list; supporting roles remain unconfirmed.

Which studio and staff are producing the anime?

The official staff list credits SATELIGHT and Staple Entertainment with animation production. Takashi Naoya is chief director and series composer; Matsuo Asami is director; Ryo Nakamura is deputy director; and Kiyoshi Tateishi handles character design. The CG directors are Hiroyuki Goto and Hiroyuki Ikeda, while music is credited to Masanori Akita and Yuichi Tsuchiya. The site has not said how the production divides 2D and CG work or how many episodes are planned.

How does the anime connect to the manga?

MURCIÉLAGO adapts Kana Yoshimura’s manga, published in Square Enix’s Young Gangan Comics line. The publisher’s Young Gangan series page describes Kuroko as a government-authorized killer sent after serious criminals and Hinako as her monitor. The anime site lists volume 29 as the newest Japanese volume, released July 24, 2026. AMC said the manga had passed 2.3 million copies in worldwide circulation when it announced the adaptation.

This is an adaptation of an established long-running manga, not a short original project made for television. That history does not reveal where season one will stop: the anime site has not confirmed an episode total, final chapter or volume, or whether more seasons are planned.

Is MURCIÉLAGO suitable for every viewer?

The official cast comments describe quick shifts between comedy and serious material and warn viewers to expect graphic violence and sexual jokes. That is useful context because the teaser’s action alone does not convey every aspect of the tone. The official material does not provide a formal age rating; viewers who need one should wait for a local distributor or ratings body rather than treating this note as a classification.

Confirmed so far: the January 2027 window, HIDIVE’s six exclusive simulcast territories, three principal voice actors and the main production staff. The exact premiere day, Japanese broadcaster and platform, episode count, additional cast, rating and season coverage remain unannounced. We will update this guide when the production publishes those details.
