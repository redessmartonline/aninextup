MURCIÉLAGO Anime Sets January 2027 Premiere; Cast Announced

The TV adaptation of Kana Yoshimura’s manga is scheduled to begin in January 2027. The official site now lists three voice cast members, while HIDIVE has announced exclusive streaming in the U.S., Canada, the U.K., Ireland, Australia and New Zealand.

When does the MURCIÉLAGO anime premiere?

MURCIÉLAGO is scheduled to begin airing and streaming in January 2027. The production announced the month during HIDIVE’s panel at Anime NYC 2026. The official anime website confirms a January broadcast and streaming start, but does not yet list an exact day or time.

AMC Global Media’s announcement lists the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, Ireland, Australia and New Zealand for HIDIVE’s exclusive simulcast. Viewers elsewhere should check their local catalog closer to release, since the announcement does not confirm availability in every country.

What is MURCIÉLAGO about?

The story centers on Kuroko Koumori, a former death-row inmate selected to serve as a state-sanctioned executioner under extraordinary legal measures. Her partner and watchdog, Hinako Tozakura, is a driver with exceptional skills. Together, they confront violent crimes and the people behind them. The official description presents the show as a dark buddy-action series built around the duo.

Cast, studio and staff

The official site now lists Hitomi Ueda as Kuroko Koumori, Minori Suzuki as Hinako Tozakura and Sumire Uesaka as Chiyo Yanaoka. These are the three characters named in the current cast announcement. SATELIGHT and Staple Entertainment are producing the animation. Takashi Naoya is chief director and handles series composition, while Matsuo Asami directs. Kiyoshi Tateishi is credited with character design. The official site lists music by Masanori Akita and Yuichi Tsuchiya.

What the January announcement tells viewers

The official website sets January 2027 as the start of the television broadcast and streaming, but gives no exact day or episode time. That distinction matters for a seasonal calendar: “January” is a release window, not a weekly slot. AniNextUp will add a precise schedule only after the production or distributor publishes one. The three cast names currently highlighted on the official site are Hitomi Ueda as Kuroko, Minori Suzuki as Hinako and Sumire Uesaka as Chiyo; additional roles should not be inferred from promotional images.

The adaptation’s central pairing also explains why the announcement describes it as a dark buddy-action story. Kuroko’s legal status and role as an executioner put her inside the justice system in an unusual way, while Hinako’s driving skills make her an active partner in the cases rather than a bystander. The official synopsis describes their work against violent offenders; it does not identify a season-long antagonist or explain how each case will be adapted.

Streaming details are regional

HIDIVE’s announcement identifies the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, Ireland, Australia and New Zealand for its exclusive simulcast. That is a named territory list, not a guarantee that the service carries the series in every country where it operates. Viewers in Latin America, continental Europe and other unlisted regions should check local platforms closer to the premiere instead of assuming that the Japanese streaming announcement applies worldwide.

The original manga has a long-running story and a more mature tone than a typical school comedy. Readers who are new to the title may want to check the publisher’s description and the anime’s own trailer before deciding whether its violent material suits them. The available announcement does not provide a formal international age rating. AniNextUp will report one only when a local distributor or ratings body publishes it.
