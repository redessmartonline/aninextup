I Got a Cheat Skill Season 2: January 2027 Release, Cast and Trailer

Official Season 2 news and schedule updates

I Got a Cheat Skill in Another World and Became Unrivaled in the Real World, Too is returning with a second TV season in January 2027. The official anime site and publisher KADOKAWA confirm the month, and the production posted its first Season 2 promotional video and key visual on October 15, 2026. The exact premiere day, Japanese channel, episode count and international streaming services have not been announced.

When does I Got a Cheat Skill Season 2 come out?

The answer available now is January 2027. The official site describes the new season as beginning broadcast and streaming that month, while KADOKAWA’s listing gives the same month-long window. Neither source gives a calendar day or a first-episode time. Treat January as a confirmed release month, not as confirmation of a January 1 premiere or a weekly schedule.

The official wording refers to Japanese release information. It does not identify a service for viewers in the United States, Latin America, Europe or other regions. A Japanese streaming notice also does not establish an international simulcast. Until a distributor publishes its own regional listing, the safest answer to “where can I watch Season 2?” is that international availability is still pending.

What did the October 2026 announcement confirm?

On October 15, the anime’s official news page published three separate updates: the first Season 2 PV, the first Season 2 key visual and two new character-and-cast announcements. The material confirms active promotion and named creative credits, but it is not a complete broadcast schedule. The key visual is official artwork from the production committee and appears above; the PV is linked from the production’s own movie page and YouTube channel.

The teaser is useful for seeing the new season’s tone and character designs, but viewers should not treat short promotional footage as a full story outline. The official site has not announced the episode total, opening or ending songs, weekly broadcast day, or a release calendar for territories outside Japan. We will update this guide when the production or a licensed distributor confirms those details.

What story will Season 2 cover?

The official Season 2 introduction gives a clearer premise than speculation based on the teaser. After welcoming the legendary Creation Dragon Ouma and Kuro—the embodiment of “Evil” hidden inside Yuti—into his group and defeating the Fist Saint, Yuya trains to make the newly acquired power of Evil his own. At the same time, the forces of Evil continue to grow. In the otherworldly Kingdom of Regar, a formal match is arranged between Yuya and the strongest Holy Sword Saint.

That setup signals two connected pressures: Yuya must understand a dangerous power, while a public contest in Regar puts him against a renowned opponent. The series’ central appeal remains the link between an ordinary modern setting and a fantasy world, but the official synopsis does not explain the full outcome of the duel or identify which light-novel volumes Season 2 adapts. Those details should remain open until the anime staff publishes a longer synopsis.

Who joins the Season 2 cast?

The October 15 cast release adds Ikumi Hasegawa as Iris Noublade and Sora Amamiya as Mai Kagurazaka. In her official comment, Hasegawa describes Iris as strong, dignified and cool, and says viewers should watch how her relationship with Yuya develops. Amamiya describes Mai as a mysterious shrine maiden who can be prickly yet considerate, with a shy side. The production has not yet detailed how either character enters the story beyond those comments and the official character listing.

The returning principal cast listed on the Season 2 page includes Yoshitsugu Matsuoka as Yuya Tenjo, Akari Kito as Kaori Hojo, Kaori Maeda as Lexia von Arselia, Ai Kakuma as Luna, Rina Hidaka as Yuti and Marina Inoue as Kuro. This is a useful confirmed lineup, though it should not be read as a complete cast list for every character in the new arc.

Who is making the new season?

The official staff page credits Shin Itagaki as director and Hiromi Kimura for character design. Millepensee is the animation studio, with Akihiro Tachibana and SHIKI credited for music and UNLIMITED PRODUCE by TMS for planning and production. The source novels are written by Miku and illustrated by Rein Kuwashima, under KADOKAWA’s Fantasia Bunko imprint. These credits give readers a concrete way to distinguish confirmed production information from fan guesses about staff changes.

What is still unconfirmed?
