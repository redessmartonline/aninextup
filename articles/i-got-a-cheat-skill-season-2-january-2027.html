I Got a Cheat Skill in Another World Season 2 Sets January 2027 Premiere, Reveals Trailer and Cast

I Got a Cheat Skill in Another World Season 2 starts in January 2027. See the official trailer news, new cast, returning staff and what remains unconfirmed.

By AniNextUp Editorial Team · October 15, 2026 · Editorial policy

I Got a Cheat Skill in Another World and Became Unrivaled in the Real World, Too will return for Season 2 in January 2027, according to the anime's official website. The October 15 announcement also unveiled the first promotional video, a new key visual and two additional cast members. The exact premiere day and international streaming platforms have not yet been announced.

When does I Got a Cheat Skill Season 2 release?

The confirmed Japanese broadcast and streaming window is January 2027. This is a month-long release window, not a specific episode premiere date. The official anime website and publisher KADOKAWA both list January 2027. Viewers should wait for an official platform and regional schedule before assuming where the sequel will stream outside Japan.

Who are the newly announced voice actors?

The October 15 production update names Ikumi Hasegawa as Iris Noublade and Sora Amamiya as Mai Kagurazaka. These additions accompany the first Season 2 PV and key visual. They are casting confirmations, not a complete episode schedule: the official site still identifies January 2027 as the broadcast and streaming window without an exact day or international platform list. Check the official production news.

Official Season 2 trailer and key visual

The production unveiled its first Season 2 promotional video (PV 1) on October 15 alongside the first key visual. You can access both directly through the official Iseleve anime website. Watch the official first promotional video below, or open the original video on YouTube if embedding is unavailable.

New characters and voice actors

Two newcomers are now confirmed. Ikumi Hasegawa voices Iris Noublade, a powerful swordswoman known as the Sword Saint. Sora Amamiya voices Mai Kagurazaka, a mysterious shrine maiden from the real world who was summoned to another world and possesses a power to purify evil. The characters expand the story's connection between Yuya's two worlds.

The official Season 2 staff and cast page also lists returning leads Yoshitsugu Matsuoka as Yuya Tenjo, Akari Kito as Kaori Hojo and Kaori Maeda as Lexia von Arselia.

Who is producing Season 2?

Shin Itagaki directs the new season, with Hiromi Kimura handling character design and Millepensee returning for animation production. The series adapts the light novels written by Miku and illustrated by Rein Kuwashima.

What happens next in Yuya's story?

Yuya Tenjo's adventure continues across the real world and a fantasy world. After confronting the Fist Saint, Yuya faces the challenge of mastering new power while the threat of evil continues to spread. The newly introduced Iris and Mai are positioned to play important roles in that conflict, although the production has not yet published a complete episode-by-episode story outline.

What is still unconfirmed?

As of October 15, 2026, the official announcements do not provide a specific January premiere day, episode count or confirmed international simulcast schedule. We will update this guide when those details are released.
