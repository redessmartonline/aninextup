Anime Crusaders X Infinite Tier List

Falcon (Wings of Darkness) is a priority for Hill or Hybrid damage, Higuru (Judgement) is a leading Ground carry, and Falcon (Wings of Light) is a high-priority support. Those are three separate roster decisions. If you can only work toward one unit, pick the one that solves the problem you’re already seeing in Infinite.

This editorial shortlist compares published rankings after the Anniversary Update. S tier identifies the picks we would prioritize within each role. A tier holds strong alternatives, especially when you already own the evolved unit or need a particular kind of coverage. Units within a cell are listed as options, without a strict numerical order. AniNextUp has not independently tested damage, cooldowns or clear times in-game.

Best Hill and Hybrid damage units

Start with Hill or Hybrid coverage if your Ground-heavy roster leaves particular waves exposed. Falcon (Wings of Darkness) is a recurring Infinite recommendation in the sources below. Goju vs Sukuno (Heian) also appears near the top of post-update roundups, while Akuzo vs Renkuro gives returning players another high-end option. If you already have one of them in its relevant form, build around it before committing resources to another unit with the same job.

Vegeto and Barusu (From Zero) appear in published meta discussions and are worth keeping in rotation. The A label here means we would generally address an empty role before replacing an established carry with one of these options. Ayzen (Perfect) is also worth a look when you need more than damage from the slot. Before evolving anything, make sure the variant name in your inventory matches the one in the table. A base unit and a named evolution can be very different investments.

Best Ground damage units

Higuru (Judgement) is our first Ground recommendation for Infinite. Undead Knight (King) and Toju (Sorcerer Killer) are two alternatives that also show up in the published Infinite meta. If one of those three is already evolved and fits your team, the next upgrade may give you more value there than a long grind to replace it. Compare how the unit performs in the waves where your current setup breaks down.

Esdead (Ice Empress) can be useful if your plan needs control alongside damage. Dunté (Infernal Wrath) is another name to check when choosing a Ground carry, while Lao (Heart) is a practical alternative if you already have the unit built. Ground rankings also depend on how much coverage your Hill or Hybrid carry supplies. A Ground unit can do its job well and still leave the team exposed elsewhere on the map.

Best support and farm choices

Falcon (Wings of Light) and Falcon (Wings of Darkness) are distinct entries. The Light form appears in the support shortlist, while the Darkness form appears among the Hill or Hybrid damage picks. If you’re following a guide that simply says ‘Falcon,’ verify the form before spending evolution materials. Mr. Champion and Useless Goddess are other support options to consider when your main damage units are already in good shape.

AI (Idol), Hesia (Sacred Flame), and Puci (Made in Heaven) give you additional support choices if the top options are unavailable or your team needs a different kind of utility. If the obstacle is that you cannot afford upgrades early enough, look separately at farm picks such as Speedcart or Loid. A farm slot makes sense when you can survive while it pays off. In a shorter stage or a difficult opening, another immediate damage option can be the better use of the slot.

Anniversary Update units to watch

The November 15 Anniversary Update introduced Eskanoor (The One), reported as the game’s first Hero unit. It also added units including Einz (Sorcerer King), Mely (Assault), Flame (Burning Fist), and Bun (Immortal). Einz already appears in some Hill or Hybrid recommendations and Bun appears in some Ground lists. The launch-week sources disagree on the exact order, so the new additions stay on this watchlist while their roles become clearer.

That distinction matters when you’re weighing a rare new drop against a unit you can build today. If the new unit covers a role you do not have, it may deserve a look even before rankings settle. If it would replace a fully built carry, compare results on the same stage before paying for the switch. Keep the evolved form, traits, and team composition as similar as you can when making that comparison.

How to build a team for Infinite

  1. Choose one Ground carry and one Hill or Hybrid carry. Having both gives you a better starting point for maps where one placement type leaves gaps in your coverage.
  2. Add a support your carries can actually use. If most of your damage comes from one invested carry, start by improving the waves that unit can already reach before chasing another late-game DPS pick.
  3. Match the rest of the roster to your first failure point. An early loss points you toward an affordable placement or a different opening. A late loss is a reason to examine coverage, damage upgrades or control.
  4. Change one slot at a time. Replay the same map and note the first wave that causes trouble. Changing several units at once makes it harder to tell which swap helped.

This tier list is focused on Infinite. For Story or other shorter stages, a unit that stabilizes the first waves with modest spending can be more useful than a late-game carry that needs several upgrades. Treat the table as a shopping list for specific jobs, and use the stage you are trying to clear as the final test.

Frequently asked questions

Who is the best unit in Anime Crusaders X?

For Infinite, start with Falcon (Wings of Darkness) for Hill or Hybrid damage, Higuru (Judgement) for Ground damage, or Falcon (Wings of Light) for support. Which one should come first depends on the role that is missing from your roster.

Is Eskanoor (The One) S tier?

Some launch-week rankings already place him among the strongest choices. This guide keeps him on the Anniversary watchlist because his early rankings are less settled than those of the established picks here.

What if I don’t have any S-tier picks?

Start with the A-tier unit you own that covers the missing placement or utility. Compare results with your current squad before chasing a new summon. In practical terms, a usable evolved alternative can improve your team sooner than a theoretical upgrade you have yet to unlock.
