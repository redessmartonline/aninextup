Haru to Gen Anime Premieres January 4, 2027: Cast and Teaser

Haru to Gen starts January 4, 2027 on tvk in Japan. See the official teaser, main voice cast and the story of a grandfather raising his granddaughter.

When does Haru to Gen premiere?

Haru to Gen premieres in Japan on Monday, January 4, 2027. AnimationID announced the first broadcast date on October 21, alongside the opening of the anime's official website, a teaser and its main cast.

The Japanese title is Haru to Gen: 70-sai, Hajimete no Kosodate, a story about beginning childcare at 70. Unlike an announcement that gives only a season or month, this update provides a specific day and a local television slot.

Where can you watch it?

The confirmed broadcaster is tvk / Television Kanagawa, with episodes scheduled on Mondays at 21:55 Japan Standard Time. That is a Japanese television schedule. It should not be treated as an international streaming launch time.

The official site lists its streaming section as coming soon. No international platform or English dub release has been confirmed in the sources checked for this report. Viewers outside Japan should therefore wait for a distribution announcement before assuming the series will appear in their local catalog.

What is the story?

Gen is a rigid, work-focused man who has lived alone since his marriage ended. At 70, he takes responsibility for his three-year-old granddaughter Haru following the death of his daughter Sakura. Raising a child forces him to confront a family role he neglected earlier in life.

The premise follows the daily adjustments of two people with very different needs: an older man learning how to care for someone and a small child bringing energy into his isolated routine. The official introduction presents their relationship as a warm family story shaped by loss and a new beginning.

Confirmed Japanese voice cast

  • Bin Shimada as Gen.
  • Hiromi Igarashi as Haru.
  • Mayuko Kazama as Sakura.

AnimationID says additional cast information will follow. The three names above are the confirmed main roles in the October 21 announcement; they are not an English dub cast.

Who is making Haru to Gen?

The adaptation is based on Yuka Oshima's comic and is directed by Kodai Sato. The official credits list music by bermei.inazawa and animation production cooperation by Studio Nanahoshi, within the AnimationID project. This wording preserves the production roles rather than assigning every part of the adaptation to a single studio.
