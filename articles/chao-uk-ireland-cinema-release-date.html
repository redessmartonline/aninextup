ChaO UK & Ireland Cinema Release Date, Trailer & Tickets

When is ChaO released in UK and Irish cinemas?

ChaO opens in cinemas across the UK and Ireland from Friday 4 December 2026. Anime Limited gave the date in its 9 November release announcement. The announced presentation is Japanese audio with English subtitles. That is the date to look for when you check a cinema’s programme, though individual venues set their own dates and times.

This is the UK and Ireland theatrical release of Yasuhiro Aoki’s film. Some listings label it ChaO (2025) because that is its production and original release year. Japan’s cinema release was 15 August 2025, according to the Japanese Film Database. The year in parentheses identifies the film, while 4 December 2026 tells you when the Anime Limited rollout begins. You may need both details if your cinema app has trouble finding the title.

Where can you buy ChaO cinema tickets?

Start with Anime Limited’s official ChaO ticket updates page. On 9 November, the address printed on the poster led to an email sign-up for ticket news. We could verify the release date and screening format, while a bookable UK or Irish performance remained unverified. A film calendar entry is a useful reminder to check back, while a cinema’s own page with a venue, date, time and booking button is where you can confirm a seat.

Start by searching ChaO and the name of your town or nearest city. If the search turns up unrelated results, use ChaO Studio 4°C or ChaO Yasuhiro Aoki. Then open the cinema’s December programme and check 4 December and the days after it. The release announcement says “from” 4 December. Screening days can differ by venue. An independent cinema may list films by programme or season, so its listings page can be easier to search than its homepage.

The Independent Cinema Office’s UK and Ireland cinema directory is another starting point if you want to check venues beyond the chains you already use. It helps you find cinema websites in your area. Open the venue’s own programme to check whether it is actually showing the film. If you are only interested in one city, the official ticket updates page and that cinema’s listings are the two places to return to as December approaches.

Before paying, check the venue address, the performance date and the audio label. Japanese with English subtitles is the version Anime Limited announced. If the booking site abbreviates that to “subtitled” or leaves the language out, confirm the format with the venue. If you need audio description or an accessible screening, use the venue’s session details. Those arrangements depend on the performance, and the release announcement gives no accessibility schedule.

If you are choosing between two cities, compare the actual screening times before arranging transport. Watch the official ChaO UK trailer

Anime Limited’s UK trailer is the clip associated with the December release announcement. It gives you a sense of the film’s comic timing and the size of the world around the couple. Stephan works at a shipbuilding company, and his life changes abruptly when Chao proposes. The trailer lets that interruption play as both a romantic premise and a collision between two societies with very different expectations.

What is ChaO about?

Stephan is a shipbuilding employee in a future where humans and mermaids live alongside one another uneasily. He has ideas for protecting the sea, though his day job gives him little influence. Chao is a princess from the mermaid kingdom, and her decision to propose to him brings him into a relationship he had no time to prepare for. Once the two start living together, Stephan has to make sense of the person in front of him and of what their marriage means to everyone watching.

That setup makes room for a comedy about everyday adjustment alongside the bigger questions about how the two communities share their world. Stephan and Chao experience the same situation from very different positions. He is accustomed to being overlooked at work. She can make a decision that changes his life in an instant, but they still have to learn how to talk to each other. The film is an original feature, so viewers can go straight into it without reading a manga or catching up on a television series.

Who made ChaO, and how long is the film?

Yasuhiro Aoki directed ChaO at Studio 4°C. Annecy’s official selection credits Eiko Tanaka for production, Hirokazu Kojima for animation and graphic work, and Takatsugu Muramatsu for music. Ouji Suzuka voices Stephan and Anna Yamada voices Chao in Japanese. The voices are the ones to listen for in the announced subtitled UK and Irish release.

Annecy lists the feature at about 90 minutes and records that it won the Jury Award in the feature film competition in 2025. Its technical listing identifies drawing on paper alongside 2D and 3D computer techniques. That combination is apparent in the shifts between the characters’ expressive movement and the larger city and shipbuilding environments. Use the cinema’s own start and finish information if you need to plan travel around a particular screening.

Will ChaO stream in the UK or Ireland?

Anime Limited’s 9 November announcement is for cinemas. It gives no UK or Irish streaming date or platform. GKIDS has a separate page for the film, and the Japanese site carries its own release information. If a search result gives you a different date or a home viewing option, check the country first. A page for another market gives you context on the film, while the local distributor determines what is available where you live.

For the same reason, use the December date for the theatrical rollout and treat any future UK or Irish digital release as a separate announcement. A YouTube trailer is available now. A local streaming listing would need to identify the full film and the territory it serves.

What should you check before booking?

Does ChaO have an English dub in UK cinemas? The announced cinema presentation uses Japanese audio and English subtitles. The release notice did not announce a dubbed presentation for the UK or Ireland.

Is 4 December the only screening day? It is the opening date for the rollout. Your nearest cinema may choose different days, or may add sessions after publishing its first programme. Check the dated listing for the venue where you intend to book.

Which title should I use when searching? Use ChaO with Studio 4°C or Yasuhiro Aoki. If a cinema shows the year 2025 and names Aoki as director, it is identifying the original Japanese film rather than giving the date of the UK and Irish run.
