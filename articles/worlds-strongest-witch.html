When did The World's Strongest Witch begin?

The Japanese premiere was October 7, 2026. YTE's official programme listing gives TOKYO MX's regular slot as Wednesdays at 22:00 JST. The series has therefore already reached its launch window; October 7 is the opening date, rather than a new date for every episode.

For Japanese streaming, YTE lists ABEMA and d Anime Store from the same evening at 22:00 JST. These are domestic distribution options. They should not be confused with an international service's upload schedule or taken as evidence that a Japanese subscription works in another country.

How do the late-night Japanese broadcast times work?

The listings use television-day notation: Wednesday 24:00 on BS11 means midnight at the start of Thursday, while Wednesday 26:05 on TV Aichi means Thursday at 02:05. Similarly, MBS's Saturday 26:38 slot falls early Sunday by the calendar. This explains why two programme guides can show different weekdays for the same listed broadcast.

Keep the station name attached to the time when setting a reminder. A later broadcast on another channel is not necessarily a delayed international episode, and the AT-X repeat slots are reruns rather than additional new episodes. Japan uses JST, UTC+09:00; your own calendar date can differ when converting an overnight slot. Related reading: Hello, I am a Witch and my Crush Wants me to Make a Love Potion! What is Lorna's Internet skill?

Lorna Hermit is expelled after receiving a skill others consider worthless. Its actual function gives her access to a strategy site for her own fantasy world. The important distinction is that she can look up useful knowledge: the premise is not simply that she has a stronger attack than everyone else.

That creates an accessible hook for viewers familiar with game guides. Information can expose a shortcut that brute force misses. Our reading of the setup is that Lorna's freedom and ingenuity matter alongside her power; it is an interpretation of the premise, not a claim about scenes we have reviewed or the outcome of later adventures.

Is the source a novel or a manga?

The original is a light novel by Mochimaru Sakaki, illustrated by riritto, published under Square Enix's SQEX Novel label. A manga adaptation also exists. The publisher's series page separates the novel volumes from its manga news, which helps readers avoid mixing their numbering. Square Enix marks novel volume six as the final volume, released October 7, 2026. That book date coincides with the anime's launch, but the two events do not establish how much material the television adaptation covers. A completed novel series does not automatically mean a complete anime adaptation.

If you want to read ahead, choose a format first and start with its first volume. Moving from an anime episode straight to a similarly numbered book or manga chapter risks skipping material. The sources cited here do not supply an episode-to-chapter conversion, so this guide does not invent one.

Who voices Lorna, and who makes the anime?

The production information reproduced by HoneyWorks credits Azusa Tachibana as Lorna, Fairouz Ai as Erimina, Honoka Inoue as Elna, Kana Hanazawa as Aries, Maria Naganawa as Lulu and Yuto Uemura as Reinhart. These are Japanese voice credits, not an English dub cast. Related reading: Chitose Is in the Ramune Bottle Cour 2 Release Date & Time.

Joji Furuta directs, Shoji Yonemura handles series composition and Yuki Morimoto designs the characters. Bridge and AISLE produce the animation. The opening theme is HoneyWorks feat. HaKoniwalily's “Eikyoryoku ∞ Idol,” with an October 7 digital release. A theme-song release is useful for listeners searching for the opening, but it does not establish an album or complete soundtrack schedule.
