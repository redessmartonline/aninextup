The World's Strongest Witch Release Date, Time & Where to Watch

When does The World's Strongest Witch premiere?

The World's Strongest Witch premieres October 7, 2026. The official Japanese site lists TOKYO MX at 10:00 p.m. JST and BS11 at midnight, with TV Aichi, AT-X and MBS joining on their respective schedules.

Where to watch

ABEMA and d Anime Store begin advance streaming in Japan on October 7 at 10:00 p.m. JST. Crunchyroll also streams the series internationally in a wide group of territories, including North America, Latin America, Europe, Africa, Oceania, the Middle East and parts of Asia.

What is The World's Strongest Witch about?

Lorna Hermit is cast aside after receiving an ability that appears useless, but her SSS-rank “Internet” skill gives her access to knowledge that changes what she can accomplish. Freed from her former family's plans, she sets out into the wider world and encounters quests that turn that unusual advantage into real power.

Who is making the anime?

The official production credits list Joji Furuta as director, Shoji Yonemura for series composition and Yuki Morimoto for character design. Animation production is handled by Bridge and AISLE.
