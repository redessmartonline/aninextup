Le Collège Noir Season 2 Trailer Revealed Ahead of October 31 Premiere

Le Collège Noir Season 2 premieres on October 31, 2026. Watch the official trailer and find confirmed streaming regions, creators and story details.

Season 2's final trailer arrives before Halloween

Le Collège Noir returns for its second season on October 31, 2026, and Toei Animation has shared a final trailer ahead of the premiere. The French supernatural animated series is created by Ulysse Malassagne and produced by Studio La Cachette in co-production with Toei Animation. The Halloween launch date was announced before the October 13 trailer update, so the new video—not the date alone—is the central news in this report.

Watch the official Le Collège Noir Season 2 trailer

Toei Animation linked the final trailer in its October 13 announcement. ADN also published a season two trailer that day. These are official promotional materials; the exact availability of the videos may vary by region.

Watch the trailer on YouTube if the embedded player is unavailable.

When does Le Collège Noir Season 2 come out?

The confirmed French premiere is Saturday, October 31, 2026. ADN lists the season for that date, and Toei Animation's announcement independently confirms it. Neither of these October 13 announcements establishes a precise time of day, so no release hour should be assumed.

Where can viewers stream Season 2?

ADN is the confirmed French streaming home. Toei Animation also identifies RTS for Switzerland and says Peppermint Anime has licensed the show for German-speaking territories, including Germany, Austria, Liechtenstein, Luxembourg, German-speaking Switzerland and South Tyrol in Italy. A license announcement does not by itself confirm a streaming platform or simultaneous October 31 launch in each territory. An English-language streaming release has not been confirmed in these announcements.

What is Le Collège Noir about?

Based on Malassagne's French comic, the story follows five young students at an isolated boarding school in the Cantal mountains. A supernatural encounter involving a missing classmate draws them into mysteries rooted in French folklore, including frightening creatures and an ancient curse. Season two continues the show's blend of mystery, fantasy and eerie adventure; specific new plot turns should not be treated as confirmed without further official details.

Who is making Season 2?

Ulysse Malassagne created the property and directs the animated adaptation. Studio La Cachette produces the animation, with Toei Animation involved as a co-production partner. ADN's October 13 announcement lists French as the original audio language and describes the first season as six 15-minute episodes; that first-season count should not be mistaken for a confirmed season-two episode count.

What remains unannounced?

The October 13 sources do not establish a global English-language streaming platform, a complete season-two episode count or a universal launch hour. AniNextUp will update this report if the rights holders publish those details.
