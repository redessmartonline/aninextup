Le Collège Noir Season 2 Trailer Revealed Ahead of October 31 Premiere

Le Collège Noir Season 2 premieres on October 31, 2026. Watch the official trailer and find confirmed streaming regions, creators and story details.

Season 2's final trailer arrives before Halloween

Le Collège Noir returns for its second season on October 31, 2026, and Toei Animation has shared a final trailer ahead of the premiere. The French supernatural animated series is created by Ulysse Malassagne and produced by Studio La Cachette in co-production with Toei Animation. The Halloween launch date was announced before the October 13 trailer update, so the new video—not the date alone—is the central news in this report.

Does the final trailer announce a new release date?

No. The October 13 trailer is a promotional update ahead of the already announced October 31, 2026 Season 2 launch. It provides a new preview of the supernatural story, not a separate premiere or an additional season announcement. Viewers should consult the distributor's regional listing for the local streaming time and language options rather than infer them from the trailer's publication date. The player below uses the final trailer published by Toei Animation Europe and linked in its October 13 announcement. The preview artwork is the official season-two visual from ADN. Video availability may vary by region.

When does Le Collège Noir Season 2 come out?

The confirmed French premiere is Saturday, October 31, 2026. ADN lists the season for that date, and Toei Animation's announcement independently confirms it. Neither of these October 13 announcements establishes a precise time of day, so no release hour should be assumed.

Where can viewers stream Season 2?

ADN is the confirmed French streaming home. Toei Animation also identifies RTS for Switzerland and says Peppermint Anime has licensed the show for German-speaking territories, including Germany, Austria, Liechtenstein, Luxembourg, German-speaking Switzerland and South Tyrol in Italy. A license announcement does not by itself confirm a streaming platform or simultaneous October 31 launch in each territory. An English-language streaming release has not been confirmed in these announcements.

What is Le Collège Noir about?

Based on Malassagne's French comic, the story follows five young students at an isolated boarding school in the Cantal mountains. A supernatural encounter involving a missing classmate draws them into mysteries rooted in French folklore, including frightening creatures and an ancient curse. Season two continues the show's blend of mystery, fantasy and eerie adventure; specific new plot turns should not be treated as confirmed without further official details.

Who is making Season 2?

Ulysse Malassagne created the property and directs the animated adaptation. Studio La Cachette produces the animation, with Toei Animation involved as a co-production partner. ADN lists French as the original audio language. Toei Animation confirms six episodes per season, and France Télévisions lists Season 2 as six 15-minute episodes.

What remains unannounced?

The October 13 sources do not establish a global English-language streaming platform, an international release timetable or a universal launch hour. AniNextUp will update this report if the rights holders publish those details.

Official sources

Toei Animation — October 13 season-two final trailer and distribution announcement; ADN — October 13 trailer and French premiere details; Toei Animation — earlier renewal announcement; France Télévisions — Season 2 format; Toei Animation — six episodes per season.

What should viewers know before Season 2?

The first season introduces the students and their isolated school through a supernatural mystery. ADN's October announcement says viewers in its market can revisit that season while waiting for the new episodes. Its listing describes the original season as six episodes of approximately fifteen minutes, with French audio.

For a returning viewer, that provides a short way to revisit the setting and the group before the new story. The announcement does not establish that every country will have the same catalog, subtitles or release schedule. Check the local service for the version available to your account.

How long is the new season?

Toei Animation's catalog lists twelve episodes across the two seasons, with six per season. France Télévisions' production listing describes the second season as six fifteen-minute episodes. The format does not establish individual release dates.

ADN announces October 31 as the start of Season 2 in France, but its cited announcement does not specify a precise release time or confirm that all six episodes will arrive simultaneously. Viewers who want to plan a marathon should check the platform's episode listing when it becomes available.

Why the Cantal setting matters

The series draws on a French comic by Ulysse Malassagne and places its young characters in a remote boarding school in Cantal. ADN's synopsis describes a group staying there during the autumn school break, when an encounter connected to a missing classmate brings folklore and a witch's curse into their lives.

That premise gives the supernatural events a specific regional setting rather than presenting the school as an unnamed haunted building. The production is French animation made with Toei Animation as a co-production partner.

What does the regional licensing announcement mean?

Toei's October 13 release names ADN in France and RTS in Switzerland and reports a license to Peppermint Anime for specified German-speaking territories. Those details are useful leads for viewers in those markets, but the announcement does not supply a single international premiere day or an English-language service.

For the Dominican Republic and other countries outside the named territories, check a local distributor's announcement before assuming the French date or platform applies. Official local listings are also the place to confirm French audio, subtitles, age ratings and any access requirements.
