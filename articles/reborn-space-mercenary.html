Reborn as a Space Mercenary Anime: Release Date & Where to Watch

Reborn as a Space Mercenary is scheduled in AniNextUp’s Fall 2026 calendar for October 4.

WHERE TO WATCHCrunchyroll

Reborn as a Space Mercenary: I Woke Up Piloting the Strongest Starship! premieres October 4, 2026 and will stream on Crunchyroll in supported regions.

Release date

The sci-fi isekai launches Sunday, October 4. Crunchyroll has also confirmed a same-day English dub.

What is it about?

After being transported into the universe of his favorite science-fiction game, Satou Takahiro begins a new life as the mercenary Hiro, piloting a powerful starship through battles, colonies and interstellar trouble.

Where to watch

Crunchyroll lists availability worldwide except Japan, China, Korea and Mongolia.
