My Crush’s Crush Anime Release Date, Trailer & English Manga Guide

When does the My Crush’s Crush anime come out?

My Crush’s Crush is scheduled for April 2027 on Japanese television. The production announced the month on November 9, 2026 in Japan, alongside a teaser and three lead cast members. That adds a specific season and month to the adaptation first announced in August 2026. The exact premiere day, airtime and episode count were still open when this guide was published.

The original title is ぼくの好きな人が好きな人, commonly romanized as Boku no Suki na Hito ga Suki na Hito. You may see that title in trailers and Japanese listings while the English manga appears as My Crush’s Crush. Search the Japanese title if you are looking for the production’s own broadcast notices, and use the English title and volume number to find the Yen Press books. For the Japanese television window, April is the most specific reminder you can set today.

Watch the official My Crush’s Crush teaser

The first teaser runs for 49 seconds and was released by avex pictures. It introduces the three leads and gives you a chance to hear the Japanese performances. The announcement also includes a rooftop visual with Sora, Miya and Asahi sharing the same frame. Keeping all three in view is a good fit for a story in which the person you are paying attention to may be looking at someone else.

For an episode stream, wait for a service to list the series and its territories.

What is My Crush’s Crush about?

Sora Akizuki has a plan simple enough to explain in one sentence. He wants to tell Miya Fuwa, a younger student, that he likes her. She turns him down because she already has feelings for Asahi Suzukaze, the president of the Clinical Trials Club. Sora joins the club to find out what draws Miya to Asahi. The situation gets more complicated when he learns that Asahi likes someone else too.

That first rejection changes the question the story asks. Sora already knows how Miya feels about his confession, but he has very little idea what she sees in Asahi or how much he understands about either of them. The club gives him a reason to spend time with the person he initially knows only as Miya’s crush. It also means the characters have to keep seeing each other after an uncomfortable conversation, which is a promising setup for a school comedy built around timing and awkwardness.

The title makes more sense when you follow the feelings in order. Sora likes Miya, and Miya likes Asahi. Asahi has a crush of her own. That is all you need to follow the setup before watching the teaser or sampling the manga. If you prefer to discover how those feelings change through the story itself, the publisher’s Volume 1 listing is a safer place to start than a character wiki that covers later chapters.

Who voices Sora, Miya and Asahi?

  • Gakuto Kajiwara as Sora Akizuki. Sora is the student whose confession starts the story. Joining the club gives him an opportunity to see how Asahi acts outside the context of Miya’s crush.
  • Nene Hieda as Miya Fuwa. Miya is the younger student Sora likes. She has an answer for him from the beginning, and the reason she gives sends him in Asahi’s direction.
  • Haruna Mikawa as Asahi Suzukaze. Asahi runs the Clinical Trials Club. Sora wants to understand why Miya likes her, but Asahi has her own romantic interests to deal with.

Kazuomi Koga directs at project No.9. Yoriko Tomita handles series composition, Takayuki Noguchi designs the characters and Rina Tayama composes the music. The November announcement identifies those roles and the three lead performances. Look to the anime site for further cast additions as the production introduces more characters.

When does the My Crush’s Crush manga come out in English?

Yen Press lists My Crush’s Crush, Vol. 1 for February 23, 2027 in paperback and digital editions. The publisher lists the volume at 208 pages and credits Sekina Aoi as the writer and Ryo Tsuzura as the artist. The manga is serialized in Hakusensha’s Young Animal ZERO. The English edition gives readers a way to try the opening volume ahead of the announced Japanese TV window.

Use the Yen Press paperback listing or search by ISBN 9798855429411 if you want a physical copy. For an ebook, start with the digital listing and ISBN 9798855429428. The ISBN is especially helpful when a retailer shows similar titles, merges the two formats into one listing or offers an imported Japanese volume alongside the English edition. Check the language and selected format before placing an order. Local delivery dates and ebook availability depend on the retailer and country.

If you want to read before watching, start with Volume 1. It lets you meet the characters in the source material without guessing which chapter the television adaptation will reach. If you want the anime to introduce the cast and the first confession, you can save the book for after the premiere. The publisher’s listing is useful in either case because it gives the English release its own date and format, separate from the spring anime schedule.

Where will My Crush’s Crush stream?

International streaming is unannounced as of November 9, 2026. The official update establishes a Japanese television window. It does not provide an overseas streaming platform, an English subtitle schedule or an English dub. If you watch from outside Japan, you still need a regional licensing announcement before you can turn April into a local watch date.

You may see a link to the Crunchyroll Store on the Yen Press paperback page. That link is for the book. To confirm whether Crunchyroll or another service will stream the anime, look for a lineup announcement or an official series page that specifies the countries it serves. The avex pictures teaser on YouTube is also available separately from the eventual episode release.

There are two updates worth tracking if you plan to watch at launch. The anime site can add the exact Japanese broadcast day, and an overseas distributor can announce a platform, regions and language options. Those updates can arrive at different times. You can follow the wider spring schedule in the AniNextUp anime calendar and browse similar titles in our romance anime section while waiting for a local listing.

Do you need to read the manga first?

You can start with the TV adaptation. This is the first anime announced for the manga, so there is no earlier season to catch up on. The three people in the teaser are the same ones who drive the opening premise. If a spring lineup shows the Japanese title instead of My Crush’s Crush, check for Sora Akizuki and the Clinical Trials Club to make sure you have the right series.

The manga route is just as simple. Begin with the first numbered volume in the language you want. Reading the English edition before the broadcast means you can spend time with the dialogue and revisit who knows what after Sora’s confession. Watching first lets you hear how the cast plays those first awkward exchanges. Since the announced anime details do not specify the adaptation’s chapter coverage, keep reading and watching as separate checklists until there are episodes to compare.

For readers keeping a spring romance watchlist, The Dangers in My Heart Season 3 is also set for April in Japan. That title has earlier seasons to catch up on. With My Crush’s Crush, your choice is simply whether to try the February book or wait for the anime.
