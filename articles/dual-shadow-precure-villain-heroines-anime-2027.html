Dual Shadow Precure Anime: 2027 Release Date & Official Teaser

Precure’s new spin-off starts with a 21-second teaser and a 2027 TV window. Here is what Dual Shadow reveals—and what viewers still need to know before adding a premiere date to their calendars.

Dual Shadow: Precure Villain Heroines is a new Precure television spin-off scheduled for 2027. Its first official video is already available, but the announcement stops short of giving a premiere month, broadcaster or streaming service. The short teaser makes its opening move through atmosphere: flowers, darkness and a line distancing its unseen speakers from the familiar Precure name.

For viewers hearing about the project for the first time, the useful distinction is that this is a separate spin-off announcement. It is not a release date for another episode of the current Precure series. Here is what the production has revealed, what the footage actually shows and which viewing details still need confirmation.

Dual Shadow release date: what does “2027” confirm?

The confirmed window is 2027 for Japanese television. Neither the official teaser title nor its description supplies a month or day. That leaves the show outside a specific winter, spring, summer or fall lineup for now. Listings that assign it to one of those seasons need a further production announcement to support that precision. Related reading: Naruto: Shadow Rising Release Date: Kishimoto’s New Manga.

A television broadcast window also does not establish a worldwide streaming launch. Fans outside Japan can add the title to a watchlist, but should not plan around a local premiere until a broadcaster or distributor gives the territory and schedule. Our January 2027 anime guide separates projects with a January announcement from titles, such as Dual Shadow, confirmed only for the year.

Watch the official Dual Shadow teaser

The 21-second “super teaser PV” below comes from the official Precure YouTube channel. Its title explicitly announces the 2027 broadcast, and the description identifies the project as the franchise’s latest television spin-off. It also links back to Toei Animation’s dedicated project page.

The preview moves from a bright field of flowers to a single illuminated flower against darkness, with watching eyes behind it. The Japanese line can be rendered as “After all, we are not Precure.” That is a statement within the promotion, not proof that the project is unrelated to Precure: the official upload itself calls it a spin-off.

What does “Precure Villain Heroines” mean for the story?

“Villain Heroines” is part of the announced title. The production announcement describes original characters and an original story, which gives this project a different starting point from a straightforward continuation of an established team. However, it has not yet supplied a detailed synopsis or named the figures glimpsed in the teaser.

Our reading of the footage is that the contrast between the flowers and the concealed eyes is designed to make the viewer question who is speaking and what role they occupy. That is visual interpretation, not an announced plot. The preview does not establish a redemption story, a horror classification, a particular enemy organization or the return of a familiar villain.

Is Dual Shadow the next main Precure season?

The official description presents it as a spin-off. That label matters when navigating a franchise with television series, films and separate projects: a new title bearing the Precure name is not automatically a numbered continuation of the current show. Dual Shadow has its own title and project page rather than an episode listing inside the current series.

Precure has already used separate productions to revisit its characters in other circumstances. For example, the official Power of Hope: Precure Full Bloom site introduced that 2023 project through its adult characters and a separate broadcast schedule. That history provides context for the spin-off format; it does not establish that Dual Shadow shares its continuity or target audience.

Where will Dual Shadow stream?

No streaming partner is named in the official teaser description reviewed for this guide. There is therefore no confirmed Netflix, Crunchyroll, HIDIVE or Disney+ listing to recommend for the new series. A service carrying another Precure installment does not automatically acquire this one.

When distribution is announced, the important details will be the licensed territories, subtitles or dubbed versions, and the relationship between the Japanese broadcast and the platform launch. Until then, the teaser is the official video available here; it is not a playable episode. Our anime streaming hub lists confirmed platform information without treating an unannounced service as a viewing option.

Cast, staff and episodes: what is still missing?

The initial materials reviewed do not provide a named voice cast, director, series-composition credit or episode total. The official upload’s production-committee copyright is not a substitute for a complete staff list. Character names and a fuller story introduction will also be important next steps, since the teaser keeps its figures largely concealed.

Those omissions make a definitive watch order premature. There is no announced prerequisite series for Dual Shadow in the teaser description. New viewers can follow the project now, while returning fans should keep theories about earlier characters separate from confirmed casting or continuity news.
