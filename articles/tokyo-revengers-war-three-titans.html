Why this arc rewards watching the earlier story

The War of the Three Titans follows the Tenjiku conflict and continues Takemichi's relationship with Mikey and the competing factions. Knowing how those relationships changed in earlier arcs provides context that a list of newly introduced characters cannot replace. This is a continuation rather than an alternate starting point for the series.

Understanding the late-night broadcast date

Japanese broadcasters sometimes use an extended late-night clock so a program after midnight remains attached to the previous evening's schedule. This is why a Friday-night anime listing can fall on Saturday by ordinary calendar time. Streaming services publish separate release windows, so use the time shown in your local catalog.

When did War of the Three Titans begin?

Tokyo Revengers: War of the Three Titans began on Japan's late-night schedule for October 2, 2026. The official Japanese schedule labels the MBS/TBS slot as Friday late night at 25:23 JST (1:23 a.m. on Saturday in standard calendar notation). CBC, BS-TBS, AT-X and regional stations have their own broadcast schedules.

Does Friday 25:23 mean Friday afternoon?

No. The Japanese broadcast listing uses an extended late-night clock: Friday 25:23 JST means Saturday at 1:23 a.m. JST on a normal calendar. The separately listed Friday 26:00 streaming time corresponds to Saturday at 2:00 a.m. JST. Viewers should use their own platform's local listing rather than treating these Japanese schedule labels as universal release times. What is the War of the Three Titans arc?

The new arc continues Takemichi Hanagaki's time-leap story after the Tenjiku conflict and brings the struggle surrounding Mikey into a new phase. Promotional material introduces major players including Takeomi Akashi, Wakasa Imaushi, Keizo Arashi and South Terano.

Opening and ending themes

JO1 performs the opening theme “IGNITE,” while go!go!vanillas performs the ending theme “Trickster.” Both songs were announced by the anime's official site ahead of the October broadcast.

What to know before watching

War of the Three Titans is a continuation of Tokyo Revengers, not a standalone reboot. It follows the events of the Tenjiku conflict and moves Takemichi's story into the next major struggle surrounding Mikey and the new factions competing for power. Tokyo Revengers FAQ

Is War of the Three Titans a new season?

It is the next major anime arc in the continuing Tokyo Revengers story.

Do I need to watch Tenjiku first?

Yes. This arc continues the existing story and assumes familiarity with Takemichi, Mikey and the events leading out of the Tenjiku conflict.

Is it on Disney+?

Disney+ is listed for international distribution by the anime's official site, while Disney's U.S. October 2026 schedule lists Hulu. New episodes are scheduled weekly; check your regional service for release times.

How often are new episodes released?

The Japanese schedule is weekly on Friday nights, with the after-midnight airing falling on Saturday in Japan. Displayed release days and times can shift by territory and by individual schedule changes. Related reading: Black Clover Season 2 Schedule & Where to Watch.

Have individual episode times changed?

Yes. The production has published schedule exceptions, so the regular weekly slot should be treated as a baseline rather than a promise that every episode appears at the same minute. Its episode 53 notice moved the MBS and TBS airing from Friday October 16 at 25:23 to 25:33 JST. Disney+ was moved from 26:00 to 26:10 on that same extended Friday schedule.

In ordinary calendar notation, those revised times are Saturday October 17 at 1:33 a.m. and 2:10 a.m. in Japan. The notice also lists changes for several regional stations. It applies to that episode and should not be projected automatically onto later installments.

How are episodes numbered?

An official advance-screening announcement identifies the first three installments of the arc as overall episodes 51, 52 and 53. That numbering can help viewers match a service's episode list to an arc-specific guide, even if a platform displays season labels differently.
