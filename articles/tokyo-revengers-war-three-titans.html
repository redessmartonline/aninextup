Why this arc rewards watching the earlier story

The War of the Three Titans follows the Tenjiku conflict and continues Takemichi's relationship with Mikey and the competing factions. Knowing how those relationships changed in earlier arcs provides context that a list of newly introduced characters cannot replace. This is a continuation rather than an alternate starting point for the series.

Understanding the late-night broadcast date

A Japanese listing of Friday 25:23 corresponds to 1:23 a.m. on Saturday by the ordinary calendar clock. This extended-hour convention is common in late-night anime programming. The streaming listing uses its own release window, and Hulu or Disney+ availability should be checked by country rather than inferred from the Japanese television schedule.

When did War of the Three Titans begin?

Tokyo Revengers: War of the Three Titans began October 2, 2026. The official Japanese schedule labels the MBS/TBS slot as Friday late night at 25:23 JST (1:23 a.m. on Saturday in standard calendar notation). CBC, BS-TBS, AT-X and regional stations have their own broadcast schedules.

Does Friday 25:23 mean Friday afternoon?

No. The Japanese broadcast listing uses an extended late-night clock: Friday 25:23 JST means Saturday at 1:23 a.m. JST on a normal calendar. The separately listed Friday 26:00 streaming time corresponds to Saturday at 2:00 a.m. JST. Viewers should use their own platform's local listing rather than treating these Japanese schedule labels as universal release times.

Where to watch

The official anime site announces Disney+ worldwide subscription streaming (excluding mainland China), while Disney's October 2026 U.S. schedule lists the arc on Hulu. Streaming availability depends on your country. The Japanese listing uses Friday late-night 26:00, meaning Saturday at 2:00 a.m. JST in ordinary calendar notation, shortly after the earliest Japanese broadcast.

What is the War of the Three Titans arc?

The new arc continues Takemichi Hanagaki's time-leap story after the Tenjiku conflict and brings the struggle surrounding Mikey into a new phase. Promotional material introduces major players including Takeomi Akashi, Wakasa Imaushi, Keizo Arashi and South Terano.

Opening and ending themes

JO1 performs the opening theme “IGNITE,” while go!go!vanillas performs the ending theme “Trickster.” Both songs were announced by the anime's official site ahead of the October broadcast.

What to know before watching

War of the Three Titans is a continuation of Tokyo Revengers, not a standalone reboot. It follows the events of the Tenjiku conflict and moves Takemichi's story into the next major struggle surrounding Mikey and the new factions competing for power. New viewers will get much more from this arc after watching the earlier Tokyo Revengers seasons and arcs.

Tokyo Revengers FAQ

Is War of the Three Titans a new season?

It is the next major anime arc in the continuing Tokyo Revengers story.

Do I need to watch Tenjiku first?

Yes. This arc continues the existing story and assumes familiarity with Takemichi, Mikey and the events leading out of the Tenjiku conflict.

Is it on Disney+?

Disney+ is listed for international distribution by the anime's official site, while Disney's U.S. October 2026 schedule lists Hulu. New episodes are scheduled weekly; check your regional service for release times.

How often are new episodes released?

The announced schedule is weekly on Fridays, although the displayed day and time can shift by territory because of time-zone differences.
