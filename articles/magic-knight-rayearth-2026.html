Magic Knight Rayearth 2026: Official Creditless Ending, Hoshiakari & Streaming

The new Magic Knight Rayearth anime began airing October 7, 2026. On October 15, the production released its creditless ending sequence featuring Kawaguchi Yurina’s “Hoshiakari” (星灯).

October 15 update: Official creditless ending video released

The production behind Magic Knight Rayearth has published a creditless version of its ending animation, set to “Hoshiakari” (星灯) by Kawaguchi Yurina. The sequence begins with Hikaru, Umi and Fuu looking toward the waves at night, then moves through atmospheric scenes featuring Princess Emeraude and other characters. It is a new official music-video release for the anime already airing—not an announcement of another season.

What does a creditless ending release mean?

A creditless ending video shows the ending animation without the usual on-screen production credits, allowing viewers to see the sequence and its music more clearly. The October 15 release is a promotional update for the existing anime; it does not confirm an additional season, a new premiere date or a change in streaming availability. The announced ending song is Hoshiakari by Kawaguchi Yurina.

Who sings “Hoshiakari,” and when was it released?

Kawaguchi Yurina's fifth digital single launched on October 15, 2026. The anime's official music page credits Katsuhiko Sugiyama with lyrics and Gary Jeon with composition and arrangement. Watch Kawaguchi Yurina’s separate “Hoshiakari” music video

Alongside the anime’s creditless ending, Kawaguchi Yurina released a separate artist music video on October 15. Directed by MONA (VeAble), it is distinct from the animated ending sequence. Universal Music Japan describes a visual journey from isolation toward renewal, including a symbolic haircut. This artist video should not be mistaken for another anime trailer.

Additional episode-two narration credit

On the same date, the production confirmed Keiko Han as the narrator of episode two. The official casting announcement describes this as an additional episode-specific credit, not a replacement of the main cast.
