GROTESQQQUE: Japan Release Date & Netflix Preview Status

When does GROTESQQQUE release?

GROTESQQQUE opened in Japanese theaters on Friday, November 6, 2026. Aniplex announced the date alongside the official key visual on July 5, and the production website lists the same opening. This is a feature film, not a new television season: it brings together three original animated stories under one theatrical title. The three chapters share the film’s creative direction and music-led presentation, while each has its own setting, cast focus and episode director.

Is GROTESQQQUE already streaming?

The announced advance-streaming window has ended. The production’s October 19 announcement says 4649GIRL (夜露死駆★少女) was offered exclusively on Netflix Japan from October 23 through November 5, 2026, ahead of the full movie’s cinema opening. That limited window is for the second story only; it is not an early release of all three chapters, and the announcement does not promise availability outside Japan. It also does not state an exact time when the preview begins or leaves the service, so viewers should use the Netflix Japan listing for current access rather than assume a midnight changeover. The advance edition included a collaboration music video for “Shoot Shoot Shoot,” the film’s opening theme by METALVERSE.

What are the three stories in the film?

The official site presents GROTESQQQUE as a collection of three different worlds that meet through original animation and music. The title and credits use the segment names ÆLIENS, 4649GIRL and NOCTURNE: NUIT GROTESQUE. They are not three parts of one continuous plot, and the material released so far does not describe a shared chronological order. That structure matters for viewers deciding whether the Japan-only Netflix preview is a complete substitute for the cinema release: it covers just one chapter.

What is ÆLIENS about?

ÆLIENS follows Ellie, an alien girl who arrives on Earth as an interplanetary goodwill ambassador. In a period when contact between planets has brought both curiosity and fear, Ellie loves Earth’s culture and ordinary everyday life. She teams up with Kento Kunikura, a civil servant in the space bureau, to look for friends. The official synopsis adds a complication: Ellie’s ability can sometimes go out of control, prompting public suspicion that she may be an invader. Kana Ichinose voices Ellie, while Gen Hoshino plays Kunikura. The setup suggests a comedic contrast between Ellie’s enthusiasm and the mistrust around her, but the official synopsis does not reveal how the alien threat is resolved.

Why is the 4649GIRL preview limited?

The second chapter takes place in GALs TOKYO, a city where morning never comes. Girls called “gal P” gather there after losing a place in ordinary life, and compete in Queen of Butterfly, or QoB, a battle game whose winner becomes Queen. Stella, a girl who has stopped attending school, enters after hearing an urban legend. The official story description says she encounters an unidentified, overwhelmingly powerful player carrying an SSS-ranked weapon, then learns that the game hides a secret. Sara Minami voices Stella. The cast also includes Ikumi Hasegawa, Saori Hayami, Maaya Uchida, Chika Anzai, Miyu Tomita, Rumi Okubo and Rie Kugimiya. This was the story selected for advance streaming; its expired Japan-only preview dates do not establish current availability of the anthology.

What is NOCTURNE: NUIT GROTESQUE?

The third story imagines a world after humanity’s extinction. Ayame, a pure-blood vampire, travels in a van with three girls who survived after being “infected” by her bite. They find a small daily life beneath a sky full of stars, but the official synopsis frames Ayame’s immortality as a source of loneliness and despair. When something changes in her, the group’s settled routine begins to crack. Nogizaka46 members Nagi Inoue, Aruno Nakanishi, Miku Ichinose and Nao Tomisato voice Ayame, Shion, Momo and Kanna. The film site lists this chapter’s episode direction and character design under Atsushi Nishigori.

Who made the movie?

Atsushi Nishigori is credited with the overall direction, screenplay and original character concepts. JOEN and Aniplex planned and produced the project, animation is by CloverWorks, and ANIMEC handles theatrical distribution. The segment teams differ: Nishigori directs ÆLIENS and NOCTURNE; Takahiro Miura directs 4649GIRL, with character design by tarou2 and action direction by Isao Hayashi. Those separate credits help explain why the anthology moves between an alien comedy, a battle-game story and a post-human vampire drama instead of maintaining one visual or narrative premise.

What music and opening events are confirmed?

METALVERSE performs the opening theme “Shoot Shoot Shoot.” Aniplex’s October 21 soundtrack announcement lists 50 tracks across three CDs, excluding the opening theme, with 35 participating artists. That release was scheduled for November 6 in Japan. The production announced three days of post-screening cast events, November 6–8. The first day included a live-viewing option for the scheduled 18:45 appearance in Shinjuku; the official notice lists event times in Japan and says performers are subject to change. Aniplex also announced three manga spin-off one-shots on Shonen Jump+: the first was scheduled for November 6, and the second for November 7; the announcement says publication starts November 6 and proceeds in sequence. These are companion publications, not a pre-existing manga source for the movie.

Where can viewers outside Japan watch?

As of November 19, the full film has opened in Japanese cinemas. The advance Netflix Japan window covered only 4649GIRL and ended on November 5. The cited announcements do not confirm an overseas theatrical date, international Netflix access, or a later streaming service for the complete film. Check the official theater list for Japanese screenings and wait for a distributor announcement covering your country before relying on a platform or date. The official trailer can help you sample the film, but it does not provide access to the complete anthology.

For release updates, use the official film website, Aniplex’s movie news page and the November 2026 anime release guide.

What changed after the Japanese premiere?

The production’s November 17 notice announces an audio-commentary option through the HELLO! MOVIE smartphone app from November 21. It synchronizes commentary with a cinema screening; downloading the app does not unlock a stream of the movie at home. The announcement names director Atsushi Nishigori and cast members Kana Ichinose, Sara Minami and Nagi Inoue, with Leila Okahama as MC.

For a Japanese cinema visit, check the theater’s current showtimes before travelling. For home viewing, the relevant missing information is a full-film distributor announcement covering your country. Neither an expired Netflix chapter preview nor an audio-commentary download supplies that release.

What if you missed the 4649GIRL Netflix preview?

Think of the Netflix Japan preview as a chance to sample one of the stories. If you watched 4649GIRL during its advance window, you have seen that chapter rather than the full selection programmed for Japanese cinemas. The other two stories are still reasons to see the anthology, and the listing for a complete screening should use the film's full title. Check the theater's runtime and its description if the booking page uses shorthand in the program title.

The advance window ended November 5 according to the schedule covered here. A link that circulated during the preview can therefore still appear in search results even when it no longer plays. It also gives no information about whether Netflix outside Japan will carry the full film. For home viewing, look for a later announcement that explicitly names the anthology and lists the markets covered. A clip, trailer or repost using a chapter's title is a different result.

If you are deciding whether the theater trip is worth it, read the short descriptions of all three chapters above rather than judging every story by the first preview. An anthology can change setting, tone and characters between chapters. Its shared creative approach is a reason to compare those choices across the program, even if one of the stories is what first drew you in.
