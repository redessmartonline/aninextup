In a World Full of Zombies Anime: January 2027 Premiere, New Trailer, Cast and Music

A main promotional video and new character voices bring the January 2027 adaptation into clearer focus, but an exact premiere day and international streaming service remain unconfirmed.

In a World Full of Zombies I'm the Only One Who Doesn't Get Attacked has a new main trailer, an official key visual and two more confirmed Japanese voice actors. Publisher Frontier Works announced the material on October 10, 2026, giving viewers a fuller picture of the adaptation before its scheduled January 2027 broadcast in Japan.

The update does not establish an exact premiere date, an episode count or an international streaming partner. Those details should remain open rather than be inferred from the release month.

When does the zombie anime premiere?

The official anime website identifies January 2027 on AT-X as the Japanese broadcast window. AT-X is the named Japanese broadcaster, not a guarantee that the show will appear on a particular international service. Neither a precise broadcast day nor a worldwide simulcast schedule is provided in the October 10 production announcement. New cast announced for the anime

The October announcement adds Rikako Ito as Tokiko Kurose and Marie Miyake as Sayaka Makiura. They join the previously revealed central performers Wataru Kato as Yusuke Takemura and Yuno Azuma as Mitsuki Fujino.

Who is making the adaptation?

Kazuya Fujishiro directs the television anime, with Yasushi Hirano overseeing series composition. Kayo Shimoyama and Makuto Okimi are credited with scripts, Megumi Ishihara with character design, and ISAO with music. Animation production is credited jointly to Acca effe and Frontier Engine. These roles come from the official Japanese staff listing; they should not be confused with unannounced English dub credits.

Opening and ending songs confirmed

The opening song is “No Reaction” by Retbear, featuring the anonymously credited vocalist unknown Vo: 0in. Retbear is a music project associated with producer Kairiki Bear. The ending is “Tsukikage” by the performance group Itsuka Saisei.

Both songs were announced alongside the new trailer. Their inclusion in the publicity package is confirmed; a full soundtrack release date was not supplied in the sources reviewed for this guide.

What is the story about?

Yusuke Takemura discovers that a zombie outbreak has devastated the world around him. Unlike other survivors, however, he appears not to attract attacks from the infected. That unusual advantage changes the usual survival-horror premise: instead of simply fleeing danger, Yusuke must decide what to do with his relative freedom while other people remain at risk.

The story originated in novels by Rokuro Uraji, illustrated by Saburo, and also has a manga adaptation drawn by Chihiro Masuda. Official promotional material presents the series as a darker, adult-oriented zombie action story; viewers should not assume it follows the tone of a conventional all-ages adventure.

Why the protagonist's immunity changes the premise

The official synopsis describes Yusuke as someone zombies do not attack while other survivors remain vulnerable. This changes a familiar survival-horror expectation: his challenge is not simply escaping the infected, but navigating a crisis that affects other people differently. That is an interpretation of the premise, not confirmation of how he uses the advantage or who survives.

Where can viewers watch it outside Japan?

The confirmed broadcaster is AT-X in Japan. No international streaming platform, territory list or local release time is established by the October 10 official announcement.
