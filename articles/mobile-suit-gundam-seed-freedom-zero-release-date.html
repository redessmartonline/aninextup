Gundam SEED FREEDOM ZERO: January 2027 Release Date, Trailer and Cast

The Gundam SEED prequel arrives in Japanese theaters on January 15, 2027, followed by North America in February. Here is what Bandai Namco Filmworks has confirmed.

When does Gundam SEED FREEDOM ZERO release?

Mobile Suit Gundam SEED FREEDOM ZERO opens in Japanese theaters on Friday, January 15, 2027. Bandai Namco Filmworks has separately confirmed a North American theatrical release in February 2027, but has not announced a specific North American opening day or theater list. These are different regional release windows.

What is the story about?

Set in Cosmic Era 74 between Mobile Suit Gundam SEED DESTINY and the 2024 film SEED FREEDOM, the new movie follows Shinn Asuka after the collapse of the Destiny Plan. As Cagalli and her allies work to establish the international peacekeeping organization COMPASS, Shinn is still searching for a direction.

When the Strike Freedom Gundam is stolen from a temporary base on Onogoro Island, Shinn and his companions must respond before the crisis escalates. The film draws on a screenplay developed by Chiaki Morosawa, Shigeru Morita and director Mitsuo Fukuda during the planning of SEED FREEDOM.

Where does FREEDOM ZERO fit in the viewing order?

The film is set after Mobile Suit Gundam SEED DESTINY and before SEED FREEDOM, making it a story between existing installments rather than a sequel set after the 2024 film. Its focus on Shinn and the formation of COMPASS helps bridge those events. The announced placement does not mean every earlier Gundam series is required viewing. Are Japanese and North American release dates the same?

No. January 15, 2027 is the announced Japanese theatrical opening. North America has a separate February 2027 theatrical window, without a confirmed day or theater list in the cited announcement. Neither date establishes a global streaming release.

Japanese and English voice cast

The Japanese cast includes Kenichi Suzumura as Shinn Asuka, Maaya Sakamoto as Lunamaria Hawke, Houko Kuwashima as Agnes Giebenrath, Daisuke Ono as Leonard Valway, Soichiro Hoshi as Kira Yamato, Akira Ishida as Athrun Zala and Tomokazu Seki as Yzak Jule.

The announced English cast includes Matthew Erickson as Shinn Asuka, Matt Hill as Kira Yamato and Sam Vincent as Athrun Zala. Other English-language roles have not all been detailed in the announcement.

Who is producing the movie?

Mitsuo Fukuda directs, with character designs by Hisashi Hirai and music by Toshihiko Sahashi. SUNRISE is credited with planning and production; Bandai Namco Filmworks is among the film's producers and distributors.

Where can you watch it?

Theatrical releases are confirmed for Japan and North America. Streaming platforms, additional territories and home-video dates have not yet been announced. Check the official Gundam channels for confirmed updates rather than assuming availability on a particular streaming service.
