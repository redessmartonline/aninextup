Anime News: October 2026 — Releases, Sequels & Streaming Updates

A running overview of October anime releases, sequels and streaming updates tracked by AniNextUp.

By AniNextUp Editorial Team · How we verify releases

WHERE TO WATCHMultiple platforms

October marks the start of the Fall 2026 anime season, with a dense run of sequels, new adaptations and streaming premieres across Crunchyroll, Netflix, Disney+ and other services. This page focuses on confirmed release and streaming updates rather than rumor.

Big returns

  • The Apothecary Diaries Season 3 returns October 2.
  • Black Clover Season 2 begins October 3.
  • Blue Box Season 2 returns October 4 on Netflix.

New adaptations

PSYREN arrives October 5, Reborn as a Space Mercenary brings sci-fi isekai to the seasonal lineup, and Suikoden: The Anime adapts Konami’s RPG world.

Major October releases

TANK CHAIR premiered October 4, while Dragon Ball Super: Beerus is set for October 11 and Cyberpunk: Edgerunners 2 arrives October 20 on Netflix.

New October premieres to track

Nia Liston: The Merciless Maiden premieres October 6 in Japan with a two-cour run from KONAMI animation. International streaming is still unannounced, so AniNextUp is tracking that separately from the confirmed Japanese schedule.

Streaming updates to watch

Netflix has confirmed October dates for The Ramparts of Ice Season 2, Blue Box Season 2 and My Happy Marriage Season 2 Special Episodes. Crunchyroll’s Fall lineup includes titles such as Firefly Wedding and The Detective Is Already Dead Season 2.

How AniNextUp handles updates

We separate confirmed premiere dates from unannounced simulcast times and territory-specific availability. When a platform, studio or official anime site publishes a change, the relevant release guide is updated rather than treating speculation as a schedule.

Keep checking AniNextUp

We’ll keep the October hub updated as premiere times, streaming territories and additional announcements are confirmed.
