What anime comes out in November 2026? This guide separates three announced worldwide streaming premieres from five movies opening in Japanese theaters. It is a dated premiere roundup, not a complete weekly TV schedule. A Japanese cinema date does not confirm an international or online release, and a global streaming announcement does not guarantee identical catalogs, languages or launch hours in every country.

Check your local Netflix or Prime Video listing for language options and availability. Only Demon’s Crest has an exact launch time in the official sources reviewed here; do not infer midnight from a date alone.

Dates, release format and timing

Bass X Machina is the first streaming premiere, on November 3. Netflix describes a lawless steampunk western where a father becomes judge and executioner while trying to protect his family. The official announcement confirms worldwide Netflix distribution, but the cited material does not give a universal launch hour, episode count or language package. Check the local catalog rather than assuming a midnight release.

Demon’s Crest arrives November 6. Universal Music’s October 2 announcement specifies 9:00 p.m. Japan Standard Time (JST, UTC+9) and says episodes 1 and 2 will appear on day one through Prime Video’s worldwide exclusive distribution. This is the only exact streaming hour confirmed in the cited sources. It is a service launch time, not a trailer upload timestamp.

Fool Night arrives November 26, with all episodes released together according to Netflix’s Japan announcement. The story is set on a cloud-covered future Earth, where humanity depends on “transfloration,” a process that turns people near death into plants. This is the confirmed binge option in this roundup. Netflix has announced a worldwide date but no common launch hour in the cited announcement.

What to know about the film dates

GROTESQQQUE opens November 6. Its separate Netflix Japan preview runs October 23–November 5 and covers the second segment, 4649GIRL (Yoroshiku Girl). That limited preview is not a worldwide streaming release of the full movie.

Rakuen Tsuihou: Kokoro no Resonance opens November 13 as a new sequel telling the story after Expelled from Paradise, according to its official site. The announcement confirms Japan’s date, not an overseas rollout.

Witch on the Holy Night and VOTOMS: The Gray Witch Part 1 both open November 20. VOTOMS is the first of a planned two-part theatrical project. Its official site announced opening-day stage greetings at TOHO Cinemas Shinjuku: 5:00 p.m. JST after the screening and 8:10 p.m. JST before the screening. Those times describe event showings, not streaming availability. See the event notice for ticket and program details.

Takopi’s Original Sin Movie opens November 27. TBS says it re-edits all six anime episodes for theaters and adds new scenes, so it is a compilation film rather than a second season. The announcement does not describe the added material in detail.

Can I watch these releases in my country?

  • Streaming: Netflix and Prime Video announcements say worldwide. Local catalogs and language options may differ.
  • Cinema: the five movie dates above apply to Japan. International release dates remain separate and should be confirmed title by title.
  • Timing: Demon’s Crest is listed for 9:00 p.m. JST on November 6. No common hour is stated for the Netflix premieres in the cited announcements.

Frequently asked questions

Which November anime can I binge?

Netflix says all episodes of Fool Night arrive together on November 26. For Demon’s Crest, the confirmed day-one drop is episodes 1 and 2; the cited announcement does not call the whole series a binge release.

Are the five movies streaming in November?

No streaming date is confirmed in the cited theatrical announcements. GROTESQQQUE’s short Netflix Japan segment preview is a separate promotion, not the full film’s global digital release.

Which dates have multiple premieres?

November 6 pairs Demon’s Crest on Prime Video with GROTESQQQUE in Japanese cinemas. November 20 has two Japanese theatrical openings. They are different formats and, in the first case, different territories.
