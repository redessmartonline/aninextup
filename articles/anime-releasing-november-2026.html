How to read this November release calendar

The dates in the table refer to different kinds of premieres. Demon's Crest has an announced worldwide Prime Video release, while Bass X Machina and Fool Night have announced worldwide Netflix premieres. Local release hours should be checked separately. The four film dates refer to Japanese cinema openings, not global streaming premieres.

If you are outside Japan and want to watch one of the listed films, its Japanese theatrical date does not guarantee a screening or online release in your country. Open the individual film guide for any confirmed international rollout; where no international date has been announced, the guide identifies that uncertainty rather than assigning an estimated release.

November's schedule has two different viewing paths

At-home premieres: The entries for Bass X Machina, Demon's Crest and Fool Night identify streaming platforms. These are the first titles to check if you want to watch from home during November. Japanese cinema openings: Rakuen Tsuihou: Kokoro no Resonance, Witch on the Holy Night, VOTOMS: The Gray Witch Part 1 and Takopi's Original Sin have theatrical dates in this tracker. Their November listings are not promises of Netflix, Prime Video or other international streaming access on those dates.
