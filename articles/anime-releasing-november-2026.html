Can I watch these releases in my country?

  • Netflix and Prime Video: the three streaming rows carry worldwide distribution announcements. Check your country’s catalog for the actual title listing, release hour and language options; no common worldwide upload hour is confirmed here.
  • Japanese cinemas: use the film’s theater list if you are in Japan. Outside Japan, follow the dedicated movie guide for a local distributor announcement. Do not treat the November opening as an online viewing date.
  • Witch on the Holy Night: a separate international theatrical rollout has been announced for January 2027. Its movie guide tracks those dates independently of the November Japanese opening.
