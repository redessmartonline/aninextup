When is Berserk of Gluttony Season 2 coming out?

Berserk of Gluttony Season 2 is scheduled for 2027. The official anime website announced the 2027 broadcast window on August 23, 2026, and Crunchyroll repeated that window at New York Comic Con on October 8. Neither announcement specifies a month or day. A particular premiere date circulating online should not be treated as confirmed until the production announces it.

Where to watch Berserk of Gluttony Season 2

Crunchyroll has announced Season 2 streaming for North America, Central America, South America, Europe, Africa, Oceania, the Middle East and the CIS. That regional list comes from Crunchyroll's October 8 announcement; it does not establish the precise launch hour or guarantee that every individual country has identical availability. Check your local Crunchyroll catalog when the premiere approaches.

What is the sequel about?

The series follows Fate Graphite, whose seemingly worthless Gluttony skill turns out to let him absorb the abilities and attributes of enemies he defeats. The new-season synopsis circulated by Crunchyroll places Fate back in the royal capital after dangerous battles alongside Greed, his sentient black sword. It also emphasizes the tension between his extraordinary powers and his desire for an ordinary life.

This is a premise-level description, not an episode-by-episode account. The October announcements do not establish how many episodes Season 2 will have or precisely which light-novel volumes it will adapt.

What has actually been confirmed?

Production of Season 2 was announced on April 27, 2026, with a commemorative visual. The 2027 broadcast window followed in August, and the official website added a teaser in October. Crunchyroll's New York Comic Con presentation separately confirmed its planned streaming territories. These are different milestones; the October teaser should not be mistaken for an announcement of an exact release date.
