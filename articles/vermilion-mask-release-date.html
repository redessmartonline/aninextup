The Vermilion Mask Release Date, Time & Where to Watch

When does The Vermilion Mask premiere?

The Vermilion Mask premieres October 10, 2026 on Crunchyroll. The confirmed simulcast time is 2:30 a.m. PT. In Japan, the anime airs Saturdays at 5:30 p.m. on the Yomiuri TV/Nippon TV nationwide network, excluding some regions.

Where to watch

Crunchyroll lists the anime for North America, Central and South America, Europe, Africa, Oceania, the Middle East, CIS and the Indian Subcontinent. Availability can still differ by individual market.

What is The Vermilion Mask about?

Peru is the pupil of legendary mask maker Gaston Roux. After the Mask of the Warrior God takes control of him and causes a tragedy that kills his friends and master, Peru vows to destroy the remaining masks Gaston created around the world.

Who is making the anime?

Tetsuaki Watanabe directs, with Gai Hazako as assistant director and Daisuke Ohigashi handling series composition. Hisashi Higashijima is character designer and chief animation director, Alisa Okehazama composes the music, and 100studio handles animation production.
