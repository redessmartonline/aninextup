When did The Vermilion Mask premiere?

The Vermilion Mask began its Japanese broadcast on October 10, 2026. The production announcement from HIKE sets the regular Yomiuri TV/Nippon TV network slot at Saturdays, 5:30 p.m. JST, with some regional exceptions. This is the domestic television appointment, not a universal streaming upload time.

That Japanese slot corresponds to 08:30 UTC. Use the station's current listing if a particular weekend changes, rather than treating the opening date as a guarantee for every subsequent episode. Viewers outside Japan should check their own distributor before converting the television hour into a watch-party reminder.

Where can you watch it?

Crunchyroll is an announced international streaming home. Yomiuri Telecasting Corporation's English production release explicitly identifies an October Crunchyroll launch. A local episode page remains the practical check for whether the show is available to your account, and which audio and subtitle options it carries. The Japanese distribution notice also names domestic services, including TVer, ytv MyDo!, ABEMA and subscription platforms. These are separate market-specific options. A listing for a Japanese service does not establish that the same title is included in an identically named service abroad, or that every platform releases an episode simultaneously.

This guide does not assign an English dub date. The confirmed production announcement and the Japanese cast list are not evidence of a dubbed release schedule. Check the language label beside the episode you intend to play instead of assuming all versions share the same availability.

What is the story, without later-episode spoilers?

Peru d'Arrest is an apprentice to mask maker Gaston Roux. A powerful mask takes control of Peru, and the resulting tragedy kills his companions and his master. Peru's response is to seek out and destroy the masks Gaston left across the world, hoping to prevent another catastrophe. Related reading: DARK MACHINE THE ANIMATION Release Date & Time.

That premise gives the journey a different purpose from collecting powerful equipment. Peru is confronting the consequences of objects he once associated with his teacher. Our interpretation is that responsibility and regret provide the emotional stakes of the battles; this is a reading of the official setup, not a review claiming that we have watched every episode.

What do the masks actually do?

The official introduction establishes that wearing a mask can confer power, with disastrous consequences as well as advantages. The Mask of the Warrior God is central to the opening tragedy. The title therefore refers to objects that drive the conflict, rather than decorative costumes alone. Related reading: PSYREN Anime Release Date, Time & Where to Watch.

For a first-time viewer, that is enough background to follow Peru's goal. The introduction does not give a complete catalogue of masks, their rules or the eventual outcome of the hunt. Treat a power shown briefly in promotional footage as a preview, not a full explanation of how every confrontation works.

Which voice actors and studio are involved?

The official English credits name Taihi Kimura as Peru, Yuuki Yamamoto as Sonarue Maywood and Kazuki Ura as Mok Sharib. Kazuhiko Inoue voices Gaston Roux, while Akio Otsuka voices the Mask of the Warrior God. These are the Japanese performances; they should not be presented as an English dub cast.

Tetsuaki Watanabe directs, Gai Hazako is assistant director and Daisuke Ohigashi handles series composition. Hisashi Higashijima designs the characters and serves as chief animation director; Alisa Okehazama composes the music. The animation company is credited as One Double-O studio on the English site and 100studio in the Japanese announcement. What are the opening and ending songs?

The announced opening is MAN WITH A MISSION's “Firestarter”; the ending is yutori's “Koe.” Yomiuri's release identifies both tracks in the second main promotional video. This helps distinguish the songs from the instrumental score credited to Okehazama when searching for music from the series.

The verified upload is on Yomiuri TV's official animation channel. Its original YouTube metadata records September 10, 2026, at 12:00:12 PDT (UTC−07:00), equivalent to 19:00:12 UTC. That dates the trailer, not the anime premiere. The official production release links this same video.

Is there a manga, and where should newcomers begin?

The anime's official site identifies the source as the manga by Nabana Naba and Dr.Poro, serialized in Shonen Gahosha's Young King OURs. Readers wanting the original should look for those creator credits and the Japanese title, 朱色の仮面, rather than an unrelated novel with a similar English name.

The announcements cited here do not provide an episode-to-chapter reading map or establish how much manga the season adapts. Starting at the beginning is the clearest reading option; matching a chapter number to an episode number could skip material.
