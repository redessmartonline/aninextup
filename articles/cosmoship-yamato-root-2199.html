What is confirmed about Cosmoship Yamato √2199?

Cosmoship Yamato √2199, also styled Space Battleship Yamato √2199 and read “Root 2199,” is a new feature-length theatrical anime in production. It was announced on October 6, 2026, the 52nd anniversary of the original television series’ first broadcast. Production I.G and Studio Khara are co-producing the animation for the first time. Who is directing it, and which studios are involved?

The official staff list credits Anno with planning, executive production, direction and design work. Izubuchi is credited with direction and design work, while Mitsuhisa Ishikawa is credited with planning. Yoshinobu Nishizaki is named for the original work, and Shin Office Academy is listed as the production entity. Production I.G and Studio Khara are the animation-production partners. These role labels matter because headlines often reduce the reveal to “Anno directs”; he is not the only director, and neither studio is described as the sole animation producer.

The I.G–Khara collaboration is their first animation co-production, according to the studios. It is a production distinction, not a promise about visual style, schedule or story. No composer, additional animation staff, characters, voice cast or distributor is named in the official materials reviewed for this update. Is this a sequel to Space Battleship Yamato 2199 or REBEL3199?

No direct sequel connection has been announced. Khara addressed the relationship before the new film’s title reveal: in an October 2024 statement, it described this project as following a different route from the remake line that began with Space Battleship Yamato 2199 and continues with Yamato yo Towa ni: REBEL3199. That is firmer guidance than guessing from the shared “2199” in the names. It distinguishes the creative tracks, but does not explain √2199’s plot, timeline, characters or whether viewers need to know earlier stories.

Anno’s statement on Khara’s site gives context for that distinction. He recalled that the original franchise already existed in multiple forms: the television series, theatrical versions, novels and manga did not all present one identical continuity. He also described the remake manga and screen version as parallel, and framed multiple Yamato worlds as part of the property’s history. This is the director’s perspective on the franchise, not a synopsis or confirmation that √2199 will use a particular alternate-history device.

Why was the announcement tied to October 6?

The date connects the project to the original Space Battleship Yamato broadcast, which began on October 6, 1974. Production I.G notes that the first TV series ran for 26 episodes and that the reveal arrived on the franchise’s 52nd anniversary. The 50th-anniversary program in 2024 included screenings, exhibitions and publications with Anno’s involvement. This timing explains the announcement’s anniversary significance; it does not imply that the new film will premiere on an anniversary.
