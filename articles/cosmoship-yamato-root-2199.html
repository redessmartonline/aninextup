What was announced for Cosmoship Yamato √2199?

On October 6, the 52nd anniversary of the original Space Battleship Yamato TV premiere, the production formally announced Cosmoship Yamato √2199 and unveiled its first teaser visual. The project is a new feature-length theatrical animation rather than a TV season.

Who is directing the new Yamato movie?

Hideaki Anno serves as planner, executive producer, director and design-works contributor. Yutaka Izubuchi also directs and handles design work. Mitsuhisa Ishikawa is credited with planning, while the original work is credited to Yoshinobu Nishizaki.

Which studios are making it?

Production I.G and Khara are jointly handling animation production. The official announcement describes this as the studios' first animation co-production, making the production partnership one of the most notable parts of the reveal.

When does Cosmoship Yamato √2199 release?

No release date or release window has been announced. The official site says further information will follow. There is also no confirmed international theatrical or streaming plan yet.

Is √2199 the same as Space Battleship Yamato 2199?

No. The title deliberately echoes Yamato 2199, but this is a newly announced theatrical project. Fans should not assume it is a conventional continuation of the existing remake line until the production provides story and continuity details.

What can the creative credits tell us?

The announced collaboration places Hideaki Anno and Yutaka Izubuchi in directing roles, with Production I.G and Khara handling animation. Those credits are meaningful production facts, but they cannot by themselves establish the film's narrative continuity, visual style or intended release year. The distinction is especially important because the title resembles Space Battleship Yamato 2199 while the official reveal describes a separate theatrical project.

Which details are still missing?

The announcement establishes a feature film and its creative leadership, but not the story's place in the existing Yamato continuities. A release date, cast, runtime and overseas distribution are also unannounced. Until the production publishes those details, the shared number in the title should not be treated as proof of a direct sequel.
