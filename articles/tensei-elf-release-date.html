Does Tensei Elf start in December 2026 or January 2027?

Both dates are correct, but they describe different releases. Tensei Elf starts its advance Japanese streaming run on ABEMA and d Anime Store in the December 30, 2026, 24:00 JST slot. Its TOKYO MX television run starts one week later, in the January 6, 2027, 24:00 JST slot. On an ordinary clock, those are December 31 and January 7 at midnight in Japan.

The publisher’s November 19 announcement confirms the advance-streaming arrangement and the television launch. This is a one-week lead for the named Japanese streaming services, rather than two separate anime seasons. If you are deciding which date belongs in your calendar, choose the service or station you can actually use before converting its time.

The series’ full romanized title is Majutsu wo Kiwamete Tabi ni Deta Tensei Elf, Moteamashita Jumyo de Ikeru Densetsu to Naru. This guide uses Tensei Elf, the shortened name associated with its official channels. Searching the longer title may help when a service has not adopted the same abbreviation.

Why does the official schedule say 24:00?

Japanese late-night programming can stay attached to the evening’s broadcast day after the calendar has changed. Wednesday at 24:00 is the instant Wednesday ends and Thursday begins. For a standard calendar app, enter Thursday at 00:00. Wednesday at 24:30 becomes Thursday at 00:30.

This changes the date you type, not the length of the announced streaming lead. Both the early stream and the TOKYO MX slot move forward by one calendar day when normalized, so the gap remains one week. Do not enter December 30 at 00:00 in Japan as a substitute for December 30 at 24:00: that would put the reminder a day early.

The simplest reminder uses the ordinary Japanese calendar time with the Asia/Tokyo timezone. Your device can then display the equivalent local time. Keep the service name in the reminder too, because matching a Japanese release time does not establish that the same episode is available through a service in your country.

Is Tensei Elf confirmed for Crunchyroll, Netflix or international Prime Video?

The Japanese schedule reviewed for this guide does not name an international streaming partner. Its Prime Video and Hulu entries are Japanese services with specific viewing arrangements. They should not be read as confirmation for the US, UK or other international catalogs. We have not established an English-dub date from the reviewed official materials.

What is Tensei Elf about?

After an accident ends his ordinary life, Leese is reincarnated as an elf who can live for a thousand years. He leaves home determined to make this life count. His travels bring him into contact with Minori, a girl whose life he saved, the legendary elf Rahab, and a local lord’s family. Along the way, he learns of signs that demons may return.

The official introduction frames the story around encounters and farewells over an elf’s long life. That is a useful distinction if you are deciding whether the premise interests you: the fantasy journey involves the people Leese meets as well as his magic. The release materials do not establish how much of the source story this season will cover, so we are not assigning an adaptation endpoint.

Who voices the characters, and which studio makes it?

The official cast page lists Daiki Yamashita as Leese and Hana Hishikawa as Minori. Haruka Terui voices Rahab, Nobuyuki Hiyama voices Grillet, Hiro Shimono voices Galius, and Megumi Ogata voices Sylpha. These are Japanese cast credits; they are not an English-dub cast announcement.

Yuki Inaba handles direction and series composition, Kaori Yoshikawa designs the characters, and studio palette produces the animation. The source work is credited to Monsho Sakakibara with manga art by kanco. The anime’s music page names ao’s “Breath” as the opening and moon drop’s “Jaa Mata, Raise demo” as the ending. The main trailer provides an official preview of the production ahead of launch.

Can you read the source material before the anime?

The official Books page distinguishes the comic adaptation from the novel. The publisher also maintains a Japanese manga page on Novema with chapter listings. Start from those publisher links if you want to identify the source edition rather than relying on a similarly titled reincarnation series.

Those Japanese pages do not establish an English edition. We have not verified an English publisher in the materials consulted for this guide. If you prefer to avoid spoilers, the introduction and trailer are enough preparation; later chapter descriptions may reveal developments beyond the opening setup.

Is the December premiere a separate special?

Does a Japanese Prime Video listing mean worldwide access?

No. A domestic listing cannot establish availability in another country or inclusion with its local Prime subscription.
