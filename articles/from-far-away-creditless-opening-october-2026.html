From Far Away Anime Releases Creditless Opening Video for “Touhikou”

The anime adaptation of Kyoko Hikawa's fantasy manga has shared its opening sequence without on-screen credits. The official video features “Touhikou” by Genin wa Jibun ni Aru and is available through the production's channel.

By AniNextUp Editorial Team · Published: October 15, 2026 · Editorial policy

What is new in the From Far Away opening?

The production behind From Far Away (Kanata Kara) announced its creditless opening on October 12, 2026, following the show's Japanese broadcast debut. The video removes the opening's staff and cast credits, letting viewers focus on the sequence's animation and music. Crunchyroll highlighted the video on October 13. This is a new promotional release for the current television adaptation, not an announcement of another season.

The official anime website lists the creditless opening among its promotional videos.

Watch the official creditless opening

The opening is set to “Touhikou” (逃光行), performed by Japanese group Genin wa Jibun ni Aru. The video was linked by the production's official account and published through NBCUniversal Anime/Music.

Open the official video on YouTube if the embedded player is unavailable in your region.

Opening and ending songs explained

The official music page credits Genin wa Jibun ni Aru for the opening theme “Touhikou.” The ending theme is a different song: “Hikari no Soba de” (光の側で), performed by Nanashi no Taro. The two tracks should not be confused when searching for the newly released opening animation.

Both credits appear on the official music information page.

When did From Far Away premiere?

The television adaptation began airing in Japan on October 4, 2026, according to the official production website. Crunchyroll carries the anime in supported territories, but availability and episode release times can differ by location. The opening-video announcement does not change the existing broadcast schedule.

Story, director and animation studio

Noriko, a high school student, finds herself stranded in an unfamiliar fantasy world where she cannot understand the local language. She is rescued by Izark, a swordsman whose own mission may put them on opposing sides of a prophecy. Their developing relationship is central to the adaptation of Kyoko Hikawa's manga.

Noriyuki Abe directs the anime at Studio DEEN, with Rika Takasugi handling series composition, Eiji Abiko designing the characters and Takeshi Hama composing the score.

Does the new video announce another season?

No. It is a creditless version of the opening from the ongoing October 2026 anime. No new season or sequel is confirmed by this release.
