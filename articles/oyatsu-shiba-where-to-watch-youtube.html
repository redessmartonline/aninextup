Where can you watch Oyatsu Shiba for free?

Watch Oyatsu Shiba on the official Oyatsu Shiba YouTube channel. KaKa Creation announced that the first 20 shorts will be released there for free, starting on Saturday, November 14, 2026. The Japanese title is おやつしば. If you are searching in the YouTube app, use that title or the channel handle @oyatsushiba to find the producer’s channel.

Before the premiere, the channel is the most reliable link to save. This guide uses the address published by KaKa Creation. Once the first short is posted, check the channel’s Shorts and Videos tabs for the episode title or number. The production also lists official accounts on X and TikTok. Start on YouTube if you want the weekly episodes, since the announcement names it as the outlet for the free run.

If a search takes you to older clips labeled Mameshiba, you may have found the original franchise. Look for the Oyatsu Shiba name or the Japanese title on the upload itself. The spinoff has its own handle, and using it is the quickest way to find the right channel without relying on translated titles or a third party’s playlist.

What is Oyatsu Shiba, and how does it connect to Mameshiba?

Oyatsu Shiba is an official Mameshiba spinoff. The name refers to snacks, and the series uses dog-like characters associated with Japanese sweets. The familiar Mameshiba idea of odd facts told by tiny characters carries over to this group. In the producer’s description, the snack characters talk about humans and share trivia. The first short is a straightforward starting point for anyone new to the franchise.

The sweets are also a way to tell the characters apart. Daifuku is a soft rice cake usually wrapped around a filling. Strawberry daifuku puts a strawberry at the center, and mame daifuku incorporates beans. Ohagi is a different sweet, commonly made with sticky rice and sweet bean paste. Keeping those names in mind helps if the Japanese channel uses a character’s snack name in an episode title or in a clip introducing the cast.

It is worth keeping the two titles separate when sharing the show. Mameshiba is the franchise name people may recognize from earlier bean-shaped characters. Oyatsu Shiba is the title attached to this group of sweets and its 2026 YouTube schedule. The producer announced this series as a short-form social release, so the one-minute format is part of the show’s design.

Who voices the snack characters?

The announcement names seven roles. Akari Kito voices Daifuku Shiba, Honoka Inoue voices Ichigo Daifuku Shiba and Natsuki Hanae voices Mame Daifuku Shiba. Miku Ito plays Matcha Daifuku Shiba, while Takuma Nagatsuka plays Yaki Daifuku Shiba. Kotone Izumi voices Ohagi Shiba and Yumiri Hanamori voices Sakura Ohagi Shiba. KaKa Technology Studio is credited for production, and Mofumofumoo performed and produced the theme song.

These are the voices from the Japanese production announcement. If you came to the series for one actor, pair the actor’s name with the snack character rather than relying on the franchise name alone. The announcement introduces the full group together and does not assign each character a particular episode date.
