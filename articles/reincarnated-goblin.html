Has Reincarnated Goblin already premiered?

Yes. So What’s Wrong with Getting Reborn as a Goblin? began its Japanese television run on October 5, 2026. The first TOKYO MX slot is Monday at 22:00 JST, equivalent to 13:00 UTC. The title also appears in Crunchyroll’s Fall 2026 lineup. This guide separates Japan’s broadcast schedule from platform availability, because an episode being scheduled on Japanese TV does not establish its availability in every country or on every subscription service.

Japan’s weekly television schedule

TOKYO MX lists Mondays at 22:00 JST. BS11 lists Monday at 24:00, and Sun TV Monday at 24:30; on a standard calendar those late-night slots fall on Tuesday at 00:00 and 00:30 JST. AT-X began on October 7 with a Wednesday 20:00 JST slot. These are different broadcasts of the same series, not four release dates for distinct editions. Use the broadcaster you actually receive when setting a reminder.

Why are some Japanese services an episode ahead?

The official on-air page gives ABEMA and Prime Video a one-week lead over terrestrial television from Episode 2. The opening release on October 5 at 22:00 JST included Episode 1 alongside Episode 2: the first episode was simultaneous with TV, while the second began the advance schedule. Other Japanese services started distributing after the television broadcast. This explains why Japanese platform listings can show different episode numbers on the same day without either schedule necessarily being wrong. Where should international viewers check?

Crunchyroll’s announced lineup includes the series, but its local title page is the practical place to check current episodes, subtitles and audio tracks. Japan’s official streaming list also names services such as Hulu, U-NEXT and d Anime Store. Those domestic listings should not be turned into worldwide licensing claims. In particular, the Japanese Prime Video advance arrangement is not evidence that every international Prime Video catalog receives episodes a week early.

What is the story about?

Akira Yagami is an office worker trusted by his colleagues who dies while saving a child from traffic. He wakes in another world as a goblin, a species whose normal lifespan is only seven days. His unusual ability, called Octogenarian in the anime site’s English introduction, gives him a much longer life. As goblins around him die, he inherits their skills. Demons, elves and dragons eventually become part of the wider circle gathering around him. Related reading: Cosmoship Yamato √2199 Release Date: Story, Cast and What’s Confirmed.

What makes Akira’s skill different?

The contrast between a seven-day goblin lifespan and Akira’s eighty years is the premise’s central advantage. It gives him time to accumulate abilities that short-lived goblins cannot develop in the same way. That makes the setting more specific than a generic reincarnated office worker gaining strength: his growth is tied to the passing of others in his community. This is a reading of the published setup, rather than a claim about how any particular later battle ends.

Who are the cast and staff?

Kikunosuke Toya voices Akira, Yoko Hikasa plays Karen, and Azusa Tachibana plays Chloe. The Japanese cast also includes Banjo Ginga as Simon, Sayumi Suzushiro as Eirene, M・A・O as Dike and Wakana Kuramochi as Eunomia. Ryuta Kawahara directs the anime at BAKKKA, with series composition and scripts by Yuichiro Momose, character design by Hiroyuki Saida and music by Takenoko Shonen. These credits describe the Japanese production; dubbed-language casting is a separate matter. Related reading: New Anime on Netflix in October 2026: What to Watch.

What are the opening and ending songs?

Sou performs the opening theme, Hello, while the ending is Chiisana Bokura by AHUB, known in Japanese as Eihabu. The production’s September announcement names both songs, and the main PV previews the opening. Their presence in promotional material helps identify the correct anime trailer when searching the long English title. A theme-song announcement should not be mistaken for an episode soundtrack release or a guarantee that a streaming service carries separate music videos.

Where can you read the manga?

The manga is written by Nazuna Miki and illustrated by Tsukasa Araki. Shueisha publishes it in Japan through Tonari no Young Jump, while Yen Press publishes the English edition. Yen Press’s Volume 1 listing is a useful starting point if you want the source story without jumping into later plot developments. It credits Caleb Cook as translator and labels the book Teen. That is the manga edition’s rating, not an independently announced age classification for the anime.

What remains unconfirmed in this guide?

The reviewed release notices do not identify how many manga chapters the anime will adapt or where its season will end. Do not choose a manga starting chapter based on an assumed final episode: begin with Volume 1 for the original version, or wait for the adaptation’s coverage to be clear. For weekly viewing, follow the appropriate local service’s episode list. For Japanese broadcast changes, the anime’s on-air page and the receiving broadcaster are the relevant references.
