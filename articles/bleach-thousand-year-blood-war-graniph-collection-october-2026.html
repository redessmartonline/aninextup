BLEACH and Graniph reveal a 17-item Shikai-inspired collection

The BLEACH: Thousand-Year Blood War anime and Japanese apparel brand Graniph have announced their first collaboration. The 17-item lineup centers on designs inspired by Zanpakuto Shikai, with additional pieces pairing the series with Graniph’s original character Beautiful Shadow.

The announcement was published by the official anime site and Aniplex on October 14, 2026. Graniph’s collection page lists the items for its official online store and Japanese retail shops. It gives a current price range of ¥3,500 to ¥17,000; check the individual listings for exact prices, sizes and stock.

What is included in the collection?

The catalog spans T-shirts, sweatshirts and hoodies, shirts, knitwear, trousers, outerwear, caps, shoulder bags, wallets, and stationery or hobby goods. The designs draw on the series’ released Zanpakuto and character themes, while selected pieces feature Beautiful Shadow. The official product listings are the best source for each item’s design, size options and availability.

When do preorders and sales begin?

Graniph’s official online store opened advance reservations at 12:00 a.m. JST on October 14 and lists the preorder period through 11:59 p.m. JST on October 19, 2026. The retailer advertises double membership points for online reservations through October 19. The in-store launch is October 20 at Graniph shops in Japan.

What should international buyers check before ordering?

The advertised preorder window and October 20 store launch apply to Graniph's Japanese sales channels. Fans outside Japan should confirm whether their country is supported at checkout, along with shipping costs, import charges and any regional restrictions. The official collaboration announcement does not guarantee overseas delivery, and a listed price in yen does not include an independently verified international total.

Which items have purchase limits?

Graniph limits caps, shoulder bags, mini shoulder bags, wallets and charms to two items per customer during the October 14–20 period. The limit applies only to those listed categories. Check the collection page for any changes before placing an order.

Can fans outside Japan order the collection?

The cited announcement confirms sales through Graniph’s official online store and shops in Japan, but does not confirm international shipping or availability in other countries. Fans outside Japan should check Graniph’s current shipping destinations and checkout terms before relying on the online listing.

Is this a new anime release announcement?

No. This is a merchandise collaboration for BLEACH: Thousand-Year Blood War. It does not announce a new episode, anime cour, broadcast date or streaming service.
