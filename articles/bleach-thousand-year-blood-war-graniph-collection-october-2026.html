BLEACH x Graniph: 17 Thousand-Year Blood War Items Launch October 20

BLEACH: Thousand-Year Blood War and Graniph launch 17 collaboration items in Japan October 20, 2026. See official designs, preorder dates and purchase limits.

By AniNextUp Editorial · October 14, 2026

Release at a glance

Collection: BLEACH: Thousand-Year Blood War × Graniph
Items: 17
Japanese store launch: October 20, 2026
Online preorders: October 14–19, 2026
Official retailer: Graniph

BLEACH and Graniph unveil 17 collaboration designs

The official BLEACH: Thousand-Year Blood War anime website announced its first collaboration with Japanese fashion brand Graniph on October 14, 2026. The 17-piece collection includes clothing and accessories inspired by Zanpakuto Shikai releases, alongside special designs featuring Graniph’s original character Beautiful Shadow.

According to the anime’s official announcement, the products launch in Japanese Graniph stores on October 20, 2026. Online preorders began on October 14 at midnight Japan time, with the advance preorder promotion continuing through October 19. The retailer lists double membership points during that period.

What products are included?

The official catalog features T-shirts, hoodies, sweatshirts, shirts, knitwear, trousers, outerwear, caps, shoulder bags, wallets and accessories. Character-inspired designs reference the weapons and release commands of the series. Exact sizes, availability and prices should be checked on Graniph’s official collection page, which may change as stock sells out.

When can fans buy the collection?

Graniph lists October 20 as the Japanese in-store release date. The official online store accepts advance reservations from October 14 through October 19. International availability or shipping eligibility has not been established by the anime announcement, so fans outside Japan should verify options with the retailer.

Are there purchase limits?

Graniph limits selected items—including caps, shoulder bags, mini shoulder bags, wallets and charms—to two per customer from October 14 through October 20. Check the retailer’s current rules before ordering.

Does this announce new BLEACH episodes?

No. This is a merchandise collaboration for BLEACH: Thousand-Year Blood War, not a new anime episode, broadcast schedule or streaming announcement. It should be tracked separately from updates about the anime’s final cour.
