BLEACH × Graniph: 17-Item Collection Launches in Japan on October 20

BLEACH: Thousand-Year Blood War and Graniph launched 17 Shikai-themed items in Japan on October 20, 2026. Check designs, current stock, sizing and the ended preorder window.

What did BLEACH and Graniph announce?

BLEACH: Thousand-Year Blood War and Japanese apparel label Graniph launched a 17-item collaboration on October 20, 2026. The collection is built around the Shikai, or released form, of characters’ Zanpakuto, with additional designs pairing the anime with Graniph’s original character Beautiful Shadow. It is a licensed fashion and goods collection, not a new season, episode announcement, or anime release date.

The official announcement names Graniph’s online store and its domestic shops as the sales channels. The date is therefore a Japanese market launch. The announcement does not promise a separate release in North America, Europe, or other territories, and it does not confirm that every size or design remains in stock after launch.

What does the Shikai theme mean for the designs?

In BLEACH, a Zanpakuto is a Soul Reaper’s weapon, and Shikai is the first released state associated with its wielder. Graniph uses that idea as a visual design brief: the collection page presents the series’ sword imagery and character motifs as wearable graphics. The theme links the merchandise to the anime without requiring a buyer to treat each item as a plot summary or a new story reveal. Related reading: A Certain Dark Item Release Date & Where to Watch.

Examples in Graniph’s official photo gallery include a Gotei 13 Shikai T-shirt, a long-sleeve design named for Zabimaru, a Kyoka Suigetsu stadium jacket, a Senbonzakura hoodie, and a Hyorinmaru mini shoulder bag. Other photos show Urahara Shop and “Death God Agent Shadow” designs. Graniph also lists selected items featuring Beautiful Shadow, so not every piece is a sword graphic.

What kinds of products are included?

The official gallery shows the 17-piece lineup across several everyday categories rather than a single T-shirt run. Its pictured products include short- and long-sleeve tops, sweatshirts and hoodies, a stadium jacket, a knit cardigan, a mini shoulder bag, a wallet, and a charm. The collection page is the authoritative place to check which other styles are available; the gallery is useful for seeing how the designs look when worn or displayed.

Some graphics rely on a character or weapon name, while others combine series iconography with a Graniph character. For example, the Gotei 13 Shikai T-shirt’s official description says it arranges the captains’ Zanpakuto into the word BLEACH and prints silhouettes of Ichigo and his companions on the back. That product-level detail is more useful than assuming every garment uses the same front print. Related reading: BLEACH: THE LAST PV, Episode 50 Date & Where to Watch.

How much does it cost, and how should you check sizing?

Prices and stock are listed by individual product, so a single collection-wide price can become misleading as listings change. When checked on October 25, Graniph’s Gotei 13 Shikai T-shirt page showed a price of ¥3,900 and sizes from SS through XXL, but marked that specific item sold out. This is a dated example, not a promise that other products have the same price or stock status.

Use each product page for the current size chart, material, care details, and inventory. For that T-shirt, Graniph describes a standard-weight cotton jersey, slight stretch, and a 100% cotton composition; its size table gives garment measurements rather than a body-size guarantee. Compare those measurements with a shirt you already own, and check the listing again before traveling to a shop because store inventory can differ from the online page.

Are preorders or purchase bonuses still available?

The advance online reservation window ran from October 14 at 12:00 a.m. through October 19 at 11:59 p.m. Japan Standard Time. Graniph advertised double membership points for reservations during that window. Both the reservation period and that points offer have ended; they should not be presented as current promotions. Related reading: d Anime Store Announces 81 Anime Titles for Fall 2026 in Japan.

The wider collection launched online and in Graniph’s Japanese shops on October 20. A temporary purchase limit also applied from October 14 through October 20 to caps, shoulder bags, mini shoulder bags, wallets, and charms, with a maximum of two per person in those categories. That dated limit has expired. Check the live listing or ask the shop about today’s availability and any new store-specific restrictions.

Can fans outside Japan order the collection?

The announcement confirms sales through Graniph’s online shop and domestic Japanese stores, but it does not provide a territory-by-territory shipping list or guarantee international delivery. Overseas buyers should set their location in Graniph’s official store and proceed only if checkout accepts their address. Before paying, confirm the displayed currency, delivery charge, import taxes, return terms, and whether the selected item can ship to the destination. A Japanese launch announcement by itself does not establish overseas availability.

Fans who cannot order directly can monitor the official product pages for restocks or ask a local retailer whether it has a legitimate regional arrangement. Treat resale listings as separate offers: the official collaboration page does not set their prices or guarantee their authenticity. Is this connected to a new BLEACH anime trailer?

No separate anime premiere is part of the Graniph announcement. The official collaboration links to the collection catalog, a special-photo gallery, and Graniph’s social accounts. It does not describe a new episode, cour, streaming platform, or anime trailer. The Shikai references explain the clothing designs; they are not evidence of new footage.

The anime itself is a separate story: the official Graniph description notes that the final Thousand-Year Blood War cour began airing in Japan on July 25, 2026. Fans searching for episode schedules or the series’ official PV should use the anime’s own news and broadcast pages, rather than treating this merchandise launch as a release update.
