New Wonder Voyage trailer spotlights the Treasures of Ruin

A new trailer for Pokémon Horizons: The Series puts four Legendary Pokémon from the Paldea region in the spotlight. Wo-Chien, Chien-Pao, Ting-Lu and Chi-Yu, collectively known as the Treasures of Ruin, appear in the latest preview for the anime's continuing Wonder Voyage storyline. Crunchyroll reported the trailer on October 9, 2026.

Who are the four Treasures of Ruin?

The quartet first appeared in the Pokémon Scarlet and Violet games. Wo-Chien is a Dark/Grass-type Pokémon, Chien-Pao is Dark/Ice, Ting-Lu is Dark/Ground, and Chi-Yu is Dark/Fire. The games associate the four with ancient objects and seals, providing background for why their appearance is important to a Paldea-centered story. The trailer's release does not by itself establish the outcome of their role in the anime.

Where does the trailer fit in Wonder Voyage?

The Wonder Voyage arc began airing in Japan on May 22, 2026. Its story continues the journey of Liko, Roy, Dot and Ult alongside the Rising Volt Tacklers. Earlier official promotional material introduced the arc's new adventures and characters; the October preview shifts attention toward the consequences of the Treasures of Ruin being unsealed. What does the new trailer leave unanswered?

The official promotional material shows the Treasures of Ruin entering the Wonder Voyage storyline, but it does not establish how the conflict will end or whether the four Pokémon will play equal roles. The October 16 Japanese story date is also not the same as the separately announced February 27, 2027 U.S. Netflix release for Wonders Ahead. Keeping those two schedules distinct helps avoid misleading international viewers.

When did the Paldea story begin?

The Japanese Paldea story began on October 16, 2026. TV Tokyo’s official listing records a 6:55–7:25 p.m. broadcast that day. As of this October 26 update, that date is in the past. It is a Japanese broadcast date, not a simultaneous worldwide streaming launch.

What the official episode synopsis establishes

TV Tokyo places the October 16 story at Orange Academy's school festival. Liko and her companions receive a request from Raifort to investigate unusual phenomena around Paldea. The incidents appear to be occurring near shrines associated with an ancient calamity. The synopsis also says Navie meets Team Star during the visit. These are details from the broadcaster's advance description, rather than a claim that the trailer reveals every event in the episode.

This makes the shrines the useful starting point for understanding the news. The story connects a familiar Paldea location with a mystery affecting several places across the region. It does not follow that the anime will reproduce the games' quest in exactly the same order. A synopsis can establish an investigation and its setting without confirming who resolves it, which Pokémon are caught or how the conflict ends. Wonder Voyage and Wonders Ahead use different release calendars

The Japanese arc name and the English season title should be kept distinct when checking availability. The Pokémon Company's October 6 international announcement names the fourth English season Pokémon Horizons: Season 4—Wonders Ahead and sets Part 1 for February 27, 2027, exclusively on Netflix in the United States. It also says further episodes will arrive later.

That announcement describes Liko, Roy and the Rising Volt Tacklers continuing their travels, with Dot, Ult, Navie and a stowaway Ditto among the characters mentioned. It provides a season-level introduction rather than a dated list for every Japanese episode. Readers should therefore avoid treating February 27 as a separately confirmed release date for this particular Treasures of Ruin story. The press release does not provide that episode-by-episode mapping.

Where the wider arc started

The official May 8 Japanese announcement sets the start of Wonder Voyage for May 22 and introduces its new adventures for Liko, Roy, Dot and Ult. Its promotional visual includes Navie, Ditto, Koraidon and Miraidon. That earlier announcement helps place the October trailer inside a continuing arc, rather than presenting it as the start of an unrelated Pokémon series.
