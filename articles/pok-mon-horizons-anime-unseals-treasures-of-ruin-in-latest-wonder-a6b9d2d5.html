New Wonder Voyage trailer spotlights the Treasures of Ruin

A new trailer for Pokémon Horizons: The Series puts four Legendary Pokémon from the Paldea region in the spotlight. Wo-Chien, Chien-Pao, Ting-Lu and Chi-Yu, collectively known as the Treasures of Ruin, appear in the latest preview for the anime's continuing Wonder Voyage storyline. Crunchyroll reported the trailer on October 9, 2026.

Who are the four Treasures of Ruin?

The quartet first appeared in the Pokémon Scarlet and Violet games. Wo-Chien is a Dark/Grass-type Pokémon, Chien-Pao is Dark/Ice, Ting-Lu is Dark/Ground, and Chi-Yu is Dark/Fire. The games associate the four with ancient objects and seals, providing background for why their appearance is important to a Paldea-centered story. The trailer's release does not by itself establish the outcome of their role in the anime.

Where does the trailer fit in Wonder Voyage?

The Wonder Voyage arc began airing in Japan on May 22, 2026. Its story continues the journey of Liko, Roy, Dot and Ult alongside the Rising Volt Tacklers. Earlier official promotional material introduced the arc's new adventures and characters; the October preview shifts attention toward the consequences of the Treasures of Ruin being unsealed. What does the new trailer leave unanswered?

The official promotional material shows the Treasures of Ruin entering the Wonder Voyage storyline, but it does not establish how the conflict will end or whether the four Pokémon will play equal roles. The October 16 Japanese story date is also not the same as the separately announced February 27, 2027 U.S. Netflix release for Wonders Ahead. Keeping those two schedules distinct helps avoid misleading international viewers.

When does the next story begin?

Japanese coverage of the new trailer identifies October 16, 2026, as the start of the upcoming Paldea-focused story. That is a Japanese broadcast date, not a confirmed worldwide streaming release date; it should not be confused with the separately announced U.S. Netflix schedule for Pokémon Horizons. Viewers outside Japan should check their regional Pokémon broadcaster or streaming service for availability rather than assume the same schedule.
