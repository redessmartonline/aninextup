When does Are You a Landmine, Chihara-san? premiere?

Are You a Landmine, Chihara-san? begins airing in Japan on the January 7, 2027 late-night broadcast slot (January 8 at 00:26 JST on a standard calendar). The official schedule places the series in MBS and TBS’s Super Animeism TURBO block, with a simultaneous broadcast across 28 stations. Japanese late-night schedules sometimes write this as Thursday at 24:26; it is the same slot as Friday at 12:26 a.m. by calendar time.

The official website lists the broadcast date and time, but its streaming section currently says “Coming soon.” No international platform or regional streaming date has been confirmed there.

What is the anime about?

English-language sources use both Are You a Landmine, Chihara-san? and the earlier rendering Are You a Jirai Girl, Chihara-san?. The English manga publisher Seven Seas uses the Landmine title; earlier anime coverage used the Jirai Girl rendering. AniNextUp keeps both names here to make the series easier to find. Related reading: Takopi's Original Sin: Thank You, See You Tomorrow Release Date.

The story follows high-school student Kuroki, who is seated next to Mai Chihara at the start of a new school term. Rumors and Chihara’s dark “jirai-kei” fashion make her seem intimidating. As Kuroki gets to know her, he finds that the girl behind the reputation is more unexpected—and often more awkward—than the rumors suggest. The official site describes it as a school comedy about the classmates’ changing impressions of each other.

Cast and staff

The official cast includes Sayumi Suzushiro as Mai Chihara, Kazuki Ura as Kuroki, Maaya Uchida as Chinatsu Iwakura, Tomokazu Sugita as Hibiya and Daisuke Ono as Kazami. Yasuaki Fujii directs, Keiichiro Ochi handles series composition, and SynergySP produces the animation. Related reading: Walking Home with You Anime.

How to read the Japanese broadcast time

The official website lists January 7, 2027 at 0:26 and names the MBS/TBS network of 28 stations. In Japan, programs after midnight are often described as a late-night slot attached to the preceding evening, which can make listings look like they disagree by one date. For a calendar entry using the civil date and clock time, the official page’s notation is January 7 at 00:26 JST. Viewers outside Japan should convert that timestamp to their own time zone rather than copying a “late night” label as a local date.

The site currently marks streaming information as coming soon. That is a real boundary on what this guide can promise: the Japanese television schedule is confirmed, while no international platform or regional release time has been named in the source reviewed here. A broadcaster’s 28-station network also does not mean the show is automatically available on a streaming service in those same markets.

What makes Mai and Kuroki’s dynamic the hook

The official synopsis begins with a class change and a seat beside Mai Chihara, whose appearance has made classmates call her dangerous. Kuroki’s first impression changes as he sees that her reserved look hides a more open, unpredictable and sometimes clumsy personality. The comedy grows from the distance between a rumor and the person sitting next to him, with their ordinary pauses and conversations doing as much work as the bigger gags.

The official cast and staff page names Sayumi Suzushiro as Mai and Kazuki Ura as Kuroki; it also credits SynergySP for animation.
