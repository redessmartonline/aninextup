When does the Season 2 English dub start?

The English dub of Reincarnated as a Sword Season 2 begins on Wednesday, October 21, 2026, on HIDIVE. HIDIVE’s October schedule lists new dubbed episodes on Wednesdays. The same schedule lists the subtitled simulcast separately, beginning September 30.
