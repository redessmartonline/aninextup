Nue's Exorcist Anime Release Date: Summer 2027, Cast & Trailer

Nue's Exorcist is confirmed for a summer 2027 TV broadcast on TV Asahi's nationwide network in Japan. Studio Hibari, three Japanese cast members and the official teaser are confirmed; an exact premiere day and international streaming service are not.

By AniNextUp Editorial Team · How we verify releases

RELEASE AT A GLANCETV anime · Summer 2027 · exact premiere date TBA

Japan: TV Asahi nationwide network · Studio Hibari · international streaming: TBA

Is Nue's Exorcist getting an anime?

Yes. Nue's Exorcist (Japanese title Nue no Onmyōji) is receiving a television anime scheduled for summer 2027 on TV Asahi's nationwide Japanese network. The announcement was made on October 12, 2026, when the production also released a teaser visual and the first promotional video. That confirms a season, broadcaster and broad broadcast window, but no exact premiere day, episode count or international streaming service has been announced. Readers searching for a specific date should treat “summer 2027” as the only confirmed schedule for now.

Who is making the anime, and who is in the cast?

Studio Hibari is producing the animation. Hiroyuki Hashimoto is directing; Hiroshi Seko is handling series composition; Shunpei Mochizuki is designing the characters; Takayuki Yamaguchi is sound director; and MONACA is composing the music.

The three initially named Japanese voice actors are Kaito Miura as Gakuro Yajima, Saori Ōnishi as Nue and Sayumi Suzushiro as Kazusa Suo. The teaser introduces the voices of Gakuro and Nue, while the official announcement presents character visuals for a wider group. More casting may be revealed later; at present, only those three roles are confirmed. No English-language cast or dub schedule has been announced.

What is the story of Nue's Exorcist?

Kota Kawae's manga follows Gakuro Yajima, a high-school student who has been able to see supernatural beings called “phantoms” since he was young. At his new school he discovers a room that other people cannot perceive. It is the home of Nue, a phantom with a fondness for games, who asks him to help deal with dangerous supernatural beings. Shueisha's official book description frames the premise as a modern onmyōji story built around Gakuro and Nue; the anime announcement describes it as a school dark fantasy about a boy's growth and supernatural battles.

That setup gives the adaptation two useful entry points: school relationships and a continuing supernatural-action conflict. It is still only a premise, however. The production has not published an episode-by-episode outline or said how much of the manga the first television season will adapt.

What does the official teaser reveal?

The official teaser is a 45-second promotional video linked from the anime's own news page. Shueisha DeNA Projects describes it as using MONACA's main theme and showing how Gakuro gains power through meeting Nue; it also introduces the first character voices. The footage is useful for hearing the leads and seeing the adaptation's initial presentation, but it is not a full story trailer and does not confirm a streaming platform or premiere date.

The original YouTube page's published metadata gives the upload time as October 12, 2026 at 4:30:33 a.m. PDT (UTC−07:00). This is the video upload time, not the time of a separate news article. The official anime site identifies the same video as its special teaser PV. The video's presence on an official channel makes the timestamp specific to this trailer, rather than an estimate taken from a press report.

When in summer 2027 will it premiere?

“Summer 2027” is the Japanese broadcast season, not a confirmed June, July or August date. The official announcement says TV Asahi's nationwide network, but does not list a weekday, time slot or first episode date. Until a schedule notice names a day and time, calendar dates circulating without an official citation remain speculation.

The TV Asahi announcement also does not name an overseas streaming partner. International viewers should wait for a separate distributor notice before assuming availability.

Should new viewers read the manga first?

No anime prerequisite has been announced. The adaptation is being introduced as its own television project, and the official pages do not tell viewers to finish a particular manga arc before watching. If you want to sample the source, Shueisha's official Jump+ page for chapter one records its May 17, 2023 publication date and summarizes Gakuro's first meeting with Nue. Shueisha lists the first collected volume as released on October 4, 2023, with 192 pages.

Reading the opening material can clarify the series' terminology and the school setting, but the published anime announcement does not say whether the adaptation will reproduce the manga scene for scene. The official pages have not yet announced a manga endpoint for the season, so recommendations such as “read through volume X first” would be guesswork.

What details are still unconfirmed?

The confirmed picture is fairly specific about the project's starting team: summer 2027 in Japan, TV Asahi nationwide broadcast, Studio Hibari, named director and core staff, three cast roles, official key art and a teaser PV. The unanswered practical questions are the exact premiere date, episode total, full cast, staff additions, international streaming, subtitles, English dub and how many manga chapters the season will cover.

For updates, the production's official anime site and its notices are the primary sources. Its October 12 posts separately confirm the adaptation and teaser visual, publish the teaser PV, and link the Jump PRESS archive. Shueisha DeNA Projects' announcement provides the staff, cast and production context, while Shonen Jump+ and Shueisha's book catalogue are useful for checking the manga's publication details. AniNextUp will update this guide when the production confirms a dated premiere or distribution partner.
