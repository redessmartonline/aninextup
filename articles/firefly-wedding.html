Firefly Wedding Anime Release Date, Streaming & Where to Watch

Firefly Wedding premieres October 9, 2026 on Crunchyroll.

Why the premise is more than a romance setup

Satoko's proposal is made under immediate threat, which means the relationship begins as a survival decision rather than a conventional courtship. That tension is central to the story's appeal: readers can expect the romance to develop alongside danger and uncertainty. For release planning, the Fuji TV and Crunchyroll times should be read as separate official schedules, not converted into one another.

Release date

Firefly Wedding premieres October 9, 2026. The Japanese broadcast begins at 11:30 p.m. JST on Fuji TV’s Noitamina block, while Crunchyroll has confirmed streaming in its announced territories as the anime airs.

Japanese broadcast vs. Crunchyroll release

Fuji TV's Noitamina block lists the Japanese television premiere for 11:30 p.m. JST. Crunchyroll separately announced an October 9 simulcast at 9:00 a.m. PT. These are platform-specific schedules, not interchangeable time-zone conversions; viewers should check their local Crunchyroll catalog for availability. Animation is handled by david production.

Is the manga complete, and where can you read it?

Yes. Shogakukan released volume 13, the final collected volume of Oreco Tachibana’s manga, on October 19, 2026. The publisher describes it as the story’s conclusion and notes that it includes a side story and a short bonus manga. Readers looking for the complete ending can follow the manga through that volume. The anime’s confirmed cast, broadcast and streaming announcements do not say how much of the source story the television adaptation will cover. Its episode count and adaptation endpoint have not been announced, so the manga’s completion should not be mistaken for confirmation that the anime will adapt all of it.

For English-language readers, VIZ’s Shojo Beat edition is still being released in collected volumes. VIZ lists volume 8 for October 13, 2026, and volume 9 as a preorder, so the English schedule trails the completed Japanese edition. Check VIZ’s official series page for current print and digital availability, and Shogakukan’s volume 13 listing for the Japanese finale. Dates and formats can differ by market and retailer. What is Firefly Wedding about?

The story follows Satoko, a noblewoman in poor health who becomes the target of an assassin. Trying to survive, she makes an extraordinary marriage proposal to Shinpei, turning a kidnapping into a dangerous relationship built around a false wedding and increasingly complicated feelings.

Studio and staff

david production is producing the anime. Takahiro Kamei directs, Yuko Kakihara handles series composition and scripts, Yukiko Aikei is credited for character designs, and NOGRID composes the music.

English dub release and cast

Crunchyroll announced the English dub on October 22, with weekly episodes beginning Friday, October 23. Its first-episode cast includes Rowan Gilvie as Satoko, Kieran Flitton as Shinpei, Jason Douglas as Mitsueda, Ben Stegmair as Kotaro, R. Bruce Elliott as Jubei, Jordan Colea as Miwako and Chris Rager as Ozoe. These are English-language performance credits; they should be kept separate from the Japanese voice cast listed in the anime’s earlier production announcements.

The same Crunchyroll notice names Samantha Herek as producer and Caitlin Glass as voice director. Those credits identify the English dub team, while the Japanese production remains at david production under director Takahiro Kamei. A localized cast announcement does not change the Japanese broadcast schedule or the original-language simulcast date.

What time is the simulcast in the Caribbean?

The published times convert to different local clocks even though both announcements give October 9. Fuji TV’s 11:30 p.m. JST broadcast corresponds to 10:30 a.m. Atlantic Standard Time (UTC−4) that same day. Crunchyroll’s 9:00 a.m. Pacific Time simulcast corresponds to noon AST for viewers in Puerto Rico or the Dominican Republic, if the series is available in their local catalog. Pacific Time is on daylight saving time (PDT, UTC−7) on October 9, so do not reuse that conversion in winter without checking the offset. The English dub has its own October 23 weekly start; use the episode’s language label to choose the version you want.
