Release date

Firefly Wedding premieres October 9, 2026. The Japanese broadcast begins at 11:30 p.m. JST on Fuji TV’s Noitamina block, while Crunchyroll has confirmed worldwide streaming as the anime airs.

When does the Firefly Wedding anime premiere?

Firefly Wedding premieres October 9, 2026 as part of Crunchyroll's Fall 2026 lineup. The anime is produced by david production.

What is Firefly Wedding about?

The story follows Satoko, a noblewoman in poor health who becomes the target of an assassin. Trying to survive, she makes an extraordinary marriage proposal to Shinpei, turning a kidnapping into a dangerous relationship built around a false wedding and increasingly complicated feelings.

Studio and staff

david production is producing the anime. Takahiro Kamei directs, Yuko Kakihara handles series composition and scripts, Yukiko Aikei is credited for character designs, and NOGRID composes the music.
