Dr. STONE TERRAFORMING: Story, Trailer and Release Status

Dr. STONE TERRAFORMING is confirmed as a new anime continuing after the TV finale and adapting the manga’s Volume 27 epilogue. Its release date, format and streaming platform have not been announced.

Official production announcement

Dr. STONE TERRAFORMING is a newly announced anime project that continues the story after the television series finale. The official anime website announced production on October 10, 2026, and confirmed that the project adapts the “TERRAFORMING” sequel episode included in Volume 27 of Riichiro Inagaki and Boichi’s manga. The announcement does not give a release date, format, staff list, cast list or streaming service.

What is Dr. STONE TERRAFORMING?

The project is an anime adaptation of a post-finale story from the original manga. The official production notice describes it as a continuation of the TV anime’s story and says the “TERRAFORMING” episode appears in manga Volume 27. That distinction answers the main search question: this is new material set after the story already adapted for television, rather than an announcement that the existing episodes will be remade.

The production has chosen the title Dr. STONE TERRAFORMING. The official announcement calls it a new anime and a continuation, but does not identify it as a fifth season, movie, special, OVA or television series. Those labels describe different formats, so using one before the staff confirms it would give viewers a false expectation about length or how it will be released. How does it fit after the Dr. STONE finale?

The official website notes that the TV anime series reached its final episode on June 25, 2026, and says TERRAFORMING depicts the story’s continuation. The source material is the sequel episode in Volume 27, which follows the original manga’s main ending. In other words, the announcement points to a continuation beyond the story already adapted for television.

Writer Riichiro Inagaki says in the official announcement that the anime had reached the manga’s final chapter, then celebrates the fact that Senku’s scientific adventure still has more story to tell. Illustrator Boichi also thanks the audience and welcomes the new adaptation. Their comments explain why this is a distinct post-finale update: the main manga ending was adapted, while Volume 27 also contains an additional sequel episode.

The official notice has not published a full synopsis for the new anime. It confirms the source chapter and that the story continues, but does not explain which events will be expanded, how many chapters the adaptation will cover, or whether the anime will add original scenes. Viewers can safely expect a further chapter in Senku and the Kingdom of Science’s adventure; finer plot predictions should wait for an official story page. Is this Dr. STONE Season 5?

Not according to the project’s confirmed branding. The production uses the distinct title Dr. STONE TERRAFORMING and describes a continuation anime, without calling it Season 5 or specifying how it connects to the earlier season numbering. Fans may use “Season 5” as shorthand while discussing the project, but it is not an official format or premiere label at this stage.

This matters for release searches. A new anime project can be a television season, a film, a special or another format, and the announcement has not settled that question. Until a format is named, there is no reliable episode count, theatrical date or weekly schedule to report.

Who is returning, and where can viewers watch it?

No cast or staff list for TERRAFORMING has been announced in the production notice. Iwasa’s credit on the celebration illustration does not establish her role on the new anime, and the previous TV staff should not automatically be treated as confirmed for this project. The same caution applies to the voice cast: the trailer uses Senku’s voice, but the official announcement does not publish a complete cast roster.
