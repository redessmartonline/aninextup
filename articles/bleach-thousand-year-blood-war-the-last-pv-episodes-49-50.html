BLEACH: THE LAST PV, Episode 50 Date & Where to Watch

What is BLEACH: THE LAST PV?

BLEACH: Thousand-Year Blood War released “THE LAST PV” on October 16, 2026. It is a promotional video for the closing stretch of the television adaptation, not an announcement of another season. The official anime site presents it alongside the final two episode dates: episode 49 and episode 50. The subtitle “The Calamity” is used for the final part in VIZ’s viewing guide, while the Japanese broadcast page identifies the numbered episodes and their Japanese TV times.

The original video is on the official BLEACH anime channel and is linked from the anime website’s October 16 notice. YouTube’s published metadata records its upload at 4:00:38 a.m. PDT (UTC−07:00) on October 16, or 11:00:38 UTC. This is the video’s timestamp, not an estimate from the article’s date. The PV runs 1 minute 42 seconds on the official video page. It promotes the final conflict without changing the confirmed broadcast schedule.

When do episodes 49 and 50 air in Japan?

The official Japan broadcast schedule lists episode 49 for Monday, October 19, 2026 at 24:00 JST on the TV Tokyo network. Japanese schedules sometimes use 24:00 to mean midnight at the end of the named date: viewers should read this slot as the start of Tuesday, October 20. Episode 50 is listed for Monday, October 26 at 24:00 JST, which is midnight at the start of Tuesday, October 27. BS TV Tokyo’s corresponding slots are October 20 and 27 at 24:30 JST.

That distinction matters when searching for “BLEACH episode 50 release date.” The date printed beside the Japanese weekday is October 26, but the clock crosses into October 27. If a local guide converts the slot to a different calendar date, it may be expressing the same broadcast time rather than reporting a postponement. The official page also notes that other Japanese stations publish their own schedules, so the TV Tokyo listing should not be assumed to cover every channel. Where can viewers stream the final two subtitled episodes?

For the United States, VIZ says episodes 49 and 50 stream on Hulu on Disney+ at 8:30 a.m. PDT on October 19 and October 26, respectively. VIZ’s guide lists Disney+ internationally for subtitles in English, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, Italian, German and French. Platform catalogs can differ by territory, so “Disney+ internationally” does not guarantee that every language or episode appears at the same local time in every country.

As of October 25, episode 49’s scheduled subtitled release date has passed. Episode 50 is due the next day on the schedule published by VIZ. Check the service available in your country and its episode listing if the title does not appear at the U.S. time; AniNextUp cannot infer regional access from a Japan broadcast listing.

When do the English and other dubs arrive?

The dub schedule is separate from the subtitled release. VIZ lists the English and Spanish Hulu dubs of episode 49 for Monday, November 23, at 8:30 a.m. PDT and episode 50 for Monday, November 30, at the same time. For Disney+, VIZ lists English, Spanish, Portuguese and German dubs on those November dates, with French dubs scheduled for December 14 and 21. VIZ describes the Disney+ availability as applying in select international markets; check the local catalog rather than assuming all language tracks are offered in each country.
