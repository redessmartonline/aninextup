BLEACH: Thousand-Year Blood War Releases “THE LAST PV”

The official anime website has released “THE LAST PV” for BLEACH: Thousand-Year Blood War. Episodes 49 and 50 have confirmed broadcast dates in Japan, with separate subtitled and dubbed streaming schedules for other regions.

By AniNextUp Editorial Team · Editorial policy

“THE LAST PV” is now available

The official BLEACH: Thousand-Year Blood War website posted the new video on October 16 under the title “THE LAST PV.” The page embeds the promotional video and does not announce a new season or a different release window. The series has already listed broadcast information for episodes 49 and 50, the next two installments in its final part.

Watch “THE LAST PV” on YouTube if the embedded player is unavailable. The video is embedded from the player linked on the official anime announcement.

When do episodes 49 and 50 air in Japan?

The official schedule lists episode 49 on the TV Tokyo network for Monday, October 19 at 24:00 JST, and episode 50 for Monday, October 26 at 24:00 JST. Because the schedule uses a 24:00 broadcast label, each slot falls at midnight on the following calendar day: October 20 and October 27, respectively. BS TV Tokyo lists the episodes for October 20 and 27 at 24:30 JST. Other Japanese stations have their own dates and times.

For Japan’s subscription and transactional video services, the official site lists episode 49 from October 20 at 12:00 noon JST and episode 50 from October 27 at 12:00 noon JST, with services rolling episodes out in sequence. The same page lists a later ad-supported release window: October 25 for episode 49 and November 1 for episode 50.

Where can viewers stream the subtitled episodes?

VIZ lists the subtitled episode schedule separately from Japan’s broadcast timetable. In the United States, episodes 49 and 50 are scheduled for Monday, October 19 and Monday, October 26 at 8:30 a.m. PDT on Hulu on Disney+. VIZ says Disney+ carries the subtitled series internationally. For viewers in the Dominican Republic, those listed times correspond to 11:30 a.m. local time on both dates.

When do the dubbed episodes arrive?

The dubbed versions follow a later schedule. VIZ lists English and Spanish episode 49 for November 23 and episode 50 for November 30 on Hulu in the U.S.; Disney+ lists the same dates for its English, Spanish, Portuguese and German dubs in supported international markets. French-dubbed episodes are scheduled for December 14 and 21. Availability and language options vary by country, so check the local service listing before watching.
