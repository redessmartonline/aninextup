When did Black Clover Season 2 premiere?

Black Clover Season 2 premiered in Japan on Saturday, October 3, 2026, at 11:00 p.m. JST on TV Tokyo and affiliated stations. The official broadcast page also lists BS TV Tokyo on October 7 at midnight. These are Japanese television times; a service in another country can publish an episode on a different local date or hour.

Crunchyroll’s Fall 2026 schedule lists subtitled episodes on Saturdays at 8:00 a.m. Pacific Time in the territories where it carries the series. The first episode became available there on October 3. On October 24, the anime’s official story page listed episode 4, numbered episode 174 in the overall series. That is the current episode as of this guide’s check date, not a forecast for later weeks. Check your own Crunchyroll catalog for regional rights, subtitle options and any schedule notice before watching.

Why is the new season on episode 174?

The original television anime ran for 170 episodes. The current season continues that count: its first four listed installments are episodes 171, 172, 173 and 174. Some platforms or fan databases may label them Season 2 episodes 1–4, while the Japanese production site uses the continuing story numbers. Both labels can refer to the same episode, so look for the title and release date as well as the number when searching.

The season is a continuation, not a reboot or a recap. Crunchyroll’s synopsis places Asta and the Magic Knights in a raid on the Spade Kingdom as they try to save their captains from the Dark Triad. New viewers will get more from that conflict if they first see the earlier anime, which introduces the squads, rivalries and magic system that drive the stakes. Where can you stream it?

Crunchyroll is the international option listed for supported territories across North and South America, Europe, Africa, Oceania, the Middle East, the CIS, the Indian subcontinent and Southeast Asia. This regional list does not mean the show is available in every country; licensing can differ, so check the service while signed into your location.

The Japanese portal lists a separate domestic rollout. Anime Times, Lemino, U-NEXT and Anime Hodai began advance streaming on October 4 at noon JST. Other Japanese services began rolling out episodes from October 8 at noon, with additional free and rental listings. These details describe Japan’s platforms; they do not establish access outside Japan. The TV Tokyo broadcast, Japanese streaming windows and Crunchyroll’s international schedule are separate release clocks.

Is the English dub available?

Yes. Crunchyroll announced the Season 2 English dub with its first episode on October 17, followed by weekly dubbed episodes. The dub is a separate track and schedule from the Saturday subtitled simulcast; do not assume both versions appear at the same time. The initial English dub cast includes Dallas Reid as Asta, Micah Solusod as Yuno and Bryce Papenbrook as Liebe. Check the show’s episode selector to see which language tracks are available in your region. Related reading: Dragon Ball Super: Beerus Episode Schedule & Where to Watch.

What are the story and production details?

Season 2 moves into the conflict with the Spade Kingdom and the Dark Triad, while Asta’s anti-magic and the Magic Knights’ teamwork remain central to the story. The official synopsis is a useful spoiler-light guide: the mission is to rescue captured captains. The episode list currently shows four entries, but the production site has not announced a total episode count in the material checked for this update. Treat claims about a 26-episode or split-cour run as unconfirmed unless the anime’s official channels publish them.

Studio Pierrot continues the animation. Ayataka Tanemura directs, Keiichiro Ochi handles series composition, Itsuko Takeda designs the characters and Minako Seki composes the music. The official music page credits WANIMA with the opening theme “消えない理由” and ONE OR EIGHT with the ending theme “Yellow Brick Road.” These details help distinguish confirmed production information from speculation about how much manga material the season will adapt.

The trailer gives a look at the return without confirming the season’s total episode count. Use the linked official video rather than reuploads, which may have different dates or regional edits.
