Where the new season fits

Black Clover Season 2 premiered on October 3, 2026. The official Japanese site lists the new television season for Saturdays at 11:00 p.m. JST on the TV Tokyo network and other stations.

Black Clover Season 2 release date and time

The new season began Saturday, October 3 at 23:00 JST in Japan. This return brings the television anime back with a new run following the earlier Black Clover series and the franchise's subsequent movie.

Where to watch

Crunchyroll is carrying the season in supported international regions, with subtitled episodes listed on Saturdays at 8:00 a.m. Pacific Time. Streaming availability, subtitles, dubs and episode-release times can vary by country, so the exact listing should be checked in the viewer's regional catalog.

What to expect from the new season

Asta and the Magic Knights return as the story moves deeper into the conflicts surrounding the Clover and Spade kingdoms. The official Season 2 campaign includes a new promotional video and updated character material, positioning the return as a major continuation rather than a recap project.

What the weekly Japanese schedule confirms

The official television plan places new episodes on Saturdays in Japan. That establishes a recurring broadcast slot, but it does not guarantee that every international Crunchyroll territory receives the same episode at the identical local hour. Viewers following the new run should check the service's episode page for their region.

Where should returning viewers start?

This is a continuation of the television story, so the earlier series provides the clearest introduction to Asta, the Magic Knights and the kingdom conflicts. The new season's October 3 start date does not make it a standalone remake. Viewers returning after a break should check which earlier episodes their local service carries, since the presence of the new season in a regional catalog does not establish the availability of every previous installment or dub track.
