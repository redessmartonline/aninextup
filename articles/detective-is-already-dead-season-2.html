What returning viewers should remember

Kimihiko's choices in the sequel follow the discoveries surrounding Siesta and the detective's legacy. The central question is not merely who will solve the next case, but how the characters act after learning what they know. That makes the first season's revelations relevant context, without assuming the second season's mysteries have already been resolved.

Why the sequel is not a reset

The second season continues a mystery shaped by the events of the first, including the consequences of Siesta's story. New viewers may understand the premise from a synopsis, but the emotional weight of Kimihiko's decisions depends on earlier revelations. For that reason, this is better approached as a direct continuation than as a standalone detective adventure.

When does The Detective Is Already Dead Season 2 premiere?

The Detective Is Already Dead Season 2 is scheduled for October 7, 2026. The announced streaming platform is Crunchyroll.

Episode 1 release time

Episode 1 premieres October 7 on Crunchyroll. Crunchyroll lists the subtitled simulcast on Wednesdays at 6:30 a.m. PT. Check the local episode listing for regional availability and schedule changes.

How to check the release time

Crunchyroll's published Wednesday 6:30 a.m. Pacific Time listing is a streaming schedule, not the Japanese television broadcast time. Viewers should check the episode page for their region and account for daylight-saving changes when converting that time locally. The premiere date and streaming hour answer different questions; neither guarantees that every territory receives the episode at an identical local clock time.

What is Season 2 about?

Season 2 continues Kimihiko Kimizuka’s story after the truth surrounding Siesta’s death has come into focus. The official introduction makes clear that the mystery is not treated as an ending: Kimihiko and the people around him still have to confront what it means to inherit the legendary detective’s will.

Staff and music

ENGI returns as the animation studio. Manabu Kurihara directs, Deko Akao handles series composition, and Yosuke Ito is credited for character design. The opening theme is “Kono Shinzō ni Hanataba o” by Koume, while Kagura Nana performs the ending theme “Hakuchūmu.”

Where can you watch it?

Crunchyroll lists the season for North America, Central America, South America, Europe, Africa, Oceania, the Middle East, CIS and Southeast Asia. Crunchyroll lists subtitled episodes on Wednesdays at 6:30 a.m. PT.
