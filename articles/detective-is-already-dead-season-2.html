Has The Detective Is Already Dead Season 2 premiered?

Yes. Season 2 began its Japanese television run on Wednesday, October 7, 2026. The official anime site lists a weekly Wednesday broadcast, and by October 24 it had posted the preview and story summary for episode 3. If you are searching for the release date today, the useful answer is that the season is already airing in Japan, rather than still being an upcoming Fall 2026 title. Catch-up availability depends on the streaming service and territory.

What time does each episode air in Japan?

The official schedule lists TOKYO MX and other stations from 10:30 p.m. Japan Standard Time every Wednesday. BS Nippon TV's listed slot is 12:30 a.m. Thursday, while AT-X is scheduled for 9:30 p.m. Wednesday. Those are separate Japanese broadcast slots, not one universal streaming time. For viewers comparing clocks, 10:30 p.m. JST on October 7 corresponded to 6:30 a.m. Pacific Daylight Time and 9:30 a.m. Eastern Daylight Time on October 7.

Where can viewers stream Season 2?

For Japan, the official On Air page says d Anime Store carries the first, fastest streaming slot at 10:00 p.m. JST on Wednesdays, ahead of television. Other services start on Monday, October 12, with weekly releases from 10:00 p.m. JST; the official list includes U-NEXT, ABEMA, Disney+ Japan, Hulu Japan, Prime Video Japan and other domestic platforms. Crunchyroll separately announced the series as part of its Fall 2026 lineup, but streaming rights and episode availability vary by territory. What is the story about?

The series follows Kimihiko Kimizuka, a boy who becomes the assistant to Siesta, a legendary detective he meets aboard a hijacked plane at 10,000 meters. They travel and investigate cases together for three years before Siesta dies. A year later, Nagisa Natsunagi asks Kimihiko for help, bringing him back into detective work and toward the truth about Siesta. The official introduction stresses that solving the mystery of her death does not finish the story: Kimihiko and his companions still have to understand the detective's mistakes and what inheriting her will demands.

Do you need to watch Season 1 first?

Season 2 is a direct continuation, so starting with Season 1 gives the character relationships and Siesta's legacy their intended context. The first season establishes why Kimihiko resists becoming involved in new cases and why Siesta's presence remains central even after her death. A recap can explain the setup, but it cannot reproduce the timing of the earlier reveals. Viewers who want to avoid spoilers should watch the original 12-episode season before moving to the sequel; the official Season 2 synopsis already refers to the mystery surrounding Siesta's death as something the group has uncovered. What new characters and cast members are confirmed?

The official cast list continues with Shū Nagai as Kimihiko, Saki Miyashita as Siesta and Ayana Taketatsu as Nagisa, alongside Kanon Takao as Yui, Saori Shirahama as Charlotte, Mai Fuchigami as Fuubi and others. New additions announced with the third promotional video include Akari Komiyama as Mia Whitlock and Nobuhiko Okamoto as Scarlet. Their introduction widens the group around Kimihiko; the production's announcement identifies them as new cast members but does not frame the update as a replacement of the returning leads.

Who makes the anime, and what are the theme songs?

ENGI handles animation production. Manabu Kurihara directs, Deko Akao is responsible for series composition and scripts, and Yosuke Ito designs the characters. Music is credited to 40mP and Yuyu. The opening theme is “Kono Shinzō ni Hanataba o,” performed by Koume, and Kagura Nana performs the ending, “Hakuchūmu.” The third PV previews parts of both songs, making it a useful first listen without requiring viewers to treat a promotional cut as a full track release.

What changed from the original July plan?

Season 2 was first expected in July 2026. The production committee later postponed the broadcast to October, citing production circumstances. The official notice does not give a more detailed cause, so it is better to report the delay as stated instead of speculating about staffing, schedule conflicts or animation quality. The later September announcement then fixed the exact Japanese start date as October 7 and confirmed the weekly time and additional cast.
