The Record of a Fallen Vampire Anime Announced for 2027

The Record of a Fallen Vampire is officially getting a TV anime in 2027. The announcement includes a teaser PV and visual, the two lead cast members and the main production staff.

By AniNextUp Editorial Team · How we verify releases

RELEASE WINDOW2027 · EXACT DATE TBA

How to interpret the 2027 announcement

A confirmed year is not the same as a confirmed season or month. The announced cast and creative staff provide concrete information about the adaptation's direction, but they cannot be used to calculate a premiere date. Until the official production names a broadcast window, a specific day or streaming service would be speculation.

When does The Record of a Fallen Vampire anime release?

The TV anime is officially scheduled for 2027. A month and exact premiere date have not been announced, so any more specific date should be treated as unconfirmed for now.

Who is making the anime?

Mankyu is directing and handling series composition. ZERO-G and Liber are producing the animation, jimao is responsible for character design, and Evan Call is composing the music.

Confirmed cast

Yuma Uchida voices Akabara “Red Rose” Strauss, while Aino Shimada voices Bridget Irving Frostheart.

What is The Record of a Fallen Vampire about?

The dark-fantasy story follows vampire king Akabara Strauss, who lost his kingdom and continues searching for his sealed queen while being pursued by dhampirs. His long quest becomes even more dangerous with the appearance of the Black Swan, an entity capable of destroying both the king and queen.

What the premise suggests about the story

Akabara Strauss's search for his sealed queen gives the conflict a personal purpose, while the pursuit by dhampirs and the threat of the Black Swan introduce competing dangers. This is AniNextUp's interpretation of the announced synopsis, not confirmation of events beyond it.

What the announced staff credits mean

Mankyu is credited with both directing and series composition, identifying responsibility for the production's overall direction and story structure. ZERO-G and Liber are named as animation studios, but the announcement does not explain how they will divide the work. Evan Call's composer credit does not establish a soundtrack release date.

Where will it stream?

No international streaming platform has been officially confirmed yet. AniNextUp will update this guide when a distributor announces streaming territories.

What to expect from the next announcement

The production has named its lead cast, studios and staff, but has not supplied a broadcast month, episode count or streaming partner. The next update may clarify the schedule or distribution; none of those details can be inferred from the teaser video alone.
