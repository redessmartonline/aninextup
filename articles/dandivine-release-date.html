When does Dandivine premiere?

Dandivine premieres in Japan on October 7, 2026. The official Aniplex schedule lists TOKYO MX, BS11, Gunma TV and Tochigi TV on October 7 at 24:30 JST, equivalent to October 8 at 12:30 a.m. JST in ordinary calendar notation, with additional broadcasts on Chukyo TV, MBS and AT-X. The production's October 6 notice changes MBS Episode 1 from October 10 at 26:08 to 26:58, equivalent to October 11 at 2:58 a.m. JST, a 50-minute delay. This exception does not replace the regular slot for later episodes.

What is Dandivine about?

Twenty years after the combining robot Dandivine defeated the mysterious Aposdoll, the world has returned to peace. When the enemy appears again in Tokyo, high school student Gekiha Shijima becomes connected to the legendary machine and a new battle begins.

Is Dandivine a sequel to an older anime?

The official description presents Dandivine as the first title in the “God of Combined” project, with an in-universe history reaching back twenty years. That backstory is part of the new series' premise, not evidence that viewers must find a separate earlier television season. Newcomers can begin with this production and follow how Gekiha responds to the return of Aposdoll. The announcement does not establish the outcome of the renewed conflict.

Who is making the anime?

Dandivine is the first title in the “God of Combined” project, built around the culture of combining robots and developed with merchandise and animation planned together. Aniplex lists LIDENFILMS on the project, with the series positioned as an original mecha production.
