Dandivine Release Date & Where to Watch

Dandivine premieres October 7, 2026.

RELEASEOctober 7, 2026
STREAMINGCrunchyroll
STUDIOLIDEN FILMS / KAYAC ANIMATION

When does Dandivine premiere?

Dandivine premieres in Japan on October 7, 2026. The official Aniplex schedule lists TOKYO MX, BS11, Gunma TV and Tochigi TV at 24:30 JST, with additional broadcasts on Chukyo TV, MBS and AT-X.

Where to watch Dandivine

In Japan, d Anime Store begins the fastest streaming release alongside the October 7 broadcast, with other services following progressively. Crunchyroll has also included Dandivine in its Fall 2026 international lineup, although availability can differ by territory.

What is Dandivine about?

Twenty years after the combining robot Dandivine defeated the mysterious Aposdoll, the world has returned to peace. When the enemy appears again in Tokyo, high school student Gekiha Shijima becomes connected to the legendary machine and a new battle begins.

Who is making the anime?

Dandivine is the first title in the “God of Combined” project, built around the culture of combining robots and developed with merchandise and animation planned together. Aniplex lists LIDENFILMS on the project, with the series positioned as an original mecha production.
