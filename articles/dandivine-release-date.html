When did Dandivine premiere?

Dandivine began airing in Japan on October 7, 2026. It is an original television mecha anime from Good Smile Company and LIDENFILMS, with animation production by LIDENFILMS and KAYAC Animation. Because that Japanese premiere date has passed, this is now a viewing guide for a series already in its weekly broadcast run, rather than a future release announcement. The official schedule lists several Japanese broadcasters and domestic streaming services, while international availability depends on the platform and territory.

What time does Dandivine air in Japan?

The official anime schedule lists TOKYO MX, BS11, Gunma TV and Tochigi TV on Wednesdays at 24:30 JST from October 7. Japanese listings use the broadcast day's date even when a late-night slot crosses midnight: 24:30 Wednesday is 12:30 a.m. on Thursday, October 8 by ordinary calendar notation. Chukyo TV's Wednesday 26:32 slot is 2:32 a.m. Thursday, and MBS's Saturday 26:08 slot is 2:08 a.m. Sunday. AT-X is listed for Fridays at 20:30 JST. These are Japanese station times, not a promise of a matching release time elsewhere.

There have also been schedule exceptions. Good Smile Company's October 6 announcement gave MBS's first episode a 26:08 slot, and the production's separate notice moved that first broadcast back by 50 minutes, to 26:58. The official site now lists the regular Saturday slot as 26:08 and, on October 22, posted a separate time-change notice for Episode 3 on MBS. Check the current station notice before planning around an MBS broadcast; do not apply a one-episode change to the whole season. Where can viewers watch Dandivine?

For Japan, the official site names d Anime Store as the simultaneous and fastest service. Its schedule also lists later distribution through services including Netflix, ABEMA, U-NEXT, Prime Video, Lemino, DMM TV, Hulu and others. “Later distribution” does not mean every service launches at the same hour, and the official page warns that streaming times can change. Check the title page in the service you use for the latest local listing.

Crunchyroll included Dandivine in its Fall 2026 seasonal lineup for viewers in its licensed territories. Availability and episode timing can vary by country, so a Japanese TV schedule should not be converted directly into a North American or European simulcast time. The Japanese production site is the clearest source for domestic station and streaming details; the relevant streaming service is the source to check for regional access, subtitles and any dub options.

What is the story about?

The official premise begins twenty years after the combining robot Dandivine defeated the mysterious enemy Aposdoll. Peace followed, but the toy manufacturer Kasuga, which became famous by turning Dandivine into a hit toy line, has declined after relying too heavily on that one success. Its president, Genjuro Shijima, longs for Dandivine's return strongly enough to welcome the disruption of peace. Aposdoll then appears in Tokyo again, sending a population that has known two decades without an attack into panic. Related reading: d Anime Store Announces 81 Anime Titles for Fall 2026 in Japan.

High-school student Gekiha Shijima enters the robot after his uncle Yusaku Kuroiwa, a former main pilot, brings him into the conflict. That setup gives the show two connected questions beyond “who wins the next fight?”: what Aposdoll wants now, and whether Kasuga can regain its former dominance in the toy industry. The combination-robot premise places the machine itself at the center of both the action and the story's commercial history. The official introduction establishes those questions, but does not disclose their answers, so they should not be treated as spoilers or confirmed outcomes.

Who makes the series, and who is in the cast?

Good Smile Company and LIDENFILMS are credited as the original creators; LIDENFILMS and KAYAC Animation handle animation production. Yi Cao directs, Toshiki Inoue oversees series composition and scripts, and mechanical design is credited to An (GOD BRAVE STUDIO). The principal cast includes Takuma Terashima as Gekiha, Haruka Shiraishi as Akane Kuzumi, Saya Aizawa as Kirei Shijima, Katsuyuki Konishi as Yusaku, and Atsushi Ono as Genjuro. The full official cast list also names Nao Toyama, Aya Yamane, Misaki Kuno, Asuna Tomari, Ryotaro Okiayu and Ruriko Aoki.

The theme-song credits offer another point of reference for the promotional video: Takanori Nishikawa performs the opening song “Zankyo ≠ Blaster,” while DayRe: performs the ending “Mikansei Ordinary.” Sony Music's September 12 announcement links the official main PV and identifies Nishikawa's song as the opening theme. That gives viewers a reliable way to distinguish the main promotional video from fan edits or clips that reuse its footage. Is Dandivine a sequel, and how many episodes are planned?

The official description presents Dandivine as an original project with a twenty-year in-story history, not as a continuation that requires watching an older television season. The premise supplies the relevant background: the earlier victory over Aposdoll and Dandivine's former pilot are part of this show's setup. As of October 25, 2026, the official pages checked for this update do not state a total episode count. Viewers should rely on new production announcements for that detail rather than assuming a standard cour length.
