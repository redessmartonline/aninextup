Hirayasumi Anime Adds Five Cast Members Ahead of January 2027 Premiere

The slice-of-life anime Hirayasumi has unveiled five more voice cast members. Its Japanese TV premiere remains scheduled for January 2027 on NHK General TV.

Five additional cast members confirmed

On October 13, 2026, the official Hirayasumi anime website announced five more performers and added cast comments and character profiles. Daisuke Namikawa voices Hideki Noguchi, Yoko Hikasa voices Yomogi Tachibana, Yume Miyamoto voices Akari Yokoyama and Yuichiro Umehara voices Ryo Ishikawa. Yuko Naito joins as the narrator. These additions expand the ensemble around Hiroto's everyday life.

Who voices the main characters?

Previously confirmed cast members include Yuma Uchida as Hiroto Ikuta, Mayu Takahashi as Natsumi Kobayashi and Rikako Aikawa as Hanae Wada. The official site lists the new and returning performers together in its cast section.

When will Hirayasumi air?

The television adaptation is scheduled to premiere on NHK General TV in Japan in January 2027. The production has not announced an exact broadcast date, an episode count or an international streaming platform. VIZ Media has also identified January 2027 as the Japanese launch window.

Story and production staff

Based on Keigo Shinzo's manga, Hirayasumi follows 29-year-old Hiroto Ikuta, an easygoing part-time worker who inherits a small house from an elderly neighbor he befriends. The story unfolds around the Tokyo neighborhoods of Asagaya and Koenji, where the people in Hiroto's orbit face uncertain futures, family changes and ordinary joys.

Kei Suezawa directs the anime, oversees series composition and writes scripts. Naoyuki Asano is responsible for character design, Mika Nishimura is art director and Kaori Sawada composes the music. Production +h. handles animation.
