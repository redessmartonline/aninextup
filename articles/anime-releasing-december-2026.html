The Apothecary Diaries Movie — December 11

The Apothecary Diaries Movie opens in Japanese theaters on December 11, 2026. The film tells a completely original story conceived by original author Natsu Hyuuga, with Maomao and Jinshi traveling south as a mystery involving a deceased consort and hidden treasure unfolds.

The movie follows the franchise's 2026 television return. See our The Apothecary Diaries Season 3 guide for the TV schedule and streaming information.
