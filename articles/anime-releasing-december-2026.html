Anime Releasing in December 2026: Confirmed Dates

A living guide to anime releases officially scheduled for December 2026. More titles will be added as studios and distributors confirm exact dates.

By AniNextUp Editorial Team · How we verify releases

December currently has a smaller confirmed anime calendar than October and November. AniNextUp lists only releases with an official date rather than filling the month with unconfirmed schedules.

Why is the December calendar shorter?

Most fall television anime begin earlier in the season, while this December list tracks only titles with an explicitly announced release day. The confirmed Apothecary Diaries film date refers to Japanese cinemas, not a worldwide streaming launch. A shorter table is more useful than adding speculative premieres or treating unannounced international dates as confirmed.

The Apothecary Diaries: The Late Lady’s Treasure — December 11

The Apothecary Diaries: The Late Lady’s Treasure opens in Japanese theaters on December 11, 2026. The film tells a completely original story conceived by original author Natsu Hyuuga, with Maomao and Jinshi traveling south as a mystery involving a deceased consort and hidden treasure unfolds.

The movie follows the franchise's 2026 television return. See our The Apothecary Diaries Season 3 guide for the TV schedule and streaming information.

How to use this December release calendar

The table shows only titles with a confirmed day in December, not every anime expected during the month. Its single current entry is a Japanese cinema premiere, so December 11 should not be interpreted as a Netflix, Crunchyroll or worldwide streaming date. The film and the television Season 3 have separate release schedules; readers looking for weekly episodes should use the linked Season 3 guide instead.

Will more December 2026 anime be added?

Yes. This calendar will expand when official anime sites, studios or distributors announce exact December dates. Titles without a confirmed day are intentionally excluded from the dated table until their schedules are official.

Where to watch December 2026 anime

The December 11 date refers to the Japanese theatrical opening of The Apothecary Diaries: The Late Lady’s Treasure. Crunchyroll has separately announced international theatrical distribution, but local cinema dates and a streaming launch are not established by the Japanese opening date. Browse the Fall 2026 anime calendar or where-to-watch hub for additional releases.

What is confirmed for December, and what is not?

The December 11 Japanese opening for The Apothecary Diaries: The Late Lady’s Treasure is a confirmed theatrical date. It does not establish an international cinema opening or an at-home streaming premiere on that day. Crunchyroll and Sony Pictures Entertainment have announced plans for international theatrical distribution, with country-specific dates to follow.

The movie also has a different schedule from the television series. Viewers catching up with Maomao's story should use our Season 3 guide for television episodes and the movie guide for theatrical updates. A film release is not automatically a new weekly television episode.

Why the December list is deliberately short

Some seasonal roundups include expected titles before their exact premiere dates are announced. This calendar takes a narrower approach: a title enters the dated table only when an official source confirms a specific day in December. That makes the list more useful for planning, even when it contains fewer entries. Check the full calendar for other confirmed releases outside this month.
