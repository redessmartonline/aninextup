Which anime have confirmed December 2026 dates?

The dated selection below currently contains a new Japanese theatrical feature and a limited theatrical revival. The Apothecary Diaries: The Late Lady's Treasure opens on December 11, while Bungo Stray Dogs: DEAD APPLE returns to selected Japanese cinemas from December 25. A separate 4K revival of Adieu Galaxy Express 999 has a December window, but the primary notices reviewed do not give an exact opening day.

December 11: The Apothecary Diaries' new film

Yomiuri TV's official announcement confirms the December 11 Japanese opening and identifies an original story conceived by series author Natsu Hyuuga. Maomao and Jinshi travel south while accompanying the remains of a consort who died five years earlier. A water city, hidden treasure and a mystery surrounding the body give the film a new setting beyond the familiar palace.

Norihiro Naganuma directs at TOHO animation STUDIO, with Yuko Kakihara writing the screenplay. Aoi Yuki and Takeo Otsuka return as Maomao and Jinshi. Those credits identify this theatrical production; they should not be used to imply that every television installment has the same production arrangement.

For a venue example, Yomiuri TV’s October 23 movie listing names TOHO Cinemas Umeda and TOHO Cinemas Namba among the Japanese cinemas. That is not confirmation of a particular performance time. Our full movie guide collects the story and distribution updates.

December 25: Bungo Stray Dogs: DEAD APPLE returns

The franchise's September 5 announcement confirms a limited revival from December 25 in 30 cinemas across Japan. It explicitly identifies DEAD APPLE as the film originally released in 2018. This is a chance to see an existing feature theatrically, not confirmation of a new sequel movie or a new television season.

The announcement links a participating-theater list. That list did not return readable content during this check, so individual venues and sessions are not reproduced here. Confirm the theater's own listing before making plans. A limited revival's starting date does not establish how long every participating cinema will keep screening it.

December window: Adieu Galaxy Express 999 in 4K

Leijisha's official September 9 notice announces the 45th-anniversary return of the 1981 second theatrical film, Adieu Galaxy Express 999: Andromeda Terminal, in a 4K-remastered version. Toei and Filmarks are named as distributors. The notice says December 2026, with the exact day and cinemas to be announced; it also warns that screening dates and periods can differ by venue.

The organizer's matching release describes Tetsuro boarding the 999 again after receiving a message from Maetel. This is a revival of an earlier film, not a newly produced 2026 story. We keep it outside the exact-date table until a primary announcement supplies the missing day.

Also on December 11: a separate Apothecary Diaries TV special

Nippon TV's October 22 notice schedules a Friday Road Show special edition for December 11 at 21:00 JST. It centers on material from the television second season's second cour and adds approximately four minutes of new footage connected to that season's closing scene.

That television program is distinct from the new cinema feature opening the same day. A broadcast listing for the special is not evidence that the theatrical film will be shown on television that evening. Readers tracking weekly episodes should use our Season 3 guide rather than treating every December franchise event as the next episode.

A Japanese broadcast time expresses a local clock, not an unlock time for a global service. Converting it to your timezone cannot establish viewing rights or streaming access.

Where can viewers outside Japan watch?

Crunchyroll's distribution announcement names Crunchyroll and Sony Pictures Entertainment for The Late Lady's Treasure internationally. The December 11 Japanese date does not supply your country's opening day, cinema sessions or streaming availability. No international release date or at-home premiere is asserted in this calendar.

How to check a screening without mixing up releases

Match the full title, release type and country first. Then check the cinema, performance date and language information on the actual session listing. A revival, a television compilation and an original feature can share a franchise name while requiring different viewing plans. An opening-day announcement is not a ticket reservation or confirmation that a particular session is on sale.
