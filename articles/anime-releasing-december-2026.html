Anime Releasing in December 2026: Confirmed Dates

A living guide to anime releases officially scheduled for December 2026. More titles will be added as studios and distributors confirm exact dates.

By AniNextUp Editorial Team · Published: October 4, 2026 · Editorial policy

December 2026 has one exact-date anime premiere in this guide and another release confirmed for the month. The Apothecary Diaries: The Late Lady’s Treasure opens in Japanese theaters on December 11. Disney has also announced Twisted-Wonderland The Animation: Episode of Savanaclaw for Disney+ in December, without specifying a day in the announcement reviewed here.

These are different kinds of releases: the first is a cinema opening in Japan, while the second is a streaming window announced by Disney Japan. Availability and timing can differ internationally. This guide separates a confirmed day from a confirmed month so that neither is mistaken for a worldwide release date.

The Apothecary Diaries film: December 11 in Japan

Maomao and Jinshi return in The Late Lady’s Treasure, the franchise's first anime feature. The Japanese opening is set for December 11, 2026. According to Crunchyroll's distribution announcement, the film is based on a story concept from creator Natsu Hyuuga and directed by Norihiro Naganuma at TOHO Animation STUDIO. Related reading: Fall 2026 Anime Release Schedule & Calendar: October–December 2026.

The premise sends Jinshi and Maomao to a southern province to investigate a mystery connected to the final wishes and remains of a deceased consort. That makes this a new film story for the pair, rather than a December date for an ordinary television episode. Our film guide covers the film's characters and trailers in more detail.

Will the movie be available outside Japan on December 11?

Not necessarily. Crunchyroll and Sony Pictures Entertainment announced theatrical distribution rights for North America and international territories excluding Asia, with India included. Their October announcement said international dates and ticket information would follow. The Japanese opening therefore does not confirm a same-day showing in the United States, the Dominican Republic or another country.

It also does not establish when the movie will be available to stream at home. When planning a visit to the cinema, look for a local distributor's announcement or a listing from your theater. Check the country, the date, and whether the showing is subtitled or dubbed before buying a ticket. A trailer available online does not mean the full film has been released online. Twisted-Wonderland: Episode of Savanaclaw — December, day TBA

Disney Japan announced that the second Twisted-Wonderland animated installment, Episode of Savanaclaw, will stream exclusively on Disney+ from December 2026. The announcement provides a month, not a specific premiere day. We have listed it separately from the December 11 film instead of assigning it an invented date.

The release follows the earlier Episode of Heartslabyul and shifts attention to Savanaclaw. For viewers looking for a series rather than a theatrical movie, its streaming format makes it a different kind of December entry. Disney Japan's announcement does not establish an identical day or catalog availability for every market; consult the Disney+ listing in your region for local details and any later schedule.

Is The Apothecary Diaries Season 3 a December premiere?

No. The third television season began in October 2026, so December viewing of an ongoing episode should not be presented as the launch of the season. Its television schedule is separate from the film's December 11 Japanese opening. The Season 3 guide covers the series, while this page tracks releases with a December date or month window. Related reading: November 2026 Anime Releases: Dates & Where to Watch.

That distinction also applies to other fall shows. A series can have new installments during December even if its premiere took place in October. Being available to watch during the month is useful information, but it is not by itself evidence of a new December season launch. For ongoing releases, check the service's episode calendar and the availability in your country.

Why does this calendar have only two entries?

This page is a checked starting point, not a promise that only two anime projects will arrive in December. A title needs an attributable December day or month before it is added here. Announcements that say only “2026,” “fall,” or “coming soon” do not identify December on their own; a theatrical date in another month should not be shifted into this list to fill it.

We distinguish the two kinds of confirmation in the table: December 11 is an exact Japanese cinema date, while Episode of Savanaclaw has a December streaming window with its day still to be confirmed in the source checked. Future local releases may be announced on different schedules. Readers can follow the full fall calendar for nearby premieres and the where-to-watch guide for platform information. How will the list be updated?

As studios, broadcasters and distributors publish more specific schedules, this guide can add the date, format and territory supported by each announcement. A change from a month-only window to an exact day should be accompanied by the source and the region it applies to. If a release is delayed, the prior date should not remain presented as current.
