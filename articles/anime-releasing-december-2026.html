The Apothecary Diaries film: December 11 in Japan

Maomao and Jinshi return in The Late Lady’s Treasure, the franchise's first anime feature. The Japanese opening is set for December 11, 2026. According to Crunchyroll's distribution announcement, the film is based on a story concept from creator Natsu Hyuuga and directed by Norihiro Naganuma at TOHO Animation STUDIO.

The premise sends Jinshi and Maomao to a southern province to investigate a mystery connected to the final wishes and remains of a deceased consort. That makes this a new film story for the pair, rather than a December date for an ordinary television episode. Our film guide covers the film's characters and trailers in more detail.

Will the movie be available outside Japan on December 11?

Not necessarily. Crunchyroll and Sony Pictures Entertainment announced theatrical distribution rights for North America and international territories excluding Asia, with India included. Their October announcement said international dates and ticket information would follow. The Japanese opening therefore does not confirm a same-day showing in the United States, the Dominican Republic or another country.

It also does not establish when the movie will be available to stream at home. When planning a visit to the cinema, look for a local distributor's announcement or a listing from your theater. Check the country, the date, and whether the showing is subtitled or dubbed before buying a ticket. A trailer available online does not mean the full film has been released online.

Spirit Circle: new December anime with 18 episodes

Twin Engine announced Spirit Circle on November 5 with a December 2026 broadcast and streaming window and an eighteen-episode run. It adapts Satoshi Mizukami’s manga, with animation by NAGOMI and EOTA. The month is confirmed; the exact premiere day and named streaming services are not specified in that announcement.

This adds a different option to the December lineup: a supernatural mystery about Fuuta Okeya and Kouko Ishigami, whose conflict reaches across earlier lives. It belongs in the series section of a watchlist, rather than beside a film’s cinema showtimes. Our Spirit Circle release and viewing guide includes the first official trailer, cast and English manga starting point.

If you are choosing between the two month-confirmed series, check both story and access. Spirit Circle introduces its mystery through a manga adaptation, while Savanaclaw is a further Twisted-Wonderland installment. A named platform is already given for the latter in Disney Japan’s notice; that does not establish identical international availability. A December window for both also does not establish that they launch together.

Twisted-Wonderland: Episode of Savanaclaw — December, day TBA

Disney Japan announced that the second Twisted-Wonderland animated installment, Episode of Savanaclaw, will stream exclusively on Disney+ from December 2026. The announcement provides a month, not a specific premiere day. We have listed it separately from the December 11 film instead of assigning it an invented date.

The release follows the earlier Episode of Heartslabyul and shifts attention to Savanaclaw. For viewers looking for a series rather than a theatrical movie, its streaming format makes it a different kind of December entry. Disney Japan's announcement does not establish an identical day or catalog availability for every market; consult the Disney+ listing in your region for local details and any later schedule.

Is The Apothecary Diaries Season 3 a December premiere?

No. The third television season began in October 2026, so December viewing of an ongoing episode should not be presented as the launch of the season. Its television schedule is separate from the film's December 11 Japanese opening. The Season 3 guide covers the series, while this page tracks releases with a December date or month window.

That distinction also applies to other fall shows. A series can have new installments during December even if its premiere took place in October. Being available to watch during the month is useful information, but it is not by itself evidence of a new December season launch. For ongoing releases, check the service's episode calendar and the availability in your country.

Why are there only three confirmed entries in this guide?

This page is a checked starting point, not a promise that only three anime projects will arrive in December. A title needs an attributable December day or month before it is added here. Announcements that say only “2026,” “fall,” or “coming soon” do not identify December on their own; a theatrical date in another month should not be shifted into this list to fill it.

The table distinguishes date precision: December 11 is an exact Japanese cinema date; Savanaclaw and Spirit Circle have December windows with their days still to be confirmed in the sources checked. Future local releases may be announced on different schedules. Readers can follow the full fall calendar for nearby premieres and the where-to-watch guide for platform information.

How to build a December anime watchlist

Start by grouping titles according to how you can actually watch them. A film with a Japanese opening date is useful if you can attend a participating cinema; it is not a substitute for an announced home-viewing date. A series with a month-only window is worth saving, but it does not yet support a calendar reminder for episode one.

Then check the territory and language version before making a purchase. A subtitled screening, dubbed screening and streaming release may have separate listings. Once a service publishes the episode schedule, check whether it releases weekly or in a batch rather than inferring the format from another title on the same platform.

For releases before this month, use November 2026 anime releases. For the following season, continue to January 2027 anime releases. These guides separate adjacent months so a January announcement does not become an unsupported December entry.

How will the list be updated?

As studios, broadcasters and distributors publish more specific schedules, this guide can add the date, format and territory supported by each announcement. A change from a month-only window to an exact day should be accompanied by the source and the region it applies to. If a release is delayed, the prior date should not remain presented as current.
