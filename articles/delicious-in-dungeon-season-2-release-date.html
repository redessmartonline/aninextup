When does Delicious in Dungeon Season 2 come out?

Delicious in Dungeon Season 2 is confirmed for October 2027. The official Japanese anime site announced the broadcast window on July 5, 2026, and Netflix’s July 6 Anime Expo update confirms an October 2027 streaming release.

Will Season 2 be on Netflix?

Yes. Netflix names the new season in its own announcement and Media Center listing. The official English anime site also displays an October 2027 Netflix banner. An exact premiere day, release hour, regional language schedule and weekly or batch rollout are not specified in those announcements.

What does the new teaser visual show?

The officially released artwork focuses on Falin, the magician Thistle and Laios’s party as their rescue journey moves deeper into the dungeon. Netflix’s published synopsis frames the continuation around the group’s effort to save Falin. The visual offers the first broad story setup for the new season without establishing a complete episode-by-episode synopsis.

Is there a Season 2 trailer?

The July release announcement introduces teaser artwork. No new Season 2 promotional video is linked in that announcement, so this guide displays the verified visual rather than assigning an earlier season’s trailer to the sequel. Check the official anime website for future video updates.

How many episodes will Season 2 have?

The sources checked on October 18, 2026 do not announce a Season 2 episode count. The first season’s 24 episodes are already available; that number should not be reused as a confirmed order for the continuation.

Who is behind Delicious in Dungeon?

The anime adapts Ryoko Kui’s manga. The official English production page credits TRIGGER for animation production. The series combines a dungeon rescue adventure with cooking and the practical challenge of feeding a party during a dangerous expedition.

Should I watch Season 1 first?

Yes: the announced rescue story continues the existing party’s journey. Watching the first season provides the context for its relationships and the stakes surrounding Falin. You can find the series through its official Netflix title page; catalog availability and language options depend on your location.
