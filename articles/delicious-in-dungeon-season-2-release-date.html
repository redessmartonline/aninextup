When does Delicious in Dungeon Season 2 come out?

Delicious in Dungeon Season 2 is confirmed for October 2027. The official Japanese anime site announced the broadcast window on July 5, 2026, and Netflix’s July 6 Anime Expo update confirms an October 2027 streaming release.

Will Season 2 be on Netflix?

Netflix is confirmed, and the official announcement also says the season will stream on other services. Netflix names the new season in its own announcement and Media Center listing. The official English anime site also displays an October 2027 Netflix banner. An exact premiere day, release hour, regional language schedule and weekly or batch rollout are not specified in those announcements.

Who is behind Delicious in Dungeon?

The anime adapts Ryoko Kui’s manga. The official English production page credits TRIGGER for animation production. The series combines a dungeon rescue adventure with cooking and the practical challenge of feeding a party during a dangerous expedition. Who is returning behind the animation?

The official English production page lists Yoshihiro Miyajima as director, Kimiko Ueno as story editor and Naoki Takeda as character designer for Season 2. Yasunori Mitsuda and Shunsuke Tsuchiya are credited for music, with TRIGGER handling animation production. Those credits give fans a concrete view of the announced creative team without implying that every episode will be made by an identical crew.

What is the party trying to do?

At the start of the series, Laios's younger sister Falin is eaten by a dragon deep in the dungeon. The party heads back down with limited supplies, turning the dungeon's monsters into food along the way. This original setup explains why cooking is closely tied to exploration and survival, rather than simply appearing between battles.
