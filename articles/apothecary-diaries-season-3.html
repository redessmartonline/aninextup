The Apothecary Diaries Season 3 Release Schedule & Where to Watch

The Apothecary Diaries Season 3 is part of the Fall 2026 schedule. AniNextUp tracks its release schedule and streaming information.

By AniNextUp Editorial Team · How we verify releases

WHERE TO WATCHCrunchyroll

The Apothecary Diaries Season 3 premiered in Japan on October 2, 2026. The official anime portal lists the main broadcast every Friday at 11:00 p.m. JST on Nippon TV's nationwide Friday Anime Night block.

Season 3 release schedule

The third season is a split-cour release. Its first cour begins in October 2026, while the official schedule places the second cour in April 2027. That means the story continues after a seasonal break rather than running straight through without interruption.

Where to watch

The official Japanese portal says streaming platforms begin carrying episodes progressively after the television broadcast. Crunchyroll carries Season 3 in supported international territories; availability and episode timing can differ by region.

What is Season 3 about?

The new story moves Maomao and Jinshi beyond the familiar confines of the rear palace. Official material teases signs of a disaster threatening the country, a mysterious shrine maiden and new responsibilities for Jinshi. Those developments push the mystery into a broader setting while keeping Maomao's investigative perspective at the center.

More Apothecary Diaries in 2026

Season 3 is not the franchise's only major 2026 release. The first theatrical film is scheduled for December 11 in Japan with a new story concept by original author Natsu Hyuuga. See our Apothecary Diaries movie guide and December 2026 anime calendar for the confirmed movie details.

The Apothecary Diaries Season 3 FAQ

Is Season 3 a split cour?

Yes. The first cour begins in October 2026 and the second cour is scheduled for April 2027.

Should I watch Seasons 1 and 2 first?

Yes. Season 3 continues Maomao and Jinshi's existing story and builds on mysteries and relationships established in the earlier seasons.
