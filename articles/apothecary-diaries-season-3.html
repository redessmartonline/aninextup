The Apothecary Diaries Season 3 Schedule & Where to Watch

When is the next episode of The Apothecary Diaries Season 3?

The official October calendar lists overall episode 53 for Friday, October 30, 2026, at 11:40 p.m. Japan Standard Time. Episode 52 aired on October 23. The series numbering continues from the first two seasons, so episode 53 is the fifth episode of Season 3, not the 53rd episode of this season.

The October 30 slot is later than the regular Friday 11:00 p.m. Japanese broadcast listed for Season 3. That distinction matters if you are matching an episode page to a local clock: the Japanese television time and a streaming service’s regional release time may differ. Japan Standard Time is UTC+9; October 30 at 11:40 p.m. JST is 10:40 a.m. in the Dominican Republic and US Eastern Daylight Time. This is the Japanese broadcast conversion, not a promise of the exact time your app will publish the episode.

What is the Season 3 release schedule?

Season 3 is planned as a split cour. The first cour began on October 2, 2026, while the second cour is scheduled to begin in April 2027. The official site has not announced the second cour’s exact date, number of episodes or weekly time slot yet. Related reading: The Apothecary Diaries Movie: Luoqing and Yinshan Character PV.

A split cour is a planned break within the same season. The April return does not mean that a separately numbered Season 4 has been ordered, and it does not tell viewers the exact week the second block will start. The calendar marks the Season 3 premiere on October 2, then lists overall episode 50 on October 9, #51 on October 16, #52 on October 23 and #53 on October 30; the final listing carries the 11:40 p.m. time change. Check the official schedule again before relying on later dates, since the portal says broadcast times can change.

Where can you watch the new episodes?

In Japan, the official Season 3 page says episodes stream progressively after the television broadcast. Its streaming section lists Japanese services including ABEMA Premium, Amazon Prime Video, Disney+, Hulu, Netflix, U-NEXT and others; the list is for Japan and should not be read as a global licensing announcement. Related reading: Black Clover Season 2 Schedule & Where to Watch.

For international viewers, Crunchyroll’s official Season 3 guide lists the series in supported territories and announced the first episode for October 2 at 8:00 a.m. Pacific Time. The service’s individual episode listing showed episode 52 on October 23. Catalog rights, language options and release clocks can differ by country, so search the app while signed into your own region. Do not assume that the 11:40 p.m. JST broadcast slot is the local Crunchyroll release time.

What is the story about this season?

The new season carries Maomao and Jinshi’s story beyond the rear palace, widening the setting to the common world and a neighboring country. Official material teases signs of a disaster, a mysterious shrine maiden and new responsibilities placed on Jinshi. Those are the season’s announced story hooks; the production has not published a complete episode-by-episode adaptation outline. Related reading: Dandivine Release Date & Where to Watch: Japan Schedule, Cast and Story.

For new viewers, the court mysteries build on relationships and discoveries from the earlier seasons. Watching Seasons 1 and 2 first makes the political stakes and Maomao’s changing relationship with Jinshi easier to follow. Season 3 is a continuation, not a reset or a standalone entry.

What do the trailer and theme songs confirm?

The official main trailer is on the verified TOHO animation channel. It introduces the season’s atmosphere and features the opening theme by Yorushika, titled 雲を抜け風下へ私だけ. The video’s original upload timestamp is August 15, 2026, at 04:23:17 PDT (11:23:17 UTC); AniNextUp records the exact timestamp and offset in the VideoObject metadata.

Who returns in the main cast?

Aoi Yūki returns as Maomao and Takeo Ōtsuka as Jinshi, the two central performances listed on the official Season 3 cast page. OLM handles the animation. These credits come from the Season 3 production page; the separate movie has its own announcements, so its cast updates should not be mistaken for a TV-season change.

How does the December movie connect to Season 3?

The franchise’s first theatrical film, The Apothecary Diaries: The Late Lady’s Treasure, is scheduled for December 11, 2026 in Japan. It is a separate film project with an original story concept by Natsu Hyūga, not the second cour of Season 3. See our movie guide for its trailer, characters and theatrical release details.
