What is Firefly Wedding? The Historical Romance Anime, Explained

Official announcement published by Crunchyroll on 2026-10-09. Read the original announcement for full details.

By AniNextUp Editorial Team

What the official source reports

With heartfelt gems like You and I Are Polar Opposites and the comedically relatable crush explored in Go For It, Nakamura-kun!! , anime romance fans have been eating well this year. But we’re not stopping yet. The fall anime season is ready to offer up stories that make your heart go pitter-patter , and for romance enthusiasts looking for something a little... bloodier ... Firefly Wedding is ready for you. But what is Firefly Wedding ? What kind of romance is it? And why are the groom’s hands so bloody? You can check out our Firefly Wedding explainer below! What is Firefly Wedding ? Based on the manga created by Oreco Tachibana, Firefly Wedding is a historical romance about a noblewoman named Satoko Kirigaya who is, unfortunately, very sick. With time running out, she hopes to secure her hand in marriage in order to make her family proud of her before she dies. There’s just one problem. She’s kidnapped by an assassin named Shinpei Goto. Satoko makes a desperate plea that changes everything. “If you join me, you can have it all. We should get married!” Shinpei accepts the offer, and with blood-soaked hands he tells Satoko, “My love can be a little intense.” That, dear romance viewers, is a definite understatement. Who Are the Main Characters of Firefly Wedding ? The two main characters of Firefly Wedding are Satoko Kirigaya and Shinpei Goto. Satoko comes from a noble family and seems to have everything going for her, but with only a short time left to live, that really puts a damper on things. It also puts a ticking clock on her life, leading her to, perhaps, make some pretty unpredictable decisions. Like agreeing to marry an assassin. To be fair, she was in the middle of a kidnapping, so that likely added to her surprising marriage proposal.

Further details from the announcement

As for Shinpei? Don’t let the smile fool you. You’ll likely find him covered in blood after cutting through his targets. That said, Satoko’s declaration of marriage leads to him becoming completely enamored by her. Whether this is a good thing or not, considering the bloody trail he leaves in his wake, is yet to be seen. But why did Shinpei kidnap Satoko in the first place? What will their arrangement mean for Satoko’s family? And just how intense is Shinpei’s love for Satoko going to get? Who is Behind the Firefly Wedding Anime? Takahiro Kamei ( Urusei Yatsura chief director) is directing the Firefly Wedding anime at studio david production. Yuko Kakihara, who has done script work for The Apothecary Diaries and series composition (and script work) for Urusei Yatsura , will be providing the scripts and series composition. Yukiko Aikei, the character designer for Magical Girl Raising Project , will be doing the character designs for Firefly Wedding , while NOGRID is composing the music and iri will be doing the opening theme, "Period." As for the voices behind the characters, we have four confirmed. You can check them out below: Satoko Kirigaya voiced by Lynn (Princess Hibana from Fire Force , Miorine Rembran from Mobile Suit Gundam: The Witch from Mercury ) Shinpei Goto voiced by Koki Uchiyama (Rin Itoshi from BLUE LOCK , Tomura Shigaraki from My Hero Academia ) Mitsueda voiced by Kenjiro Tsuda (Kishibe from Chainsaw Man , Kento Nanami from JUJUTSU KAISEN ) Kotaro Ogawa voiced by Kento Ito (Doppo Kannonzaka from Hypnosis Mic -Division Rap Battle- Rhyme Anima , Hirotaka Nifuji from Wotakoi: Love is Hard for Otaku ) Why Should You Watch Firefly Wedding ? If you’re someone who likes it when the vow exchange is from a marriage of convenience, leading to real feelings developing along the way (and those feelings potentially being very dangerous), then Firefly Wedding is probably a good fit. Nothing quite beats the moment when someone in the middle of the “this arrangement is purely beneficial” trope starts to realize that the benefit is the love felt along the way. Add in the historical setting and a deadly groom who isn’t afraid to spill some blood, and you have a recipe for what could be an intriguing fall anime romance. Perhaps Satoko can fix him, or maybe she’ll fall for the assassin with the unsettling smile who genuinely seems to be falling for her. But is him falling for her a good thing? Whatever the case, Firefly Wedding is ready to be the next anime romance you can’t stop talking about. When Does Firefly Wedding Premiere? Firefly Wedding will premiere right here on Crunchyroll on October 9, 2026!
