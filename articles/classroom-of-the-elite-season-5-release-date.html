Classroom of the Elite Season 5: 2027 Release & Two-Part Format

Classroom of the Elite Season 5’s first half will air in 2027. The production has confirmed a two-part adaptation of Year 2’s second semester and released a teaser visual video. Here is what that establishes—and what it does not yet tell us about dates, episodes and streaming.

When will Classroom of the Elite Season 5 come out?

The first half of Classroom of the Elite Season 5 is confirmed to air in 2027. The official November 22 announcement identifies the project as 5th Season: Year 2, Second Semester — First Half. It does not name a month, season or exact premiere day in the material checked on November 24, 2026.

That is more specific than a production announcement alone, but it is still a release window rather than a dated premiere. For now, save the first half under 2027. Do not attach an episode-one reminder to January or April unless a later production notice supplies that date. The November 22 date is when this update was published, not when the new episodes start.

What does the two-part format mean?

The production says the second semester of the Year 2 storyline will have a first half and second half. Its 2027 broadcast statement specifically names the first half. This confirms a division in the upcoming adaptation, but does not establish when the second half airs or how long the break between them will be.

Keep three questions separate: the story covered, the number of episodes and the broadcast schedule. A two-part description answers the first at a broad structural level; it does not automatically answer the other two. It would therefore be premature to label this a confirmed twenty-four-episode season, promise consecutive weekly broadcasts across both parts, or assign the second half to a particular year.

It also does not, by itself, give the latter half a new season number. Until the production publishes a different label, use its own first-half and second-half terminology. That makes it easier to recognize future notices without confusing an update about the same project with the announcement of another sequel.

Where will Season 5 stream? Is an international service confirmed?

The reviewed Season 5 announcement does not specify an international streaming platform, territories or an English-dub date. Those details should be checked in a distributor announcement for the new season. A service carrying earlier episodes is a useful place to look for catch-up viewing, but it is not sufficient evidence of the next season’s rights or release time.

Before subscribing specifically for Season 5, look for a notice naming the new installment and your country. Check whether it covers subtitles, a dub or both. A Japanese broadcast window does not establish when an overseas catalog will unlock the episodes, and the production’s two-part plan does not tell viewers whether any eventual streamer will follow the same timing.

Why does the official website still show an April date?

The current on-air page is still headed for 4th Season and lists an April 1, 2026 launch. It also contains Japanese streaming arrangements for that installment. Those are older-season details, not a newly announced April premiere for Season 5.

This matters when searching across a site that hosts several years of announcements. Read the season number next to a schedule before copying the date into a watchlist. The same applies to search snippets: an April date and a fresh Season 5 headline can appear together because they come from different sections of the production website. They do not establish that the new season will launch on the same month and day.

Watch the official Season 5 teaser visual video

The November 22 news page links a teaser visual movie for the new installment. That description is more precise than calling it a full episode preview. The video is an official promotional starting point, but this article does not treat it as evidence that an episode is already available or that every announced character has a newly revealed scene.

Open the official teaser on YouTube. The illustration on this page is original AniNextUp editorial artwork, not a screenshot or an official character visual. Use the video itself to see the production’s promotional material.

Which part of the story comes next?

The new installment’s announced subtitle places it in the second semester of the students’ second year. For context, the Season 4 story archive includes the uninhabited-island survival examination. This guide keeps that broad transition spoiler-light rather than summarizing the outcome of individual confrontations.

The important viewing distinction is that this is a continuation, not an introductory reboot. If you are returning after a break, finish the earlier season before watching detailed character discussions attached to the new promotion. A teaser can show familiar faces without explaining the alliances, school rules and private motivations that give those appearances meaning.

Readers already following the novels should distinguish a broad semester label from a confirmed adaptation endpoint. The production notice does not list the last novel volume or chapter that the first half will reach. Predictions about pacing may be interesting, but they should remain predictions rather than appear in a release guide as a verified episode plan.

Who is making Season 5, and which cast members are listed?

The staff and cast included with the November 22 teaser announcement credit Lerche for animation, Noriyuki Nomata as director and Ko Shigenobu for series composition. The original novels are by Syougo Kinugasa, with original character designs by Tomoseshunsaku.

The listed cast includes Shoya Chiba as Kiyotaka Ayanokoji, Akari Kito as Suzune Horikita, Ayana Taketatsu as Kei Karuizawa and Yurika Kubo as Kikyo Kushida. The announcement contains a wider cast list as well. These are the credited Japanese performers; they do not constitute a new English-dub announcement or a promise about each character’s screen time.

Can you read the Year 2 novels in English?

English editions of the Classroom of the Elite: Year 2 light novels are available. As one verified example, the official distributor’s Volume 5 record lists Kinugasa, illustrator Tomoseshunsaku and ISBN 9781685796532. Its description places the book at the beginning of the second semester.

That book context is helpful, but it is not an official instruction to skip directly to Volume 5 after the anime. An adaptation may compress, rearrange or omit material, and the new announcement does not provide a chapter-by-chapter correspondence. If you want the fullest version of the Year 2 story, begin that novel sequence at Volume 1 and follow its publication order.

When buying, check that the listing says Light Novel, the correct school year and the desired volume number. Manga editions and light novels are different formats, even when their covers or translated titles look similar. A book’s publication date also has no bearing on the television premiere: the distributor’s July 2023 date for this edition is historical publishing information, not a new anime release.

Add a separate entry for the second half only when its own schedule becomes available, rather than assigning a speculative date from the first-half announcement.
