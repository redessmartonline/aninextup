Aoashi Season 2: Release Date and Where to Watch

Aoashi Season 2 premieres October 4, 2026 on Crunchyroll. Release date, studio and streaming details for Fall 2026.

What changes after Ashito reaches the A team

Promotion brings Ashito into a group where individual talent must translate into decisions that benefit the whole side. The new competitive level gives the sequel a clear development question: how will he adapt his reading of the game and teamwork under greater pressure? That is an interpretation of the announced A-team premise, not a prediction of match results.

How Season 2 raises the sporting stakes

Ashito's promotion to the A team changes the kind of challenge he faces: talent alone is no longer enough when everyone around him is competing at a higher level. The sequel therefore has a natural focus on adaptation, decision-making and team play. Viewers comparing schedules should also keep NHK's 5:00 p.m. Japanese broadcast separate from the later Japanese subscription-streaming window.

When does Aoashi Season 2 premiere?

Aoashi Season 2 is scheduled for October 4, 2026. The announced streaming platform is Crunchyroll.

Aoashi Season 2 streaming schedule

In Japan, NHK E-Tele broadcasts Season 2 on Sundays at 5:00 p.m. JST, beginning October 4. Subscription streaming begins October 4 at 11:00 p.m. JST on Hulu, Netflix, U-NEXT and Anime Times, with additional Japanese services beginning October 8. Crunchyroll lists subtitled episodes on Sundays at 2:00 a.m. Pacific Time in supported territories.

What is it about?

Aoi Ashito joins the elite A team and faces a much harsher level of competition.

Where can you watch it?

Crunchyroll has announced Season 2 for international regions including North America, Central America, South America, Europe, Africa, Oceania, the Middle East, CIS, India and Southeast Asia. Japanese streaming includes Hulu, Netflix, U-NEXT and Anime Times from October 4.

What to remember before Season 2

Ashito's promotion to Esperion's A team is the essential starting point for the sequel. New viewers will have more context for his relationships with teammates and coaches if they watch the first season before the October 2026 continuation. The official schedule confirms a Sunday Japanese broadcast and a later domestic streaming window; it does not mean that every listed Japanese service is available internationally.
