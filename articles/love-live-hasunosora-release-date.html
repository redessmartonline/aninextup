Love Live! Hasunosora Release Date: January 10, 2027 & Episodes

Love Live! Hasunosora Girls’ High School Idol Club begins its seven-episode TV anime on January 10, 2027. The Japanese schedule is confirmed, while an international streaming service remains unannounced in the sources below.

When does the Love Live! Hasunosora anime come out?

The Japanese premiere is January 10, 2027. TOKYO MX starts the series at 22:30 JST on Sundays. Sun TV lists 23:30 the same evening. BS11 lists “January 10 at 24:30,” which means Monday, January 11 at 00:30 on a standard calendar. Japanese schedules sometimes use times above 24:00 to keep a late-night program attached to the previous evening.

The official schedule also names these stations. All times below are JST:

  • KBS Kyoto: Sundays at 24:00 from January 10 (Monday, January 11 at 00:00).
  • TV Aichi and RKB Mainichi Broadcasting: Sundays at 25:20 from January 10 (Monday at 01:20).
  • Shizuoka Broadcasting: Sundays at 25:50 from January 10 (Monday at 01:50).
  • Hokkaido Broadcasting: Mondays at 25:29 from January 11 (Tuesday, January 12 at 01:29).
  • Hokuriku Broadcasting: Sundays at 16:00 from January 17.

These are Japanese television times, not an international streaming timetable. The official notice says broadcast times can change, so check local listings near the premiere. Related reading: Hello, I am a Witch and my Crush Wants me to Make a Love Potion! Release Date & Watch Guide.

How many episodes will there be?

The official schedule lists seven episodes, covering April through June of the project’s 106th-class story. Those months describe events within the story; they are not an April-to-June broadcast window. The announcement does not give a dated episode calendar, runtimes, or a finale date. The short episode count confirms the season’s scope, but it is not enough to calculate the final broadcast date.

Where can you watch Hasunosora outside Japan?

No international streaming platform is identified in the official release information cited here. The confirmed outlets are Japanese broadcasters. Check back for a distributor announcement before assuming that Crunchyroll, Netflix or another service will carry the series in your country. Dub and subtitle availability also remain unconfirmed. What is the story about?

The setting is the private Hasunosora Girls’ High School in Kanazawa, Ishikawa. The official introduction describes a girl once known for her smile arriving with spring. New meetings and partings bring the girls' pasts into their present, as they pursue the changes they hope to make in themselves.

The school-idol setting ties music and shared ambitions to everyday school life. For newcomers, the useful distinction is that this announcement concerns the television series. The broader Hasunosora project also includes music, live performances and other animation, so a film screening or concert date should be checked separately.

Bandai Namco Filmworks separately notes that the Hasunosora theatrical film Bloom Garden Party opened in May 2026. The film and this TV anime are distinct releases within the same project. The cited announcements do not say that the film is required viewing before the January series, so a watch order should not be assumed. Cast and animation staff

The newly introduced students include Maika Kinjo, Aoi Reisawa and Mion Shinowa. Their Japanese cast members are 星宮じゅりあ, 朝陽花菜 and 湯浅かなえ, respectively. The official credits also list returning club members and their performers.

The announcement also names Haruna Sakurai as Ginko Momose, Fuka Hayama as Kosuzu Kachimachi, Rin Kurusu as Hime Anyoji, Miu Miyake as Ceras Yanagida Lilienfeld, and Amane Shindo as Izumi Katsuragi. Together with the three new students, these are the Japanese cast members identified in the release. Related reading: Dreamland Anime: Release Date, Episodes & Where to Watch.

Go Kurosaki directs, with a screenplay by Fumiaki Maruto and character designs by Haruko Iizuka. Sublimation handles animation, Sunrise is credited for planning and production, and Yoshiaki Fujisawa composes the music.
