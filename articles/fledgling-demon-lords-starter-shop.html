The Fledgling Demon Lord's Starter Shop Anime Premieres January 2027

The Fledgling Demon Lord's Starter Shop has confirmed a January 2027 Japanese TV launch alongside a new key visual, second teaser PV and a new cast announcement.

By AniNextUp Editorial Team · How we verify releases

RELEASE WINDOWJANUARY 2027 · EXACT DATE TBA

What makes the shop setting unusual

Instead of beginning with a quest to defeat a Demon Lord, this story starts after that victory has made a village's adventurer economy less useful. Murabito's struggling shop gives the fantasy world an everyday problem, while Mao's claim to be the defeated Demon Lord disrupts that quiet setting. The January 2027 announcement establishes a month, not a specific premiere day.

When does The Fledgling Demon Lord's Starter Shop anime release?

The TV anime is officially scheduled to begin broadcasting in January 2027 on TOKYO MX, MBS, BS11 and other Japanese channels. An exact premiere day has not been announced yet.

What changed in the latest announcement?

The October 6 update revealed a new key visual and the anime's second teaser PV. It also confirmed Yōhei Azakami as the voice of Ash, the hero who defeated the Demon Lord ten years before the story begins.

Cast and studio

Reiji Kawashima voices Murabito, Rumi Ōkubo voices Mao and Yōhei Azakami voices Ash. Animation production is handled by project No.9. Jun Taira directs, Keiichirō Ōchi handles series composition, Katsuyuki Satō provides character designs and Takurō Iga composes the music.

What is the anime about?

Ten years after a hero defeated the Demon Lord and restored peace, Murabito struggles to keep his late father's item shop alive in a village that adventurers no longer need. His routine changes when he meets Mao, a mysterious golden-eyed girl who claims to be the supposedly dead Demon Lord.

Why the peaceful setting creates a new problem

With the Demon Lord defeated, Murabito's village no longer attracts the adventurers who once supported his father's shop. Peace is therefore an economic problem for the protagonist rather than simply a happy ending to a fantasy war. Mao's claim to be the defeated Demon Lord introduces a second uncertainty: whether the past conflict is truly finished. This reading follows the official premise and does not confirm whether Mao's claim is true.

Where will it stream?

No international streaming platform has been officially confirmed yet. The current announcement covers Japanese television. AniNextUp will update this page when an official distributor confirms streaming territories.

Does the teaser confirm an episode schedule?

No. The January 2027 broadcast month is confirmed, but the teaser does not establish an exact premiere day or episode count. Those details will require a separate scheduling announcement.
