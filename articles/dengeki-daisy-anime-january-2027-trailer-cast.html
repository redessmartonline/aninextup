Dengeki Daisy Anime Sets January 2027 Premiere, Reveals Trailer and Cast

When does Dengeki Daisy premiere?

Dengeki Daisy is scheduled to begin airing in Japan in January 2027. The anime's official September 19 announcement confirmed a two-consecutive-cour broadcast, meaning the show is planned to continue across two television seasons without a scheduled seasonal break. The precise first-airing date and episode count have not been announced.

Crunchyroll has separately announced that it will stream the anime in 2027. Availability and release timing can vary by territory; a detailed regional schedule has not yet been provided.

Who voices the main characters?

Yoshino Aoyama voices Teru Kurebayashi, while Taku Yashiro plays Tasuku Kurosaki. The supporting cast includes Tomokazu Sugita as Souichirou Kurebayashi, Yuka Terasaki as Riko Onizuka, Setsuji Satou as Masuda and Kouhei Amasaki as Akira. These roles are listed on the official anime website.

Which studio is making Dengeki Daisy?

Studio DEEN is producing the television anime. Souta Ueno directs, Sawako Hirabayashi handles series composition, Ayaka Murakami is responsible for character designs and Masaru Yokoyama composes the music. The team is adapting Motomi's completed manga, which originally ran in Shogakukan's Betsucomi magazine between 2007 and 2013.

What is the story about?

Teru Kurebayashi is a high school student who finds comfort in messages from a mysterious sender called “Daisy” after her older brother's death. An accident at school leaves her working for the abrasive custodian Tasuku Kurosaki, whose unexpected concern for her hints at secrets beneath his rough exterior. The story combines romance, suspense and questions about trust and identity.

What remains unconfirmed?

The production has not provided an exact January premiere day, total episode count or complete country-by-country streaming schedule. AniNextUp will update this report when those details are announced rather than treating speculation as confirmed information.
