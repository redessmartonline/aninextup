Dengeki Daisy Anime Sets January 2027 Premiere, Reveals Trailer and Cast

The long-awaited adaptation of Kyousuke Motomi's romance-suspense manga has a confirmed broadcast window, a full promotional video and a two-cour schedule.

When does Dengeki Daisy premiere?

Dengeki Daisy is scheduled to begin airing in Japan in January 2027. The anime's official September 19 announcement confirmed a two-consecutive-cour broadcast, meaning the show is planned to continue across two television seasons without a scheduled seasonal break. The precise first-airing date and episode count have not been announced.

Crunchyroll has separately announced that it will stream the anime in 2027. Availability and release timing can vary by territory; a detailed regional schedule has not yet been provided.

Who voices the main characters?

Yoshino Aoyama voices Teru Kurebayashi, while Taku Yashiro plays Tasuku Kurosaki. The supporting cast includes Tomokazu Sugita as Souichirou Kurebayashi, Yuka Terasaki as Riko Onizuka, Setsuji Satou as Masuda and Kouhei Amasaki as Akira. These roles are listed on the official anime website.

Which studio is making Dengeki Daisy?

Studio DEEN is producing the television anime. Souta Ueno directs, Sawako Hirabayashi handles series composition, Ayaka Murakami is responsible for character designs and Masaru Yokoyama composes the music. The team is adapting Motomi's completed manga, which originally ran in Shogakukan's Betsucomi magazine between 2007 and 2013.

What is the story about?

Teru Kurebayashi is a high school student who finds comfort in messages from a mysterious sender called “Daisy” after her older brother's death. An accident at school leaves her working for the abrasive custodian Tasuku Kurosaki, whose unexpected concern for her hints at secrets beneath his rough exterior. The story combines romance, suspense and questions about trust and identity.

What remains unconfirmed?

The production has not provided an exact January premiere day, total episode count or complete country-by-country streaming schedule. AniNextUp will update this report when those details are announced rather than treating speculation as confirmed information.

What does a two-cour broadcast actually confirm?

The official announcement describes two consecutive cours, which confirms a planned continuous television run across two broadcast seasons. It does not state an episode total, and it should not be converted automatically into a particular number such as 24 or 26. The announcement confirms January 2027 as the start window but leaves the first broadcast day and weekly airtime open. Those details normally belong to a station schedule or a later production notice. Keeping the window, run format and exact episode count separate avoids turning a planning term into an unannounced episode order.

Where can viewers outside Japan watch?

Crunchyroll has announced a 2027 stream, but the current materials do not provide a country-by-country list or a specific launch time. A general streaming announcement is useful, yet it does not guarantee that every territory will receive the same version or episode on the same day. Viewers should check the local catalog when the title is added and distinguish a Japanese broadcast time from a regional streaming schedule. The existing announcement also does not confirm subtitles, dubbing or a release cadence, so those details remain open.

How does the anime connect to the original manga?

Kyousuke Motomi’s Dengeki Daisy ran in Shogakukan’s Betsucomi magazine from 2007 to 2013, and the publisher lists the complete manga in 16 volumes. That publication history gives context for the adaptation without predicting how the television staff will divide the story. The official announcement confirms a new anime, its staff and a two-cour plan; it does not say that the series will reproduce every chapter or add a new ending. The publisher’s synopsis centers on Teru’s messages from Daisy and the unexpected bond that grows after she meets Kurosaki, which supports the romance-and-suspense description used here.
