Dengeki Daisy Anime Sets January 2027 Premiere, Reveals Trailer and Cast

The long-awaited adaptation of Kyousuke Motomi's romance-suspense manga has a confirmed broadcast window, a full promotional video and a two-cour schedule.

When does the Dengeki Daisy anime premiere?

The television anime is scheduled to begin broadcasting in Japan in January 2027. The official announcement describes a two-consecutive-cour run, which means the planned broadcast spans two cours in succession. The production has not announced the exact first episode date, a weekly airtime or an episode total. January is the confirmed start window; it should not be presented as a specific day.

“Two cours” is a broadcast-format description, not a guaranteed episode count. It tells viewers the planned run continues across two programming blocks, but the official materials do not say whether the anime will use 24 episodes, 26 episodes or another total. The official series website and Aniplex announcement are the sources to check for a later station schedule.

Where will Dengeki Daisy stream?

Crunchyroll has announced plans to stream the anime in 2027. That announcement does not yet give a launch day or a complete country-by-country list. Japanese television broadcast and international streaming are separate schedules: a Japanese episode time does not automatically establish when it appears in every regional catalog. Viewers outside Japan should check the Crunchyroll listing for their account region once the title page and schedule are available. Subtitle languages, dubbing and same-day availability have not been confirmed in the current announcements.

Who voices Teru and Kurosaki?

Yoshino Aoyama voices Teru Kurebayashi, and Taku Yashiro plays Tasuku Kurosaki. The announced supporting cast includes Tomokazu Sugita as Souichirou Kurebayashi, Yuka Terasaki as Riko Onizuka, Setsuji Satou as Masuda and Kouhei Amasaki as Akira. These names and roles appear in the official anime materials. The cast announcement identifies the listed characters; it should not be treated as a complete cast list for every person in the manga.

Which studio and staff are producing the series?

Studio DEEN is producing the television anime. Souta Ueno directs, Sawako Hirabayashi handles series composition, Ayaka Murakami designs the characters, and Masaru Yokoyama composes the music. The production has confirmed these core credits alongside the main promotional video. It has not published a full production schedule or stated how many manga chapters each episode will cover.

What is Dengeki Daisy about?

Teru Kurebayashi loses her older brother and is left with a phone he gave her. Messages from a mysterious person using the name “Daisy” become a source of comfort. At school, Teru gets pulled into work for Tasuku Kurosaki, a blunt custodian whose actions show more concern than his manner suggests. Their relationship develops alongside questions about Daisy’s identity and the secrets surrounding Teru’s brother. Related reading: Now That We Draw Anime Sets January 2027 Premiere.

The premise combines a school romance with suspense and a mystery about trust. That mix helps explain why the story is not only a “who is Daisy?” puzzle: Teru and Kurosaki’s changing relationship is also central. The official synopsis sets up those elements, but it does not reveal how the anime will adapt the manga’s later turns. This guide avoids spoilers and does not assume that the series will cover every chapter.

Is the original manga complete, and where can readers find it?

Kyousuke Motomi’s manga ran in Shogakukan’s Betsucomi magazine from 2007 to 2013. Shogakukan lists the completed work in 16 volumes. This gives anime viewers a clear distinction between the source story, which has an ending, and the television adaptation, whose coverage has not been announced. A two-cour plan alone does not confirm whether the anime will adapt the full manga, skip material or use a different ending.

Readers can check Shogakukan’s official volume 16 listing for the completed Japanese edition. The official anime site also links its story, cast and news updates. Availability of the manga in other languages depends on local publishers and retailers; the Japanese publisher listing does not guarantee an English or Spanish edition in every market.

What is confirmed so far?

Confirmed: a January 2027 start in Japan, a planned two-cour broadcast, Studio DEEN, the announced cast and staff, and a 2027 Crunchyroll streaming plan. Still pending: the exact first-airing date, episode total, station and time, regional streaming calendar, subtitle and dub details, and the manga chapters the anime will cover. Keeping those answers separate makes it easier to spot the difference between an official update and an estimate repeated online.
