Should you watch the main anime before the Omake Shorts?

If you want the jokes to land with some character context, start with Episode 1 of I’m Dating a Dark Summoner, then try Episode 1 of Omake Shorts. That is a suggested viewing order. HIDIVE describes the shorts as a bonus series with the returning cast in sketch scenarios and has not published a required episode-by-episode watch order. You can also sample a short first if the sketch format is what brought you here.

The main anime introduces Amona, a dark summoner, and Roni, a priest, through the fantasy romantic comedy adapted from Shaoh’s manga. The bonus series takes those familiar characters and puts them into different roles and settings. Seeing the main series first gives you a point of comparison: a workplace sketch can play on what you already know about the characters’ usual roles. That background can help explain a joke without requiring you to finish the season.

There is no published pairing that tells you to alternate one regular episode with one omake. For a simple weekend watch, start with the main series if you want the story, then open the shorts when you want sketches. If you are already caught up with the main show, start the shorts at their first entry. The Friday and Sunday release slots are two schedules for different productions, so the latest Friday short is not a sign that the next Sunday episode is available early.

Release schedule for the shorts and the main series

HIDIVE’s November lineup lists Omake Shorts on Fridays beginning November 6, 2026. The bonus series has 12 episodes. HIDIVE’s October lineup lists the main anime on Sundays beginning October 4. The two schedules explain why a new title may appear on Friday without a new chapter of the main story. If you are following both, give each series its own reminder rather than treating Friday and Sunday as release times for two versions of the same episode.

The official schedule gives a starting date and weekday for the shorts. We could not verify an exact premiere hour or a complete set of individual episode dates from those announcements. Check the current HIDIVE listing before setting a local-time reminder. The 12-episode count tells you the planned length of the bonus series. A final date calculated by adding eleven weeks would still depend on every week running without interruption, including the holiday period.

What does “omake” mean here?

Omake means an extra or bonus. In this release it refers to a set of new chibi comedy sketches, with smaller, exaggerated versions of the characters. HIDIVE’s examples include working in a café or office and going back to school. Those premises shift the setting and give the cast a new situation to react to. If you are watching primarily to see where Amona and Roni’s relationship goes in the fantasy story, continue with the regular anime. If you want to see what the same characters do in a different setting, the shorts are the relevant listing.

HIDIVE credits Acali Arimiya with directing the bonus series, designing its characters and co-writing the scripts with Araki Humisato. WWWave Corporation’s Deregula studio handles the animation. Rei Takano returns as Amona, Yuki Nishiyama as Roni and Semmai Hodentei as Imp. Those returning voices can make the sketches feel familiar even when the characters’ surroundings change. The separate production credits and dedicated episode count are additional ways to tell the shorts apart from the main show.

The main story starts with a contrast that carries into the spin-off. Roni is the serious priest in the adventuring party, and Amona is the newcomer whose summoning powers seem at odds with his role. The fantasy series builds its comedy from how the two behave together despite those opposing roles. The sketches can take that contrast out of the party and put it to work in a classroom or behind a counter. If the first short leaves you wondering how the pair met, the main anime is where that setup belongs.

You may also encounter a Japanese listing that mentions a Deregula version of the main anime. AT-X uses that label for its own October broadcast of the original series. It refers to an edition of the main anime on a Japanese channel. The November HIDIVE listing is identified by the Omake Shorts title and its separate sketch format.

Official trailer and English-language options

The official YouTube trailer has English subtitles. That gives English-speaking viewers a way to sample the sketches and see how the chibi designs look in motion. HIDIVE’s announcement describes the trailer’s subtitles and does not give an English dub date for the shorts. If spoken English is important to you, check the episode’s audio information in your regional catalog before starting it. Subtitle settings tell you what text can be shown on screen, while the audio setting tells you which language you will hear.

For a quick decision, use the trailer to check whether you want the sketch-comedy format, then use the full title on HIDIVE to find the episodes. If you want the relationship setup first, open the main anime and come back to the shorts afterward. That route works whether you are arriving during the November premiere or catching up after several episodes have been added.
