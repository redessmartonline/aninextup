The Fable Season 2: January 9, 2027 Premiere, Cast and Visual

The Fable Season 2 starts on Nippon TV in Japan on January 9, 2027 at 24:55 JST (January 10 at 00:55). A new main visual and six additional cast members are confirmed; international streaming remains unannounced.

October 14 update: Yamaoka arc cast and main visual

The official production has revealed the main visual for The Fable Season 2's Yamaoka arc and confirmed six additional Japanese voice actors. Koichi Yamadera voices Yamaoka, with Takanori Hoshino as Azami, Hiroyuki Yoshino as Yukari, Satoshi Mikami as Koji Mizuno, Taiten Kusunoki as Jiro and Masayuki Fujiwara as Suzumushi.

The official anime homepage now confirms January 9, 2027 at 24:55 JST on Nippon TV. Japanese television listings use the January 9 broadcast day for the slot, which falls on January 10 at 00:55 JST by calendar date. An international streaming service remains unannounced.

When does The Fable Season 2 premiere?

The second season of The Fable is scheduled on Japan's Nippon TV network for Saturday, January 9, 2027 at 24:55 JST (technically Sunday, January 10 at 00:55). This is the official Japanese television schedule, not a confirmed international simulcast time. The new season adapts the Yamaoka arc from Katsuhisa Minami's manga.

Why does the January 9 listing become January 10 on the clock?

Nippon TV's Japanese schedule uses January 9 at 24:55 JST, a late-night television convention. On a standard calendar clock that is January 10 at 00:55 JST. These labels describe the same Japanese broadcast slot, not two separate premieres. No international streaming hour follows automatically from the Japanese television schedule.

Confirmed returning voice cast

The official anime website currently confirms returning cast including Kazuyuki Okitsu as Akira Sato, Miyuki Sawashiro as Yoko Sato and Kana Hanazawa as Misaki Shimizu. Further cast additions should be checked against the production's latest announcements.

Production and official teaser

Ryosuke Takahashi directs the adaptation at Tezuka Productions. The September 15, 2026 official announcement unveiled a teaser video for the next chapter of the story. The official production website hosts the promotional materials and the April 2026 teaser visual. Readers can view the authentic artwork and teaser on the official website.

What is The Fable about?

Akira Sato is an exceptionally skilled assassin ordered to live a normal life without killing for a year. His effort to stay out of violence is challenged by new conspiracies. The anime adapts Katsuhisa Minami’s manga, published by Kodansha.

Where to watch

Nippon TV is confirmed for the Japanese broadcast. An international streaming release for Season 2 has not been confirmed in this announcement. Do not assume that a Season 1 streaming service will also carry the new season.

What remains unconfirmed?

The episode count, international distribution and English dub schedule have not been confirmed in the cited announcements.
