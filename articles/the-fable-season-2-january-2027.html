The Fable Season 2: January 2027 Premiere and New Cast

The Fable returns with the Yamaoka arc in January 2027.

When does The Fable Season 2 premiere?

The second season of The Fable is scheduled to begin on Japan's Nippon TV network on January 9, 2027 at 24:55 JST, which is 12:55 a.m. on January 10 in standard calendar notation. The new season adapts the Yamaoka arc from Katsuhisa Minami's manga.

New voice cast

The announced cast includes Koichi Yamadera as Yamaoka, Takanori Hoshino as Azami, Hiroyuki Yoshino as Yukari (Eucalyptus), Satoshi Mikami as Koji Mizuno, Taiten Kusunoki as Jiro and Seiyu Fujiwara as Suzumushi.

Where to watch

Nippon TV is confirmed for the Japanese broadcast. An international streaming release for Season 2 has not been confirmed in this announcement. Do not assume that a Season 1 streaming service will also carry the new season.

Official sources and updates

Check the official The Fable anime website for the latest production announcements and official promotional artwork. AniNextUp will update this article when more distribution details are confirmed.
