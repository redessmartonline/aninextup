October 14 update: Yamaoka arc cast and main visual

The official production has revealed the main visual for The Fable Season 2's Yamaoka arc and confirmed six additional Japanese voice actors. Koichi Yamadera voices Yamaoka, with Takanori Hoshino as Azami, Hiroyuki Yoshino as Yukari, Satoshi Mikami as Koji Mizuno, Taiten Kusunoki as Jiro and Seiyu Fujiwara as Suzumushi.

The official anime homepage now confirms January 9, 2027 at 24:55 JST on Nippon TV. Japanese television listings use the January 9 broadcast day for the slot, which falls on January 10 at 00:55 JST by calendar date. An international streaming service remains unannounced.

When does The Fable Season 2 premiere?

The second season of The Fable is scheduled on Japan's Nippon TV network for Saturday, January 9, 2027 at 24:55 JST (technically Sunday, January 10 at 00:55). This is the official Japanese television schedule, not a confirmed international simulcast time. The new season adapts the Yamaoka arc from Katsuhisa Minami's manga.

Why does the January 9 listing become January 10 on the clock?

Nippon TV's Japanese schedule uses January 9 at 24:55 JST, a late-night television convention. On a standard calendar clock that is January 10 at 00:55 JST. These labels describe the same Japanese broadcast slot, not two separate premieres. No international streaming hour follows automatically from the Japanese television schedule.

Confirmed returning voice cast

The official anime website currently confirms returning cast including Kazuyuki Okitsu as Akira Sato, Miyuki Sawashiro as Yoko Sato and Kana Hanazawa as Misaki Shimizu. Production and official teaser

Ryosuke Takahashi directs the adaptation at Tezuka Productions. The September 15, 2026 official announcement unveiled a teaser video for the next chapter of the story. The official production website hosts the promotional materials and the April 2026 teaser visual. Readers can view the authentic artwork and teaser on the official website.

What is The Fable about?

Akira Sato is an exceptionally skilled assassin ordered to live a normal life without killing for a year. His effort to stay out of violence is challenged by new conspiracies. The anime adapts Katsuhisa Minami’s manga, published by Kodansha.

Where to watch

Nippon TV is confirmed for the Japanese broadcast. An international streaming release for Season 2 has not been confirmed in this announcement. Do not assume that a Season 1 streaming service will also carry the new season.

What does the Yamaoka arc announcement show?

The production's October 14 visual announcement places Akira at the center, with Yamaoka and the assassins Azami and Yukari behind him. Yoko and Misaki also appear in the artwork. The official description presents the image as a sign of danger approaching the ordinary life Akira is trying to protect.

The visual establishes the characters and tone of the next chapter, but it is not an episode-by-episode synopsis. Viewers who want to avoid spoilers can use the artwork and teaser as introductions and leave the outcome of Yamaoka's plans to the anime. The announcement confirms the arc and Japanese schedule without giving a complete release calendar for international viewers.

Who are the newly announced Japanese voices?

Koichi Yamadera plays Yamaoka, while Takanori Hoshino voices Azami and Hiroyuki Yoshino voices Yukari. The additional cast also includes Satoshi Mikami as Koji Mizuno, Taiten Kusunoki as Jiro and Seiyu Fujiwara as Suzumushi. The production's official character page lists the Japanese performers alongside the characters.

These roles expand the cast around the returning leads rather than replacing Akira and Yoko. The announced credits describe Japanese performances; they do not confirm an English dub or its actors. If a distributor announces other language versions, the voice cast and timing should be checked for that specific edition.

Can you watch the first season while waiting?

The first anime season aired in 2024. Its availability may differ by country, so viewers can check their local Disney+ or Hulu catalog for the first season and its audio and subtitle choices. That earlier availability is useful for catching up, but it is not a licensing announcement for Season 2. As of this article's source check, the cited Japanese announcements do not name an international platform for the continuation.

For readers who prefer the source material, Kodansha USA's series page lists Katsuhisa Minami's manga in English. The manga and anime are separate formats, and the production has not announced a chapter-by-chapter adaptation schedule for every episode of Season 2.

How to follow the Japanese schedule

The production lists the series on Nippon TV's Saturday schedule at 24:55, starting January 9. That notation places the first slot at 12:55 a.m. on Sunday, January 10 in Japan. Broadcast schedules may change, and the official listing does not set a worldwide streaming time. Follow the production or a local distributor for later platform, subtitle and episode details.
