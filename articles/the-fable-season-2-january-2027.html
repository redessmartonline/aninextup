The Fable Season 2 Confirmed for January 2027: Cast, Staff and Teaser

Official release information

When does The Fable Season 2 premiere?

The second season of The Fable is scheduled to begin on Japan's Nippon TV network in January 2027, with the exact day and time still unannounced. The new season adapts the Yamaoka arc from Katsuhisa Minami's manga.

Confirmed returning voice cast

The official anime website currently confirms returning cast including Kazuyuki Okitsu as Akira Sato, Miyuki Sawashiro as Yoko Sato and Kana Hanazawa as Misaki Shimizu. Further cast additions should be checked against the production's latest announcements.

Production and official teaser

Ryosuke Takahashi directs the adaptation at Tezuka Productions. The September 15, 2026 official announcement unveiled a teaser video for the next chapter of the story. The official production website hosts the promotional materials and the April 2026 teaser visual. Readers can view the authentic artwork and teaser on the official website.

What is The Fable about?

Akira Sato is an exceptionally skilled assassin ordered to live a normal life without killing for a year. His effort to stay out of violence is challenged by new conspiracies. The anime adapts Katsuhisa Minami’s manga, published by Kodansha.

Where to watch

Nippon TV is confirmed for the Japanese broadcast. An international streaming release for Season 2 has not been confirmed in this announcement. Do not assume that a Season 1 streaming service will also carry the new season.

What remains unconfirmed?

The precise January premiere day, episode count, international distribution and English dub schedule have not been announced.
