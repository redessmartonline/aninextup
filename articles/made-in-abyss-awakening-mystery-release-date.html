When is the Made in Abyss movie’s North American premiere?

Saturday, December 5, 2026 is the date HIDIVE announced for the North American premiere of Made in Abyss: Awakening Mystery. The screening is part of Anime Frontier in Fort Worth, Texas. If you can get to Fort Worth, this is the announced opportunity to see Part 1 in North America in 2026. If you are looking for an online release or a theater near home, the next announcement to follow is HIDIVE’s wider distribution plan.

Check the Anime Frontier website for the convention program and badge rules before buying travel or admission. Look up the film under the full subtitle, Awakening Mystery, so you can distinguish the screening from a franchise panel or a showing of an earlier film. Use the event listing to confirm the room, start time, language and entry requirements. A convention premiere can have its own admission rules, and the distributor’s date alone does not tell you whether you need to reserve a seat.

For viewers in Canada or elsewhere in the United States, the North American premiere describes the first regional screening. The announcement names the Texas event and leaves the 2027 rollout details for later. If a local theater posts a listing, check the subtitle and the city before treating it as a screening of this installment.

When did Made in Abyss: Awakening Mystery release?

The first installment in the Made in Abyss theatrical series opened in Japanese theaters on October 23, 2026. The date is a Japan release, not a worldwide premiere. The official Japanese title is Gekijō Series Dai-ichi-bu: Made in Abyss — Mezameru Shinpi (劇場シリーズ【第一部】メイドインアビス 目覚める神秘). HIDIVE uses Awakening Mystery in its English-language announcement.

October 23 is the original Japanese theatrical date. The December 5 screening in Texas is the subsequent North American premiere. These dates identify two different events for the same film, so use the one that corresponds to where you plan to watch.

Made in Abyss Part 2 is confirmed, but undated

On October 23, the official film site confirmed production of Part 2, describing it as the continuation of Part 1. The announcement also revealed a second key visual featuring the Curse Fleet, Hail Hex, led by Srajo. This is the clearest answer for readers asking whether the theatrical project will continue: yes, another installment is in production.

The announcement did not give Part 2 a release date, production schedule, runtime, distributor, or streaming plan. It did not publish a separate staff or cast list for the new installment. Part 1’s confirmed credits therefore should not automatically be presented as Part 2 credits. The project has established that the next film is being made, while its timing and production details remain unknown.

What is the first film about?

The official introduction recaps the journey from the sixth layer of the Abyss and points toward the seventh. Riko, Reg, Nanachi, and Faputa leave the Village of the Hollows behind after its collapse and continue deeper into the unknown. The setup follows the events of the second television season; the film is a continuation of that journey, not a remake of the original series or an announced third TV season.

The official introduction includes significant story detail from earlier chapters, including Prushka’s transformation into Riko’s White Whistle and the fate of the village. New viewers who want to avoid those reveals can begin with the television seasons and consult the film’s synopsis afterward. The project site does not describe the full plot of Part 1 or explain how many manga chapters it adapts, so a precise adaptation breakdown is not confirmed.

Who made the film and who returns?

The official credits list Akihito Tsukushi as the original creator, Masayuki Kojima as director, Hideyuki Kurata as screenwriter, and Kinema Citrus as the animation studio. Character design is credited to Yuka Kuroda and Kazuchika Kise, with Kevin Penkin composing the music. These familiar series credits connect the film to the existing anime, but the official page identifies this project as the first part of a theatrical series.

The principal returning voices include Miyu Tomita as Riko, Mariya Ise as Reg, Shiori Izawa as Nanachi, and Misaki Kuno as Faputa. The film also lists Sumire Morohoshi as Tepaste, Takanori Hoshino as Cravagli, Nana Mizuki as Srajo, Akino Watanabe as Nishagora, Shunsuke Takeuchi as Yataramaru, Nao Toyama as Sherumi, and Yume Miyamoto as Menae. Those names are confirmed for Part 1; a Part 2 cast announcement has not been made.

The trailer and official introduction establish the project’s title, release, and story context. Promotional footage should not be treated as confirmation of scenes or plot points that the production has not described in its written synopsis.

What was the Japanese opening-week bonus?

The official first-week campaign ran from October 23 through October 30, 2026. Participating screenings included a short Hime-sama Nikki mini-anime after the feature, and visitors received one of two Akihito Tsukushi illustrated mini shikishi designs at random. The mini-anime expands an April Fools’ Day illustration into a short screening bonus. The announced campaign applied to the Japanese opening week.

HIDIVE’s North American announcement does not say the short or the illustration will accompany the Texas screening. If you are going to Anime Frontier for the feature, use the convention program for any additional items it lists.

When will HIDIVE release the movie?

HIDIVE announced on November 10 that it will distribute Made in Abyss: Awakening Mystery in 2027. Its announcement leaves the exact date, countries and release format open. A streaming date, a general theatrical rollout and English dub availability would each require further confirmation. If you want to know when to subscribe or book tickets, the next useful detail is a title-specific listing for your country.

HIDIVE already has a history with the series and introduced the first television season outside Japan in 2017. Existing catalog pages for earlier seasons are useful for catching up, but their availability varies by region. Before making plans around a search result, check that the listing names Awakening Mystery and gives an actual start date or session for your location. The November 10 distribution announcement is the starting point for the 2027 release.

What should you watch before Awakening Mystery?

  1. Made in Abyss (Season 1) introduces Riko, Reg and the expedition into the Abyss.
  2. Made in Abyss: Dawn of the Deep Soul continues the story after Season 1 and sets up the next stage of their journey.
  3. Made in Abyss: The Golden City of the Scorching Sun (Season 2) brings the group to the sixth layer and introduces the story that the new movie follows.
  4. Made in Abyss: Awakening Mystery continues from Season 2 as Part 1 of the new theatrical series.

If you watched the first season years ago and jumped straight into Season 2, Dawn of the Deep Soul is the installment to go back for. It is a separate feature from Awakening Mystery. If you finished Season 2 and only need a refresher, start with its final episodes. They explain the group’s circumstances and Faputa’s connection to the journey without requiring you to read the new film’s synopsis first. The first two recap films cover the first season, so viewers who already watched the television version can move directly to Dawn of the Deep Soul.
