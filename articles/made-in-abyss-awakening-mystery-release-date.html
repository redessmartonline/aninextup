When does Made in Abyss: Awakening Mystery release?

The film is scheduled to open in Japanese theaters on October 23, 2026. The official Japanese title identifies it as Made in Abyss: Mezameru Shinpi (目覚める神秘), the first part of a theatrical series. “Awakening Mystery” is an English rendering of the subtitle; the official pages reviewed publish the title in Japanese.

The announced date applies to Japan. As of October 20, the official film site and theater listing do not provide an overseas cinema date or a streaming release. Viewers outside Japan should wait for a distributor or local exhibitor to confirm availability.

What is the movie about?

The official introduction follows Riko, Reg, Nanachi and Faputa as their journey continues toward the Abyss’s seventh layer. The film is presented as the first entry in a new theatrical series, rather than as a confirmed third television season. The official site has not published an international synopsis or schedule.

First-week theater bonus: a mini-anime after the film

For screenings from October 23 through October 30, the official site says theaters will show the first Hime-sama Nikki mini-anime after the feature. The announcement also lists an illustrated mini shikishi by Akihito Tsukushi, distributed randomly in one of two designs. Theater participation and remaining stock may vary, so check with the individual cinema before attending.

Read the official first-week bonus announcement →

Returning staff and cast

The official staff page lists Masayuki Kojima as director, Hideyuki Kurata for series composition and screenplay, Kinema Citrus for animation production, and Kevin Penkin for music. Returning principal cast members include Miyu Tomita as Riko, Mariya Ise as Reg, Shiori Izawa as Nanachi and Misaki Kuno as Faputa.

Where to watch outside Japan

Only the Japanese theatrical opening is confirmed in the sources reviewed for this update. No international theatrical date, regional distributor, or streaming service and date are confirmed on the official film pages. AniNextUp will update this guide when a regional announcement is available.
