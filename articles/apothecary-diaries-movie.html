October 14 update: Luoqing and Yinshan character trailer

The official anime portal has released the fourth character video for The Apothecary Diaries movie, focusing on Luoqing and Yinshan. The announcement is dated October 14, 2026; it displays no publication hour or timezone, so no precise announcement time is assigned here.

Does the new character PV change the release date?

No. The October 14 video expands the introduction of Luoqing and Yinshan, but the film remains scheduled to open in Japanese theaters on December 11, 2026. The character reveal is separate from an international distribution announcement; it does not confirm an overseas premiere or streaming date.

Two characters connected to the central mystery

Luoqing is a consort who died in the rear palace five years earlier. Yinshan once served as her head attendant and now looks after Muqing in the Bi household. The new footage connects their shared history to the mystery Maomao must investigate, including Luoqing’s wish to return home five years after her death.

This is a character promotional video, rather than a new release-date announcement. The movie’s Japanese opening remains December 11, 2026. An international cinema date or streaming debut should not be inferred from this update.

New voice cast: Miyuki Sawashiro and Ai Mikami

The official production announcements confirm Miyuki Sawashiro as Luoqing and Ai Mikami as Yinshan. These October 14 casting reveals accompany the character PV and do not change the Japanese theatrical release date of December 11, 2026.

October 13 update: Kaja-dan trailer and new cast confirmed

The latest official character video for The Apothecary Diaries: The Late Lady’s Treasure introduces Kaja-dan, a band of river pirates drawn into the mystery surrounding a hidden treasure. The production published the video announcement and a separate cast announcement on October 13, 2026. The movie’s Japanese theatrical opening remains December 11, 2026; this character reveal does not announce a new international release date.

Kensho Ono joins the movie as Suija

Kensho Ono voices Suija, the group’s leader, who is searching for treasure hidden by his late father. The official trailer announcement also confirms Ryota Takeuchi as Suija’s loyal subordinate Eisho and Jiro Saito as Kaja, the previous leader who died a year earlier.

What the new character trailer shows

As Maomao, Jinshi and their companions travel to the southern city on the water to return the remains of a consort who died five years earlier, Kaja-dan takes an interest in the visiting party. The video introduces Suija, his sister and his subordinate, connecting the pirates’ search for their former leader’s treasure to the film’s wider mystery.

Why the movie is separate from Season 3

The film has its own mystery built around Maomao and Jinshi, rather than merely combining television episodes into a feature. Its December theatrical date should therefore be tracked separately from the split-cour Season 3 broadcast schedule. For overseas viewers, the important unanswered question is not whether the film exists, but when a distributor confirms a local cinema or streaming release.

When does The Apothecary Diaries movie release?

The Apothecary Diaries: The Late Lady’s Treasure opens in Japanese theaters on December 11, 2026. The movie is a theatrical project with a completely original mystery rather than a simple compilation of television episodes.

Is the movie an original story?

Yes. Original author Natsu Hyuuga provides the story concept. Maomao and Jinshi become involved in a mystery connected to a deceased consort, a southern water city, pirates and a hidden treasure, giving the film its own storyline within the series' world.

Who is making the movie?

Norio Naganuma directs, Yuko Kakihara handles the screenplay and TOHO animation STUDIO produces the animation. Aoi Yuki returns as Maomao and Takeo Otsuka as Jinshi.

December 11 is the announced Japanese theatrical opening.
