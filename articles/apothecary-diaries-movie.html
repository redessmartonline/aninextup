Latest confirmed update: Luoqing and Yinshan

The official portal’s October 14 character PV introduces Luoqing and Yinshan, two figures connected to the movie’s central mystery. Luoqing was a consort who died five years earlier; Yinshan served as her head attendant and now cares for Muqing in the Bi household. The accompanying announcements cast Miyuki Sawashiro as Luoqing and Ai Mikami as Yinshan. These reveals add characters and voice actors, but do not change the Japanese release date or establish a date outside Japan.

The official video is a character promotional clip, not a new full-length film trailer. It follows earlier October videos introducing Maomao and Jinshi, then the Bi household and the Kaja-dan river pirates. Those announcements add context to the ensemble and the mystery, while the production’s confirmed theatrical date remains December 11. YouTube records the Luoqing and Yinshan PV as uploaded at 4:00:35 p.m. PDT (UTC−7) on October 13, 2026; that timestamp is October 14 in Japan. When does The Apothecary Diaries movie release?

The Apothecary Diaries: The Late Lady’s Treasure opens in Japanese theaters on December 11, 2026. The official announcement calls it a new theatrical film with an original story concept by series author Natsu Hyuuga. It is not a recap of the television episodes and is not Season 3. The film follows Maomao and Jinshi as they travel to a southern water city to return the remains of a consort who died five years earlier. A mystery involving her past, a hidden treasure and river pirates draws them into the story.

The Japanese date is confirmed, but it should not be treated as a worldwide release. Crunchyroll announced international theatrical distribution; that confirms an overseas distributor, not one common date for every country. Local cinema dates and any later streaming launch require separate announcements.

Who is making the film?

Norio Naganuma directs, Yuko Kakihara writes the screenplay and TOHO animation STUDIO produces the animation. Aoi Yuki returns as Maomao and Takeo Otsuka as Jinshi. The recently announced supporting roles broaden the group connected to the southern province and the film’s investigation. The character videos give viewers a first look at that setting, but do not disclose the full solution to the mystery.

Do you need to watch the TV anime first?

No official prerequisite viewing order has been announced. The movie is presented as a new story centered on Maomao and Jinshi, so a newcomer can follow the central investigation from its own setup. Watching the television seasons first is still a useful choice if you want the character relationships, palace politics and Maomao’s investigative habits to feel familiar before the film begins. That is a viewing recommendation, not a stated admission requirement or a claim that the movie continues a specific season finale.

Are advance tickets available in Japan?

The production's official ticket page announced online sales of a teaser-visual advance ticket card beginning August 17, 2026 at 2:00 p.m. Japan time. It lists a general price of ¥1,500 including tax, with shipping charged separately for online orders.

That announcement concerns a Japanese ticket product, not international theater dates. Availability and other ticket versions can change, so check the official ticket and local cinema listings before purchasing. Overseas release dates and a later streaming launch still require separate distributor announcements.

What details are still unconfirmed?

As of October 23, the official release information confirms the Japanese opening date, core staff, returning leads and the new character cast. A runtime, age rating, international cinema dates by country, subtitle options and a home-streaming date should not be assumed from the Japanese listing. Those details can arrive later from local distributors or theaters. If you are planning a cinema visit outside Japan, check your country’s exhibitor or distributor listing instead of converting December 11 into a worldwide date.

How the movie fits beside Season 3

Season 3 and the movie are separate projects with separate release schedules. Season 3 began in October 2026, while the film is scheduled for Japanese theaters in December. The movie has its own original mystery, so its release should be tracked separately from the television cour and its weekly episodes. A theatrical date also says nothing by itself about home video or streaming.
