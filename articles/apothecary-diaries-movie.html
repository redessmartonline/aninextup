The Apothecary Diaries: The Late Lady’s Treasure Release Date & Trailer

October 12 update: New character trailers released on consecutive days

The Apothecary Diaries: The Late Lady’s Treasure has begun a four-day run of official character-focused promotional videos ahead of its December 11, 2026 Japanese theatrical release. The anime's official portal published the first video on October 11, spotlighting Maomao, Jinshi and their traveling party, followed by a second video on October 12 introducing the Bi clan, which governs Minanshu, the setting of the film. The production's schedule also lists a video about the Kaja group for October 13 and a feature on Jakusei and Ginzan for October 14; those future videos are not yet described here as released.

What the October 11 Maomao and Jinshi video reveals

In newly revealed scenes, the emperor sends Maomao and Jinshi to a city built on water to investigate the remains of a mid-ranking consort who died five years earlier. Maomao is fascinated by a rare medicine but questions why the consort's last wish concerned a return home so long after her death. Jinshi faces the responsibilities associated with his true standing as the emperor's younger brother. Familiar faces including Gaoshun, Basen, Lahan, Lihaku, Rikuson and Lakan appear, alongside threats from waterborne raiders and assassins. These details come from the production's official character-video announcement, not an assumed adaptation of an existing novel arc.

What the October 12 Bi clan video adds

The second character video turns to the Bi clan, the family that governs Minanshu. The production also shared a comment from Mariya Ise, who voices Muqing, a boy connected to the movie's central mystery. The sequence of short character videos gives viewers more context about the film's new setting and supporting cast without revealing the mystery's resolution.

Official updates: October 11 Maomao and Jinshi video announcement · October 12 Bi clan video announcement · Official film news index. The original theatrical trailer remains embedded below; these new character clips are separate releases.

Why the movie is separate from Season 3

The film has its own mystery built around Maomao and Jinshi, rather than merely combining television episodes into a feature. Its December theatrical date should therefore be tracked separately from the split-cour Season 3 broadcast schedule. For overseas viewers, the important unanswered question is not whether the film exists, but when a distributor confirms a local cinema or streaming release.

When does The Apothecary Diaries movie release?

The Apothecary Diaries: The Late Lady’s Treasure opens in Japanese theaters on December 11, 2026. The movie is a theatrical project with a completely original mystery rather than a simple compilation of television episodes.

Is the movie an original story?

Yes. Original author Natsu Hyuuga provides the story concept. Maomao and Jinshi become involved in a mystery connected to a deceased consort, a southern water city, pirates and a hidden treasure, giving the film its own storyline within the series' world.

Who is making the movie?

Norio Naganuma directs, Yuko Kakihara handles the screenplay and TOHO animation STUDIO produces the animation. Aoi Yuki returns as Maomao and Takeo Otsuka as Jinshi.

Where to watch: theaters versus streaming

December 11 is the announced Japanese theatrical opening. Crunchyroll has separately announced international theatrical distribution, but that does not establish a single worldwide cinema date or an international streaming launch. Check local cinema announcements for theatrical showings; a streaming date should be reported only after a separate official confirmation.
