When does The Apothecary Diaries movie release?

December 11, 2026 in Japanese theaters. An international streaming date has not been announced.

Watch the official trailer

Is the movie an original story?

Yes. Original author Natsu Hyuuga provides the story concept for a new mystery involving Maomao and Jinshi, a deceased consort, a southern water city, pirates and a hidden treasure.

Who is making the movie?

Norio Naganuma directs, Yuko Kakihara handles the screenplay and TOHO animation STUDIO produces the animation. Aoi Yuki returns as Maomao and Takeo Otsuka as Jinshi.

Where to watch

The December 11 date currently refers to the Japanese theatrical release. AniNextUp will update this page when international theatrical or streaming plans are officially announced.
