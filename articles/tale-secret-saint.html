When does A Tale of the Secret Saint premiere?

A Tale of the Secret Saint premieres October 3, 2026. Crunchyroll carries the series internationally as part of its Fall 2026 lineup.

Where to watch

The announced Crunchyroll territories include North America, Central America, South America, Europe, Africa, Oceania, the Middle East, CIS, the Indian Subcontinent, Southeast Asia and Korea. Availability and episode timing can still vary by individual market.

What is A Tale of the Secret Saint about?

The story follows a swordswoman who believes she has little talent until memories of a previous life reveal that she was once a powerful Saint. That knowledge gives her extraordinary magical potential, but openly revealing it would also expose her to enemies connected to her former life.

Who is making the anime?

FelixFilm is the animation studio for the adaptation. The series combines fantasy adventure with a reincarnation mystery built around the heroine hiding abilities that could completely change how others see her.

What makes the premise different?

Rather than starting with a hero who understands her powers, the central conflict comes from remembering a past identity and deciding how much of that strength can safely be used in the present.
