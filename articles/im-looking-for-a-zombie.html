What drives the story beyond zombie survival

Aki's search for her missing father gives the journey a personal goal beyond escaping danger. That changes how to approach the series: the ruined city is not only a threat-filled setting but also a place where the characters must follow clues and make decisions about whom to trust. The same-day dub announcement is useful for viewers deciding which language version to follow from the premiere.

When does I'm Looking For a Zombie premiere?

I'm Looking For a Zombie premieres October 3, 2026. Crunchyroll begins streaming the series alongside its Japanese release, with the first English-dubbed episode also launching the same day.

Where to watch

Crunchyroll lists availability across North America, Central America, South America, Europe, Africa, Oceania, the Middle East, CIS and India. Exact release times and language options can differ by territory.

What is it about?

Aki travels with her friends through a zombie-ravaged city in search of her missing father. The search gives the group a reason to move through dangerous territory instead of simply finding a safe place to wait. The premise leaves the father's whereabouts unresolved, so readers should not treat his fate as confirmed.

What makes this zombie story different?

The official Japanese anime site places the story thirteen years after the outbreak known as the “Red Day,” when most survivors live in isolated villages. Aki belongs to a generation without memories of the world before the collapse. That detail changes the journey's perspective: the ruined outside world is frightening, but it is also unfamiliar, expansive and sometimes beautiful to the characters. This is AniNextUp's interpretation of the published premise, not a prediction about future episodes.

Why Aki's search matters

Aki leaves her village with childhood friends Natsuki and Haru to look for her father, later meeting Yu and Sakura. The official introduction frames the adventure as both difficult and enjoyable. Rather than promising uninterrupted horror, the premise combines an unresolved family search with the discovery of a world the younger characters have never known. Viewers interested in character relationships and exploration may therefore find a different emphasis here from a conventional survival-only zombie series.

Is there an English dub?

Yes. Crunchyroll confirmed a same-day English dub. Madeline Dorroh voices Aki, with Arianna Contardo as Natsuki, Blythe Melin as Haru, Giselle Fernandez as Yu and Anairis Quiñones as Sakura.
