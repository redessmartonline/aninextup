Sasaki and Peeps Season 2 shares its creditless themes

Sasaki and Peeps Season 2 has released creditless versions of its opening and ending animation. Crunchyroll reported the clips on October 9, 2026, following the season's Japanese television debut. The opening features MADKID's “Unbroken,” while the ending uses “Sukipi no Okage!” by nonfic! Both credits can be checked against the anime's official music page.

For viewers following the new season, the clips offer a convenient way to revisit its theme sequences separately from the episodes. A creditless release removes the production names normally overlaid on the animation. It does not mean the animation has no credited creators, and it should not be confused with a newly announced episode or an extended edition of the television series.

Where to find the opening and ending videos

The two sequences are presented in Crunchyroll's October 9 announcement. Use the opening and ending sections there to locate the corresponding players. This article links to that announcement rather than inserting a video address that has not been checked against the release.

Expect a television theme sequence rather than a full episode. These clips are best approached after starting Season 2 if you prefer discovering characters and situations through the story itself. Opening and ending animation can reveal imagery that a viewer would rather encounter in context, even when the accompanying news announcement contains no plot summary. MADKID returns with “Unbroken”

The official opening-theme page identifies “Unbroken” as the Season 2 opening and lists it on MADKID's thirteenth single, “Unbroken / Bonbaraman,” with an October 14, 2026 release date. That date has already passed as of this article's October 26 source check; it is a music-product date, not a second premiere for the anime.

The same page includes a comment from MADKID member LIN. In paraphrase, he says the lyrics were written with Sasaki and Peeps' thoughts, choices and changing emotions in mind, and describes working with Q-MHz on the song. This provides an attributable explanation of the track's connection to the protagonists, rather than asking listeners to treat a fan interpretation as an official statement.

MADKID also performed the first season's opening, “FLY,” according to the series' music index. The new title therefore marks a change of song while retaining the opening artist. When searching music services or video platforms, including both “Unbroken” and “MADKID” helps distinguish the Season 2 theme from the earlier opening. nonfic! performs “Sukipi no Okage!”

The ending's Japanese title is すきぴのおかげ！, and its artist is のんふぃく！, rendered here as nonfic! The official ending-theme page is the clearest reference when English reports display an unfamiliar spelling or a broken character in the group's name.

That page lists a December 8, 2026 music release and describes a planned six-track product with multiple editions. In the accompanying artist comment, the group presents the theme as a bright, positive pop song about affection. Those details concern the announced release; they do not establish that every track or edition is already available internationally.

The three dates serve different purposes: October 7 is the Japanese television launch, October 14 is the opening single's listed release, and December 8 is the ending product's announced date. Hearing a song in an episode before its physical release is not a contradiction. The creditless clip, commercial recording and television broadcast are separate ways of encountering the music. When Season 2 began and where to check streaming

The official broadcast schedule lists an October 7, 2026 launch with a one-hour premiere. AT-X and TOKYO MX began that Wednesday, while BS Nippon TV's opening broadcast followed on October 8. These are Japanese broadcast dates, so they should not be read as a single worldwide streaming time.

For Japan, the schedule also lists Prime Video from October 7 and a later rollout to additional services from October 12. Crunchyroll's announcement identifies the season as part of its fall simulcast lineup. Check the show's listing in your own region for available episodes, subtitles and audio options; a Japanese platform schedule alone cannot establish those details for another country.

The series behind the music

The official introduction follows an office worker whose pet bird is a sage from another world. Peeps gives Sasaki access to magic and travel between worlds, while supernatural encounters also complicate his life in modern Japan.

The Season 2 staff credits name Yushi Ibe as director, Yamato Ouchi as assistant director, Deko Akao for series composition and Saori Nakashiki for character design, with animation by SILVER LINK. The adaptation credits Buncololi's original work and Kantoku's original character designs. These production details place the theme release within the continuing adaptation without implying another season has been announced.
