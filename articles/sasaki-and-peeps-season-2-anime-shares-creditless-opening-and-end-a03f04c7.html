Sasaki and Peeps Season 2 Anime Shares Creditless Opening and Ending Videos

Sasaki and Peeps Season 2 has released creditless opening and ending videos, featuring MADKID's Unbroken and nonfic!'s Sukipi no Okage!.

Season 2 opening and ending videos are now available

The second season of Sasaki and Peeps has released creditless versions of both its opening and ending animation sequences. These versions remove the overlaid production credits so viewers can focus on the animation and music. Crunchyroll reported the release on October 9, 2026, shortly after the season began broadcasting in Japan.

Who performs the opening and ending songs?

MADKID performs the opening theme, “Unbroken.” The ending theme is “Sukipi no Okage!” (Japanese: すきぴのおかげ！), performed by the idol group nonfic! (のんふぃく！). Both song credits are also listed by the anime's official Japanese website, which helps resolve the garbled artist name and song title in the English news report.

The opening single is scheduled for October 14, 2026, according to the official music page; the single date is separate from the October 7 anime premiere. For the actual animation sequences, use the videos embedded in Crunchyroll's original announcement; this page does not embed unverified video URLs.

Is October 14 another anime premiere date?

No. October 14, 2026 is the announced release date of MADKID's opening-theme single, Unbroken, rather than a new premiere for the anime. Sasaki and Peeps Season 2 began Japanese television broadcasts on October 7. The newly shared creditless opening and ending clips are promotional videos of the existing theme sequences, not additional episodes.

When did the second season begin?

The anime's official website lists October 7, 2026, as the start of its Japanese television broadcast. Crunchyroll is also streaming the new season as part of its fall 2026 lineup. The series follows office worker Sasaki and his unusual pet bird Peeps, whose magical abilities draw Sasaki into adventures beyond his ordinary life.

What creditless videos do and do not announce

Creditless opening and ending videos showcase the existing theme sequences without on-screen credits. Their release is promotional material for Season 2, not an announcement of new episodes, a further season or a change in broadcast timing.

Production and official sources

The second season is directed by Yushi Ibe at animation studio SILVER LINK., with Yamato Ouchi as assistant director, Deko Akao handling series composition and Saori Nakashiki credited for character design. The announcement concerns new music videos, not an additional season renewal or a change to the broadcast schedule.
