Bless Anime Reveals Koki Uchiyama as Akiharu Nakano Ahead of January 2027 Premiere

The upcoming fashion-and-makeup drama Bless has introduced another character ahead of its Japanese television premiere in January 2027.

What is new in the Bless anime announcement?

On October 15, 2026, the production introduced Akiharu Nakano (Nakano Akiharu), a first-year high-school student who studies makeup with the Mirror group. Koki Uchiyama voices the character. A new character promotional video featuring his voice was released alongside the casting news. This is a character-focused preview, not a new full-series trailer.

According to the character profile distributed with the announcement, Nakano is quiet, highly skilled and fast at makeup application. He greatly admires Hikaru Oya, another figure associated with the academy. The update is part of the production's series of weekly character introductions.

Is the Akiharu Nakano video a new series trailer?

No. The October 15 announcement from Aniplex identifies this as a character PV that reveals Koki Uchiyama's performance as Akiharu Nakano. It does not replace a full-series trailer or establish a specific first-episode date. The separately confirmed television window remains January 2027 in Japan.

When does Bless premiere?

Bless is scheduled to begin airing in January 2027 on Fuji TV's noitaminA block in Japan. The official anime website and studio listing confirm the month and broadcaster. An exact premiere day, international streaming platform and region-by-region release schedule have not been announced in the sources reviewed for this update.

What is Bless about?

Based on Yukino Sonoyama's manga, the story follows Aia Udagawa, a former model who has lost confidence in his dream of becoming a makeup artist, and Jun Sumisaki, a student who wants to model but struggles to take the first step. A school festival competition becomes a turning point for both, as they begin pursuing their ambitions through makeup and fashion.

The series is being animated by A-1 Pictures. Shotaro Kitamura directs, with series composition and scripts credited to Fukuro Kamiza, character designs by Honoka Yokoyama and music by Ryo Yoshimata.
