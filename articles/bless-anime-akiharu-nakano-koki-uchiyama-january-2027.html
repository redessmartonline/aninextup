Bless Anime Reveals Koki Uchiyama as Akiharu Nakano Ahead of January 2027 Premiere

The upcoming fashion-and-makeup drama Bless has introduced another character ahead of its Japanese television premiere in January 2027.

By AniNextUp Editorial Team · Published: October 15, 2026 · Editorial policy

What is new in the Bless anime announcement?

On October 15, 2026, the production introduced Akiharu Nakano (Nakano Akiharu), a first-year high-school student who studies makeup with the Mirror group. Koki Uchiyama voices the character. A new character promotional video featuring his voice was released alongside the casting news. This is a character-focused preview, not a new full-series trailer.

According to the character profile distributed with the announcement, Nakano is quiet, highly skilled and fast at makeup application. He greatly admires Hikaru Oya, another figure associated with the academy. The update is part of the production's series of weekly character introductions.

Is the Akiharu Nakano video a new series trailer?

No. The October 15 announcement from Aniplex identifies this as a character PV that reveals Koki Uchiyama's performance as Akiharu Nakano. It does not replace a full-series trailer or establish a specific first-episode date. The separately confirmed television window remains January 2027 in Japan.

When does Bless premiere?

Bless is scheduled to begin airing in January 2027 on Fuji TV’s noitaminA block in Japan. An exact day is not specified in the official production listings checked for this October 26 update. International streaming is now confirmed: Crunchyroll announced on October 22 that it will stream the series worldwide excluding Japan and China. This supersedes the earlier version of this article, which said no platform had been announced. Related reading: Le Collège Noir Season 2 Trailer Revealed Ahead of October 31 Premiere.

What is Bless about?

Based on Yukino Sonoyama's manga, the story follows Aia Udagawa, a former model who has lost confidence in his dream of becoming a makeup artist, and Jun Sumisaki, a student who wants to model but struggles to take the first step. A school festival competition becomes a turning point for both, as they begin pursuing their ambitions through makeup and fashion.

The series is being animated by A-1 Pictures. Shotaro Kitamura directs, with series composition and scripts credited to Fukuro Kamiza, character designs by Honoka Yokoyama and music by Ryo Yoshimata.

What Uchiyama says about playing Nakano

In his comment published by Aniplex on October 15, Uchiyama says the makeup and modeling world was unfamiliar territory for him when he encountered the manga. He describes enjoying that fresh perspective and being pleased to join an anime dealing with an unusual subject. He also identifies Nakano as a high-school student learning makeup at Mirror who deeply respects Hikaru Oya.

His acting approach centers on expressing what the reserved character carries inside. That is a useful distinction for readers meeting Nakano through a short preview: saying little does not mean having little motivation. The announcement offers a starting point for understanding the performance, without revealing how Nakano's relationships or ambitions will develop across the adaptation.

Why Mirror matters to the story

Kodansha's official description of the second manga volume introduces Mirror as a school for aspiring makeup artists. For Aia, entering that environment means encountering other people with the same professional goal, including potential rivals. Jun's modeling ambitions continue alongside his training, with the publisher describing her attempts to enter casting calls and build on their work together.

This background explains why a supporting student's introduction matters beyond adding another name to the cast. Mirror broadens the story's focus from an individual dream to the pressures of learning a craft around talented peers. These are elements of the source manga's published premise, rather than confirmation that the anime will adapt a particular volume in full or follow an announced chapter-by-chapter schedule.

Hikaru Oya has also received a cast announcement

There has been another relevant update since Nakano's introduction. Aniplex announced on October 22 that Tarusuke Shingaki voices Hikaru Oya, accompanied by Oya's own character PV. Keeping the announcements separate avoids confusing the two roles: Uchiyama plays Nakano, while Shingaki plays the figure Nakano admires.

In his accompanying comment, Shingaki describes Oya watching Aia with feelings that include admiration and frustration. That provides another official perspective on the cast's relationships while leaving their development for the series itself. Neither casting update establishes a new premiere day.

Reading Bless before the anime

Kodansha USA publishes the manga in English in print and digital formats. Its series introduction emphasizes Aia and Jun challenging other people's expectations of their appearance and abilities. Readers interested in the underlying story can begin with the first volume; the character PV is promotional material for the adaptation, rather than a substitute for that opening narrative.
