When does TANK CHAIR premiere?

TANK CHAIR premieres in Japan on October 4, 2026. TOKYO MX begins the series Sundays at 11:00 p.m. JST, with BS11 following at 12:30 a.m. Additional broadcasts begin on CBC October 5 and MBS October 6.

TANK CHAIR weekly schedule

The main TOKYO MX broadcast is scheduled every Sunday at 11:00 p.m. JST. The official anime site says Prime Video begins advance streaming in Japan at 11:30 p.m. on the same day as the main broadcast.

Where to watch TANK CHAIR

Prime Video is the first officially announced streaming service in Japan. International platform availability can differ by territory, so AniNextUp will update this guide when additional distributors confirm their schedules.

What is TANK CHAIR about?

Nagi Taira was once a powerful assassin. After protecting his sister Shizuka from gunfire, he remains unresponsive until murderous intent is directed toward him. Shizuka begins taking her brother into battles against increasingly dangerous enemies in an attempt to fully awaken him.
