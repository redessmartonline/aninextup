How the siblings' relationship drives the premise

Nagi's unusual response to murderous intent creates an action-story paradox: the danger Shizuka wants to survive is also what can briefly bring her brother back into action. Her determination to awaken him gives the battles a family motive rather than making them isolated fights. The synopsis does not establish whether the plan will succeed, so that outcome remains an open question.

Why the streaming schedule needs a regional check

The Sunday 11:30 p.m. Prime Video window is explicitly a Japanese advance-streaming arrangement. It does not establish that Prime Video in every other country carries the show. The Wednesday rollout on other Japanese services is a separate distribution stage. For readers abroad, the most useful question is which local platform has confirmed rights, not merely which Japanese service is first.

When does TANK CHAIR premiere?

TANK CHAIR premieres in Japan on October 4, 2026. TOKYO MX begins the series Sundays at 11:00 p.m. JST, with BS11 following at 12:30 a.m. Additional broadcasts begin on CBC October 5 and MBS October 6.

TANK CHAIR weekly schedule

The main TOKYO MX broadcast is scheduled every Sunday at 11:00 p.m. JST. The official anime site says Prime Video begins advance streaming in Japan at 11:30 p.m. on the same day as the main broadcast.

Where to watch TANK CHAIR

Prime Video has the earliest announced Japanese streaming window, starting Sundays at 11:30 p.m. JST on October 4. The official distribution notice also lists other Japanese subscription platforms starting sequentially on Wednesdays at 11:30 p.m. JST from October 7. These Japanese schedules do not establish availability on Prime Video or other services outside Japan.

What is TANK CHAIR about?

Nagi Taira was once a powerful assassin. After protecting his sister Shizuka from gunfire, he remains unresponsive until murderous intent is directed toward him. Shizuka begins taking her brother into battles against increasingly dangerous enemies in an attempt to fully awaken him.

What to check before watching outside Japan

The confirmed Prime Video advance window and the later Wednesday streaming rollout both describe services in Japan. Neither is proof that a viewer in another country can find the series in the same Prime Video catalog. The practical next step is to check a distributor's announcement for your territory or the episode page in your local service. The official schedule also separates TOKYO MX's Sunday broadcast from BS11's after-midnight airing; these are different channels, not separate episode premieres.
