Release date and where to watch

The headline question has a confirmed answer for Japan: Witch on the Holy Night opens in Japanese theaters on November 20, 2026. The film is an animated adaptation of the TYPE-MOON work, produced by ufotable, with distribution credited to Toho and Aniplex on the official project site. That date is a cinema opening, not a streaming launch. International theatrical dates are a separate rollout, and neither announcement should be treated as proof that the movie will appear on a particular subscription service.

For viewers outside Japan, the announced international schedule identifies January 28, 2027 for Latin American markets and January 29 for the United States and Canada. Those dates do not establish one worldwide day: local distributors can schedule screenings differently, and some territories may receive the film later. Check a local cinema listing once tickets are available. As of this update, no home-video or streaming date has been announced by the project site. The practical answer to “Where can I watch it?” is therefore theatrical release in the listed markets, with streaming availability still unknown.

What is the movie about?

The movie adapts a story built around three very different young people. Aoko Aozaki is a high-school student learning to become a Magician; Alice Kuonji is the witch with whom she lives and trains; Soujuurou Shizuki is a boy who comes from the mountains to the city and enters their orbit. The official introduction describes their meeting as the start of Aoko’s story. It is useful context for new viewers because it identifies the central trio without requiring knowledge of another TYPE-MOON anime. Related reading: Aikatsu Stars! The setup is not simply a school fantasy. Aoko has responsibilities in ordinary school life as well as a demanding apprenticeship, while Alice’s practice of magic forms part of their shared household. Soujuurou’s arrival disrupts the routine the two have established. The official synopsis presents that encounter as a turning point; it does not disclose the film’s full plot or promise how much of the original story the movie will cover. Anyone looking for a scene-by-scene adaptation breakdown should wait for a more detailed official synopsis or the completed film.

Is it connected to Fate?

The title is connected to TYPE-MOON, the creative company behind the original work, but this movie is not advertised as a sequel season to Fate or another numbered anime. The official project credits Kinoko Nasu and TYPE-MOON for the original work, and Hirokazu Koyama for original character design, with Takashi Takeuchi credited for original character concepts. That context helps explain why the film may interest fans of TYPE-MOON while also setting a clear expectation: the official material introduces Aoko, Alice and Soujuurou as the focus of this story, rather than presenting the movie as a continuation of a separate series.

Studio, staff and theme song

ufotable handles animation production and screenplay production. Masaru Takeuchi is the director, Moe Tsuzuki is character designer, and Hikaru Kondo is chief director. The official staff list also names Masato Nagamori as chief animation director, Yuuichi Terao for photography and finishing direction, and Hideyuki Fukasawa for music. These credits are more useful than guessing at a production schedule: they establish who is attached, but they do not confirm the film’s running time, number of parts, or the amount of material adapted. What has not been announced?

The Japanese opening is confirmed for November 20, 2026, but individual theaters may list different showtimes. January 2027 international dates apply to the named territories above; they do not confirm a release date for every country. No streaming platform, digital rental date, Blu-ray date, runtime, or episode count has been announced on the official project site. If a later distributor announcement adds those details, they should be reported with the applicable region and source instead of extrapolated from the theatrical schedule. For planning, use the date that matches your location, then verify the theater’s own listing and language options. A Japanese premiere does not mean same-day access abroad, and an international theatrical date does not mean the film will stream that week. If you are deciding whether to watch as a newcomer, the official story outline provides the necessary starting point: Aoko’s apprenticeship, Alice as her housemate and fellow practitioner, and Soujuurou’s arrival. Additional franchise background may enrich the experience, but the project’s official introduction does not state that prior viewing is required.

Other territory dates and streaming plans remain unconfirmed.
