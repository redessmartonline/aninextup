When does the Nanatsu-ya anime come out?

Nanatsu-ya: Shinobu and Her Jewelry Box is scheduled to air as a TV anime in 2027. Pony Canyon announced the adaptation on November 11, 2026, with a teaser video, a key visual and the core production staff. The announcement gives the year. A month, Japanese broadcaster and exact premiere day are still pending, along with the voice cast and episode count.

The manga’s Japanese title is 七つ屋 志のぶの宝石匣. Kodansha’s English reader uses Nanatsu-ya: Shinobu and Her Jewelry Box, with a hyphen. Searches for Nanatsuya without a hyphen or Nanatsuya Shinobu no Hōsekibako can also lead to this adaptation. The subtitle and Tomoko Ninomiya’s name are useful identifiers if a search returns other stories about jewelry or pawnshops.

The year applies to the television adaptation. Kodansha separately lists November 13, 2026 for the Japanese release of manga volume 28. If you see the November date beside the series title, check whether the listing is for a numbered book. You can follow future anime dates through the AniNextUp release calendar as they are confirmed.

What is Nanatsu-ya: Shinobu and Her Jewelry Box about?

Shinobu Kurata helps at Kurata-ya, her family’s long-running Tokyo pawnshop, when she has time away from high school. She can pick up on something in gemstones that other people miss. Kodansha describes it as an aura, a quality that gives her an unusual edge when someone brings a piece into the shop. For Shinobu, an appraisal can begin with what she senses and turn into a question about what the customer knows.

Akisada Kitagami works in high-end jewelry sales. The same kinds of stones that cross Shinobu’s counter move through a very different world in his job, with wealthy clients and an established brand behind him. His family background is tied to Kurata-ya, which took him in when he was young. Kodansha’s synopsis also identifies him as the fiancé Shinobu’s grandfather chose for her. That arrangement gives their conversations personal stakes before either character gets into the secrets surrounding his past.

The Japanese publisher’s introduction starts with two diamonds. One comes into the pawnshop, while a client asks Akisada to appraise another. Both pieces raise questions. It is a useful preview of the series’ structure because it gives Shinobu and Akisada something concrete to investigate from their respective workplaces. A stone can matter to the person who brought it in for reasons the price alone cannot explain.

Readers who enjoy a mystery built around ordinary transactions may find the pawnshop setting as important as Shinobu’s gift. A person can come in intending to sell, borrow against an object or simply find out what it is worth. Each motive changes what the owner is willing to say. Shinobu’s ability gives her a lead, while the shop gives the story a place for people with otherwise unrelated lives to cross paths.

Who is making the anime?

Chiaki Kon directs at J.C.STAFF. Pony Canyon credits Seishi Minakami for series composition and Mai Furuki for character design. The November reveal names the production staff, while the Japanese voices for Shinobu and Akisada have yet to be announced. If you see a cast name circulating, compare it with the anime’s official staff page before attaching it to a character.

Tomoko Ninomiya also created Nodame Cantabile, and Kon directed its Paris-hen and Finale anime installments. That is a useful connection for viewers who know Ninomiya through her music series. Here, the source material follows a different cast and a different line of work. You can start with Shinobu’s first chapter without knowing the earlier manga or its adaptations.

Where can you read the Nanatsu-ya manga in English?

Start with Chapter 1 on Kodansha’s K MANGA. It is listed under the hyphenated English title and marked free by the reader. Check the service in your country for access. Later entries can carry different labels or require points. If the chapter list opens on a more recent entry, sort or scroll back to Chapter 1 to meet Shinobu and Akisada in order.

MangaPlaza also lists an English digital edition of the series. Check the available chapters and access terms on the storefront you intend to use. If you already read through one service and are considering switching, compare chapter numbers first. A title can be present on two platforms while their chapter listings or purchase options differ.

Kodansha’s Kiss magazine serializes the Japanese manga, and the Japanese catalogue lists numbered collected volumes. Volume 28 is scheduled for November 13 in Japan. That date is for the collected Japanese edition. Readers looking for the English translation should use the digital chapter listings above. If you import a book, confirm the language on the product page before ordering.

Should you start with the manga or wait for the anime?

Choose Chapter 1 if you want a complete opening scene and time to see how the appraisal mystery begins. Choose the teaser if you mainly want to see the character designs and get a feel for how the adaptation presents Kurata-ya. The announcement has yet to give a chapter cutoff for the TV series, so there is no official volume to skip to after the anime. Starting at the beginning is the simplest choice if you decide to read ahead.

If you are more interested in the family mystery than the details of the jewelry trade, pay attention to how differently Shinobu and Akisada enter each case. Shinobu sees what comes in from the neighborhood. Akisada sees what circulates among buyers and clients with access to expensive stones. The first chapter lets you decide whether you want to follow both sides of that story before committing to later chapters.

If you are coming to the series through Nodame Cantabile, the first chapter also gives you a way to compare how Ninomiya introduces a specialized world. You can follow who owns each piece, who evaluates it and what each person hopes to learn, even when you know little about gemstones. The opening case introduces the two leads through the work they do. Their shared history adds a longer question alongside the immediate appraisals. Readers looking for a single starting point can use the chapter link above, while viewers who want to keep the cases a surprise can follow the anime’s release updates and stick to the teaser.
