A Certain Dark Item Release Date & Where to Watch

Release at a glance

  • Japanese premiere: October 9, 2026.
  • Japanese TV schedule: AT-X lists Friday at 10:30 p.m. JST; Tokyo MX and BS11 list 24:30, which means 12:30 a.m. on October 10.
  • International streaming: Crunchyroll includes the series in its Fall 2026 lineup and lists the subtitled release. Check your local catalog for availability.
  • Animation studio: J.C.STAFF.

A Certain Dark Item is now airing in Japan. The series began on October 9, 2026. Its late-night schedule can be confusing because Japanese listings sometimes use a 24-hour clock that runs past midnight: 24:30 on October 9 is 12:30 a.m. on October 10. The official schedule lists the Japanese stations and services; Crunchyroll's Fall 2026 lineup is the source for the international subtitle listing.

Where can you watch A Certain Dark Item?

Crunchyroll lists the show as part of its Fall 2026 season. Weekly streaming availability and release timing can differ between countries. Search the title in the Crunchyroll app or website while signed in from your location before planning around that time.

In Japan, the official anime site lists AT-X, Tokyo MX and BS11 for television. It also lists ABEMA and ABEMA Premium around the premiere, followed by additional subscription services from October 16, including d Anime Store, DMM TV, Hulu, U-NEXT and Prime Video. Those Japanese service listings do not establish availability in other countries. A platform's brand name can appear in more than one market with a different catalogue, so the local service page is the best check.

What is the story about?

The anime follows ITEM, a covert team operating in Academy City's criminal underworld. Its established members are Shizuri Mugino, Rikou Takitsubo, Frenda Seivelun and Saiai Kinuhata. An official introduction brings in Hanano Choubi, a new girl connected to the group as its fifth member. The premise shifts attention from the schools and public heroics often associated with the wider A Certain setting to the dangerous assignments carried out in its shadows.

The official first-episode synopsis gives readers a concrete starting point: ITEM is sent to investigate a company conducting ability-development research under the cover of medical treatment. That setup explains the team's role without giving away later turns. The story's central question is not simply whether ITEM can complete a job; the series also puts pressure on how a team stays together when its members work in a system that treats people as tools.

How does this fit into the A Certain franchise?

A Certain Dark Item is based on the ITEM-focused light novels by Kazuma Kamachi, with illustrations by Nilitsu, and belongs to the broader A Certain franchise. Its focus on Mugino and her team offers a different entry point from stories centered on Academy City's students. Viewers who already know the setting may recognize the city's split between its polished public image and its hidden operations. The anime's own official story and character pages introduce the people and conflict it needs; they do not publish a required watch order for the wider franchise.

Cast, staff and music

J.C.STAFF is producing the television anime. Tatsuyuki Nagai directs, Shogo Yasukawa handles series composition, and Shigeki Kimoto designs the characters. The official staff and cast page lists Ami Koshimizu as Shizuri Mugino, Aya Suzaki as Rikou Takitsubo, Maaya Uchida as Frenda Seivelun, Chinatsu Akasaki as Saiai Kinuhata and Hana Hishikawa as Hanano Choubi.

East Of Eden performs the opening theme, “Ghost Sequence,” and Kishida Kyoudan & The Akeboshi Rockets performs the ending theme, “Genshi Kuzushi (Meltdowner).”

The official site has released multiple videos. The embedded March teaser introduces the project, while the July main trailer reveals the premiere date and theme songs. For new episode announcements and schedule changes, use the official news page alongside the broadcast schedule.

What to check before watching

If you are outside Japan, use Crunchyroll's listing to confirm the show appears in your regional catalogue; the Japanese television timetable is not a worldwide streaming schedule. If you are following the broadcast in Japan, remember that the 24:30 entries fall just after midnight on the next calendar day. These distinctions answer the two practical questions readers often have after seeing the October 9 premiere date: which local service carries the show, and whether a late-night listing refers to Friday or Saturday in Japan.

How to read Friday’s schedule from North America

The Japanese stations are covering the same weekly series at different times. AT-X starts on Friday evening in Japan. Tokyo MX and BS11 group their 24:30 slot with Friday programming, even though a phone or calendar shows Saturday after midnight. If you are checking a Japanese program guide, start with Friday’s listings and scroll to the late-night block. If you are setting a local calendar event using Japan Standard Time, use Saturday at 12:30 a.m. for the Tokyo MX and BS11 slot.

For international streaming, start with your local Crunchyroll episode listing. A date given for a Japanese TV station describes that station’s broadcast. The local catalog is where you can see which episode your account can play. To keep up with the show, bookmark the series page after identifying the correct adaptation and check its newest numbered episode. If a weekly episode is missing, look for a service update or a Japanese schedule change before assuming the season has ended.

Can you start with A Certain Dark Item?

Episode 1 introduces the team through an assignment, which gives new viewers an immediate way to understand how the members work together. Mugino is the team’s Level 5 member. Takitsubo is the tracker, Frenda brings explosives and traps, and Kinuhata handles defense. As you watch, keep track of who finds the target, who protects the group and who decides how the mission proceeds. These roles are enough to follow the starting conflict even if Academy City’s terminology is new to you.

Longtime viewers may recognize characters and places, while newcomers can use the official story and episode index to place each chapter of the anime. Its introduction is a spoiler-light starting point. If you are watching week to week, open only the entry for the episode you are about to watch. Later entries describe situations you may prefer to discover on screen.

The seasonal listing identifies an international subtitled release. For an English dub, open the audio menu on the specific episode you plan to watch and check local availability. Check the subtitle options separately if you want the Japanese cast with English captions. A catalog entry can establish that a show is available to you while still leaving the audio options dependent on the episode.
