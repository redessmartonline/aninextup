A Certain Dark Item Release Date & Where to Watch

When does A Certain Dark Item premiere?

A Certain Dark Item premieres October 9, 2026. The series expands the A Certain universe by moving the focus away from Academy City's public-facing students and into the criminal organizations operating in its hidden side.

Where to watch

A Certain Dark Item is included in Crunchyroll's Fall 2026 international streaming lineup. Availability and episode timing can differ by territory, so viewers should check the listing in their regional Crunchyroll catalog.

What is A Certain Dark Item about?

The story follows ITEM, a four-girl elite unit that performs work Academy City cannot acknowledge publicly. Kinuhata Saiai specializes in nitrogen-based defense, Frenda Seivelun uses explosives and traps, Takitsubo Rikou tracks ability users, and Mugino Shizuri is one of Academy City's seven Level 5 espers.

The official introduction centers on the appearance of a mysterious “fifth” girl. Her encounter with ITEM draws the group into a deeper part of Academy City's underworld, making the series a darker companion story within the larger A Certain franchise.

What has been confirmed?

The official anime site has released a main promotional video and confirmed both opening and ending theme performers as part of the pre-premiere campaign. J.C.STAFF handles the animation production.

Official teaser PV

Watch the official teaser for A Certain Dark Item, released by the Toaru Project channel. The production has also announced a later main PV; see the official anime website for its latest promotional footage.

Japan broadcast and streaming schedule

The official schedule lists an October 9, 2026 broadcast at 10:30 p.m. JST on AT-X. Tokyo MX and BS11 list 12:30 a.m. JST on October 10 (shown as October 9 at 24:30 in Japanese listings). ABEMA Premium lists a midnight-after-October-8 window, while ABEMA's regular listing is midnight-after-October-9. A one-time advance stream of episode 1 took place on October 3. Additional Japanese platforms, including U-NEXT, Hulu Japan and d Anime Store, list October 16 availability. These Japan-specific services and times do not establish worldwide access. Check the official broadcast schedule for changes.

Confirmed staff and cast

Director Tatsuyuki Nagai leads the adaptation at J.C.STAFF, with series composition by Shogo Yasukawa, character design by Shigeki Kimoto and music by Maiko Iuchi. The confirmed cast includes Ami Koshimizu (Shizuri Mugino), Aya Suzaki (Rikou Takitsubo), Maaya Uchida (Frenda Seivelun), Chinatsu Akasaki (Saiai Kinuhata) and Hana Hishikawa (Choubi Kano).
