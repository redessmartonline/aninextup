When will Frieren Season 3 be released?

Frieren: Beyond Journey’s End Season 3 is scheduled to begin broadcasting in Japan in October 2027 on the Nippon TV network. The official March 27, 2026 announcement confirms the month and the Golden Land Arc. It does not give an exact day, broadcast hour or episode count.

What is the Golden Land Arc?

The next season is titled 黄金郷編, commonly rendered in English as the Golden Land Arc. Its announcement spotlights Macht of the Golden Land, described as the last and strongest of the Seven Sages of Destruction. That is the extent of the story detail needed to understand the teaser without discussing later manga events.

What is the latest official update?

The production began a Season 3 countdown-art campaign on September 26, 2026. Its first illustration, by character designer Keisuke Kojima, features Frieren, Fern and Stark and marks the year remaining until the planned broadcast. Where will Frieren Season 3 stream?

The Japanese announcement names Nippon TV’s network for television. It does not establish an international streaming service or a global simulcast timetable. Crunchyroll’s announcement coverage confirms that it carries the first two seasons; that alone is not a Season 3 distribution confirmation.

What should viewers know before the new season?

Frieren follows an elven mage’s life after the defeat of the Demon King, exploring what her journey with human companions means across different lifespans. The official introduction places Season 3 after the two completed seasons. Viewers catching up can follow those in release order before this continuation. Why the subtitle matters

The official name for the continuation is Season 3: Golden Land Arc. That subtitle identifies the story segment the production has chosen to adapt; it is more specific than saying only that Frieren is coming back. The announcement introduces Macht of the Golden Land as a key character and describes him as the last and strongest of the Seven Sages of Destruction. It confirms that Frieren and her companions will confront him, but it does not reveal the full shape of the season or its episode count.

This is an adaptation update, not a new series with an independent starting premise. Viewers who have not finished the earlier seasons may want to catch up before October 2027, but the current notice does not publish a viewing order or streaming plan. The official Japanese broadcast window is the confirmed detail; the international catalog remains a separate announcement to watch for. How precise is the October 2027 date?

It is a month, not a day. The anime’s official announcement says the third season will air from October 2027 on the Nippon TV network. It supplies no particular Sunday, weekday or time slot. The teaser visual is official artwork for the Golden Land storyline, but it is not a schedule card and cannot be used to infer the exact premiere.
