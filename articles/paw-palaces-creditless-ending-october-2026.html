Paw & Palaces Releases Creditless Ending Video Featuring “Chill Choose!”

The Japanese-dubbed edition of Paw & Palaces has unveiled a creditless ending sequence featuring Riko Azuna's song “Chill Choose!” The anime's official Japanese website confirmed the video and music credits on October 10, 2026.

What is new in the ending announcement?

The latest promotional update focuses on the closing sequence, not a new season or a change to the broadcast schedule. A creditless ending removes the overlaid production credits, allowing viewers to see the animation without text covering the imagery. The October 10 update on the Japanese production website also pointed fans toward an episode-five preview.

That makes the clip useful both for fans following the Japanese-dub release and for viewers interested in the show's music and artwork. It is a promotional sequence rather than a full episode; the embedded video below is the official ending clip referenced by the announcement.

Official creditless ending video released

The official website for Paw & Palaces (Japanese title: Shikin Gonyanbo: Shikinjo Neko Keibishitsu) announced the creditless ending video on October 10, alongside an episode-five preview. The new sequence highlights the series' feline cast and provides a clear look at the ending animation without the on-screen staff credits.

Does the creditless ending include a new episode?

No. A creditless ending is the show's existing closing animation presented without overlaid staff credits. The October 10 promotional video is separate from the episode-five preview mentioned in the same announcement. It also does not announce a new season or an international streaming date; viewers looking for full episodes should use an officially licensed service in their region.

Ending theme: “Chill Choose!” by Riko Azuna

The official announcement credits Riko Azuna with writing and composing “Chill Choose!”, with arrangement by NRP. The same notice separately identifies “Truly” as the opening theme, also performed by Azuna. This distinction matters because some secondary reports have mixed up the two songs.

About the Japanese dub

The series follows six cats who guard the cultural memories held within the Forbidden City's treasures. The Japanese-dubbed edition began broadcasting in Japan on October 10, 2026. Crunchyroll also carries the show in supported regions, although audio options and availability vary by territory.

What is Paw & Palaces about?

The fantasy series follows six cats connected to the Forbidden City's historical treasures. Its premise mixes animal characters with stories about protecting cultural memories, giving the ending sequence a different context from the action-heavy previews common to seasonal anime. The creditless release is an opportunity to focus on the closing animation itself.

Where can viewers watch it?

What does the music announcement confirm?

Riko Azuna is credited for both theme songs, but they serve different roles: “Chill Choose!” closes the episodes, while “Truly” opens them. The production notice names NRP as the ending track's arranger. Fans looking for the song featured in this video should therefore search for the ending title, not the opening.
