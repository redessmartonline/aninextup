Dragon Ball Super: Beerus Release Date, Streaming & Latest Details

Dragon Ball Super: Beerus is set to premiere in Japan on October 11, with a special advance screening at New York Comic Con on October 8.

WHERE TO WATCHStreaming details TBA

Dragon Ball Super: Beerus premieres in Japan on October 11, 2026. The official Dragon Ball site has confirmed the date as the series prepares to bring Goku, Beerus, Frieza and new threats back to the screen.

Release date

The Japanese premiere is scheduled for Sunday, October 11. Before the broadcast, the first episode will receive an advance screening during the Dragon Ball Super: Beerus special panel at New York Comic Con on October 8.

What is confirmed?

Akira Toriyama is credited for the original work, story and character design. The announced opening theme is “ONOGAMI” by MEGATERA・ZERO, while VK Blanka performs the ending theme.

Where to watch

Broadcasting and streaming are confirmed for October 2026, but availability can differ by territory. AniNextUp will update this section as platform details are officially confirmed for additional regions.
