Full Clearing Another World under a Goddess with Zero Believers: October 11 Premiere and Official Trailer

The fantasy anime arrives October 11, 2026, with a first promotional trailer and confirmed production staff.

By AniNextUp Editorial Team

October 11 premiere

Full Clearing Another World under a Goddess with Zero Believers begins airing in Japan on October 11, 2026. Crunchyroll includes it in its Fall 2026 streaming lineup, with availability depending on territory.

Story and staff

Makoto Takatsuki finds himself in a fantasy world with weak abilities compared with his classmates. He teams up with Noah, a goddess with no followers, to face its challenges. HORNETS is the animation studio, Toshinori Fukushima directs, Toko Machida handles series composition and Noriko Ito designs the characters.

Official trailer

The first promotional trailer, announced September 30, previews the opening theme “Mirai no Ashiato” by 310. View the trailer in the Crunchyroll announcement. An embedded player requires verification of the official YouTube channel and video ID.
