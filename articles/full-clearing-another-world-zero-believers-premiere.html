Why the weak-hero premise changes the journey

Makoto begins at a disadvantage beside classmates with stronger abilities, while his ally Noah lacks the followers normally associated with a powerful goddess. Their partnership gives the adventure an underdog structure: progress depends on how they use limited resources, not on an immediate display of overwhelming strength. The Japanese BS Asahi premiere is confirmed separately from Crunchyroll's region-dependent streaming availability.

October 11 premiere

Full Clearing Another World under a Goddess with Zero Believers premieres on BS Asahi in Japan on October 11, 2026 at 11:30 p.m. JST. TOKYO MX follows on October 12 and AT-X on October 13. Crunchyroll lists the series in its Fall 2026 lineup for select regions; check local availability.

Does the October 11 premiere apply to every platform?

No. October 11 at 11:30 p.m. JST is the announced BS Asahi Japanese television premiere. TOKYO MX and AT-X have later Japanese broadcast dates, while Crunchyroll's Fall 2026 lineup lists availability in supported regions without establishing one universal local premiere time. Viewers should distinguish the earliest Japanese broadcast from the schedule shown by their own streaming service.

Story, creative team and the central partnership

Makoto Takatsuki enters a fantasy world with weaker abilities than his classmates and joins forces with Noah, a goddess who has no followers. Their shared lack of status sets up an underdog partnership, although the synopsis does not establish how they overcome later obstacles. HORNETS handles animation; Toshinori Fukushima directs, Toko Machida oversees series composition and Noriko Ito designs the characters.

Official trailer

The first promotional trailer, announced September 30, previews the opening theme “Mirai no Ashiato” by 310. View the trailer in the Crunchyroll announcement. The promotional video is also listed by the official anime website.

Cast, music and streaming

Sohei Horikane voices Makoto Takatsuki, Ikumi Hasegawa voices Noah Titan, Yukina Shuto voices Lucy, and Hitomi Sekine voices Aya Sasaki. The opening theme is “Mirai no Ashiato” by 310, and the ending is “Fall in Dream” by Suupeas.

In Japan, d Anime Store, U-NEXT and Anime Hodai begin streaming October 11 at 11:30 p.m. JST. Additional Japanese services start October 17. International availability and release times vary; consult Crunchyroll in your region.
