Has the anime premiered?

Full Clearing Another World under a Goddess with Zero Believers began its Japanese television and same-time streaming run on Sunday, October 11, 2026. The confirmed first broadcast is BS Asahi at 11:30 p.m. Japan Standard Time (JST). That is 2:30 p.m. UTC on October 11, but it is not a universal streaming time: platforms in other countries publish their own schedules, and the Japanese time should not be mistaken for a local Crunchyroll listing.

The official anime site lists a weekly Sunday 11:30 p.m. JST slot for BS Asahi and the Japanese services d Anime Store, U-NEXT and Anime Hodai. Its schedule also lists TOKYO MX from Monday, October 12 at midnight, AT-X from Tuesday, October 13 at 9 p.m., and additional Japanese services from Saturday, October 17 at 11:30 p.m. Those are Japan schedules. Viewers outside Japan should check the title page in their local service account for current territory, subtitle, dub and episode availability.

What is the story about?

Makoto Takatsuki is a high-school student and avid role-playing-game player who is transported to another world with his class after an accident. The other students receive powerful abilities; Makoto instead gets weak stats and the lowly designation of apprentice mage. He is not introduced as a secretly unbeatable hero. His game knowledge matters because he studies rules, trains patiently and looks for practical uses for limited skills. That changes when Makoto meets Noah, a minor goddess with no believers. She asks him to become her first follower. The official anime site frames the pair's journey as a difficult fantasy adventure: Makoto must survive in a world where faith has consequences, while Noah has her own hidden past. The unusual title is a plot premise, not a claim that the anime is a game adaptation. It is based on Isle Osaki's light novels; the official site separately credits a manga adaptation by Shiroi Hakuto.

What does the first episode set up?

The official guide calls episode one “Makoto Takatsuki Becomes a Believer.” Its synopsis explains why Makoto is left behind while classmates with stronger gifts are recruited by different countries, and how the overlooked apprentice mage encounters Noah. Episode two moves him to Macallan, where he registers as an adventurer and takes on quests. The third listing introduces Lucy, an elf mage who becomes his first party companion. These summaries help new viewers understand the starting arc without guessing how much of the books the season adapts. Related reading: Gundam SEED FREEDOM ZERO: Release Date, Viewing Order & Cast.

The appeal is the contrast between familiar RPG language and a protagonist who cannot solve everything with a high-level skill. Makoto studies rules, tests limited abilities and relies on allies. Noah's lack of followers also gives the goddess a vulnerable position. Their relationship makes faith and trust part of the adventure's progression.

Cast, staff and music

Sohei Horikane voices Makoto, Ikumi Hasegawa plays Noah, Yukina Shuto is Lucy and Hitomi Sekine is Aya Sasaki. Supporting cast includes Masaaki Mizunaka as Michio Fujiwara, Lynn as Nina, Kensho Ono as Ryosuke Sakurai, Sayumi Suzushiro as Sophia and Rumi Okubo as Marie. The official site lists Toshinori Fukushima as director, Toko Machida for series composition and scripts, Noriko Ito for character design, AstroNoteS for music and HORNETS for animation production. The first PV offers an early listen to “Mirai no Ashiato,” a useful way to hear how the production presents its fantasy adventure.

This is separate from the Crunchyroll article date and Japanese broadcast time. Light novel, manga and anime: which should you start with?

J-Novel Club's official English listing describes the novels as the source: Makoto has weak stats, an apprentice-mage class and a ten-year life limit, while Noah's blessing gives him a goal. The listing is useful if you want the longer prose version and volume-by-volume continuation. The manga is a separate adaptation, illustrated by Hakuto Shitori in English publication; it offers a comics format. The anime adds voice performances, music and a weekly schedule. Official materials do not say it covers every novel volume, so do not assume the season completes the story.

No official guide maps anime episodes to novel volumes, so a post-finale starting point is unconfirmed. Overlap's volume 13 listing says it was released in Japan on April 20, 2026; that confirms print continuation, not the anime's stopping point.

What has not been confirmed?

The official pages reviewed confirm the Japanese schedule, named cast and staff, theme songs and first episode guide. They do not state a total episode count, final broadcast date, or universal international release time. Nor is an English dub schedule announced. Treat service availability shown in your country as the reliable answer rather than converting Japan's slot into a promise for every region.
