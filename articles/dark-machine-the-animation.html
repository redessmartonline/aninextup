How the game premise creates the central conflict

Kairi's search for Senki gives the journey through Golden Valley a goal beyond winning matches. His encounter with Rudra draws him into DARK MACHINE's competitive environment, where understanding the game and the people involved may matter as much as the competition itself. That is a reading of the published premise, not a prediction of match outcomes or later plot twists.

Why two different premiere dates can appear

Fuji TV writes the first broadcast as Tuesday, October 13 at 25:45 JST, using the late-night television convention. On a standard calendar that is Wednesday, October 14 at 1:45 a.m. JST. Crunchyroll separately lists October 13 for its simulcast with regional times in its current weekly schedule. International viewers should use their regional platform schedule rather than converting the Fuji TV slot into an assumed worldwide release time.

When does DARK MACHINE THE ANIMATION premiere?

DARK MACHINE THE ANIMATION premieres October 13, 2026. Fuji TV lists the first broadcast for Tuesday at 25:45 JST, which is 1:45 a.m. JST on October 14 in standard clock notation. Kansai TV follows on October 15 at 26:45 JST, while BS Fuji begins October 20 at 24:30 JST. Broadcast times may change.

What time does DARK MACHINE release on Crunchyroll?

The October 11 weekly schedule lists Episode 1 for October 13 with regional times. Use the column for your country rather than treating the Japanese TV slot as the streaming hour.

Crunchyroll lists October 13 for the subtitled simulcast, with regional airtimes listed in its current weekly calendar. Use the current regional timetable; the Japanese television hour does not establish one global streaming time.

What viewers should know about Golden Valley

Golden Valley is the setting where Kairi's search for Senki brings him into contact with Rudra and the DARK MACHINE competition. The announced setup gives the protagonist two overlapping motivations: finding a person and learning to navigate a competitive game environment. That distinction may help newcomers follow the story without assuming that winning matches is his only objective. The official premise does not disclose how his search ends.

Where to watch

Crunchyroll has confirmed the anime for North America, Central America, South America, Europe, Africa, Oceania, the Middle East, CIS and India. In Japan, unlimited streaming begins progressively on October 14 at 12:00 JST on d Anime Store, Hulu, Lemino, DMM TV, FOD, Bandai Channel, U-NEXT, Anime Hodai, Amazon Prime Video, Anime Times, TELASA, J:COM STREAM, milplus and ABEMA Premium. Free latest-episode distribution is also scheduled through ABEMA, TVer and Niconico.
