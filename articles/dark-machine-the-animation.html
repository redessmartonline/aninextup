What is the difference between the broadcast dates?

Japanese television schedules often label programming after midnight as part of the previous broadcast day. Fuji TV's “October 13, 25:45” therefore means 1:45 a.m. on October 14 in Japan, while Kansai TV's October 15 at 26:45 means 2:45 a.m. on October 16. BS Fuji's October 20 at 24:30 corresponds to 12:30 a.m. on October 21. The official project schedule lists these station starts separately and notes that times can change.

Those conversions describe the Japanese broadcasts, not a promise that a streaming episode appears at the same moment everywhere. A service can show a date in a viewer's local time zone, which may be the previous calendar day. Use the current listing for the platform and country where you watch when planning each episode. Who are Kairi, Rudra and Senki?

Fuji TV's series introduction says Kairi travels to Golden Valley after his grandfather's final request: find Senki. There he encounters Rudra, who is being pursued by a faction connected to Ajima. That meeting leads Kairi into the competition and brings his own abilities into view.

Senki is a title associated with the strongest DARK MACHINE player; Ajima is identified as its seventh holder in the broadcaster's premise. That detail helps explain why Kairi's search leads toward someone with influence in Golden Valley. These are introductory details from the official synopsis, so the outcome of the search remains for the episodes to reveal.

Japanese cast and creative team

Fuji TV credits Kensho Ono as Kairi and Miyuki Sawashiro as Rudra. Its cast list also names Koki Uchiyama as Shin, Riho Sugiyama as Blanc and Yuichi Nakamura as Ajima.
