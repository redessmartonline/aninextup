New Anime on Netflix in October 2026: What to Watch

AniNextUp’s guide to notable anime releases on Netflix during October 2026.

By AniNextUp Editorial Team · How we verify releases

WHERE TO WATCHNetflix

Netflix’s October 2026 anime slate is led by several confirmed returns. Netflix has announced The Ramparts of Ice Season 2 for October 1 PT / October 2 JST, Blue Box Season 2 for October 4 with weekly episodes, and My Happy Marriage Season 2 Special Episodes for October 25.

October highlights

  • The Ramparts of Ice Season 2 — October 1 PT / October 2 JST, new episodes weekly
  • Ranma 1/2 Season 3 — early October
  • Blue Box Season 2 — October 4, new episodes weekly
  • Rooster Fighter — October 10
  • Cyberpunk: Edgerunners 2 — October 20
  • My Happy Marriage Season 2 Special Episodes — October 25

What to watch first

Blue Box Season 2 is one of the month’s major weekly returns, while The Ramparts of Ice Season 2 opens the month with another romance-focused sequel. Later in October, My Happy Marriage adds special episodes on October 25.

Weekly releases vs. one-day drops

Not every Netflix anime follows the same release model. Blue Box Season 2 and The Ramparts of Ice Season 2 are confirmed for weekly episodes, while specials and other titles can use different schedules. AniNextUp tracks each title separately so a monthly Netflix list does not imply that every show drops its full season at once.
