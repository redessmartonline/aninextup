October’s release pattern, checked against Netflix

By October 23, the first two weekly premieres in Netflix’s romance lineup have already begun. The Ramparts of Ice Season 2 started October 1 in Pacific Time (October 2 in Japan) and is listed with weekly episodes; Blue Box Season 2 began October 4 and also follows a weekly schedule. Those dates refer to the first episode, not a full-season drop. For a viewer planning a weekend watch, the distinction changes whether the series can be binged or needs a week-by-week follow-up.

My Happy Marriage Season 2 — Special Episodes was scheduled for October 25. Netflix labels these as special episodes, so they should not be confused with the start of a new numbered season. Cyberpunk: Edgerunners 2 arrived October 20 as a separate Netflix title; its official listing describes a standalone ten-episode story. The anime releases on different dates and formats even though all appear under the month’s anime searches.

Which October release is a standalone story?

Netflix describes Cyberpunk: Edgerunners 2 as a standalone, ten-episode story featuring a new group of mercenaries in Night City. Blue Box Season 2 and The Ramparts of Ice Season 2 continue their respective series with weekly releases. Netflix's October 25 My Happy Marriage listing instead identifies special episodes connected to its second season. Checking that label helps returning viewers find the right entry in their watchlist and avoids treating the specials as an announced third season.
