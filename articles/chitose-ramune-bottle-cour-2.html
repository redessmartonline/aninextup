A simple viewing order for returning fans

Episode 13.5 is a recap of earlier events, not the first new story installment of the second cour. Anyone returning after the broadcast break can watch the recap as a refresher, then continue with the regular story episodes beginning October 13. Because services may file specials separately, checking episode titles is more reliable than assuming every numbered entry advances the plot.

What to watch for in Cour 2

The announced continuation moves from the Asuka storyline toward material involving Haru Aomi and the girls' basketball club. That gives returning viewers two useful points of orientation: where the earlier character relationships stand, and how the focus shifts as the next arc develops. The promotional video previews the continuation, but it should not be treated as proof of an unannounced episode-by-episode adaptation order.

When does Chitose Is in the Ramune Bottle Cour 2 begin?

Cour 2 begins October 13, 2026. The official schedule lists AT-X on Tuesdays at 11:00 p.m. JST, while TOKYO MX, BS11, Sun TV and KBS Kyoto air it at 24:30. Other Japanese stations follow at later times.

Is there an episode before Cour 2?

Yes. The official site confirms that episode 13.5, a recap of the story so far, airs during the week before the Cour 2 premiere. The regular second-cour story then resumes on October 13.

How to distinguish the recap from new episodes

The schedule distinguishes recap episode 13.5 from the October 13 continuation. On streaming services, specials may be numbered differently, so checking the episode title is more reliable than treating the recap as the first new story installment. Returning viewers can use the recap to refresh the Asuka storyline before following the new cour.

What comes next?

The second cour continues the Asuka storyline and moves into material centered on Haru Aomi, a member of the girls' basketball club. The second Cour 2 PV previews both parts of the continuing story.
