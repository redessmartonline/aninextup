Quick answer: Cour 2 is already underway

Chitose Is in the Ramune Bottle Cour 2 began in Japan on October 13, 2026. The official schedule lists weekly Tuesday broadcasts, with different local stations starting at different times. It is the continuation of the same split-cour adaptation, rather than an announcement of a separately titled second season. The official site also scheduled episode 13.5, a recap of the first part, for the week before the new story resumed.

What time does Chitose Is in the Ramune Bottle Cour 2 air?

The Japanese network schedule lists AT-X at 11:00 p.m. every Tuesday, with repeat broadcasts Thursday at 11:00 a.m. and Monday at 5:00 p.m. TOKYO MX, BS11, Sun TV and KBS Kyoto list a Tuesday 24:30 start; Fukui Broadcasting follows at 24:59, and TV Aichi at 26:05. These late-night labels refer to the following calendar day: 24:30 Tuesday means 12:30 a.m. Wednesday in Japan. Use the station’s stated broadcast day when comparing schedules, and check the official page for changes before planning around a live airing.

What is episode 13.5, and where should returning viewers start?

Episode 13.5 is an official recap of the first cour, not the next numbered chapter of new story. The anime’s ON AIR page says it aired in the week before the Cour 2 premiere. Viewers who watched the earlier episodes can continue with the October 13 broadcast; anyone who wants a refresher can look for the recap separately, because streaming services may file specials differently from regular episodes. The official news archive later listed advance cuts and summaries for episodes 14, 15 and 16, giving readers a way to follow the weekly rollout without relying on unofficial episode numbering. What story does Cour 2 cover?

The second PV confirms two useful story signposts. It returns to the Asuka storyline introduced in the first cour and previews the “Haru” arc, centered on Haru Aomi, a girl in the school’s basketball club. The anime’s official introduction frames this part of the story around the end of spring, new encounters and setbacks, and young people pursuing dreams they cannot let go. That gives returning viewers context without claiming an episode-by-episode adaptation order that the production has not announced. The official news feed’s October 23 update was for episode 16’s preview images and synopsis, the latest weekly episode item listed when this guide was checked. What is the series about, and why is Fukui part of it?

The story follows Saku Chitose, a second-year student at Fujishi High School who is academically capable, athletic and socially confident. His popularity makes him admired by some classmates and a target of gossip from others. In the first story arc, a teacher asks Saku to help a classmate who has become withdrawn; that assignment complicates the polished image people have of him and gives the series room to explore how teenagers see one another. The official anime describes Fukui as the setting. That detail is more than a location label: the production’s official pages and local collaboration materials connect the school-life story to real places and community activity in the prefecture.

Who voices the main cast, and who makes the anime?

Shogo Sakata voices Saku Chitose. The central cast also includes Manaka Iwami as Yuko Hiiragi, Hina Yomiya as Yua Uchida, Rumi Okubo as Haru Aomi, Ikumi Hasegawa as Yuzuki Nanase and Chika Anzai as Asuka Nishino. Yuji Tokuno directs the series at feel., with series composition by Naruhisa Arakawa, character designs by Sumie Kinoshita and music by Yoshiaki Fujisawa. Cour 2’s opening theme is “Saku” by Cider Girl, and its ending theme is “Dokonimo Ikenai Mama” by aruma. The official staff-and-cast page is the best reference for romanization and credit updates. Is Cour 2 the same as Season 2, and how many episodes are there?

The official Japanese campaign calls this “Cour 2,” meaning the second broadcast block of the split-cour TV adaptation. It is reasonable to search for it as “Part 2” or “season 2,” but the official release notice does not announce a new Season 2. The current schedule and news page identify weekly episodes through episode 16, but the material reviewed for this update does not state the final episode count. A split-cour label alone is not enough to calculate that number, so this guide leaves the total unconfirmed.

What is confirmed, and what remains unknown?

Confirmed details include the October 13 Japanese return, the weekly station and domestic streaming schedule, the recap’s placement, Cour 2’s principal story threads, the credited cast and staff, and the two theme songs. The official site links the anime to Hiromu’s light-novel series, illustrated by raemz and published under Shogakukan’s Gagaga Bunko imprint; its Books page lists the novels for readers who want to start from the source. The official broadcast page does not provide a single international release time, and the episode-count endpoint is not stated in the checked material. Keep those questions open until the rights holder or a regional distributor publishes an explicit answer.
