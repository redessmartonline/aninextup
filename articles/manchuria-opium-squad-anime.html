Manchuria Opium Squad Anime Officially Announced

The historical crime manga Manchuria Opium Squad is officially getting an anime adaptation. The first announcement visual is out, but the release date and production details have not been revealed yet.

RELEASE DATETBA

Is Manchuria Opium Squad getting an anime?

Yes. An anime adaptation of Manchuria Opium Squad was officially announced on October 5, 2026. The announcement was accompanied by a first anime visual.

No release date has been announced. The initial announcement did not confirm a premiere year or season. AniNextUp will add a date only when the production committee or an official distribution partner confirms one.

Studio, staff and cast

The animation studio, main staff and voice cast are also TBA. Official information says additional production details will be announced through the anime's official website and social channels.

Where will it stream?

No streaming platform or international distributor has been announced. Any platform listing before an official announcement should be treated as unconfirmed.

What is Manchuria Opium Squad?

The crime-suspense story is set in Manchuria in 1937 and follows Isamu Higata, a former soldier who becomes involved in the illegal opium trade while trying to save his sick mother. The manga is written by Tsukasa Monma. Tsurushima is now handling the manga art, with Shikako credited for the original art design.

What was confirmed on October 5?

The confirmed news is the anime adaptation itself and the release of its announcement visual. A premiere date, format, studio, staff, cast and streaming service remain unannounced.
