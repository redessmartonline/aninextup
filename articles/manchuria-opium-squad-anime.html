What is Manchuria Opium Squad?

The crime-suspense story is set in Manchuria in 1937 and follows Isamu Higata, a former soldier who becomes involved in the illegal opium trade while trying to save his sick mother. The manga is written by Tsukasa Monma. Tsurushima is now handling the manga art, with Shikako credited for the original art design.

Why the historical setting matters

The manga's announced premise places Isamu Higata in Manchuria in 1937, with his mother's illness motivating his involvement in the illegal opium trade. That setting gives the crime story personal stakes and a specific historical backdrop, rather than presenting the trade as a generic fantasy conflict. The adaptation announcement does not yet explain which manga chapters the anime will cover or how it will depict those events; those details remain unknown.
