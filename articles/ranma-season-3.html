How to read the Japanese broadcast time

The 24:55 notation is a late-night television convention: it means 12:55 a.m. on the next calendar day, even though the program is listed under Saturday's broadcast schedule. Readers comparing Japanese listings with Netflix should therefore distinguish the broadcast day from the clock date, then check their regional Netflix catalog rather than assuming simultaneous worldwide availability.
