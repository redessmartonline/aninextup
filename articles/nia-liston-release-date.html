What a two-cour commitment tells viewers

Two consecutive cours indicate a longer planned television run than a typical single-cour premiere, but they do not establish an exact episode count without a separate announcement. The 9:25 p.m. TOKYO MX broadcast and 9:55 p.m. Japanese advance-streaming window also refer to different distribution methods. Neither is evidence that the series is available internationally on those same services.

When does Nia Liston premiere?

Nia Liston: The Merciless Maiden premieres October 6, 2026 on TOKYO MX, MBS and BS Nippon TV. The official site says the anime will run for two consecutive cours.

Japan broadcast and streaming schedule

TOKYO MX begins the series October 6 at 9:25 p.m. JST. MBS and BS Nippon TV also begin October 6 on their respective late-night schedules. ABEMA and d Anime Store receive the earliest domestic streaming window from 9:55 p.m. JST, with U-NEXT and Anime Hodai following October 9 and additional Japanese services from October 12.

Is international streaming confirmed?

HIDIVE’s official October schedule lists Nia Liston as a Tuesday simulcast starting October 6. That confirms HIDIVE in its supported territories; it does not establish worldwide access. Japan’s ABEMA, d Anime Store and other service dates remain separate.

What is Nia Liston about?

A legendary warrior who once defeated a god is reincarnated as the frail noble girl Nia Liston. Rather than settling into a quiet second life, Nia continues searching for powerful opponents while also becoming involved in school life and the fantasy world’s “Magic Vision” entertainment business.

Studio and staff

KONAMI animation produces the series. Motoki Nakanishi serves as director, Haruta Segi as assistant director, Gigaemon Ichikawa handles series composition, and Yuki Ito and Kana Tosaoka are credited with character design. Naoki Endo composes the music.

What the two-cour format does not establish

Two consecutive broadcast cours indicate a longer planned run, but the announcement does not specify an exact episode count. The Japanese television and streaming slots also do not establish an overseas simulcast time; HIDIVE’s October 6 simulcast announcement establishes a separate regional streaming release.

See the Fall 2026 anime calendar for more October premieres and the streaming hub for confirmed platform availability.

What the official Blu-ray listing confirms

The official Japanese anime website lists Blu-ray BOX 1 for March 24, 2027 and specifies that it contains episodes 1–12. That establishes the contents of the first home-video box, but it does not establish the total number of episodes across both cours. Readers should not turn a two-cour broadcast announcement into an unsupported full-series episode count.

Why the premise combines combat and entertainment

Nia's desire to challenge strong opponents sits alongside her involvement in the world's Magic Vision entertainment industry. Those two interests create a contrast between a warrior's appetite for battle and the public-facing life of a young noblewoman. This is AniNextUp's interpretation of the official synopsis, not a claim that any unannounced storyline or outcome is confirmed.

What the opening and ending songs add

The official production identifies Watame Tsunomaki's “Respawn!!” as the opening theme. It also identifies “Tokimeki Un Deux Trois♪” as the ending theme, performed by the character unit featuring Nia, Hildetora and Reliared. These credits provide a useful distinction between the show's action-oriented premise and its in-world Magic Vision entertainment motif, without implying anything about future episodes.
