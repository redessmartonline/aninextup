When does the Spirit Circle anime come out?

Spirit Circle begins broadcasting and streaming in December 2026. Twin Engine announced the adaptation on November 5, alongside a first promotional video. The project is planned for 18 episodes, with NAGOMI and EOTA handling animation. The announcement does not give a calendar day or weekly release timetable.

For readers building a watchlist, the useful distinction is between a confirmed month and a confirmed premiere date. December is official; a specific Friday, weekend or launch time would currently be an assumption. See our anime release calendar for other upcoming titles.

What is Spirit Circle about?

Fuuta Okeya is a schoolboy who sees ghosts and keeps a mark on his cheek covered by a bandage. His encounter with transfer student Kouko Ishigami turns that ordinary-looking detail into a threat: she recognizes him as an enemy from earlier lives. Their conflict leads into a mystery stretching across different identities and eras.

The appeal is the question behind that hostility. How much responsibility should someone carry for another lifetime? Viewers interested in a connected supernatural mystery have a different reason to follow this title than simply looking for a new fantasy setting. That is our spoiler-free reading of the premise, rather than a review of episodes.

Cast and production staff

The announced voices are Kazuki Ura as Fuuta, Anna Nagase as Kouko, Eri Yukimura as Rune and Yuichi Nakamura as East. Nagase also performs the ending theme, Koko ga Donna Sekai demo.

Tatsuo Sato is credited as director and series composer. Twin Engine describes the anime as his final directorial work; assistant director Kentaro Mizuno and the production team carried it through to completion after his death in April 2026.

Where can you watch Spirit Circle?

The Japanese announcement confirms broadcasting and streaming, but does not name services or establish availability for individual countries. It should not be read as confirmation of a Netflix, Crunchyroll or Prime Video release. English subtitles, dubbing and regional launch times remain details to verify when distribution information is published.

Can you read the manga in English?

Yes. Seven Seas publishes Spirit Circle in English, listing six volumes. Its sixth volume is the final volume, so readers can choose a completed source story while waiting for the adaptation.

Start with volume one if you want to meet Fuuta and Kouko without relying on an episode-to-chapter prediction. An 18-episode announcement alone does not tell us which chapters each episode will adapt or whether scenes will be rearranged.
