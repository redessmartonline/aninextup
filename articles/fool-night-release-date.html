Fool Night Release Date: November 26 on Netflix

Fool Night premieres worldwide on Netflix on November 26, 2026, with all episodes released together.

By AniNextUp Editorial Team · How we verify releases

WHERE TO WATCHNetflix · worldwide premiere

When does Fool Night release?

Netflix announces November 26, 2026 as the worldwide premiere, with all episodes released together. The announcement does not specify a local release hour.

What is Fool Night?

The series is a dystopian human drama produced by Sunrise and Shaft. Its future world depends on a technology that transforms people into plants as humanity struggles with a shortage of oxygen.

Where can you watch it?

Netflix is the announced exclusive streaming home. Check your local Netflix title page for language options and release timing. The announcement does not establish a universal hour or an episode count.

What the announcement means for viewers

One launch date, not a weekly schedule. Netflix's August 21 Anime NYC announcement says the entire series will become available on November 26. That makes this a batch release rather than a confirmed weekly rollout. Viewers planning a watch session can expect the episodes to arrive together, but Netflix has not supplied a precise release hour in that announcement.

Worldwide distribution does not mean identical viewing options. The announcement describes an exclusive global Netflix launch, but it does not provide a country-by-country list of audio, subtitles or local release times for this title. Check the title's Netflix listing in your region rather than assuming the same language catalog everywhere.

Why Fool Night stands out

The premise turns oxygen into a social and moral dilemma: people may undergo a transformation into plants in a world where humanity relies on the oxygen those plants produce. That setup makes the series more than a conventional post-apocalyptic survival story. Its central question is whether a person's chance to escape suffering can be separated from the needs of the wider community. This is AniNextUp's interpretation of the officially described premise, not an additional plot revelation.
