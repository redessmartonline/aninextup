The Moon on a Rainy Night (Amayo no Tsuki) will premiere as a TV anime in 2027. The October 7 update introduces the first PV, the voices of the leads and additional production details.

What changed?

Iori Saeki will voice Saki Kindaichi, while Sora Amamiya will voice Kanon Oikawa. Tomoe Makino directs at CompTown, Shogo Yasukawa handles series composition and scripts, Ayumi Nishihata is responsible for character design, and jizue composes the music.

What the official synopsis reveals about Saki and Kanon

According to the official anime introduction, Saki Kindaichi meets Kanon Oikawa on her way to a piano lesson shortly before entering high school. They reunite as classmates. Kanon is hard of hearing and has kept her distance from others, while Saki questions the assumptions made about her by people at school. Their gradually developing connection gives the story a character-focused premise rather than a mystery about a release-date reveal. What is The Moon on a Rainy Night about?

Based on Kuzushiro's manga, the story follows Saki Kindaichi and Kanon Oikawa, two high-school classmates whose relationship gradually develops after Saki meets Kanon on her way to a piano lesson shortly before high school begins. Kanon is hard of hearing, and the story places particular emphasis on communication, understanding and the distance between people.
