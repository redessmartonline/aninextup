The Moon on a Rainy Night Anime: 2027 Release, Trailer & Cast

The Moon on a Rainy Night is officially set for 2027, and its first full promotional video has revealed the two lead voice actors and more of the production team.

By AniNextUp Editorial Team · How we verify releases

RELEASE WINDOW2027 · Exact date TBA

The Moon on a Rainy Night (Amayo no Tsuki) will premiere as a TV anime in 2027. The October 7 update is the adaptation's most substantial reveal so far: its first PV introduces the voices of the leads and confirms additional production details.

What changed?

Iori Saeki will voice Saki Kindaichi, while Sora Amamiya will voice Kanon Oikawa. Tomoe Makino directs at CompTown, Shogo Yasukawa handles series composition and scripts, Ayumi Nishihata is responsible for character design, and jizue composes the music.

Why the 2027 window matters

The anime adaptation had already been announced, but the new 2027 broadcast window gives viewers a concrete year to track. An exact month and premiere date have not been announced, so any more specific release claim remains unconfirmed.

What is The Moon on a Rainy Night about?

Based on Kuzushiro's manga, the story follows Saki Kindaichi and Kanon Oikawa, two high-school classmates whose relationship gradually develops after an encounter on a rainy night. Kanon is hard of hearing, and the story places particular emphasis on communication, understanding and the distance between people.

Where to watch

A specific streaming service has not been announced for the 2027 TV broadcast. Kodansha previously said the anime is planned for streaming in various countries worldwide, but platform and territory details remain pending.

What remains unconfirmed?

The exact premiere date, broadcast season, episode count and specific streaming platforms have not been announced. AniNextUp will update this guide when official information becomes available.
