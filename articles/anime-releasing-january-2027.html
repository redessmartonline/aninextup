A Japanese broadcast time is not a worldwide streaming time. JST is UTC+9; convert only after your distributor confirms which release timetable applies. An absent platform in this roundup means it is not established here, rather than proof that no license exists.

Which January anime will stream on Netflix or Crunchyroll?

SAKAMOTO DAYS Season 2 is confirmed for Netflix in January. Crunchyroll has announced Shangri-La Frontier Season 3; consult its individual guide for the platform announcement. Sekiro’s dedicated guide also explains its announced Crunchyroll regions. Those service announcements do not establish a common worldwide release hour or identical language options.

For titles whose sources only establish Japanese television, the safest viewing plan is to wait for a regional distributor’s schedule. A domestic list of Japanese services should not be read as a worldwide license announcement.

January anime movies: Japan and overseas dates

Gundam SEED FREEDOM ZERO opens in Japan on January 15. Its North American announcement gives February 2027 as a separate theatrical window. Aikatsu Stars! Hoshiboshi no Progress opens in Japan on January 22; an overseas date or streaming premiere should not be inferred from that opening.

How to build your Winter 2027 watchlist

Start with the titles whose service is available in your country, then add January-only projects as provisional plans until their first-day schedules are published. Keep cinema releases separate from at-home viewing. Episode totals also need their own confirmation: a two-cour run describes television blocks, while Hasunosora’s seven-episode plan and Sekiro’s eight-episode version are explicit counts.

Frequently asked questions

Is this every anime airing in January 2027?

No. It is a selection of 14 confirmed releases with existing AniNextUp guides and linked primary sources. Other series and continuing weekly episodes may also air that month.

Does a Japanese premiere date mean I can stream it that day?

Only if your local distributor confirms it. The television schedule, international streaming timetable, dub release and theatrical opening can all differ.

Where are the trailers?

Open each title’s guide for its verified official videos. This roundup does not present a compilation video as an official trailer for all 14 productions.
