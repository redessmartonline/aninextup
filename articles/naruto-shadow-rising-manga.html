Naruto: Shadow Rising Release Date: Kishimoto’s New Manga

What is Naruto: Shadow Rising?

Shadow Rising is the name attached to a newly announced Naruto manga drawn by Masashi Kishimoto. The project was revealed during the Naruto “What’s Next?” panel at New York Comic Con on October 10, 2026, local time. The official Naruto recap, published the following day in Japan, describes it as a new manga and says the teaser that closed the panel showed manga storyboards. That distinction answers the first question readers are asking: this announcement concerns a comic, not a confirmed new television season.

Kishimoto created the original Naruto manga, which ran in Weekly Shonen Jump from 1999 to 2014. The official announcement does not yet say whether this will be a one-shot, a short series, a longer serialization, or a special chapter. It also does not name a magazine issue, digital service, publisher release date, or chapter count. Until the rights holders provide those details, descriptions such as “new series” should be treated cautiously.

When will Naruto: Shadow Rising be released?

No publication date has been announced. The official Naruto recap directs readers to the franchise’s Naruto Jump Super Stage at Jump Festa 2027 for further information. Jump Festa takes place at Makuhari Messe on December 19 and 20, 2026. The event’s official stage listing schedules the Naruto segment for Saturday, December 19, from 16:55 to 17:25 Japan Standard Time (JST, UTC+09:00). That is a confirmed event slot, not a manga release date. The stage could provide news, but its schedule alone does not guarantee which specific details will be revealed.

There is no reliable official basis yet for a 2026 or 2027 chapter premiere, a weekly or monthly schedule, or an English-language launch. Readers looking for a date should check the official Naruto news page and Jump Festa’s Naruto stage listing after the event. A retailer listing or an unsourced social post should not be confused with a publisher’s announcement. Related reading: Naruto × Ninkū Anime: Four Episodes, Official Teaser and Release Status.

What does the official teaser reveal?

The super teaser is brief and uses illustrated panels rather than a full plot synopsis. The official recap describes six previous Hokage faces carved into the mountain above the Hidden Leaf Village, followed by a seventh stone face still under construction. It then shows Naruto holding a Hokage hat, Kakashi smiling, Sasuke standing, Sakura looking fondly at a baby, and a clash between Naruto and Sasuke. The footage shows these characters but does not identify the baby or explain the conflict.

The developing monument and the characters’ appearances have prompted timeline theories. They may be useful clues to discuss, but the official site has not confirmed a chronology, a connection to a particular Boruto arc, or whether the images depict sequential events. For now, the accurate answer to “Where does it fit?” is that the official synopsis and timeline remain unannounced.

Is Shadow Rising a new Naruto anime?

No anime adaptation of Shadow Rising has been announced. The NYCC recap covers several separate Naruto projects, and it is easy to mix them up because they were promoted during the same panel. The new manga is drawn by Kishimoto. Separately, the franchise announced NARUTO×NINKŪ, a four-episode crossover anime supervised by Kishimoto and NINKŪ creator Koji Kiriyama. The crossover has its own teaser, premise, and production announcement. Neither project should be presented as the other, and the manga teaser is not confirmation that Shadow Rising will be animated.

The panel also included additional franchise news, including the NARUTO CARD GAME. Searchers should distinguish the manga from the separately announced NINKŪ anime.

Where will the manga be published, and where can English readers read it?

The official recap has not named the magazine, app, print volume, or digital platform for the new story. It has not announced translations, regional distributors, preorder details, or an English release. Because the original Naruto manga was serialized in Weekly Shonen Jump, readers may expect a familiar route, but that history is not confirmation of the new project’s publication format. Wait for a specific announcement from the franchise or publisher before relying on a platform or retailer date.

When details arrive, readers should check the first chapter date, release frequency, format and regional digital availability.

What is confirmed, and what is still unknown?

Confirmed by the official Naruto announcement: Kishimoto is drawing a new Naruto manga; the announcement teaser includes the Hokage monument and members of Team 7; and the franchise points to the Naruto Jump Super Stage at Jump Festa 2027 for follow-up information. The official event schedule lists that stage for December 19, 2026, 16:55–17:25 JST. Not confirmed as of October 26, 2026: a manga release date, serialization length, magazine or app, full story, timeline, English edition, or anime adaptation. The exact upload time and timezone of the teaser are also not stated on the official recap.
