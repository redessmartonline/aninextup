Demon's Crest Anime Release Date, Prime Video & Official PV

Demon's Crest premieres worldwide November 6, 2026, exclusively on Prime Video.

When does Demon's Crest release?

Demon's Crest premieres worldwide on November 6, 2026. Unlike many seasonal anime with separate Japanese and international schedules, the official site explicitly describes the release as a worldwide Prime Video exclusive.

Where to watch Demon's Crest

Prime Video has worldwide exclusive streaming rights beginning November 6. That makes the platform the confirmed destination for the anime rather than a Japan-only broadcaster followed by an unconfirmed international simulcast.

What is Demon's Crest about?

Sixth-grader Yuuma Ashihara takes part in a test of Actual Magic, a full-sensory full-dive VRMMO. After Yuuma and his classmates defeat a boss and try to log out, the boundary between the game's world and reality begins to collapse.

Who is making the anime?

Production I.G is producing the adaptation of Reki Kawahara's light novel series. Kawahara is also known for Sword Art Online and Accel World. The official promotional campaign pairs the virtual-world imagery of Actual Magic with an increasingly unsettling real-world setting.

Opening and ending themes

Ado performs the opening theme “Shinka.” The production later confirmed Soala's “ReaL” as the ending theme alongside its second promotional video.
