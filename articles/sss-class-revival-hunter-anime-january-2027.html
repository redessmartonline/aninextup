When does SSS-Class Revival Hunter premiere?

January 2027 is the confirmed Japanese television premiere window for SSS-Class Revival Hunter (Japanese title Shishite Ikiru SSS-kyu Hunter). The official site lists Fuji TV's +Ultra programming block and other channels. An exact premiere day and episode count have not yet been announced.

What does the second trailer confirm about the premiere?

The October 11, 2026 promotional video introduces more of Kim Gongja's story and its cast, but no exact broadcast date. The production currently specifies January 2027 on Fuji TV's +Ultra block and other Japanese channels. An episode count and international streaming schedule remain unannounced. Second official trailer reveals new characters

On October 11, 2026, the production published its second promotional video after a New York Comic Con preview. YouTube’s original metadata credits HAYATE’s channel and records the upload at 6:30:00 p.m. PDT (UTC−07:00) on October 10—10:30:00 a.m. JST on October 11. That is the video’s upload timestamp, not an anime broadcast time. The trailer follows F-rank hunter Kim Gongja as he gains a rare ability tied to his own death and encounters the Sword Emperor, rival hunters and powerful guild leaders.

Confirmed Japanese voice cast

The latest announcement adds eight performers to the two previously confirmed leads:

  • Takeo Otsuka as Kim Gongja.
  • Satoshi Hino as Sword Emperor.
  • Toshiki Masuda as Flame Emperor.
  • Megumi Han as Witch of the Black Dragon.
  • Sayaka Kinoshita as Paladin.
  • Risa Shimizu as Countess.
  • Makoto Furukawa as Poisonous Viper.
  • Natsumi Fujiwara as Inquisitor.
  • Yuka Iguchi as Master Alchemist.
  • Shunsuke Sakuya as Blade Saint.

These names reflect the official October 11 cast announcement, not speculation based on the webcomic's character roster.

Who performs the ending theme?

The anime's ending theme is a collaboration between THE SPELLBOUND and BOOM BOOM SATELLITES. The artists were announced on October 11, but the song title and full audio were not revealed at that time. No opening theme should be assumed from this announcement.

Studio, director and production staff

STUDIO fu is producing the anime, with Hiroaki Sakurai directing, Sayuri Oba handling series scripts, and Takahiro Yoshimatsu designing the characters. Ryo Kawasaki and Tomoyuki Kono are credited for the music. These are the announced production roles as of October 2026.

Where can you watch SSS-Class Revival Hunter?

Crunchyroll has confirmed streaming in North, Central and South America, Europe, Africa, Oceania, the Middle East, the CIS, the Indian subcontinent, Indonesia, the Philippines, Malaysia and Singapore. This is a distribution plan, not an exact same-day release timetable: the Japanese broadcast date, local episode time and subtitle availability are separate details. Crunchyroll has not published territory-specific launch times, so check the local catalog when the January schedule becomes more specific.

What is SSS-Class Revival Hunter about?

A mysterious tower filled with monsters attracts hunters chasing wealth and prestige. Kim Gongja begins as an overlooked F-rank hunter who envies the powerful Flame Emperor. When he gains an extraordinary S-rank ability, he discovers a dangerous condition: the power activates only when he dies. Together with the Sword Emperor, he begins a high-stakes climb that challenges the usual rules of the hunter genre.

What does Gongja’s death-triggered skill do?

Tapas’s official English webcomic listing clarifies the rule behind the hook: Gongja receives a skill that copies another hunter’s ability, but the exchange costs his life. After the Flame Emperor kills him, Gongja copies a second power that sends him back in time on death. The two linked rules explain the revival premise and why each fatal attempt can change his next move. This is source-material context; it does not confirm which chapters the anime will adapt.

The anime adapts a Korean fantasy story that originated as a web novel by Sinnoa and later became a popular webcomic. The animated adaptation is a Japanese television production.

Where can you read the source material?

Tapas hosts the official English webcomic listing and credits Neida, Sinnoa and Bill K; Yen Press lists its English print volumes. These give readers options to start online or collect physical books, but they describe the source comic rather than the anime’s episode order. The production has not said how many chapters or story arcs the January 2027 season will cover, so a volume count is not yet a viewing guide.
