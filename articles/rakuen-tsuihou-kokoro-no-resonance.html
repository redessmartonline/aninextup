When does Kokoro no Resonance release?

November 13, 2026 in Japanese theaters. An international streaming date has not been announced, so AniNextUp is not listing a global streaming platform yet.

Watch the official PV

What is the movie about?

The new film continues the world of 2014's Expelled from Paradise. Gabriel and other agents pursue the mysterious Fallen, former security agents who escaped to Earth after the events involving Angela Balzac.

Staff and cast

Seiji Mizushima returns as director, with Gen Urobuchi writing the screenplay, Masatsugu Saito handling character design and NARASAKI composing the music. Toei Animation produces the film.

Where to watch

The November 13 date currently refers to the Japanese theatrical release. International theatrical and streaming availability remains to be announced.
