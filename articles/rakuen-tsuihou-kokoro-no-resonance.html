New main trailer and key visual revealed

The official film project has released a new main trailer and key visual for Rakuen Tsuihou: Kokoro no Resonance, the sequel to Expelled from Paradise. The footage follows Gabriel and fellow agents as they investigate the Fallen, while a preview of the new theme song accompanies the action. Character designer Masatsugu Saito drew the new key visual.

The official site dates its main trailer announcement to October 12 and its theme song announcement to October 13, 2026, in Japan. Neither announcement specifies an exact publication time. The film remains scheduled for Japanese theaters on November 13, 2026.

“Beyond the Night” theme song: artists and release dates

TeddyLoid wrote, composed and arranged “Beyond the Night.” It features Ayane Sakura, who voices Gabriel, and Nagi Inoue of Nogizaka46. The song arrives digitally on November 11, 2026, with a CD release scheduled for November 18, 2026.

These updates concern the Japanese theatrical opening and music release. International cinema and streaming dates have not been confirmed in the announcements. Related reading: Noa Is My Senior, and My Friend Anime: 2027 Release, Main Cast and First Visual.

What connects the sequel to Expelled from Paradise

The conflict between DEVA's virtual society and people living on Earth's surface remains a useful lens for approaching Kokoro no Resonance. The sequel's pursuit of the Fallen suggests that crossing between those worlds continues to carry political and personal consequences. Returning creative staff offers continuity, but the announced premise should not be mistaken for a full account of the film's plot.

Do viewers need to see the first film?

Kokoro no Resonance is explicitly presented as a sequel to Expelled from Paradise. Its returning DEVA-versus-Earth setting and references to earlier events mean the 2014 film offers relevant background for Gabriel's investigation of the Fallen. The official synopsis does not establish that prior viewing is mandatory, but watching the first movie is the clearest way to understand the world and its history before the new theatrical release.

Planning to watch outside Japan

November 13 is the confirmed Japanese theatrical opening, not a worldwide streaming premiere. No international subscription-platform date is established by that announcement. When does Kokoro no Resonance release?

Rakuen Tsuihou: Kokoro no Resonance opens in Japanese theaters on November 13, 2026. It is the long-awaited sequel to the 2014 science-fiction anime film Expelled from Paradise.

What is the movie about?

The sequel returns to the conflict between the virtual society of DEVA and life on Earth. Gabriel and other agents pursue the mysterious Fallen—former security personnel who escaped to the surface after the events involving Angela Balzac—expanding the world established by the first movie.

Who is making the sequel?

Seiji Mizushima returns as director and Gen Urobuchi writes the screenplay. Masatsugu Saito handles character design, NARASAKI composes the music and Toei Animation produces the film, reuniting core creative staff from the original project. Where to watch

The November 13 date currently refers specifically to the Japanese theatrical release. An international streaming date has not been announced, so AniNextUp is keeping theatrical and streaming availability separate until an official platform announcement is made.

Who voices Gabriel and the other characters?

The film's official cast list names Ayane Sakura as Gabriel, Saori Hayami as Arma and Yoshino Aoyama as Danelle. Sally Amaki voices Raguel, Sumire Morohoshi voices Penemue and Rie Kugimiya returns as Angela Balzac. Sakura also appears on the theme song in character as Gabriel, connecting the music announcement to the film's lead.

These credits come from the Japanese production's announced cast. They do not establish an English dub or the language options that might accompany a future international release. Those details will depend on any later distributor or platform announcement. Related reading: Witch on the Holy Night Movie: Release Date and Theaters.

How does the Fallen investigation begin?

The official synopsis places the story in the year 2400, when humanity lives in the digital world of DEVA. After events associated with Angela's rebellion, a group of security agents escapes to Earth with physical bodies and hardware containing their personal data. Known as the Fallen, they become the subject of a search ordered by DEVA.

Gabriel and her fellow agents must pursue that assignment on the surface, where the effects of a nanohazard remain. The setup gives returning viewers a connection to Angela's earlier story while introducing a new investigation; the official synopsis does not reveal its outcome.

What does the main trailer show?

Aniplex says the main trailer uses new footage centered on Gabriel's perspective and concludes with a look at the new Mega Arhan machine. The promotional video offers a preview of the sequel's action and theme song, but it is not a substitute for a complete synopsis or a release announcement outside Japan.
