New main trailer and key visual revealed

The official film project has released a new main trailer and key visual for Rakuen Tsuihou: Kokoro no Resonance, the sequel to Expelled from Paradise. The footage follows Gabriel and fellow agents as they investigate the Fallen, while a preview of the new theme song accompanies the action. Character designer Masatsugu Saito drew the new key visual.

The official site dates its main trailer announcement to October 12 and its theme song announcement to October 13, 2026, in Japan. Neither announcement specifies an exact publication time. The film remains scheduled for Japanese theaters on November 13, 2026.

“Beyond the Night” theme song: artists and release dates

TeddyLoid wrote, composed and arranged “Beyond the Night.” It features Ayane Sakura, who voices Gabriel, and Nagi Inoue of Nogizaka46. The song arrives digitally on November 11, 2026, with a CD release scheduled for November 18, 2026. Related reading: Noa Is My Senior, and My Friend Anime: 2027 Release, Main Cast and First Visual.

These updates concern the Japanese theatrical opening and music release. International cinema and streaming dates have not been confirmed in the announcements.

What connects the sequel to Expelled from Paradise

The conflict between DEVA's virtual society and people living on Earth's surface remains a useful lens for approaching Kokoro no Resonance. The sequel's pursuit of the Fallen suggests that crossing between those worlds continues to carry political and personal consequences. Returning creative staff offers continuity, but the announced premise should not be mistaken for a full account of the film's plot.

Do viewers need to see the first film?

Kokoro no Resonance is explicitly presented as a sequel to Expelled from Paradise. Its returning DEVA-versus-Earth setting and references to earlier events mean the 2014 film offers relevant background for Gabriel's investigation of the Fallen. The official synopsis does not establish that prior viewing is mandatory, but watching the first movie is the clearest way to understand the world and its history before the new theatrical release. Planning to watch outside Japan

November 13 is the confirmed Japanese theatrical opening, not a worldwide streaming premiere. No international subscription-platform date is established by that announcement. Fans outside Japan should look for a separate distributor or cinema notice before making release-day plans.

When does Kokoro no Resonance release?

Rakuen Tsuihou: Kokoro no Resonance opens in Japanese theaters on November 13, 2026. It is the long-awaited sequel to the 2014 science-fiction anime film Expelled from Paradise.

What is the movie about?

The sequel returns to the conflict between the virtual society of DEVA and life on Earth. Gabriel and other agents pursue the mysterious Fallen—former security personnel who escaped to the surface after the events involving Angela Balzac—expanding the world established by the first movie. Related reading: The Moon on a Rainy Night Anime: 2027 Release, Trailer & Cast.

Who is making the sequel?

Seiji Mizushima returns as director and Gen Urobuchi writes the screenplay. Masatsugu Saito handles character design, NARASAKI composes the music and Toei Animation produces the film, reuniting core creative staff from the original project.

Where to watch

The November 13 date currently refers specifically to the Japanese theatrical release. An international streaming date has not been announced, so AniNextUp is keeping theatrical and streaming availability separate until an official platform announcement is made.
