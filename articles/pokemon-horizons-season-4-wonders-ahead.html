Pokémon Horizons Season 4: February 27 Netflix Date, Story and Trailer

When does Pokémon Horizons Season 4 come out?

Part 1 of Pokémon Horizons: Season 4—Wonders Ahead premieres February 27, 2027 on Netflix. The Pokémon Company International announced the date on October 6, 2026. Its English-language release confirms the same day for viewers in the United States, while the company's Latin American announcement also gives February 27 for that region.

This is the start of Season 4's international Netflix rollout. It should not be read as a promise that every episode will arrive at once: the official wording identifies the launch specifically as Part 1 and says additional episodes will follow later.

Where can you watch Wonders Ahead?

Netflix is the confirmed exclusive home for Part 1 in the United States and Latin America. The English press release covers the U.S.; Pokémon's official Spanish-language release separately confirms Latin America. Viewers in territories outside those announcements should check their regional Pokémon and Netflix listings, because no single worldwide date is stated in the sources reviewed here. The first three seasons are already available on Netflix in the U.S., according to the official announcement. That gives new viewers a direct catch-up route before the February premiere. Availability can differ by country, so the title's presence in one Netflix catalog does not establish another catalog's schedule.

How many episodes are in Part 1?

An episode count has not been announced for Part 1. Pokémon also has not dated the later Season 4 batches. “Part 1” confirms a divided release, but it does not reveal the size of the first batch or the total number of Season 4 episodes. We will treat those figures as unknown until Pokémon or Netflix publishes them.

What is Pokémon Horizons Season 4 about?

The official synopsis begins after Laquium is no longer a threat. Liko, Roy and the Rising Volt Tacklers set out to experience the Pokémon world afresh aboard the Brave Olivine. Their next travels include unusual Pokémon encounters, learning about Pokémon sleep styles and racing alongside Legendary Pokémon. Related reading: THE ONE PIECE: February 2027 Release Date, Episodes & Trailer.

The new chapter continues the characters' growth with their partner Pokémon rather than resetting the story. Pokémon's synopsis also names Dot and Ult among the returning travelers. New faces include a stowaway Ditto and a lively girl named Navie, both highlighted as part of the adventures ahead. The announcement does not identify a single new central villain or disclose the outcome of these storylines.

Do you need to watch the earlier seasons first?

Wonders Ahead is a continuation, so watching the earlier Pokémon Horizons seasons is the clearest way to understand Liko and Roy's development, the Rising Volt Tacklers and why the end of the Laquium threat matters. The official material calls Season 4 a new chapter, but it does not describe it as a reboot or a stand-alone entry.

If time is limited, viewers should at least finish the most recent Season 3 episodes before starting Part 1. The Season 4 synopsis openly builds on the previous conflict, so skipping directly to Wonders Ahead may reveal where that storyline leaves the cast. What does the official trailer confirm?

The 47-second trailer presents the returning group, the Brave Olivine's renewed journey and quick looks at the new encounters described in the synopsis. It is promotional footage rather than an episode guide, so it does not confirm the Part 1 episode count or later release dates.

What time will Season 4 appear on Netflix?

Pokémon's announcements give the calendar date but do not publish an exact Netflix release time. February 27, 2027 falls on a Saturday. A viewer should use the title page inside their own regional Netflix account as the final availability check on release day, because catalog updates and displayed times can depend on territory and account location. AniNextUp will not convert the date into an unsupported midnight estimate.

Searching Netflix for Pokémon Horizons is also more reliable than looking for a separate series page: the official release identifies Wonders Ahead as Season 4 of the continuing show. If Netflix later lists a countdown or episode total, that platform data would answer questions the October announcements leave open.

What remains unknown?

Pokémon has confirmed the title, February 27 date, Netflix platforms for the announced regions, returning team and basic premise. The Part 1 episode count, later-part dates and schedules for other territories remain unannounced. Those distinctions matter when planning a catch-up: February 27 is a verified regional Part 1 premiere, not a confirmed worldwide full-season release.
