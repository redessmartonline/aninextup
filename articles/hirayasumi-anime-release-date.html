Release date and broadcaster

Hirayasumi is confirmed for January 2027 on NHK General in Japan. The exact day and international streaming platform remain unannounced.

What is confirmed about the January 2027 release?

The official announcement confirms a January 2027 television broadcast on NHK General in Japan. That is a release month and broadcaster, not a specific episode date or a worldwide streaming agreement. Readers outside Japan should wait for an international distributor announcement before assuming that a local service will carry the series on the same day.

What to expect from Hirayasumi

Hirayasumi is adapted from Keigo Shinzo's slice-of-life manga, which follows Hiroto and the people around him through everyday encounters and changes in their lives. Its appeal lies in relationships and ordinary routines rather than a tournament or fantasy conflict. The anime's teaser introduces the adaptation, but it does not establish an episode count or confirm how much of the manga will be covered.

Five new voice cast members announced October 13

The official anime team announced five additional performers: Daisuke Namikawa as Hideki Noguchi, Yoko Hikasa as Yomogi Tachibana, Yume Miyamoto as Akari Yokoyama, Yuichiro Umehara as Ryo Ishikawa, and Yuko Naito as the narrator. See the October 13 official cast announcement.

Cast and staff

Yuma Uchida voices Hiroto Ikuta, Mayu Takahashi voices Natsumi Kobayashi and Rikako Aikawa voices Hanae Wada. Production +h. animates the series. Kei Suezawa directs and writes the scripts, Naoyuki Asano designs characters, Mika Nishimura is art director and Kaori Sawada composes music.

How the preview differs from the premiere

The October 7 New York screening was an early preview event, not the start of the Japanese television broadcast or a confirmed worldwide streaming launch. NHK's January 2027 window remains the public broadcast plan, with an exact day and overseas platform still to be announced.

What is Hirayasumi about?

The story follows carefree Hiroto and his cousin Natsumi as they navigate ordinary life, friendships and family around the house Hiroto inherited from a neighbor.

Why Hirayasumi is a different kind of anime premiere

Rather than following a tournament, fantasy adventure or mystery, Hirayasumi focuses on everyday relationships. Hiroto's relaxed outlook contrasts with the worries of people around him, including his younger cousin Natsumi. Their shared life in a modest house provides the setting for a story about work, friendship, family and the uncertainty of growing up.

The adaptation is based on Keigo Shinzo's manga, serialized in Shogakukan's Weekly Big Comic Spirits. Its official introduction places the story in Tokyo neighborhoods including Asagaya and Koenji, with the house Hiroto receives from an older neighbor at the center of the narrative.

October 13 official casting update

The October 13 official production announcement identifies five new performers. The release month is unchanged; the announcement does not establish an exact January premiere date or overseas simulcast.

Who are Hiroto, Natsumi and Hanae?

VIZ Media's cast announcement introduces Hiroto Ikuta as a 29-year-old part-time worker whose easygoing attitude shapes how he approaches daily life. His friendship with Hanae Wada, the older neighbor he calls Granny, leads to the home becoming an important part of his story. Yuma Uchida and Rikako Aikawa voice Hiroto and Hanae in Japanese.

Natsumi Kobayashi, voiced by Mayu Takahashi, is Hiroto's younger cousin. VIZ describes her move from Yamagata to Tokyo to attend art school and her private ambition to become a manga artist. Her concerns and Hiroto's relaxed manner offer two different perspectives on the future as they share a home and meet the people around them.

What does the key visual reveal?

The official visual brings Hiroto and Natsumi together outside the Asagaya house. VIZ identifies the other figures as Hiroto's friend Hideki Noguchi, Natsumi's classmate Akari Yokoyama and real-estate agent Yomogi Tachibana. The image introduces the ensemble and setting; it does not reveal the ending or establish which manga chapters will be included in the first season.

The official English anime site also lists Ryo Ishikawa and a narrator among the announced roles. The October cast update supplies their Japanese voices. An eventual English dub and its performers should be checked against a distributor's own announcement when one becomes available.

Who is making the anime?

Kei Suezawa is credited for direction, series composition and screenplay, while Production +h. handles animation. The official staff page names Naoyuki Asano for character design, Mika Nishimura as art director and Kaori Sawada for music. These credits identify the adaptation's announced creative team; neither the staff list nor the teaser confirms a total episode count.

Readers interested in the original can look for Keigo Shinzo's Hirayasumi manga. VIZ says the series began serialization in Weekly Big Comic Spirits in 2021. The manga offers another way to explore its characters while viewers wait for the television adaptation; its publication schedule is separate from the anime's Japanese broadcast window.
