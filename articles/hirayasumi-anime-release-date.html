What is confirmed about the January 2027 release?

The official announcement confirms a January 2027 television broadcast on NHK General in Japan. That is a release month and broadcaster, not a specific episode date or a worldwide streaming agreement. Readers outside Japan should wait for an international distributor announcement before assuming that a local service will carry the series on the same day.

What to expect from Hirayasumi

Hirayasumi is adapted from Keigo Shinzo's slice-of-life manga, which follows Hiroto and the people around him through everyday encounters and changes in their lives. Its appeal lies in relationships and ordinary routines rather than a tournament or fantasy conflict. The anime's teaser introduces the adaptation, but it does not establish an episode count or confirm how much of the manga will be covered.

Cast and staff

Yuma Uchida voices Hiroto Ikuta, Mayu Takahashi voices Natsumi Kobayashi and Rikako Aikawa voices Hanae Wada. Production +h. animates the series. Kei Suezawa directs and writes the scripts, Naoyuki Asano designs characters, Mika Nishimura is art director and Kaori Sawada composes music.

How the preview differs from the premiere

The October 7 New York screening was an early preview event, not the start of the Japanese television broadcast or a confirmed worldwide streaming launch. NHK's January 2027 window remains the public broadcast plan, with an exact day and overseas platform still to be announced.

Why Hirayasumi is a different kind of anime premiere

Rather than following a tournament, fantasy adventure or mystery, Hirayasumi focuses on everyday relationships. Hiroto's relaxed outlook contrasts with the worries of people around him, including his younger cousin Natsumi. Their shared life in a modest house provides the setting for a story about work, friendship, family and the uncertainty of growing up.

The adaptation is based on Keigo Shinzo's manga, serialized in Shogakukan's Weekly Big Comic Spirits. Its official introduction places the story in Tokyo neighborhoods including Asagaya and Koenji, with the house Hiroto receives from an older neighbor at the center of the narrative.
