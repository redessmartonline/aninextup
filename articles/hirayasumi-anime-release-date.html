When does Hirayasumi come out on Netflix?

Hirayasumi will stream worldwide on Netflix starting in January 2027. The anime’s production team announced the platform and month on November 9, 2026. January is also the window for NHK General’s television broadcast in Japan and for streaming on U-NEXT in Japan. The sources reviewed for this update give no exact launch day for Netflix or NHK. They also leave open whether the services will release the first episode on the same day.

The September news gave viewers a Japanese broadcaster and a month. November’s update adds the worldwide streaming destination, so readers who saw older coverage saying international distribution was pending now have an answer. The important detail for your watchlist is Netflix in January 2027. Check the eventual Netflix listing in your country for the exact date and the audio and subtitle options available there.

Where to watch Hirayasumi in Japan and internationally

  • Netflix worldwide: The official production announcement confirms streaming from January 2027. It gives no weekly release pattern, episode count or English dub details.
  • NHK General in Japan: The Japanese television broadcast is scheduled for January 2027. The station’s eventual broadcast schedule is the place to check for the first episode’s airtime in Japan.
  • U-NEXT in Japan: The same November announcement names U-NEXT as an additional streaming service. It also gives January 2027 as the start window.

If you are following the anime from outside Japan, a future NHK time in JST will help you understand the television schedule. Netflix still needs to give its own timing. For viewers planning to watch together, wait until your regional listing supplies a day and time before sending an invitation or blocking off a night.

The title also belongs to a live-action adaptation. If a search result already shows an episode or a cast of on-screen actors, check which version you opened. The October 7, 2026 Japan Society event in New York was an advance screening of a preview version of the anime’s first episode. Its date belongs to that event, while the public television and streaming launches are scheduled for January.

What is Hirayasumi about?

Hiroto Ikuta is 29 and gets by on part-time work. He strikes up a friendship with Hanae Wada, an older neighbor whose company matters to him long before her bungalow becomes his home. When Hiroto inherits it, his 18-year-old cousin Natsumi moves in while attending art school. He is comfortable letting tomorrow take care of itself. She has to find time and courage for the work she wants to do. Living under the same roof makes each of them more aware of how the other gets through the day.

Asagaya and Koenji, in Tokyo’s Suginami Ward, give the story a world beyond the house. The routines of a neighborhood leave room for a friend to drop by, a classmate to become important, or an ordinary conversation to sit with someone longer than expected. The premise will appeal to viewers who want a series where a visit or a change of season can carry the weight of an episode. The house is also a way to keep Hanae present in the story after the inheritance.

Who is in the Hirayasumi anime cast?

Yuma Uchida voices Hiroto Ikuta, Mayu Takahashi voices Natsumi Kobayashi and Rikako Aikawa voices Hanae Wada. Hiroto and Natsumi share the house at the center of the story, while Hanae’s friendship with Hiroto explains how he came to live there. That makes the September teaser a useful introduction even if you have never opened the manga.

The October cast update named Daisuke Namikawa as Hideki Noguchi, Yoko Hikasa as Yomogi Tachibana, Yume Miyamoto as Akari Yokoyama, Yuichiro Umehara as Ryo Ishikawa and Yuko Naito as the narrator. Hideki is Hiroto’s friend, Akari is Natsumi’s classmate, and Yomogi works in real estate. Kei Suezawa directs and handles series composition and screenplay, with Production +h. animating and Naoyuki Asano designing the characters. These are the Japanese production credits. Check Netflix’s eventual listing if you want to watch with an English dub.

Where to start the Hirayasumi manga in English

Start with Hirayasumi, Vol. 1 by Keigo Shinzo. VIZ Signature released the English edition on May 21, 2024, and VIZ offers a free preview of Volume 1. The publisher lists paperback and digital formats. For the English paperback, the ISBN is 978-1-9747-4691-0. Use it if a bookstore search shows multiple languages or jumps to a later volume.

The manga began serialization in Shogakukan’s Weekly Big Comic Spirits in 2021. The opening volume gives you time with Hiroto and Hanae and lets you see how Natsumi fits into the household. It is the place to start whether you want to read one volume before January or continue through the numbered editions. The anime’s official materials have yet to specify which chapters the season will cover, so the book number is more reliable than a guessed episode-to-chapter conversion.

If you want the anime to introduce the characters first, save the VIZ preview for after the premiere and avoid the summaries for later volumes. Some publisher descriptions reveal events well past the opening setup. If you already read the first volume, the VIZ series page is a useful way to find the next one and check which editions are available in English.

January 2027 is the confirmed window. The November streaming announcement leaves the exact Netflix date and time, the release pattern and the dub lineup open. Can you start with the anime if you saw the live-action drama?

Yes. Both adaptations draw on Keigo Shinzo’s manga, and the anime introduces Hiroto, Natsumi and the house from the beginning. There is no viewing order that requires you to finish the drama before starting the anime. If you remember the basic premise and want to compare the versions, the opening stretch gives you a common reference point: Hiroto’s friendship with Hanae and the arrival of Natsumi in Tokyo.

For a first look at the source, VIZ lists Volume 1 at 192 pages. Read the preview if you want to see how Shinzo uses the space between conversations before committing to a full volume. The anime teaser offers a different sample of the same setting through movement, voices and music. Pick the preview that matches how you want to meet the characters, then start with Volume 1 or Episode 1.
