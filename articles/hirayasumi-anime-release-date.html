Hirayasumi Anime: January 2027 Release, Trailer & Cast

The anime adaptation of Keigo Shinzo's manga will air on NHK General in January 2027.

Release date and broadcaster

Hirayasumi is confirmed for January 2027 on NHK General in Japan. The exact day and international streaming platform remain unannounced.

Official teaser trailer

Cast and staff

Yuma Uchida voices Hiroto Ikuta, Mayu Takahashi voices Natsumi Kobayashi and Rikako Aikawa voices Hanae Wada. Production +h. animates the series. Kei Suezawa directs and writes the scripts, Naoyuki Asano designs characters, Mika Nishimura is art director and Kaori Sawada composes music.

What changed and why it matters

VIZ Media's new teaser and cast reveal provide the first character performances. An early episode screening was scheduled for October 7, 2026 in New York, ahead of the Japanese TV broadcast. The preview is not an international streaming release.

What is Hirayasumi about?

The story follows carefree Hiroto and his cousin Natsumi as they navigate ordinary life, friendships and family around the house Hiroto inherited from a neighbor.

Where to watch

NHK General is confirmed in Japan. Streaming platforms and territories outside Japan are not confirmed.

FAQ

Is the exact January date known? No, only the month is confirmed.
Is the trailer official? Yes, the VIZ teaser above is for this adaptation.
Is international streaming confirmed? Not yet.
