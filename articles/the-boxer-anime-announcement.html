The Boxer Anime Trailer: Official Teaser, Cast and Release Date

The acclaimed Korean boxing webtoon by JH is being adapted into a television anime. A teaser, four lead voice actors and key production staff are confirmed, but no premiere date has been announced.

Official WEBTOON Entertainment announcement

Is The Boxer getting an anime?

Yes. The Boxer, the webtoon by JH, has been confirmed as a television anime. The adaptation was announced at the Crunchyroll Showcase during New York Comic Con on October 8, 2026. LINE Digital Frontier published its Japanese announcement the next day with the main cast, production staff and an official teaser PV. This is a confirmed project rather than a rumor, but the announcement does not give a premiere date.

When does The Boxer anime come out?

No release month, year, episode count or Japanese broadcast slot has been announced as of October 23, 2026. Crunchyroll has said it will stream the series, but the available announcement does not name a launch date or a complete list of territories. Treat posts predicting a 2027 debut, a weekly schedule or a particular episode total as speculation unless the production or platform confirms those details. When a date arrives, check whether it refers to the Japanese broadcast, Crunchyroll’s release, or both; those can be separate announcements.

Who is in the confirmed voice cast?

The first four announced performers are Koki Uchiyama as Yu, Kensho Ono as Ryu, Tomokazu Seki as trainer K, and Toshiyuki Toyonaga as J. LINE Digital Frontier’s Japanese release uses the names Yuki and Ryusanpaku for Yu and Ryu in the Japanese edition of the source work. English-language coverage generally uses the romanized names Yu and Ryu. These are naming differences across editions, not extra characters. The announcement identifies the roles, but it does not yet provide a complete cast list for every character in the webtoon.

Which studio is making the anime?

Animation production is being handled jointly by Japan’s breccia and South Korea’s STUDIO JEMI. Takehiro Kubota and KANG Wonyeong are the co-directors. Toshiya Ono is responsible for series composition and scripts, and Masahiro Suwa is the character designer. BLUE RIGHTS is credited as creative producer, while HAYATE is named as producer. These credits come from the project announcement; no episode count, production schedule or broadcast staff additions have been confirmed.

What is The Boxer about?

The story begins with trainer K searching for one last prodigy to train. He finds Yu, a teenage boy being beaten in an alley, who appears detached from the violence around him. K notices that Yu’s movement and reactions reveal an unusual natural ability, even though the boy shows no obvious fighting spirit. The series follows Yu into competitive boxing and also gives attention to the challengers who face him. That premise sets up a contrast between physical talent and the emotional or personal costs that may come with it; it does not mean every opponent is simply a hurdle for the protagonist.

Where can you read the original webtoon?

The Boxer is a Korean webtoon written and drawn by JH. It began serialization on NAVER WEBTOON in 2019, and LINE Manga carried its Japanese edition from 2020 through 2022. LINE Digital Frontier reported more than 1.06 billion views worldwide as of September 2026. That is a publisher-reported figure, not an independently audited sales total. Readers can use the official LINE Manga page to find the Japanese edition; availability and language options depend on the service and region.

How should readers track new details?

Keep the announcement, cast list and release schedule separate when comparing updates. A project announcement confirms that the adaptation exists; a cast release confirms performers and roles; a schedule page is what establishes a premiere date or weekly broadcast time. For The Boxer, the first two are available, but the production has not published a schedule. A streaming commitment also does not tell viewers the date or country-by-country catalog. Checking the production partners’ official pages helps distinguish a new confirmed detail from a prediction repeated by fan accounts.

What has not been announced?

There is no confirmed premiere date, broadcast network, episode count, episode length, international territory list or schedule for future trailers. Crunchyroll’s streaming commitment answers where the project is expected to be available in at least some markets, but it does not establish when every country will receive it.
