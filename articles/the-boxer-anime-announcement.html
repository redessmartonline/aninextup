The Boxer Anime: Release Date, Cast, Studios & Teaser

The acclaimed Korean boxing webtoon by JH is being adapted into a television anime. A teaser, four lead voice actors and key production staff are confirmed, but no premiere date has been announced.

Official WEBTOON Entertainment announcement

Is The Boxer getting an anime?

Yes. The Boxer, the Korean webtoon created by JH, has been confirmed as a television anime. The adaptation was announced during the Crunchyroll Showcase at New York Comic Con on October 8, 2026. WEBTOON Entertainment announced the partnership, and LINE Digital Frontier published the Japanese project details the following day, including cast, staff and an official announcement PV. This confirms that the anime is in production; it does not confirm when it will premiere.

When will The Boxer anime be released?

No premiere month, year, Japanese broadcast slot or episode count has been announced as of October 23, 2026. Crunchyroll has said it plans to stream the series in the future, but that statement is not a launch schedule. It also does not identify every country where the show will be available. Treat a predicted 2027 debut or a claimed weekly schedule as speculation unless the production committee, broadcaster or platform publishes it.

For viewers tracking updates, keep three questions separate: when the Japanese broadcast begins, when streaming starts, and which territories receive the stream. A date for one service or country would not automatically answer the other two. AniNextUp will update this guide when an official source supplies those details.

Who is in The Boxer anime cast?

The first four announced voice actors are Koki Uchiyama as Yu, Kensho Ono as Ryu, Tomokazu Seki as trainer K, and Toshiyuki Toyonaga as J. The Japanese announcement uses the character names Yuki and Ryusanpaku for Yu and Ryu, while English-language coverage commonly uses Yu and Ryu. These are differences in how the names are rendered across editions, not additional characters. The announcement identifies these four roles; it is not a complete cast list for the full webtoon.

Which studios and staff are making the anime?

Japan’s breccia and South Korea’s STUDIO JEMI are handling animation production. Takehiro Kubota and KANG Wonyeong are the co-directors. Toshiya Ono is credited with series composition and scripts, and Masahiro Suwa is the character designer. BLUE RIGHTS is the creative producer, while HAYATE is named as producer. These are the project’s announced credits; no episode count or production timetable has been released.

What is The Boxer about?

The story follows Yu, a teenager whose extraordinary reactions and movement catch the attention of trainer K. K is searching for a final prodigy to train when he encounters Yu being attacked. Yu appears emotionally detached, but K recognizes a rare physical talent. The boxing story then places Yu against opponents who have their own ambitions and pressures.

That setup gives the series a different question from a conventional underdog story: what happens when ability arrives before motivation or emotional connection? The premise invites readers to watch how Yu, K and the people they meet respond to that imbalance. It does not establish that every opponent is merely an obstacle or reveal how the anime will adapt later developments. This summary stays with the officially introduced setup and avoids spoilers.

Where can you read the original webtoon?

JH’s The Boxer began serialization on NAVER WEBTOON in 2019. LINE Manga carried its Japanese edition from 2020 through 2022. LINE Digital Frontier reported more than 1.06 billion worldwide views as of September 2026; that is a publisher-reported platform figure, not an independently audited sales total or a count of anime viewers. Readers can find the Japanese edition on the official LINE Manga page. Language options and access depend on the service and region, so check the platform’s listing for your location.

What is confirmed, and what remains unknown?

Confirmed details include the television adaptation, the four named voice actors, the listed production staff, and Crunchyroll’s future streaming plan. Still unknown are the premiere date, broadcast network, episode total, episode length, complete international territory list and timing for another trailer. A platform commitment tells viewers where the anime is expected to stream in some markets; it does not answer when it will appear in each catalog. This distinction helps separate confirmed news from fan predictions.
