Romelia War Chronicle: Release Date & Where to Watch

Romelia War Chronicle premieres October 3, 2026 on Crunchyroll. Release date, studio and streaming details for Fall 2026.

RELEASE DATEOctober 3, 2026
STREAMINGCrunchyroll
STUDIOAtra

When does Romelia War Chronicle premiere?

Romelia War Chronicle begins streaming October 3, 2026, ahead of its Japanese television premiere on October 5. The production is planned as a two-cour television anime, giving the postwar story more room than a single short seasonal run.

Where to watch

Crunchyroll carries the anime internationally outside Japan, mainland China and South Korea. In Japan, Anime Times, U-NEXT and Netflix begin streaming on October 3 at 9:00 p.m. JST. Regional release timing may vary.

What is Romelia War Chronicle about?

Romelia helped Prince Henri defeat Demon King Zelgis, only for Henri to end their engagement after the victory. Instead of treating the Demon King's defeat as the end of the story, Romelia returns to a homeland still devastated by war and decides to rebuild it herself.

Her work includes organizing an army, restoring the economy and confronting political conspiracies. The premise therefore focuses on what happens after the traditional fantasy victory, when a country still has to recover from the damage left behind.

Why the October 3 and October 5 dates are different

These dates refer to two ways of accessing the same series, not two separate premieres. The October 3 date describes the announced streaming rollout, while October 5 is the Japanese television broadcast start. For viewers outside Japan, neither date alone guarantees that an episode will appear at the same hour on every regional service. Check the listing for your country before planning to watch on release day.

Why rebuilding is a different kind of challenge

Romelia's return home puts military organization, economic recovery and political resistance on the same level as battlefield success. The premise asks whether a victory over a single enemy can become lasting stability for ordinary people. That gives viewers a way to understand the story's focus without presuming which rebuilding efforts will succeed.

Who may enjoy Romelia's postwar fantasy?

The official premise begins where many fantasy adventures end: after the Demon King has been defeated. Romelia's next tasks involve soldiers, reconstruction and competing political interests rather than a new quest to defeat that same enemy. Readers who prefer strategy and the consequences of war may find that emphasis more relevant than the broken engagement alone. This is AniNextUp's interpretation of the announced setup, not a claim about later episodes or Romelia's ultimate success.

Who is making the anime?

Animation studio Atra produces the series. Koji Saito serves as series director, Aya Satsuki handles series composition and scripts, and KOHTA YAMAMOTO composes the music. Yuna Nemoto voices Romelia von Graham.
