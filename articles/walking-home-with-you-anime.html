When does Walking Home with You premiere?

The anime is officially scheduled to begin airing on Nippon TV in April 2027. An exact premiere day has not yet been announced.

What changed?

The adaptation now has both a confirmed television format and release month. The announcement also introduced the first anime teaser visual, turning the earlier adaptation announcement into a concrete Spring 2027 release target.

What is Walking Home with You about?

Mai Matsuda's school romantic comedy follows Shun Sato, a new high-school student who gave up his soccer dream after an injury, and his unusual upperclassman Naoki Sato. Their relationship develops through the so-called Hyper Going-Home Club and the everyday detours they share after school.

Why this is not simply a sports comeback story

Shun's abandoned soccer dream is part of the setup, but the announced story centers on the everyday connection he develops with Naoki through their after-school routine. That makes the injury an important piece of character background rather than proof that the anime will focus on competitive soccer. The official teaser announcement does not establish whether Shun returns to the sport, so a comeback arc should not be treated as confirmed.

Where did Walking Home with You begin?

Mai Matsuda writes and illustrates the original manga. The anime announcement identifies Futabasha's Manga Action and Web Action as its publication venues and describes the adaptation as a youth romantic comedy about Shun and his senior Naoki. Naoki is nicknamed Chokki-chan, a reference to her habit of saying she wants to go straight home. Through the Hyper Going-Home Club, the pair spend time together after school instead of following a conventional club's practice schedule. Shun's injury is part of his background, while their shared detours give the story its day-to-day focus.

Does the teaser visual contain a release day?

No. The production's October announcement supplies an April 2027 television window and a first visual, but not a specific calendar day or a distributor outside Japan. A future cast or trailer announcement may add detail; until then, the month is the appropriate level of precision for a viewing calendar.

The manga setup behind the title

The Japanese title is Hōkago Kitaku Biyori, and the story starts with a simple after-school choice: whether to go straight home or spend time with someone who wants company. The manga follows Naoki, an upperclassman known for her “straight-home” habit, and Shun, a younger student drawn into her routine. That small-scale premise gives the series a different kind of romantic-comedy engine from a competition story or a high-stakes school mystery. The hook is the daily negotiation of time, companionship and personal boundaries.

The anime’s April window is therefore useful for readers deciding whether to follow an adaptation of the manga, but the official announcement does not yet tell us how many chapters or volumes the television season will cover. It is too early to promise a particular arc, ending point or episode count. For now, the reliable checklist is short: April 2027 in Japan, Nippon TV, a teaser visual, and further cast, staff and streaming information still pending.
