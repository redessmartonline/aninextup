Cyberpunk: Edgerunners 2 Release Date, Episode Count & Netflix

Night City returns with a new crew. Cyberpunk: Edgerunners 2 premieres globally on Netflix on October 20.

WHERE TO WATCHNetflix

When does Cyberpunk: Edgerunners 2 release?

Cyberpunk: Edgerunners 2 premieres globally on Netflix on October 20, 2026. CD PROJEKT RED and TRIGGER return to Night City, but this is a new standalone installment rather than a direct continuation of David's story.

How many episodes?

The new series is confirmed for 10 episodes. Netflix describes it as a self-contained story of redemption and revenge with an entirely new crew of mercenaries and a reimagined animation style.

What is the story?

Weak, a former cyberpunk legend, has to find a new purpose while living without chrome. At the same time, nomad D follows a path of revenge that draws her toward corporate secrets. Their stories become intertwined across Night City.

Who is making Edgerunners 2?

CD PROJEKT RED and animation studio TRIGGER reunite for the project. Kai Ikarashi directs, Kanno Ichigo serves as lead character designer, and Bartosz Sztybor and Masahiko Otsuka are among the returning creative leads.

Where to watch

Cyberpunk: Edgerunners 2 streams exclusively on Netflix beginning October 20.
