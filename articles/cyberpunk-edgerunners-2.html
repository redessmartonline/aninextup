What a standalone sequel changes

The new ten-episode story returns to Night City without continuing David's personal arc. Weak's search for purpose without chrome and D's pursuit of revenge provide two different starting points for exploring the city's pressures. Viewers familiar with the first series will recognize the setting, but should not assume the new protagonists repeat the original crew's journey.

Why the new cast matters

Night City remains the shared setting, but a standalone story with new protagonists gives the sequel room to explore different consequences of its violent, technology-driven world. When does Cyberpunk: Edgerunners 2 release?

Cyberpunk: Edgerunners 2 premieres globally on Netflix on October 20, 2026. CD PROJEKT RED and TRIGGER return to Night City, but this is a new standalone installment rather than a direct continuation of David's story.

Is this a continuation of David's story?

The new series shares Night City with the original but introduces Weak and D as its central characters. The announced story is standalone rather than a direct continuation of David's personal journey. The confirmed ten-episode format also does not establish whether Netflix will release every episode simultaneously.

How many episodes?

The new series is confirmed for 10 episodes. Netflix describes it as a self-contained story of redemption and revenge with an entirely new crew of mercenaries and a reimagined animation style.

What is the story?

Weak, a former cyberpunk legend, has to find a new purpose while living without chrome. At the same time, nomad D follows a path of revenge that draws her toward corporate secrets. Their stories become intertwined across Night City.

Who is making Edgerunners 2?

CD PROJEKT RED and animation studio TRIGGER reunite for the project. Kai Ikarashi directs, Kanno Ichigo serves as lead character designer, and Bartosz Sztybor and Masahiko Otsuka are among the returning creative leads.

Where to watch

Cyberpunk: Edgerunners 2 streams exclusively on Netflix beginning October 20.
