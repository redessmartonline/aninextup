Cyberpunk: Edgerunners 2 Release Date, Episode Count & Netflix

Night City returns with a new crew. Cyberpunk: Edgerunners 2 premieres globally on Netflix on October 20.

WHERE TO WATCHNetflix

Cyberpunk: Edgerunners 2 arrives on Netflix on October 20, 2026. Netflix describes the follow-up as a new standalone story set in the world of Cyberpunk 2077.

How many episodes?

The new installment is confirmed for 10 episodes. It follows a fresh group of mercenaries rather than continuing the first season’s story directly.

What is the story?

The new story centers on redemption and revenge in Night City, with a new crew and a reimagined animation style.

Where to watch

Cyberpunk: Edgerunners 2 will stream on Netflix beginning October 20.
