When does Cyberpunk: Edgerunners 2 come out?

The Netflix release date is October 20, 2026. Both Netflix Tudum and CD PROJEKT RED’s official anime website publish that date.

The new anime is a Netflix exclusive. Open the official Netflix title page to check the available episodes. Local availability, audio and subtitle options should be checked in your country’s catalog.

How many episodes, and is this David’s story?

Netflix confirms a standalone, ten-episode story with a different crew. Night City connects the two installments, but the new story follows a separate group rather than promising a continuation of David’s journey.

What is the new story about?

The official synopsis follows Weak, a former edgerunner legend trying to rebuild his life without chrome, and D, a nomad pursuing revenge and dangerous corporate secrets. Their paths form part of a wider story about family, obsession and legacy.

Who is making the anime?

CD PROJEKT RED and TRIGGER are producing the installment. The official site credits director Kai Ikarashi, lead character designer Kanno Ichigo, and story creatives Bartosz Sztybor and Masahiko Otsuka.

The official character page lists Kentaro Tone as Weak and Koki Uchiyama as D, the two characters at the center of the published synopsis. The full character and voice cast list is on CD PROJEKT RED’s official site.

Meet the new Night City crew

The official character profiles offer a spoiler-light introduction to the people around Weak and D. Weak was once a celebrated edgerunner known as King; now his former reputation contrasts with a life without cybernetic enhancements. D, meanwhile, is a Snake Nation netrunner whose search for the person responsible for killing his clan gives his pursuit of revenge a personal starting point.

Another figure in the ensemble is Roman Carax, a young film enthusiast looking for genuine stories in a city dominated by braindance. Talia comes from a corporate background and has also lived under the influence of the Maelstrom gang. These descriptions come from the production's character introductions; they establish who the characters are at the outset without revealing how their stories end.

The Japanese voice credits on the official page include Kentaro Tone as Weak, Koki Uchiyama as D, Momoka Terasawa as Roman and Akari Kito as Talia. Avida, voiced by Akemi Okamura, is also among the announced characters. Those are the Japanese cast credits; viewers should check Netflix's language menu for the credits and audio available in their own region.

How does it connect to Cyberpunk 2077?

Both installments take place in the world of Cyberpunk 2077, and the shared setting gives them a common visual and social backdrop. The official description identifies this installment as a new standalone story with a new cast. That means a newcomer can approach the second installment on its own terms, while someone familiar with Night City may recognize aspects of the setting.

The anime is a series in the game's universe, not an expansion that must be installed or a game purchase required to watch it. Netflix is the viewing platform. For players interested in related game updates or other franchise news, the official Cyberpunk site is the appropriate place to check separate announcements; those announcements should not be confused with the anime's episode list.

What can viewers expect from the creative team?

CD PROJEKT RED and animation studio TRIGGER collaborate again, with Kai Ikarashi directing this installment. The official credits also name Kanno Ichigo as lead character designer and Bartosz Sztybor and Masahiko Otsuka among the story creatives. Returning collaborators and a new director help distinguish the production's continuity from its new story and characters.

The series is aimed at mature viewers. Its Netflix listing describes it as gritty and violent; ratings, subtitles and available audio can differ by country. Check the title's local rating and content information before watching, particularly when choosing a program for a household with younger viewers.
