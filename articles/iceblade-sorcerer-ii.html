What happens at the start of Season 2?

The official episode introduction describes the Magicks War as a newly organized team competition that crosses academy boundaries. Ray White forms a team with Amelia Rose and Ariane Olgren, while Evi and Albert also prepare to enter. Ray gives Amelia and Ariane separate training to address their weaknesses before the contest.

This setup gives returning viewers a clear point of entry: the season brings familiar students together around a contest, while their preparation and relationships create challenges of their own. The official summary of the next episode also describes Ariane visiting Ray's family home for training. These are introductory details, not a claim about the tournament's eventual winner or later revelations.

Who voices Ray and his friends in Japanese?

TBS credits Junya Enoki as Ray White, Iori Saeki as Amelia Rose and Akira Sekine as Ariane Olgren. The broadcaster's cast listing also names Miyu Kubota as Elisa Griffith, Azumi Waki as Rebecca Bradley and Kaede Hondo as Clarisse Cleveland. These are the Japanese voice credits; cast and audio availability can differ for other language editions.

TBS lists Masahiro Takata as director, series composer and sound director for this season, with Mariko Kawamoto handling character design and ZERO-G producing the animation. The first season's credits and the new season's credits should be checked separately when comparing the production teams.

Opening and ending songs

The official music page identifies “Diamond Dust” by Sizuk as the opening and “COMPRESSION :[” by Maaya Uchida as the ending. The production announced creditless versions of the first episode's opening and ending on October 9. Those videos present the theme sequences without the usual overlaid credits; they are promotional clips rather than additional episodes.

Is there an English dub?

Crunchyroll announced on October 24 that the Season 2 English dub was streaming, with Kevin D. Thelwell as Ray and Alexis Tipton as Amelia in its Episode 1 cast listing. Check the series page in your country for currently available dubbed episodes and languages; a dub announcement does not establish the same rollout for every territory.
