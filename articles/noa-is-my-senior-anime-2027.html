When will Noa Is My Senior, and My Friend premiere?

The television anime Noa Is My Senior, and My Friend (Noa-senpai wa Tomodachi.) is scheduled to air in 2027. The production confirmed the year in an October 11, 2026 update alongside its first key visual and the two main voice actors. No exact month, day, television channel or international streaming service has been announced. A more precise release date would be speculation.

Meet the two confirmed lead voice actors

The official cast announcement puts the central coworkers in focus:

  • Rina Kawaguchi voices Noa Saotome, an accomplished art director at a video game company who hides a more vulnerable side behind her professional reputation.
  • Tomoya Ito voices Rihito Otsuka, a second-year employee who tries to conserve energy and avoid unnecessary drama.

These are the two announced leads as of October 12. Other characters may appear in the series, but no additional voice cast should be treated as confirmed until the production announces it.

What does the new key visual show?

The first key visual depicts Noa and Rihito sitting together, emphasizing the unlikely friendship at the center of the story. It is different from the earlier teaser visual released when the anime adaptation was announced in July 2026. Studio and production staff

feel. is animating the series. Shinsuke Yanagi is directing, Keiichiro Ochi is responsible for series composition, and Yumiko Yamamoto is designing the characters. The core staff were identified with the original adaptation announcement, and the October cast update provides a clearer sense of the show's lead performances.

What is the story about?

Noa Saotome seems to have her career under control. As an art director at a game development company, she earns the respect of managers and younger colleagues alike. Rihito Otsuka, meanwhile, is in his second year at the company and approaches daily life with a determinedly low-energy attitude. One day, he accidentally discovers a side of Noa she has carefully concealed at work.

That encounter begins an unusual after-hours friendship. Rather than centering on a fantasy world or a high school, Noa Is My Senior, and My Friend explores how two very different adults negotiate workplace expectations, personal boundaries and the meaning of being friends. Where did the original manga come from?

The anime adapts Enma Akiyama's manga, which began running in Shueisha's Weekly Young Jump in July 2023. Eleven collected volumes had been released in Japan by July 2026, and Volume 12 was released in Japan on October 19, 2026. The manga's continuing serialization means the anime is adapting an established ongoing story rather than an original television screenplay.

Where will the anime stream?

What is the original manga's setting?

Enma Akiyama's manga places its leads at a video game company. The official anime introduction describes Noa as a respected art director and Rihito as a junior employee who discovers a side of her she normally hides at work. Their friendship develops after the working day ends.

The professional setting gives the series a different backdrop from a school comedy, while the official material leaves later story developments for viewers to discover. The production has named the lead cast and animation staff, but has not supplied a precise 2027 premiere date or confirmed an international platform.
