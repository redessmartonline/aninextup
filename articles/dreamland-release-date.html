How Terrence's fears shape the premise

Dreamland connects its fantasy setting to Terrence's personal experience: confronting a fear of fire is the starting point for his journey as a Traveler. The premise suggests a story about how fears are experienced and faced, rather than a journey driven only by exploration. This is editorial interpretation, not a claim about events beyond the published synopsis.

Streaming versus television dates

Crunchyroll's weekly calendar published October 11 places Episode 1, in Japanese and French editions, on Saturday, October 17. Earlier September announcements listed October 16. The October 11 weekly calendar now places Episode 1 on Saturday, October 17; this is the current service schedule used here. ADN separately confirms October 17 for its own service. Japan's television premiere is in the October 17 late-night slot (October 18 at 2:00 a.m. JST in ordinary calendar notation). Viewers should check their regional Crunchyroll listing instead of assuming the Japanese 2:00 a.m. JST broadcast is the worldwide streaming time.

When does Dreamland release?

Crunchyroll's Spanish-language weekly calendar dated October 11 lists Episode 1 on October 17, 2026. ADN's own announcement lists October 17 for its streaming launch. These platform dates should not be treated as one universal release date. Check the current local episode listing before setting a reminder. Its Japanese television broadcast is listed in the October 17 late-night ANiMAZiNG!!! slot on ABC TV and the TV Asahi network at 26:00, equivalent to October 18 at 2:00 a.m. JST. The international streaming date and Japanese television date are separate schedules.

Where to watch Dreamland

Crunchyroll lists the series for North America, Central America, South America, Europe, Africa, Oceania, the Middle East and CIS. Availability can still vary within individual catalogs, so viewers should check their local service listing.

What is Dreamland about?

Terrence has lived with a fear of fire since losing his mother. When he finally confronts that fear inside his nightmares, he becomes a Traveler—someone capable of exploring Dreamland, a world created from humanity's dreams and nightmares.

Who is making the series?

Dreamland adapts the French comic by Reno Lemaire. Juan Pablo Machado directs at Ellipse Animation and La Chouette Compagnie, with series composition credited to Jean-Luc Cano and Antoine Maurel. The Japanese version features a localized voice cast for its domestic broadcast.

Opening and ending themes

The Japanese-version promotional campaign also confirmed Faulieu. for the opening theme “movin'on” and Ai Tomioka for the ending theme “dream catcher.”
