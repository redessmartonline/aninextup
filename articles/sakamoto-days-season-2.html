When does Sakamoto Days Season 2 come out?

SAKAMOTO DAYS Season 2 premieres in January 2027. Netflix has announced the month, but it has not confirmed a specific day or release hour. Until the service or the anime’s production committee posts a dated schedule, January is the most precise confirmed window.

The second season will stream on Netflix. The announcement does not give a separate release schedule for other services, so this guide will be updated if additional platforms or territory-specific dates are confirmed.

What does the new trailer reveal?

The official character trailer introduces three faces expected to matter in the next chapter: Etsuko Satoda, Sakamoto’s former teacher at JCC; Amane Yotsumura, a student at the assassin training institution; and Satoru Yatsumura, whose history is tied to Sakamoto. Netflix’s October update also revisits the season’s central setup: Sakamoto and Shin go undercover at JCC while members of ORDER face a dangerous assignment in Kyoto. What is Season 2 about?

The story follows Sakamoto and Shin as they enter JCC, Japan’s assassin training institution, to investigate the elusive figure known as X, or Slur. At the same time, ORDER members Nagumo, Shishiba, and Osaragi travel to Kyoto for an assignment that turns into an ambush. Netflix’s synopsis says the season will also bring the shared past of Sakamoto, Nagumo, Rion, and Slur into focus.

That setup gives the sequel two connected threads: Sakamoto’s undercover work at the school and a wider conflict involving the assassin organization. The official materials point to those story elements without confirming how many manga chapters the season will adapt.

Who is in the Sakamoto Days Season 2 cast?

Netflix’s current Season 2 updates identify the returning characters Natsuki Seba and Akira Akao, alongside new characters Etsuko Satoda, Amane Yotsumura, and Satoru Yatsumura. The latest Netflix announcement does not provide voice-actor credits for every new character, so we are keeping the character list separate from unconfirmed casting details.

Netflix previously listed the Season 1 Japanese leads as Tomokazu Sugita (Taro Sakamoto), Nobunaga Shimazaki (Shin Asakura), Ayane Sakura (Lu Shaotang), Nao Tōyama (Aoi Sakamoto), and Hina Kino (Hana Sakamoto). Those are Season 1 credits; AniNextUp will update this section when Netflix confirms the Season 2 voice cast.

Where can you watch Season 1?

Netflix lists both parts of Season 1 on its service. Catching up before January is the clearest way to follow Sakamoto’s move from retired hitman to shop owner whose old world keeps pulling him back in.

Who is making Sakamoto Days Season 2?

TMS Entertainment is producing the new season. Netflix credits Daisuke Nakajima as director and Masaki Watanabe as supervisor, with Taku Kishimoto handling series composition. Yo Moriyama is responsible for character design, and Yuki Hayashi is composing the music. These are the production credits announced for Season 2, rather than a prediction about how individual episodes will be staffed.

For viewers who enjoyed the contrast between ordinary family life and high-stakes fights in the first season, the premise of the sequel keeps both sides of that world in view. The JCC investigation sends Sakamoto back into an institution connected to his past, while the Kyoto assignment shifts attention to the professional assassins of ORDER.

What should you remember before watching?

Taro Sakamoto has left his former life as a feared assassin behind and now runs a neighborhood store with his family. Shin, a young man who can read minds, becomes an important ally as threats from Sakamoto’s past return. Season 1 establishes their partnership and the wider network of assassins that makes a quiet retirement difficult.

The upcoming investigation also centers on X, known as Slur, whose connection to Sakamoto and his former associates is central to the new season’s setup. Nagumo, Shishiba, and Osaragi belong to ORDER, so their Kyoto mission offers another perspective on the conflict. If these names sound unfamiliar, watching both parts of Season 1 on Netflix is a useful starting point before the sequel arrives.

Is there an exact release date or episode schedule?

January 2027 is a confirmed month, not a specific premiere date. Netflix has not announced the total episode count or whether episodes will arrive weekly for this season. A previous release pattern should not be treated as a guarantee: check the official Netflix listing for your region as the premiere approaches, particularly if you want to plan around a local release time.
