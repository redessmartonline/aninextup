About AniNextUp

Learn how AniNextUp tracks anime release dates, streaming availability, official trailers and seasonal schedules.

AniNextUp is an independent anime release and streaming guide focused on helping viewers quickly find when anime premieres, where it streams and what has been officially confirmed.

We build release calendars, weekly trackers and individual guides using official announcements from studios, distributors, streaming services and anime production committees whenever possible.

Our editorial approach

Release dates and availability can change by region. We distinguish confirmed information from details that are still to be announced, and update guides when official schedules change. Official trailers may be embedded to add context, but AniNextUp's articles, organization and release tracking are independently produced.

Corrections

If you find an outdated date, platform or attribution, please use our contact page so we can review it.
