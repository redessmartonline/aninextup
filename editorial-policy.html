AniNextUp Editorial Policy

How we verify release dates, streaming availability, trailers and changes before publishing them.

What AniNextUp publishes

AniNextUp is an independent anime release and streaming guide. Our goal is practical: help viewers understand what is releasing, when it releases, where it can be watched and which details are actually confirmed.

How we verify information

We prioritize first-party sources: official anime websites, production committees, studios, publishers, distributors and official streaming services. When useful, reputable industry reporting can help us discover an announcement, but important release claims are checked against the strongest available source whenever possible.

Confirmed vs. TBA

We do not turn rumors, leaks or estimates into confirmed dates. If an episode count, platform, international release time, cast member or other detail has not been reliably announced, we label it TBA or explain that it remains unconfirmed.

Trailers and images

We prefer official key visuals and official trailers. Trailer embeds are added only when we can identify a reliable official upload. We avoid presenting fan art, unofficial reuploads or unrelated artwork as official promotional material.

Updates

Release guides are living pages. We update them when a date changes, a streaming service announces availability, a trailer arrives or another useful official detail becomes available. A modification date is used when a page receives a meaningful editorial update.

Corrections

If information becomes outdated or we identify an error, we correct the page rather than preserving an inaccurate claim. Readers can report a possible error through our contact page.

Editorial authorship

Release guides are published by the AniNextUp Editorial Team. Research and organization may use digital tools to help track announcements efficiently, but factual release claims are intended to be grounded in identifiable sources and reviewed before publication.

Why we publish

AniNextUp exists to reduce the work viewers have to do across scattered announcements, time zones and streaming catalogs. We aim to add value through organization, context, release tracking and clear separation between confirmed and pending information.
